## HOW THIS WORKS

1. **Invite sent** → logged here with the exact note used
2. **Response received** → tell Claude what they said, Claude drafts the reply
3. **Conversation continues** → log each exchange; Claude helps keep it productive
4. **Goal** → every conversation should move toward: referral, advice, introduction, or job intel

**Status codes:**
- 🟡 PENDING — invite sent, no response yet
- 🟢 REPLIED — they responded, conversation active
- 🔵 WARM — ongoing productive convo
- ✅ CONVERTED — led to referral / intro / job lead
- ❌ NO RESPONSE — 2+ weeks, move on
