## WHAT MAKES A SHOWCASE POST FAIL

- Showing a screenshot without explaining what it does or why it matters
- Listing tools without showing outputs ("I used Excel and Power BI")
- No key finding or insight (just "here's my dashboard")
- Vague results ("improved efficiency significantly")
- No document/image attachment — text-only posts get far less engagement
- Posting about a project then saying "DM me for the file" — friction kills reach

### Post cadence suggestion
- **Week 1:** Share the model/analysis (post the project)
- **Week 2:** Share a key finding from the analysis (insight post)
- **Week 3:** Share a "lesson learned" from building it (process reflection)
- This gives 3 posts per project, same as Kavish Kaul's series approach

This positions Ashnad as someone who thinks like an FP&A analyst (forward-looking, driver-based, scenario-tested) — not just someone who knows Excel.

### The automation angle
Ashnad's unique differentiator: Ultimate Trading Terminal background = automation, Python, data pipelines. This is rare in the UAE FP&A community. Frame it as:
> "I automated the data pipeline so I could focus on the analysis, not the data wrangling."

This is what Mary Huseynova (Director FP&A, Dubai Holding) already does in her role (Power BI + PL SQL + VBA + Automation). Positioning early on automation = positioning for where FP&A is going.
