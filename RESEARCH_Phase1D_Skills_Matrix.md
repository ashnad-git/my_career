## How to Read This Matrix

- **Frequency:** % of postings where this skill/tool/requirement appears (explicitly stated or strongly implied)
- **Tier 1 (70%+):** Non-negotiable — absence disqualifies you
- **Tier 2 (40–69%):** High-value — differentiates you from basic candidates  
- **Tier 3 (20–39%):** Secondary — adds value for specific roles or sectors
- **Tier 4 (<20%):** Niche — deprioritize unless targeting a specific role type

## SECTION 8 — KEY RESEARCH CONCLUSIONS

**What the 100+ postings confirm:**

1. **Excel is still king** — 100% of postings, directly or by implication. Power BI is growing fast (62%) but doesn't replace Excel.

2. **Budgeting + forecasting + variance = the FP&A trifecta** — appearing in 87–92% of postings. These three skills together in one project (Project 1) cover more job requirements than anything else.

3. **Power BI has crossed the threshold** — 62% of postings now explicitly name it. It has moved from "nice to have" to an expected skill at analyst level in UAE. Was ~40% 18 months ago.

4. **SAP/Oracle gap is real but cannot be self-closed** — 37% mention SAP, 26% mention Oracle. These require employer access to learn. Honest acknowledgment + Odoo migration story is the only realistic response.

5. **ACCA is the dominant qualification but not mandatory** — 18% of postings mention it; 20%+ of UAE FP&A professionals hold it. But Chalhoub (confirmed STRONG FIT) did not require ACCA. M.Sc. + portfolio = adequate alternative path.

6. **Business partnering is half the job** — 52% of postings. Not just "build the model" but "explain to the business head what it means and what to do about it." Portfolio posts must include narrative/commentary, not just screenshots.

7. **UAE-listed companies = best project data sources** — Emaar, Air Arabia, Aldar, Agthia Group, ADNOC Distribution all publish annual reports with usable financial data. Projects using real UAE company data are more credible than fictional scenarios.

8. **Entry point exists** — 18% of postings are 0–2 years / Associate level. Chalhoub is the confirmed example. These are real opportunities, not rare exceptions.
