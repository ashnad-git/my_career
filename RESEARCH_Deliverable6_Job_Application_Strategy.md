# Deliverable 6 — Job Application Strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Purpose:** How to apply effectively given the experience gap, timeline, and market realities  
**Context:** Contract ends Jan 28 2027; notice deadline ~Oct 28 2026; 5 months to find a role

---

## 1. The Core Challenge

You are applying for roles that typically require FP&A-specific experience you don't have. Every application strategy must account for this gap while leveraging your real strengths:
- M.Sc. Financial Economics (exceeds education bar)
- EY brand (Big Four — respected in UAE)
- Odoo ERP migration (real, verifiable, technical)
- Systems transformation story at Promotech
- Technical depth (Python, automation — rare in FP&A candidates)

---

## 2. Application Volume Strategy

**Target:** 80–100 applications over 5 months  
**Monthly breakdown:** 15–20 applications/month

**Do NOT:**
- Apply to 200 roles in week 1 with the same generic CV
- Apply to roles requiring 5+ years FP&A
- Apply to UAE nationals-only roles
- Apply to remote USD roles (not relevant to Dubai market)

**DO:**
- Tailor the cover letter for every application (resume can be standard, cover letter must explain your narrative)
- Apply to the same company through multiple channels (LinkedIn + company career page + recruiter)
- Follow up 1 week after applying (via LinkedIn message to hiring manager or HR)

## 3. Application by Phase

### Phase 1: Now (no portfolio yet)
**Who to apply to:** Tier 1 only (FP&A Associate, junior Financial Analyst with vague requirements)
**Application volume:** 10–15 applications
**Approach:**
- Lead with EY brand + M.Sc. + Odoo ERP migration story
- Mention that you're building FP&A portfolio (signal forward motion)
- Apply via Easy Apply + LinkedIn message to hiring manager

**Cover letter frame:**
```
"I bring 2+ years of finance operations experience, Big Four training (EY), and hands-on ERP 
implementation (Tally → Odoo migration) that most candidates don't have. While I'm building 
dedicated FP&A portfolio projects to demonstrate modeling skills, my foundation in finance 
operations and technical adaptability make me a strong candidate for an associate-level role."
```

### Phase 2: After Project 1 Complete (FP&A Model)
**Who to apply to:** Tier 1 (continue) + Tier 2 (Management Reporting, FP&A 2-4 yr)
**Application volume:** 20–25 applications
**Approach:**
- Feature the FP&A model prominently: "I built a complete FP&A model covering budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and scenario modeling — here's the GitHub link"
- Now the cover letter has concrete proof, not just potential

### Phase 3: After Project 2 Complete (Power BI Dashboard)
**Who to apply to:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and select Tier 3 (Strive-style)
**Application volume:** 30–40 applications
**Approach:**
- Full portfolio narrative: Excel model + Power BI dashboard = proof of FP&A + BI skills
- Mention portfolio on CV itself (GitHub link, project names)

## 4. Where to Apply

**Priority order:**

1. **LinkedIn UAE** — highest volume of Finance Analyst / FP&A roles; Easy Apply makes it fast
2. **Company career pages directly** — some companies don't post on LinkedIn; reduces competition
3. **Indeed UAE** — good for salary-visible roles; different set from LinkedIn
4. **Recruitment agencies** — Hays, Robert Half, Michael Page, Talent 360, Korn Ferry UAE; they work with companies that aren't posting publicly
5. **GulfTalent / Bayt** — secondary sources; worth checking weekly

**Filters to use (LinkedIn):**
- Location: Dubai (+ Abu Dhabi for higher salary)
- Date posted: Past 30 days (refresh weekly)
- Experience level: Entry level + Associate
- Title search: "Financial Analyst" OR "FP&A Analyst" OR "Finance Analyst" OR "Planning Analyst" OR "Management Reporting"

## 5. CV Tailoring Rules

**Your resume should NOT change dramatically per application** — but do adjust these:

| Element | How to tailor |
|---|---|
| Professional summary | Match 3–4 keywords from the job description |
| Skills section | Move skills mentioned in JD to the top of your skills list |
| Promotech bullets | Emphasize the bullet most relevant to the role (ERP for ERP-heavy roles, reporting for reporting roles) |
| GitHub portfolio link | Always include once you have projects |

**What does NOT change:**
- Timeline (dates, companies, titles)
- Education section
- Core narrative (finance operations → FP&A transition)

## 6. The Cover Letter Formula

Every cover letter should have these 5 elements:

**Para 1 — The hook (why you, why this role):**
"I'm applying for the [Role] at [Company] because [specific reason — company stage/industry/type of work]. I bring [1 specific differentiator that matches this role]."

**Para 2 — Your foundation (what you have):**
"My background combines Big Four training at EY, 2+ years of finance operations experience at Promotech Advertising where I led a Tally-to-Odoo ERP migration, and an M.Sc. in Financial Economics."

**Para 3 — Address the gap proactively:**
"I'm building a portfolio of FP&A projects — [what you've completed]. These demonstrate [budgeting/forecasting/Power BI] — the core skills this role requires." OR "I recognize your role requires [X] years of dedicated FP&A experience; I'm building dedicated portfolio projects to demonstrate these exact capabilities while bringing [unique assets]."

**Para 4 — Close:**
"I'd welcome the opportunity to walk you through my portfolio and discuss how my analytical approach and technical depth can contribute to your finance team."

## 7. Handling the Experience Gap in Interviews

The question you will definitely get: "You don't have direct FP&A experience — why should we hire you over someone who does?"

**Framework for answering:**

1. **Acknowledge, don't dodge:** "You're right that my prior roles haven't been titled FP&A — I want to address that directly."

2. **Reframe your experience:** "At Promotech, I didn't just process transactions — I rebuilt the finance function from scratch, led an ERP migration, and established reporting frameworks. That systems thinking is core to what FP&A requires."

3. **Show the project evidence:** "To bridge the gap, I've built [Project X] — a complete FP&A model covering budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis. Here's what it shows..."

4. **Differentiate uniquely:** "Most FP&A candidates have the accounting exposure I have, but few have also built automated data pipelines, worked with ERP implementation, and designed financial models independently. I bring that technical depth."

5. **Commit to learning:** "I'm also pursuing my CMA and committed to growing quickly — I learn fast and prefer to be stretched."

## 10. Red Flags to Avoid in Applications

- ❌ Roles asking for **specific ERP experience (SAP/Oracle)** as hard requirement — skip
- ❌ Roles with **UAE National preference** (even if not exclusive) — very low conversion
- ❌ Roles with **exact years FP&A** as mandatory (not "preferred") for 4+ years — skip
- ❌ Roles at **companies with bad Glassdoor reviews** (6-day weeks, micromanagement) — you're trying to escape this
- ❌ Roles paying **less than AED 6,000** — you're targeting upgrades, not lateral accounting moves
