## COVER LETTER FORMULA
*(Use for every STRONG FIT and GOOD FIT application — 3 sentences max)*

**Sentence 1:** Why this company specifically (mention something real about them — sector, brand, size)

**Sentence 3:** What you'll do for them in the role (forward-looking, analytical framing)

> "Chalhoub Group's position as the leading luxury goods distributor in the Middle East makes this FP&A Associate I role particularly compelling — the scale of financial planning across 14+ brands is exactly the environment I want to develop in. My background in ERP migration at Promotech, combined with my M.Sc. in Financial Economics and ongoing CMA pursuit, directly maps to the technical and analytical requirements of this role. I am building an FP&A portfolio (budgeting models, variance analysis, Power BI dashboards) and am keen to apply that analytical capability within Chalhoub's FP&A function from day one."
