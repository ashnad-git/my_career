## 7. Content Strategy (LinkedIn Posts)

**Frequency:** 1 post per week minimum. Quality over quantity.

**Content pillars (what to post about):**

**Pillar 1 — Portfolio Project Updates (core)**
- Post when each project is complete
- Show the actual model/dashboard (screenshot)
- Explain: What business problem, what you built, what insight it revealed
- Don't just say "I made an FP&A model" — show a screenshot and explain one key finding

**Pillar 2 — UAE Finance Market Observations**
- What you notice from reading job postings (skills in demand, salary ranges)
- "I analyzed 18 FP&A job postings in Dubai — here's what they actually require"
- Note: This post can be written now — you already have the data

**Pillar 3 — Finance Concepts (Explained Simply)**
- "What is a rolling forecast and why do FP&A teams use it?"
- "Variance analysis in 60 seconds"
- Demonstrates domain knowledge; attracts recruiters who are searching for these terms

**Pillar 4 — Career Transition Story (occasional)**
- Honest posts about transitioning from accounting ops to FP&A
- "What I found when I tried to pivot from accounting to financial analysis"
- Authenticity builds engagement; hiring managers appreciate intellectual honesty

**Format that works on LinkedIn:**
- Short paragraphs (2–3 lines max per paragraph)
- End with a question to drive comments
- First 2 lines must grab attention (before "See more" cut-off)
- 1 screenshot / image per post (gets 2–3x more reach)

## 8. Network Building Actions

**Who to connect with:**
- Finance managers / FP&A heads at target companies (they hire)
- Recruiters specializing in finance (Hays, Robert Half, Michael Page, Talent 360, Korn Ferry UAE)
- LinkedIn people who post about FP&A (their followers = your audience)
- Fellow finance professionals transitioning (mutual support community)

**Engagement strategy (before you've built a portfolio):**
- Comment on 3–5 posts per day in UAE finance / FP&A space
- Thoughtful comments (not "Great post!") — add a perspective, ask a question
- This builds visibility before you have projects to show

**Direct message approach (for recruiters):**
```

and currently transitioning into FP&A / Financial Analyst roles. I've been building a portfolio 
of financial models and dashboards. Would you be open to connecting? Happy to share my work.
```
