## DAILY ROUTINE — 45-60 MINUTES/DAY

### MORNING (20 min) — Applications + Job Search

**Step 1 — Apply to 1–2 jobs from the pipeline (15 min)**
- Open JOB_PIPELINE.md → find the top "To Apply" entries
- Copy the cover letter formula → personalise for the company (2 min max)
- Submit via LinkedIn Easy Apply or company site
- Move the job row from "Active Pipeline" to "Applied — Awaiting Response" in JOB_PIPELINE.md
- Record: Company | Role | Date Applied | Method

**Step 2 — Check for new jobs (5 min)**
- Open LinkedIn → Jobs → Search: "FP&A analyst Dubai" → Sort by: Most recent → check anything posted in past 24 hrs
- Open LinkedIn → Jobs → Search: "financial analyst Dubai" → Sort by: Most recent → same
- If you find something relevant: add it to the Active Pipeline table in JOB_PIPELINE.md
- *Claude will do the full weekly job search. Your daily task is just to check the past 24 hours.*

---

### MIDDAY OR EVENING (15 min) — LinkedIn Networking

**Step 3 — Send 2 connection requests (5 min)**
- Open RESEARCH_Phase1C_LinkedIn_People_Database.md
- Pick 2 people from the Tier 1 / high mutual connections list (Nandini Vijayan 21 mutual, Muhammad Danish Ali 14 mutual, etc.)
- Send a connection request with a personal note (template below)
- Log in JOB_PIPELINE.md Networking Log

**Connection request template (copy, fill in [brackets]):**
> "Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching the FP&A community in Dubai. Your career path from [their background] to [their current role] is genuinely inspiring — particularly [one specific detail from their profile]. I'm making a similar transition (accounting + M.Sc. Financial Economics → FP&A) and would love to connect with experienced professionals in this space."

**Step 4 — Engage with 3 posts (5 min)**
- Open LinkedIn feed → find posts by Dubai finance professionals (FP&A, Financial Analyst)
- Leave a genuine comment (not "great post!") — add a thought, ask a question, share a relevant observation
- This builds visibility with hiring managers before you apply

**Step 5 — Post or share once (optional, 5 min)**
- Once per week minimum: share something finance-related (an insight from your Excel learning, a market observation, a project progress update)
- Even "I'm building an FP&A model this week — learning X" gets engagement in the finance community
- Do not feel pressure to make it perfect. Frequency beats quality at this stage.

---

### EVENING (5 min) — Note What You Did

You do not fill in the daily log. Claude does it by asking you questions at the end of every session.

Your only job at the end of the day: **remember what you did** so you can answer Claude's questions at the next session.

If you did something between sessions (applied to a job, sent a connection, watched an Excel tutorial), just keep a mental note or a quick text to yourself. Claude will ask about it and log it properly.

**Step 6 — If you hit a learning milestone today (1 min)**
If you completed a topic outside a session (e.g., finished VLOOKUP practice on your own), drop Claude a quick message: "Finished VLOOKUP practice today, felt confident." Claude will update the log and tick the checklist.

## DAILY DO-NOT-DO LIST

- ❌ Do not spend more than 60 min/day on job search + applications (diminishing returns)
- ❌ Do not apply without at least 2 personalised sentences in the cover letter
- ❌ Do not skip the application log — if it's not logged, it didn't happen
- ❌ Do not wait until projects are done to apply — EY + M.Sc. = apply now
- ❌ Do not apply to UAE National only roles — skip immediately
- ❌ Do not apply to remote-only aggregator posts (Hire Feed, Quik Hire, Jobs Ai) — spam companies
- ❌ Do not apply to roles requiring 5+ years FP&A experience — waste of time at this stage
- ❌ Do not DM recruiters cold without a LinkedIn connection first
