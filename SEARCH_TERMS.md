## TIER 6 — PLATFORM-SPECIFIC SEARCHES

### LinkedIn
- Use "Past week" filter, not "Past 24h" (too few results daily)
- Also try "Posts" search: `"hiring FP&A analyst" Dubai` — catches recruiter posts
- LinkedIn Recruiter InMail: search for "Finance Director Dubai" to find hiring managers

### Indeed UAE (ae.indeed.com)
- Use `?sort=date&fromage=7` for recent only
- Search with Dubai location filter
- Key terms to try:
  - `FP&A Analyst Dubai`
  - `Financial Analyst Dubai`
  - `Finance Analyst Dubai`
  - `Financial Planning and Analysis Dubai`
  - `Finance Business Partner Dubai`
  - `Budget Analyst Dubai`
  - `Management Accountant Dubai` (usually pulls accountants)
  - `Revenue Analyst Dubai` (hospitality skewed)
  - `Commercial Finance Dubai`

### Naukrigulf (naukrigulf.com)
- Direct URL pattern: `naukrigulf.com/{role}-jobs-in-{location}`
- Key URLs:
  - `/financial-analyst-jobs-in-dubai`
  - `/financial-analyst-jobs-in-uae`
  - `/fp-a-analyst-jobs-in-uae`
  - `/management-accountant-jobs-in-dubai`
  - `/finance-executive-jobs-in-dubai`
  - `/finance-analyst-jobs-in-dubai`
  - `/financial-planning-jobs-in-uae`
  - `/budget-analyst-jobs-in-uae`
  - `/commercial-analyst-jobs-in-uae`

### Bayt.com
- Search: "Financial Analyst Dubai UAE"
- Filter: Finance department, Dubai location
- Key URL pattern: `bayt.com/en/jobs/financial-analyst-jobs-in-dubai`

### GulfTalent
- `gulftalent.com/jobs/financial-analyst-jobs`
- Usually shows more senior roles but worth checking

### Company Career Pages (direct — not via aggregators)
- Chalhoub Group: chalhoubgroup.com/en/careers

- Majid Al Futtaim: majidalfuttaim.com/careers
