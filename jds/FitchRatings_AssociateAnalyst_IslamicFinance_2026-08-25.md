## Full Job Description (verbatim)

As one of the world's top three credit ratings agencies, Fitch Ratings plays a critical role in global capital markets by providing supplementary credit analysis, ratings, research, and commentary to financial market participants. For over 100 years, Fitch Ratings has been creating value for global markets through its rigorous analysis and deep expertise. Fitch Ratings is part of Fitch Group, a global leader in financial information services with operations in more than 30 countries.

**About the Team**

The Islamic Finance team operates across continents and time zones, combining deep local insight with a global perspective in this evolving industry.

**We'll Count On You To**

Based in Dubai, you will primarily support senior analysts in various functions tied to Islamic Finance initiatives. Your role will involve data management, preliminary analysis, and active collaboration in global projects.

**Key Responsibilities**

Based in Dubai, the Associate Analyst will primarily support Senior Analysts in various functions tied to Islamic Finance initiatives.
Assist in the preparation and review of sukuk templates and relevant documentation alongside senior team members.
Support data collection and preliminary analysis for the rating coverage.
Review sukuk legal documentation and transaction structures collaboratively.
Engage in global cross-sector projects for comprehensive Islamic Finance solutions.
Participate in meetings with issuers, bankers, and legal teams, gaining insights and providing essential support.

**You May Be a Good Fit If You Have**

An outstanding graduate with relevant bachelor's or higher degree.
Strong analytical skills with an eagerness to understand credit analysis and Islamic Finance.
Ability to coordinate effectively across different time zones and be flexible for essential meetings.
Effective verbal and written communication skills for seamless collaboration.
Self-motivated with a proactive attitude towards learning and task execution.
Modelling, data analysis, and accounting skills, with an eye for detail.

**What We Offer**

Join a thought leader in the Islamic Finance sector.
Engage in a challenging, analytically rigorous global environment.
Exposure to a diverse range of projects covering multiple regions.
Clear pathways for career development within Islamic Finance.
A flexible hybrid working model in a culture that values Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion.
Optional CFA certification training via Fitch Learning.
