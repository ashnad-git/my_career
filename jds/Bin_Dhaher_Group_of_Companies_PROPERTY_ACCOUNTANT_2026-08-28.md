## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are a **property management company** looking for a detail\-oriented and reliable **Property Accountant** to manage the day\-to\-day accounting and financial requirements of our properties.

Key Responsibilities

* Handle daily accounting and financial transactions for managed properties.
* Record and monitor income, expenses, invoices, payments, and receipts.
* Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly financial reports.
* Monitor accounts payable and accounts receivable.
* Reconcile bank accounts and property\-related accounts.
* Track rental income, service charges, deposits, and other property\-related collections.
* Coordinate with property managers, tenants, suppliers, and other departments regarding financial matters.
* Review invoices and supporting documents before processing payments.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and monthly financial closing.
* Maintain accurate and organized accounting records and documentation.
* Monitor outstanding payments and follow up on receivables.
* Ensure accounting transactions are properly recorded and comply with company policies.
* Support the management team with financial information and analysis as needed.

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Previous experience in property accounting, real estate, hospitality, or property management is preferred.
* Good knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Experience with accounting software and Microsoft Excel.
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good organizational and time\-management skills.
* Ability to handle multiple properties and daily operational requirements.
* Strong communication and coordination skills.
* Able to work independently and take ownership of assigned responsibilities.

What We’re Looking For

We are looking for someone who is **hands\-on, organized, dependable, and proactive**—someone who can manage the daily accounting requirements of our properties and ensure that financial records and transactions are accurate, timely, and properly controlled.

Pay: AED3,500\.00 \- AED4,500\.00 per month
