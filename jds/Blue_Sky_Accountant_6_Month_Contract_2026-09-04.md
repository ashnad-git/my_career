## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Summary

We are seeking a detail\-oriented and reliable Accountant to support our finance department for a period of six (6\) months, office based on working hours. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, assisting with day\-to\-day accounting operations, preparing reports, and ensuring compliance with company policies and UAE financial regulations.

Key Responsibilities

* Record daily financial transactions and maintain accurate accounting records.
* Prepare and process purchase orders, invoices, payment vouchers, receipts, and journal entries.
* Monitor and record inventory stocks.
* Reconcile bank statements and company accounts.
* Monitor accounts payable and accounts receivable.
* Assist in preparing monthly financial reports and management reports.
* Prepare VAT\-related documentation and ensure compliance with UAE tax regulations.
* Maintain the fixed asset register and depreciation schedules.
* Assist in payroll preparation and salary reconciliations, if required.
* Monitor outstanding customer payments and follow up on collections.
* Support external auditors by providing required financial documents and reports.
* Organize and maintain accounting files and financial records.
* Assist with budgeting and cash flow monitoring.
* Perform other accounting and administrative duties as assigned by management.

Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum of 2–3 years of accounting experience, preferably in facilities management or maintenance industries.
* Knowledge of UAE VAT regulations and accounting standards.
* Proficient in Microsoft Excel and accounting software (QuickBooks).
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
* Ability to maintain confidentiality and work independently.
* Good written and verbal communication skills in English.

Preferred Skills

* Experience handling supplier payments and customer collections.
* Ability to prepare financial reports and perform account reconciliations.
* Knowledge of UAE labor\-related payroll processes is an advantage.
* Ability to meet deadlines and manage multiple tasks efficiently.

Pay: From AED2,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
