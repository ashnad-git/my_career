- Recommendations to improve sales, profitability, and cost efficiency

**Budgeting & Forecasting**
- Support preparation of annual budgets and financial forecasts for Retail Division
- Work with Retail Operations and Brand teams on realistic sales and expense assumptions

**Store & Brand Performance**
- Conduct financial performance reviews across individual stores and brands
- Analyse store-level operating costs, sales trends, margins, profitability
- Identify underperforming stores; investigate underlying financial and operational drivers
