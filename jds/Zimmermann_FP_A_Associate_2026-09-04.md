**Our Brand Story** 




 ZIMMERMANN is a global luxury fashion brand, founded in Sydney in 1991 by sisters Nicky and Simone Zimmermann. Renowned for its signature approach to sophisticated femininity, distinctive prints, and exceptional craftsmanship, the brand is present across Australia, Europe, the Americas, the Middle East and Asia.
 



  





 Nicky Zimmermann’s creative vision and Simone Zimmermann’s strategic leadership have formed the foundation of the brand’s success. Their partnership – balancing design innovation with strong commercial direction – remains central to ZIMMERMANN’s identity and growth.
 



  





 Today, ZIMMERMANN continues to redefine modern luxury with collections that celebrate self\-expression. Grounded in Australian sun\-soaked optimism, the brand resonates with women around the world. Our global teams are united by a shared commitment to creativity, quality, and evolving the brand with integrity and purpose.
 



  





 At ZIMMERMANN, our people are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to empowering talented individuals to build meaningful careers within an inclusive, collaborative, and forward‑thinking environment – where craftsmanship and innovation are celebrated, and individuality is valued.
 



  





**The Opportunity** 




 ZIMMERMANN is seeking a talented FP\&A Associate, to join our Finance team, where you’ll play an important role in providing financial support for the management of the P\&L and Balance Sheet for the Middle East region. In this position, you’ll help support the year\-end audit, indirect tax calculations, invoice booking and payments, month\-end closing, budget preparation and tracking across the regional markets.
 



  





 This opportunity is ideal if you’re motivated by financial analysis, problem\-solving, collaboration, driving accuracy, and enjoy contributing meaningful, measurable impact within a dynamic and fast‑paced environment.
 



  





 As an FP\&A Associate, you’ll contribute to providing financial planning, reporting, and analytical support, bringing together commercial acumen, analytical thinking, stakeholder partnership, attention to detail, financial governance, and hands\-on execution to deliver outcomes that support ZIMMERMANN’s continued evolution. You’ll collaborate closely with global, cross‑functional teams, helping shape how the Finance function operates today and grows into the future.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 




 As an FP\&A Associate, your responsibilities will include:
 


* Support month\-end close, P\&L analysis, and balance sheet reconciliations.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting activities.
* Partner with finance, retail, supply chain, and business stakeholders to support decision\-making.
* Prepare management reports, board materials, and ad\-hoc financial analysis.
* Support audit, tax, compliance, and financial control requirements.
* Contribute to finance projects, administrative activities, and process improvements.



  





**What You Will Bring** 




 To succeed in this role, you will bring:
 


* A tertiary qualification in Finance, Accounting, Commerce, or a related discipline.
* Advanced knowledge of MS office, particularly Excel.
* A high level of enthusiasm and the ability to work in a fast\-paced environment.
* Ability to communicate clearly and effectively.
* A “hands\-on” proactive approach and the ability to work in a team environment.
* Previous experience with SAP (highly regarded but not essential).
