## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are seeking an experienced **Accountant** with a strong background in **UAE retail trading**, preferably in the **furniture, carpets, home décor, or related trading industry**.

The ideal candidate should have hands\-on experience in inventory accounting, import consignments, and retail financial operations, along with excellent knowledge of **Zoho Books**.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting operations.
* Handle accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations.
* Maintain accurate inventory records and stock valuation.
* Process and monitor import consignments, supplier invoices, and landed cost allocations.
* Reconcile inventory with physical stock and warehouse records.
* Prepare VAT returns and ensure compliance with UAE tax regulations.
* Generate monthly financial reports, MIS reports, and management accounts.
* Coordinate with suppliers, logistics companies, and internal departments.
* Maintain the general ledger and ensure accurate financial reporting.
* Assist with annual audits and statutory requirements.

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **Minimum 3–5 years of UAE accounting experience** in **retail trading**.
* Experience in **furniture, carpet, home furnishing, or similar trading companies** is highly preferred.
* Strong knowledge of **inventory accounting**, stock control, and consignment management.
* Hands\-on experience with **Zoho Books** is mandatory.
* Good understanding of UAE VAT regulations.
* Proficient in Microsoft Excel and accounting software.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* Able to work independently and meet deadlines.

Preferred Candidate

* Currently based in the UAE.
* Immediate joiners or candidates with short notice periods will be given preference.
