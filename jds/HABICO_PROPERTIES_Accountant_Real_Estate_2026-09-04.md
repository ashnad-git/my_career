## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Accountant – Dubai Real Estate

**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Industry:** Real Estate

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced **Accountant** with strong knowledge of the **Dubai real estate industry** to manage day\-to\-day accounting operations, financial records, transactions, and reporting. The ideal candidate should understand real estate commission structures, property transactions, client payments, and UAE accounting and compliance requirements.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage daily accounting transactions, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, receipts, payments, and reconciliations.
* Maintain accurate records of company expenses, income, commissions, and financial transactions.
* Handle **real estate sales and commission accounting**, including agent commission calculations and payouts.
* Reconcile bank accounts, cash transactions, and company accounts on a regular basis.
* Track property\-related payments, deposits, refunds, and outstanding balances.
* Prepare monthly financial reports, management reports, and account statements.
* Monitor receivables and payables and follow up on outstanding payments.
* Maintain proper documentation and filing of invoices, receipts, contracts, and financial records.
* Coordinate with sales, administration, management, and external parties regarding financial matters.
* Assist with payroll processing and employee\-related financial records when required.
* Ensure compliance with **UAE VAT regulations**, tax requirements, and applicable accounting standards.
* Assist with VAT returns, supporting schedules, and coordination with auditors/tax consultants.
* Support the preparation of budgets, cash\-flow reports, and financial forecasts.
* Identify discrepancies, accounting errors, or unusual transactions and resolve them promptly.
* Maintain confidentiality of company and client financial information.
* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties as assigned by management.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* **2–5 years of accounting experience in the UAE**, preferably within Dubai real estate.
* Strong understanding of **Dubai real estate transactions and commission structures**.
* Good knowledge of **UAE VAT and basic UAE tax compliance**.
* Experience with accounting software and Microsoft Excel/Google Sheets.
* Strong numerical and analytical skills with excellent attention to detail.
* Ability to manage multiple transactions and meet deadlines.
* Good communication and coordination skills.
* UAE experience is mandatory; **real estate accounting experience is highly preferred**.

Preferred Skills

* Experience handling **property sales commissions and agent settlements**.
* Familiarity with Dubai real estate payment structures and transaction processes.
* Knowledge of RERA/DLD\-related financial documentation is an advantage.
* Experience with accounting software such as **Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero, or similar**.
* Ability to work independently and maintain accurate financial records.

What We Offer

* Competitive salary based on experience.
* Professional and dynamic real estate environment.
* Opportunity to work closely with management and the sales team.
* Career growth and development opportunities.
