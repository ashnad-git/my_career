## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an **experienced Senior Accountant** with strong practical knowledge of **UAE accounting, bookkeeping, VAT, Tally, financial reporting, and compliance requirements**.

The ideal candidate should be capable of handling the **end\-to\-end accounting function**, maintaining accurate books, ensuring timely VAT compliance, and supporting management with reliable financial information.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage the **complete day\-to\-day accounting and bookkeeping process**.
* Maintain accurate **ledgers, journals, vouchers, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and general accounts**.
* Handle accounting entries and maintain company accounts using **Tally**.
* Perform regular **bank, customer, supplier, and general ledger reconciliations**.
* Prepare trial balances, profit \& loss statements, balance sheets, and other financial reports.
* Manage the **month\-end and year\-end closing process**.
* Handle the **end\-to\-end UAE VAT process**, including transaction review, VAT calculations, reconciliations, preparation and filing of VAT returns, and maintaining supporting documentation.
* Ensure proper accounting treatment of **VAT, input tax, output tax, and VAT adjustments**.
* Maintain records and documentation required for **VAT compliance and audits**.
* Ensure accounting practices comply with applicable **UAE laws, regulations, and financial reporting requirements**.
* Stay updated on relevant changes in **UAE tax, accounting, finance, and regulatory requirements** and advise management where applicable.
* Have working knowledge of **UAE banking requirements, transaction/reporting thresholds, banking documentation, and related compliance requirements**.
* Review and verify invoices, payments, receipts, expenses, and supporting documents.
* Monitor **cash flow, receivables, payables, outstanding payments, and collections**.
* Prepare ageing reports and follow up on outstanding receivables and payables.
* Coordinate with banks, auditors, suppliers, customers, tax consultants, and other external parties when required.
* Assist with **audit preparation and provide required financial records and supporting documents**.
* Establish and maintain proper accounting controls and identify discrepancies or irregularities.
* Ensure all accounting records are **accurate, complete, properly documented, and up to date**.
* Maintain strict confidentiality of financial and company information.
* Provide financial analysis, accounting summaries, and reports to management when required.
* Supervise or guide junior accounting staff, where applicable.
* Perform other finance and accounting responsibilities assigned by management.

Requirements

* **3–7\+ years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably with UAE experience.
* Strong practical knowledge of **UAE accounting and bookkeeping**.
* **Excellent hands\-on knowledge of Tally** is essential.
* Strong understanding of **double\-entry accounting, ledgers, reconciliations, AP, AR, and financial statements**.
* Thorough practical knowledge of **UAE VAT and the complete VAT filing process**.
* Good understanding of applicable **UAE tax, finance, banking, and regulatory requirements**.
* Strong MS Excel skills.
* Ability to independently identify and rectify accounting discrepancies.
* Strong analytical, numerical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good English communication and reporting skills.
* Ability to work independently and take **full ownership of accounting responsibilities**.
* Professional, organized, reliable, and trustworthy.

**Preferred:** UAE VAT/accounting experience, experience dealing with auditors and banks, and relevant accounting qualification such as **CA, ACCA, CMA, or equivalent**.

**We are looking for a Senior Accountant who can independently manage the company's accounting function, confidently handle Tally and UAE VAT compliance, and provide accurate and timely financial information to management.**

Pay: AED4,000\.00 \- AED4,500\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
