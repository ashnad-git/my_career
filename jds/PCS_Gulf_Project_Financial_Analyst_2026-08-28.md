## Full Job Description (verbatim)

PCS Gulf excels in IT and financial consulting, driven by the belief that optimal solutions arise from a diverse network of experts collaborating across IT, Finance, and business strategy. ****Roles And Responsibilities:****
* Working with all GTDP Project Managers for project Online Financial queries.
* Creating SAP report for Admin.
* Working with Admin and Finance to reconcile Project Financials.
* Managing a centralized tracker for upcoming financial milestone payments.
* Working with admin to post project financials in right category and with right nomenclature.
* Working on expense report from SAP.


****Requirements:****
* Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Systems, Engineering or other related field equivalent work experience.
* 3\+ years of admin and financial and Quality Management experience. Excel in Microsoft Excel, previous experience in financial reconciliations.
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills (oral \& written) and ability to deal with different levels of people within the organisation, analysis and synthesis capabilities, financial tracking, Expertise in Microsoft packages, including Project, PowerPoint, Word and Excel
