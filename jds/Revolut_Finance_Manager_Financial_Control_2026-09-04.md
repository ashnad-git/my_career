Their insight shapes everything from daily operations to long\-term plans and plays a key role in how we scale.

 Up to shape what's next in finance? Let's get in touch.
 



  





**What you'll be doing** 




  




* Preparing monthly accounts in ERP Oracle NetSuite in accordance with IFRS
* Coordinating the accounting and month\-end closing process in the UAE
* Understanding and communicating financial performance, risks, and key metrics to local leadership
* Enhancing the balance sheet substantiation process in coordination with group programs and tools
* Ensuring accurate and timely closing of the books for monthly and annual reporting
* Preparing detailed financial reporting and analysis on movements in key general ledger accounts
* Ensuring accuracy and compliance with local tax regulators on submission of timely returns
* Developing and monitoring a robust control environment, with a focus on driving operational readiness
* Delivering process improvements to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of the monthly, quarterly, and annual reporting process via automation and control
* Coordinating external audit, in conjunction with group audit requirements
* Managing local accounting policies, ensuring latest changes in IFRS and group policies are implemented
* Contributing to financial control processes and procedures



  





**What you'll need** 




  




* A bachelor’s degree in a relevant field
* A CPA or CA qualification
* 7\+ years of experience in finance and controlling functions
* Impressive accounting knowledge with proven experience in IFRS and financial reporting
* A structural and logical approach to solving problems
* Solid analytical skills and a hands\-on approach to working with data
* Experience within a fast growing organisation
* Great motivation and focus on delivery
