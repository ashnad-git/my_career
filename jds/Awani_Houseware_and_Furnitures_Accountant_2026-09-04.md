**Job Description**

We are looking for a detail\-oriented and result\-driven Accountant to join our team in Abu Dhabi. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records handling day\-to\-day accounting activities supporting Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable functions, and preparing financial reports.

**Key Responsibilities**

\* Maintain the system of accounts and accurate records of all company transactions and assets.

\* Record and process daily accounting transactions, journal entries, adjustments, and financial data.

\* Implement and follow accounting procedures and processes established by the company.

\* Review Accounts Receivable (AR) \& Accounts Payable (AP) \& balances and support weekly payment runs.

\* Process invoices, receipts, payments, expenses, and other accounting documents accurately.

\* Ensure proper handling and recording of financial transactions within designated limits.

\* Liaise with suppliers regarding payments, invoices, receipts, statements, and required documents.

\* Reconcile bank accounts, customer accounts, supplier accounts, and other financial records.

\* Assist in preparing monthly \& weekly financial statements and management reports.

\* Analyze financial data and provide accurate reports as required by management.

\* Maintain proper filing and documentation of accounting records.

\* Ensure accuracy, completeness, and confidentiality of financial information.

\* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties assigned by management.

\* Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.

\* \*\*2–3 years of relevant experience as an Accountant.\*\*

\* Strong knowledge of bookkeeping and accounting principles.

\* Good knowledge of financial statements and accounting procedures.

\* Proficiency in accounting software.

\* Expert knowledge of MS Excel.

\* Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

\* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
