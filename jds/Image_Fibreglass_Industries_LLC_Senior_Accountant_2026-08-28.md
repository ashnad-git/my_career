A leading **Fiberglass Factory** in **Umm Al Quwain** is looking to hire an experienced **Senior Accountant** to join our team.

**Job Responsibilities:**

* Manage and review daily accounting transactions and financial records.
* Prepare monthly and annual financial reports.
* Handle **accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations**.
* Monitor cash flow, expenses, and company financial activities.
* Prepare and review invoices, payments, receipts, and journal entries.
* Manage inventory and production cost accounting.
* Assist in budgeting, forecasting, and cost analysis.
* Ensure compliance with **UAE VAT and applicable accounting regulations**.
* Coordinate with auditors and provide the required financial documents.
* Supervise accounting activities and ensure accuracy of financial records.

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.

* Experience with accounting software and **ERP systems**.
* Advanced knowledge of **Microsoft Excel**.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines.
* Good command of English; Arabic is an advantage.
