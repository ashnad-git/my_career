## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Accountant / Commercial Coordinator – Interior Design \& Fit\-Out

Key Responsibilities

\* Prepare accurate costings, budgets and pricing for interior design and fit\-out projects.

\* Understand and price different scopes including interior design, fit\-out, joinery, FF\&E, materials, finishes, MEP, civil works and procurement.

\* Prepare client proposals, quotations and commercial offers based on project scope, drawings and BOQs.

\* Ensure appropriate profit margins and mark\-ups are applied to project pricing.

\* Review supplier and subcontractor quotations and compare costs to ensure competitive and commercially viable pricing.

\* Prepare detailed BOQs, cost breakdowns and pricing schedules where required.

\* Coordinate with designers and project managers to understand the scope before preparing proposals.

\* Ensure proposals accurately reflect the scope of work, exclusions, payment terms, timelines and deliverables.

\* Track approved project budgets against actual costs and identify potential cost overruns.

\* Monitor project profitability from initial proposal through execution.

\* Prepare variations, additional works and revised quotations for clients.

\* Manage client invoicing, payment schedules, supplier payments and project accounts.

\* Maintain accurate financial records and reporting across multiple projects.

Mandatory Requirements

\* Previous experience in an interior design, fit\-out, construction or architecture company is essential.

\* Strong understanding of interior design and fit\-out services and project workflows.

\* Proven ability to price projects accurately and prepare professional client proposals.

\* Strong understanding of BOQs, material costs, joinery, FF\&E, subcontractor pricing and fit\-out costs.

\* Must understand mark\-ups, margins, overheads and project profitability.

\* Strong Excel skills and experience with accounting/estimation software.

\* Excellent attention to detail and ability to work with drawings, specifications and project information.

\* Strong communication skills and ability to coordinate with designers, procurement and project teams.

\* Knowledge of UAE VAT and accounting practices.

Ideal candidate: Someone who can take a project scope, understand what is required, calculate the actual cost, apply the correct margins, and produce a clear, professional client proposal ready to send.

Job Types: Full\-time, Permanent

Experience:

* Accounting: 3 years (Required)

Work Location: In person
