**Role Summary:** 




 We are hiring a Finance \& Cost Accountant to own the day\-to\-day books and the production accounting.
 



 This is a hands\-on role, not a management position. You will personally execute the accounting work in QuickBooks Online and personally track inventory, work\-in\-progress and finished goods on the production side. You will work closely with the CEO and the atelier team.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities:** 




 Finance and accounting
 


* Daily bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online entities accounts payable, accounts receivable, journal entries, expense categorization.
* Bank, credit card and petty cash reconciliations.
* Monthly close: accruals, prepayments and preparation of month\-end financial statements.
* VAT filings and supporting schedules in line with UAE FTA requirements.
* Payroll entries and related reconciliations.
* Maintaining a clean audit trail and supporting documentation for all transactions.



 Cost and production accounting
 


* Tracking raw material inventory, including purchase recording, valuation and reconciliation against physical counts.
* Maintaining work\-in\-progress records: which pieces are in production, materials consumed, labour applied, stage of completion.
* Monthly production close reconciling production output to inventory movements and ensuring COGS is recorded correctly.



  





**Qualifications:** 



* Five to six years of accounting experience.
* Strong working knowledge of QuickBooks Online (essential).
* Demonstrated experience with inventory accounting and cost accounting not just general ledger work.
* Solid grasp of UAE VAT and FTA filing requirements.
* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or Commerce. Professional qualification (CA, CMA, ACCA part\-qualified or qualified) is an advantage.
* Highly organized, detail\-driven, and comfortable owning month\-end close independently.
* Discreet you will handle confidential financial and client information.
* Written and spoken English required.



 Send your CV and a short note about your relevant experience. Please include your notice period and expected salary.
