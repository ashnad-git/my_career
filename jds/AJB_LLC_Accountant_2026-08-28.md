**Role Description**

This is a full\-time, on\-site role for an accountant, based in Sharjah. The accountant will be responsible for managing financial transactions, preparing financial statements, reconciling accounts, managing budgets, and ensuring compliance with financial regulations. Coordination with other departments to ensure the accuracy and timeliness of financial data will also be required.

**Qualifications**

* Proven proficiency in Accounts Management, Financial Reporting, and Budgeting.
* Strong skills in Invoice Processing, Accounts Receivable, and Accounts Payable management.
* Experience in preparing financial statements and reconciling accounts.
* Knowledge of Tax Compliance, Auditing, and Regulatory Requirements for financial processes.
* Strong proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel.
* Exceptional attention to detail, problem\-solving abilities, and organizational skills.
* Ability to work collaboratively in a team and communicate effectively with colleagues and external stakeholders.
* Previous experience in manufacturing or industrial sectors is an advantage.
* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field is required; CPA or equivalent certification is a plus.
