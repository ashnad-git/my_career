Rapyd is looking for a detail-oriented and analytically driven FinOps Pricing-Cost Analyst to join our FinOps-Pricing team in Dubai, covering Israel, Dubai, and Europe. This is not a purely operational role - it offers real analytical exposure, from supporting cost-saving initiatives to helping build out the team's AI-driven reconciliation tools.
