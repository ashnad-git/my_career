## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Title:** Accountant / Finance Manager

**Company:** Nexrevive – Creative Event Agency **Location:** Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, UAE (on‑site) **Job Type:** Full‑time

**About Nexrevive** Nexrevive is a Dubai‑based creative event agency that turns clients' goals into brand experiences that create real impact. We deliver events end to end \- creative, 3D design, production, technical AV and on‑ground management \- for corporate, government, festivals and brand launches across the UAE and KSA.

**The Role** We're looking for a detail‑driven Accountant who can also think like a finance manager \- owning the day‑to‑day books and compliance, while planning the agency's finances and helping grow and invest its funds. You'll keep Nexrevive accurate, compliant and audit‑ready, and work directly with the founder to make the money work harder: managing cash, improving margins and finding smart opportunities to increase income.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Maintain accurate day‑to‑day bookkeeping and general ledger.
* Prepare and issue client invoices and follow up on receivables and collections.
* Manage supplier and vendor payments, staff payroll and expense claims.
* Track project budgets versus actuals and report on project profitability and margins.
* Handle VAT calculation, filing and compliance, and support UAE corporate tax requirements.
* Prepare monthly financial reports, cash‑flow statements and management accounts.
* Plan the agency's finances \- build budgets, forecasts and cash‑flow plans to guide growth.
* Manage and grow company funds and reserves \- identify safe, smart places to invest surplus cash to increase income and returns.
* Advise the founder on financial strategy, cost optimisation and ways to improve profitability.
* Reconcile bank accounts, petty cash and supplier statements, and support year‑end audit.
* Flag risks, variances and cash‑flow issues early to the founder.

**Requirements**

* 3\+ years of accounting experience, ideally in the UAE.
* Solid knowledge of UAE VAT and general familiarity with UAE corporate tax.
* Financial planning and analysis experience, with a strong understanding of managing cash, reserves and investment options.
* Proficiency in accounting software (e.g. QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Tally or Xero).
* Strong Microsoft Excel and financial‑modelling skills.
* Commercial mindset \- able to spot opportunities to save cost, improve margin and grow funds.
* High attention to detail, accuracy and confidentiality.
* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field.
* English essential; Arabic is a plus.

**Nice to Have**

* Experience in an events, production, agency or other project‑based business.
* Part‑qualified ACCA / CMA / CA / CFA or working towards a qualification.
* Experience managing company funds, treasury or investments.

**What We Offer**

* Own the finance function of a fast‑growing agency, from bookkeeping to financial strategy.
* Direct access to the founder and real influence over how the business grows its money.
* Room to grow into a senior finance / finance‑manager role as the business scales.

**How to Apply** Send your CV and a short note about your experience to **info@nexrevive.com**. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

Pay: AED3,000\.00 \- AED6,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
