**Divisional Accountant**
=========================

**Dubai, UAE**

  

**About Us**
------------


Kingspan Data Solutions is a global leader in mission\-critical data centre infrastructure, delivering innovative solutions that support the performance, efficiency, and scalability of today's high\-performing data centres.


As one of the fastest\-growing divisions within Kingspan Group plc, Kingspan Data Solutions operates across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, and Asia\-Pacific. Our businesses combine specialist expertise in engineering, manufacturing, installation, and lifecycle services to deliver integrated solutions for hyperscale, colocation, and enterprise data centre customers worldwide.


With a strong focus on innovation, sustainability, and operational excellence, we continue to invest in our people, technology, and capabilities to support the evolving demands of the global data centre market.

**About the Role**
------------------


We are looking for a Divisional Accountant to join our Finance team in Dubai, supporting the financial reporting, governance, and performance management activities of Kingspan Data Solutions.


Working closely with finance teams across multiple international businesses, you will play a key role in delivering accurate financial reporting, insightful analysis, and robust financial controls. This is an excellent opportunity to gain exposure to a fast\-paced, international environment while supporting strategic decision\-making and continuous improvement initiatives across the division.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

* Ensure the accurate and timely submission of weekly and monthly financial reporting information across the division
* Review and analyse financial data, investigating significant variances and ensuring completeness and accuracy
* Support local finance teams with reporting requirements, system queries, and reporting submissions
* Assist in the preparation and continuous improvement of divisional and Group reporting packs
* Support half\-year and year\-end Group reporting activities
* Assist with annual budgeting, forecasting, and strategic planning processes
* Prepare and submit divisional reporting data, ensuring compliance with Group requirements
* Review divisional overhead expenditure with budget holders, identifying opportunities for cost optimisation
* Support balance sheet reviews and follow\-up actions with local finance teams and management
* Help maintain a strong financial control environment and ensure compliance with Group Accounting policies
* Support acquisition integration activities, including process alignment, reporting implementation, and finance systems onboarding
* Contribute to audit preparation, finance process improvements, and reporting standardisation initiatives

**Qualifications**
------------------

* Professional accounting qualification such as **ACA, ACCA, or CIMA** (part\-qualified or fully qualified)
* Experience in management reporting and the preparation of management accounts
* Strong understanding of financial controls, reporting processes, and accounting principles
* Experience working with ERP systems and financial reporting platforms
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and experience using Power BI
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving capabilities with a high attention to detail
* Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast\-paced environment
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
* Strong systems aptitude and ability to learn new technologies quickly
* Experience supporting acquisitions, integrations, or multi\-site businesses would be advantageous
* Willingness to travel as required

**Benefits**
------------


Permanent and full\-time employees enjoy a competitive salary, career development opportunities, and the chance to work within a growing global business at the forefront of data centre infrastructure.


At Kingspan Data Solutions, you will be part of a collaborative and forward\-thinking finance team, with opportunities to influence business performance, support strategic growth initiatives, and develop your career within a truly international environment.

 **Ignite your career. Redefine success.**
