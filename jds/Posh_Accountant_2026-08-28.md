## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Posh Fusion Fashion Clothing LLC (Posh Abaya)** is looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to join our team.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and financial transactions.
* Maintain and reconcile, on an up\-to\-date basis, **accounts payable, accounts receivable, payments, and receipts**.
* **Bank reconciliation and payment gateway reconciliation**.
* Preparation, checking, and submission of **reports to management and external agencies**.
* Monitor expenses, cash flow, and financial records.
* Support **payroll, WPS, and other finance\-related activities** when required.
* Preparation and finalisation of **monthly financial reports, including the Profit \& Loss statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement**.
* Assist with **VAT calculations, returns, and UAE tax compliance**.
* Coordinate with auditors, banks, suppliers, and other relevant parties.
* Ensure compliance with company accounting policies and UAE regulations.

**Requirements**

* **2–3 years of UAE experience** in accounting.
* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE accounting and VAT regulations**.
* Experience with accounting software; **Odoo knowledge is an advantage**.
* Strong knowledge of **MS Excel** and financial reporting.
* Good analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good English and Hindi communication skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.

**Salary**

**AED 3,500 – 4,500 per month**, depending on experience and qualifications.

**What We Offer**

* Opportunity to grow with a growing UAE fashion brand.
* Professional and supportive working environment.
* Exposure to retail, manufacturing, and multi\-branch accounting operations.
