## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are seeking a highly experienced **Senior Oracle Fusion Business Analyst** with strong expertise in **Oracle Fusion Cloud applications** and **government business processes** to support the analysis, design, implementation, optimization, and continuous improvement of enterprise solutions. The role requires a deep understanding of **business process analysis, requirements gathering, functional design, process mapping, stakeholder engagement, testing support, and change management**, with a strong focus on public sector and government operating models.


The consultant will work closely with business users, functional consultants, technical teams, solution architects, project managers, and implementation partners to translate government business requirements into effective Oracle Fusion solutions that are compliant, efficient, scalable, and aligned with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and digital transformation objectives.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Business Analysis \& Process Leadership**

* Lead end\-to\-end business analysis activities for Oracle Fusion Cloud implementations, enhancements, and support initiatives.
* Analyze current\-state and future\-state business processes across government functions and translate them into clear functional and business requirements.
* Facilitate workshops, interviews, and working sessions with stakeholders to gather, validate, and prioritize requirements.
* Document business needs, process flows, use cases, user stories, functional specifications, and acceptance criteria.
* Identify process gaps, inefficiencies, control weaknesses, and opportunities for automation and standardization.
* Support business process reengineering initiatives to align government operations with Oracle Fusion best practices.
* Work closely with functional teams to ensure business requirements are accurately reflected in solution design.

**Oracle Fusion Application Support**

* Support business analysis across Oracle Fusion modules such as:
* + Financials
	+ Procurement
	+ Projects
	+ HCM
	+ SCM
	+ Grants
	+ Assets
	+ Expenses
	+ Budgeting and Planning
* Understand Oracle Fusion application capabilities and map them to government business requirements.
* Assist in defining configuration requirements, business rules, approval flows, and operational controls.
* Validate that Oracle Fusion functionality supports statutory, operational, and reporting requirements.
* Collaborate with functional consultants to ensure business processes are configured correctly and efficiently.
* Support issue analysis, root cause identification, and resolution of business and functional defects.

**Government Business Process Expertise**

* Analyze and document government\-specific business processes, policies, and compliance requirements.
* Support processes related to:
* + Budget preparation and control
	+ Public expenditure management
	+ Procurement and tendering
	+ Vendor and supplier management
	+ Grants and fund management
	+ Asset tracking and lifecycle management
	+ Payroll and workforce administration
	+ Project and program monitoring
	+ Financial reporting and statutory compliance
* Understand public sector governance structures, approval hierarchies, audit requirements, and segregation of duties.
* Ensure business processes align with government regulations, internal controls, and policy frameworks.
* Support transformation of manual or legacy government processes into standardized Oracle Fusion workflows.
* Assist in defining controls for transparency, accountability, traceability, and compliance.

**Requirements Gathering \& Documentation**

* Prepare detailed business requirement documents, process maps, gap analyses, fit\-gap assessments, and solution summaries.
* Translate business needs into functional requirements that can be used by functional and technical teams.
* Maintain traceability between business requirements, solution design, testing, and deployment deliverables.
* Document business rules, exception handling, approval matrices, and reporting needs.
* Support preparation of training materials, user guides, and operational procedures.
* Ensure documentation is clear, complete, accurate, and aligned with project governance standards.

**Testing \& Validation**

* Support unit testing, system integration testing (SIT), user acceptance testing (UAT), and regression testing activities.
* Develop test scenarios, test scripts, and expected results based on business requirements.
* Validate Oracle Fusion configurations, workflows, reports, and transactions against business expectations.
* Coordinate with business users during UAT and support defect logging, triage, and resolution.
* Verify that data migration, process flows, and reporting outputs meet business and compliance requirements.
* Support cutover, go\-live readiness, and post\-implementation validation activities.

**Stakeholder Engagement \& Change Management**

* Act as a liaison between business stakeholders, functional teams, technical teams, and project leadership.
* Facilitate communication and alignment across departments, agencies, and implementation teams.
* Support change impact assessments and identify business readiness requirements.
* Assist in user adoption planning, training coordination, and stakeholder communication.
* Participate in governance meetings, design reviews, and project status updates.
* Build strong working relationships with government users and leadership teams to ensure successful solution adoption.

**Reporting, Controls \& Compliance**

* Support business reporting requirements including operational, management, statutory, and compliance reports.
* Work with functional and technical teams to define reporting needs and validate outputs.
* Ensure business processes and system configurations support auditability, transparency, and regulatory compliance.
* Assist in defining controls for approvals, access, data integrity, and transaction monitoring.
* Support preparation of evidence and documentation for audits, reviews, and governance checks.
* Identify opportunities to improve reporting accuracy, timeliness, and usability.

**Continuous Improvement \& Support**

* Monitor business process performance and identify opportunities for optimization and automation.
* Support post\-go\-live stabilization, issue resolution, and continuous improvement initiatives.
* Analyze recurring business issues and recommend corrective and preventive actions.
* Maintain business process documentation, knowledge repositories, and support materials.
* Contribute to lessons learned, process standardization, and best practice adoption across programs.
* Support future enhancements, release impact assessments, and business readiness activities.
