## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Merchandise Analyst – Roles \& Responsibilities

A **Merchandise Analyst** supports the merchandising and buying team by analyzing sales, inventory, product performance, and market trends to ensure the **right products are available in the right quantity, at the right time, and at the right price**.

Key Roles \& Responsibilities

* **Sales \& Performance Analysis**
* Analyze sales data by product, category, brand, store, and channel.
* Track sales trends, growth, sell\-through, margins, and profitability.
* Identify best\-selling, slow\-moving, and non\-performing products.
* Prepare regular sales and performance reports for management.
* **Inventory Management**
* Monitor inventory levels across stores, warehouses, and online channels.
* Identify overstock, understock, and slow\-moving inventory.
* Support stock replenishment and allocation decisions.
* Analyze stock cover and inventory turnover.
* Recommend stock transfers between locations based on demand.
* **Merchandise Planning**
* Support seasonal and annual merchandise planning.
* Assist in preparing category\-level sales and inventory forecasts.
* Analyze historical data to support future buying decisions.
* Track actual performance against planned budgets and targets.
* **Product \& Category Analysis**
* Evaluate product/category performance and profitability.
* Identify product gaps and opportunities for assortment improvement.
* Analyze product lifecycle and recommend continuation, replacement, markdown, or discontinuation.
* Support new product launches through data\-driven insights.
* **Pricing \& Promotion Analysis**
* Analyze the effectiveness of promotions, discounts, and campaigns.
* Monitor pricing and competitor activity.
* Evaluate the impact of markdowns on sales and margins.
* Recommend pricing or promotional strategies based on performance.
* **Forecasting \& Demand Planning**
* Develop sales forecasts using historical sales and market trends.
* Support demand planning for seasonal periods and new launches.
* Identify potential changes in customer demand and buying patterns.
* Work closely with buying and supply chain teams to improve forecast accuracy.
* **Reporting \& Dashboards**
* Develop and maintain daily, weekly, and monthly merchandising reports.
* Create dashboards using **Excel, Power BI, or other BI tools**.
* Present key insights and recommendations to the merchandising team.
* Highlight variances against sales, margin, stock, and inventory targets.
* **Cross\-Functional Coordination**
* Work closely with **Merchandising, Buying, Retail Operations, Finance, Supply Chain, Marketing, and E\-commerce** teams.
* Coordinate with stores to understand product and inventory requirements.
* Support buyers and merchandisers with data for purchasing decisions.
* **Market \& Competitor Analysis**
* Monitor competitor pricing, assortment, promotions, and product launches.
* Research market trends and customer buying behavior.
* Identify opportunities to improve the company's product assortment.
* **Process Improvement**
* Identify opportunities to improve merchandise planning and reporting processes.
* Ensure accuracy and consistency of merchandising data.
* Automate recurring reports and improve data visibility.
* Maintain accurate product, sales, and inventory databases.

Key KPIs

* Sales growth
* Gross margin
* Sell\-through %
* Stock turnover
* Stock cover
* Inventory ageing
* Markdown %
* Forecast accuracy
* Stock availability
* GMROI (Gross Margin Return on Investment)
* Overstock and stock\-out levels
* Category profitability

Ideal Candidate Profile

For a **retail/home\-furnishing business such as Danube Home**, I would look for someone with:

* **3–6 years of experience** in merchandise analysis, retail analytics, merchandising, or category management.
* Strong **Advanced Excel** skills — Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP/VLOOKUP, Power Query, formulas, dashboards.
* Knowledge of **Power BI** is an advantage.
* Strong understanding of sales and inventory metrics.
* Experience working with **ERP/retail systems**.
* Good analytical and numerical skills.
* Ability to convert data into actionable business recommendations.
* Retail/home furnishing/FMCG/e\-commerce experience preferred.
