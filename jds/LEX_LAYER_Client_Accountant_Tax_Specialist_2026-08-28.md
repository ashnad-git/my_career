## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**The Role**

We are looking for a meticulous **Accountant** to manage the financial records and tax compliance for a portfolio of our client companies.

Your goal is to ensure that every client's books are done "the right way"—fully compliant with UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) regulations and IFRS standards to ensure our clients remain penalty\-free and financially organized.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1\. Client Bookkeeping \& Management**

* Maintain accurate, up\-to\-date ledgers for a portfolio of client companies (mostly SMEs and holding structures).
* Handle bank reconciliations, expense categorization, and invoicing using accounting software Xero, QuickBooks, or Zoho.
* Ensure all records are audit\-ready at any given time.

**2\. Tax Compliance (VAT \& Corporate Tax)**

* Prepare and file **VAT Returns (5%)** for clients, ensuring all input/output tax is calculated correctly to avoid fines.
* Assist in the preparation of **Corporate Tax** filings (9%), ensuring clients understand their liabilities and exemptions (e.g., Small Business Relief).
* Register new clients for TRN (Tax Registration Numbers) and Corporate Tax with the FTA.

**3\. Financial Reporting**

* Generate monthly or quarterly financial statements (Balance Sheet, P\&L, Cash Flow) for clients to review.
* Identify discrepancies or "red flags" in client accounts and resolve them proactively.
* Create financial reports on real estate properties and investments based in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

**4\. Advisory Support**

* Answer technical queries regarding what is a deductible expense versus what is not under UAE law.
* Coordinate with the Legal Operations team to ensure financial records match the company’s legal structure.

**What We’re Looking For**

* **Experience:** Minimum 2–3 years of accounting experience **specifically in the UAE**. You must understand the local tax landscape.
* **Education:** Degree in Accounting, Finance, or Commerce. (ACCA/CPA/CMA part\-qualified or qualified is a plus).
* **Technical Skills:**
* Mastery of **Excel** (VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables).
* Proficiency in cloud accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks).
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT Law and the new Corporate Tax Law.
* **Languages:**
* **English:** Must be fluent (Written \& Spoken) as all government portals and reporting are in English.
* **Spanish:** A **huge bonus**. Being able to explain accounting concepts to our clients in their native language is a major competitive advantage.
* **Traits:** You are obsessed with accuracy. You do not cut corners. You understand that a small mistake in accounting can lead to heavy fines for the client.

**What We Offer**

* **Professional Environment:** Work alongside lawyers and strategists in a high\-growth consultancy.
* **Stability:** A key role in the recurring revenue side of our business.
* **Growth:** Opportunity to head the accounting department as our client portfolio expands.
