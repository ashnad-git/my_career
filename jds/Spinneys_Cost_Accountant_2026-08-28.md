## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**POSITION SUMMARY:**




  





 Spinneys, the UAE’s leading premium retailer is seeking a detail\-oriented
 **COST ACCOUNTANT** 
 with strong SAP experience to join our finance team in Dubai.
 



  





 This role is responsible for monitoring and analyzing production costs, inventory movements, and material variances — including wastage and shrinkage — to support accurate costing, inventory control, and operational decision\-making. This role works closely with production, warehouse, and supply chain teams to ensure cost data integrity from raw material intake through finished goods.
 



  





**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:**




  




* *Maintain and analyze standard costing, actual costing, and variance reports (material, labor, and overhead variances)*
* *Monitor stock movements (stock in/stock out) and reconcile physical inventory against system records in SAP*
* *Investigate and report on wastage, shrinkage, and yield variances on the production floor; identify root causes and recommend corrective actions*
* *Perform month\-end and year\-end closing activities related to cost of goods sold (COGS), inventory valuation, and work\-in\-progress (WIP)*
* *Conduct periodic and annual physical stock counts/cycle counts and reconcile discrepancies*
* *Prepare cost analysis reports for management, including production cost breakdowns, scrap analysis, and cost\-saving opportunities*
* *Maintain accurate Bills of Materials (BOMs) and routing data in SAP to ensure correct cost roll\-ups*
* *Collaborate with production, warehouse, and procurement teams to track raw material consumption and finished goods output*
* *Support budgeting and forecasting processes related to manufacturing costs*
* *Ensure compliance with internal controls, audit requirements, and company policies related to inventory and cost accounting*
* *Identify and implement process improvements to reduce inventory losses and improve cost accuracy*



  





**REQUIRED EXPERIENCE \& QUALIFICATIONS:**




  




* *Bachelor's / Master’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field (CMA or equivalent is a plus)*
* *Minimum 3–5 years of experience in cost accounting, preferably within a food manufacturing/production/factory environment*
* *Hands\-on experience with SAP (FI/CO modules) is required*
* *Proven experience tracking inventory movements, stock reconciliation, and analyzing wastage/shrinkage*
* *Strong understanding of standard costing, variance analysis, and inventory valuation methods (FIFO, weighted average, etc.)*
* *Familiarity with BOMs, production orders, and material master data in SAP*
* *Advanced Excel skills; experience with reporting/BI tools is a plus*
* *Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills with high attention to detail*
* *Ability to work cross\-functionally with production, warehouse, and supply chain teams*
* *Good communication skills, both written and verbal*
