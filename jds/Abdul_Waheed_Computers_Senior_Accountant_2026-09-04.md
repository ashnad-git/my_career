**About the Role**

Abdul Waheed Computers is a Sharjah\-based trading company dealing in refurbished and used laptops, tablets, and computers, with international business exposure. We are looking for a reliable and experienced Senior Accountant to take full ownership of the company's day\-to\-day accounts and financial operations.

This role covers everything from money in and money out, cash flow, banking, suppliers, and customers, to inventory costing, import/export expenses, VAT, payroll, and monthly reporting. The ideal candidate is hands\-on, trustworthy, organised, and comfortable working independently in a small business environment.

Main Responsibilities

Cash, Banking \& Payments  
\- Manage daily cash, bank accounts, transfers, cheques, and LC/TT payments  
\- Reconcile bank accounts, cash books, petty cash, and supplier/customer accounts

Accounts Payable \& Receivable  
\- Record supplier invoices and arrange timely payments  
\- Prepare customer invoices and follow up on outstanding payments

Import/Export \& Inventory Costing  
\- Calculate full landed cost of imported goods, including freight, customs duty, insurance, clearing, and shipping charges  
\- Track import shipments from purchase through to final stock costing  
\- Record foreign currency transactions and monitor exchange\-rate impact  
\- Reconcile inventory records with warehouse stock and accounting records  
\- Factor in refurbishment, repair, and freight costs when calculating product margins

Reporting \& Compliance  
\- Prepare monthly P\&L statements, balance sheets, cash flow reports, and management summaries  
\- Monitor margins by product, shipment, or category  
\- Manage UAE VAT records, filings, and payments  
\- Maintain accurate books, supporting documents, and audit records  
\- Process payroll and staff payments, including WPS requirements

Coordination \& Reporting to Management  
\- Liaise with banks, customs brokers, clearing agents, auditors, and tax consultants  
\- Provide the owner with regular updates on cash flow, costs, risks, and overall business performance

Requirements

\- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field  
\- Minimum 3 years of accounting experience  
\- Experience in a senior, sole\-charge, trading, import/export, or electronics accounting role preferred  
\- Good knowledge of UAE VAT and general UAE accounting requirements  
\- Experience with import costing, customs duty, freight, and multi\-currency transactions  
\- Strong Excel skills and experience with accounting software (Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, or similar)  
\- Good spoken and written English; Arabic, Urdu, or Hindi an advantage  
\- High level of honesty, accuracy, confidentiality, and financial discipline  
\- Able to manage the accounts function independently with minimal supervision
