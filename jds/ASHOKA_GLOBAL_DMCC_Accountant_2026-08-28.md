## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Title**: Accountant

Job Summary:

An Accountant in a commodities company is responsible for managing financial transactions related to the buying, selling, and trading of commodities such as oil, gas, agricultural products, metals, and other natural resources. The role involves tracking inventory, managing trade\-related transactions, and ensuring compliance with accounting regulations. The accountant ensures the accuracy of financial reports, assists in cost management, and provides critical financial insights into the commodity market's volatility and pricing trends.

Key Responsibilities:

* **Financial Record Keeping for Commodity Transactions**:
* Maintain accurate and up\-to\-date records for all commodity transactions, including purchases, sales, and inventory movements.
* Record the cost of goods sold (COGS) based on the acquisition and sale price of commodities.
* Track and adjust records according to market price fluctuations and contractual obligations (spot prices, forward contracts, etc.).
* **Inventory Management and Reconciliation**:
* Monitor and reconcile commodity inventory, ensuring accurate reporting of physical holdings and valuing the inventory at the current market price.
* Perform regular stocktakes and ensure commodity stock levels are consistent with financial records.
* **Accounts Payable and Receivable**:
* Manage accounts payable for commodity suppliers, ensuring timely and accurate payments.
* Oversee accounts receivable related to commodity sales, ensuring payments are received within agreed terms, and resolve any payment discrepancies or delays.
* **Financial Reporting**:
* Prepare and analyze monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements, including profit and loss statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.
* Report on commodity trading activities, pricing trends, and market conditions to senior management.
* Ensure financial statements reflect the true cost of commodity production, transportation, and storage.
* **Commodity Price and Risk Management**:
* Assist in monitoring commodity prices and their impact on the financial performance of the company.
* Record the effect of price fluctuations and hedging activities on financial statements.
* Collaborate with risk management teams to assess commodity price risks and mitigate them through derivatives or contracts.
* **Tax Compliance and Regulatory Reporting**:
* Ensure compliance with relevant tax laws and industry regulations affecting commodity transactions, including VAT, excise duties, and other specific taxes.
* Prepare and submit tax returns, ensuring proper documentation for commodity\-related transactions.
* Stay updated on the regulatory changes in commodity trading and taxation, ensuring compliance.
* **Cost Control and Profitability Analysis**:
* Analyze the costs of commodity procurement, production, and distribution to assess profitability and help the company optimize costs.
* Provide insights into price trends and their effect on cost margins, identifying areas where efficiencies can be gained.
* **Audit and Compliance**:
* Assist in the preparation of audits and liaise with auditors to provide necessary documentation.
* Ensure compliance with both internal controls and external regulations governing commodity trading and reporting.
* Ensure that all transactions related to the buying and selling of commodities are accurately recorded in line with industry standards.
* **Transaction Reconciliation and Banking**:
* Reconcile commodity transactions with bank statements, ensuring that payments and receipts align with financial records.
* Manage and track cash flow, particularly in volatile markets, ensuring the company has sufficient liquidity for trading and operations.
* **Customer and Vendor Relations**:
* Maintain relationships with suppliers, buyers, and other stakeholders in the commodities industry to ensure smooth financial operations.
* Resolve any issues with pricing, contracts, or payments promptly to maintain business continuity.

Skills and Qualifications:

**Education**:

* A Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or a related field.
* Professional certifications (e.g., CPA, ACCA, CMA) are advantageous but not mandatory.

**Experience**:

* 2\+ years of experience in accounting, preferably within the commodities or trading industry.
* Familiarity with financial modeling and reporting in a commodities environment.
* Experience in commodity accounting, including tracking physical goods, pricing strategies, and market trends.

**Skills**:

* Strong understanding of accounting principles and cost accounting practices.
* Knowledge of commodity markets and the impact of price volatility on financial reporting.
* Familiarity with commodity trading contracts, hedging, and derivative accounting.
* Proficiency in accounting software (e.g., SAP, QuickBooks, Oracle) and Microsoft Excel for data analysis and reporting.
* Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and problem\-solving abilities.
* Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written, to interact with internal teams, external vendors, and clients.
