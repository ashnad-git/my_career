## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Summary

The Accountant is responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, processing daily accounting transactions, preparing financial reports, ensuring compliance with company policies and UAE regulations, and supporting the Finance Department in achieving organizational objectives.

Key Responsibilities

* Record and maintain daily financial transactions accurately.
* Prepare and post journal entries into the accounting system.
* Perform bank, cash, supplier, and customer account reconciliations.
* Manage Accounts Payable (AP) and Accounts Receivable (AR).
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.
* Assist in month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Monitor cash flow and prepare cash forecasts.
* Ensure compliance with UAE VAT and Corporate Tax requirements.
* Maintain the General Ledger and ensure accuracy of account balances.
* Process supplier payments and follow up on outstanding customer collections.
* Maintain fixed asset registers and calculate depreciation.
* Assist with internal and external audits by providing required documentation.
* Verify invoices, purchase orders, and supporting documents before processing payments.
* Prepare account schedules and financial analyses as requested by management.
* Maintain accurate filing of accounting records and financial documents.
* Ensure compliance with company financial policies and accounting standards.
* Identify discrepancies and recommend corrective actions.
* Support budgeting and forecasting activities.
* Coordinate with other departments regarding financial matters.
* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties assigned by management.

Qualifications

* Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Professional qualification (ACCA, CMA, CPA, or equivalent) is an advantage.
* Minimum 3–5 years of accounting experience (preferably in the automotive industry).
* Knowledge of IFRS, UAE VAT, and UAE Corporate Tax.
* Proficiency in ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, Odoo, Tally, or similar) and Microsoft Excel.
* Strong analytical, numerical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
* Good communication skills in English (Arabic is an advantage
