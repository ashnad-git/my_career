## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Commercial Finance Analyst

Location: Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Department: Commercial Finance

Reports to: Head of Commercial Finance

Employment type: Full-time

Role Overview

Key Responsibilities
Partner with commercial and sales teams to build, challenge and refine budgets, forecasts and business cases.
Prepare monthly management reporting, including variance analysis of revenue, gross margin and operating costs against budget and forecast.
Develop and maintain financial models to support pricing, customer profitability and investment decisions.
Analyse sales performance by event, customer and region, and clearly communicate the drivers behind the numbers.
Support the annual budgeting and quarterly reforecasting cycles, consolidating inputs from across the business.
Track and report on key commercial KPIs, identifying risks, opportunities and corrective actions.
Evaluate capital expenditure and new business proposals, providing ROI, payback and sensitivity analysis.
Identify and communicate trends, risks and opportunities to senior finance and commercial leadership.
Support month-end close activities, including accruals, prepayments and cost centre reviews.
Work with the finance team to continuously improve reporting tools, templates and processes.

Candidate Profile
3–5 years of experience in commercial finance, FP&A, or management accounting.
Strong financial modelling and Excel skills; experience with Power BI or similar tools is a plus.
Ability to translate complex financial data into clear, business-focused insights.
Comfortable presenting and influencing at senior level.
Experience in a media, events, or technology environment is advantageous.
CIMA/ACCA part-qualified or qualified preferred.
