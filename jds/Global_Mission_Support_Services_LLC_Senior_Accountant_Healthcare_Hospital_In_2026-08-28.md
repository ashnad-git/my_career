**Job Summary:** We are seeking an experienced and professionally qualified Senior Accountant to join our healthcare/hospital finance team in Nauru. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in accounting and financial reporting, with proven experience working in a hospital, medical centre, clinic, or healthcare organization.

**Responsibilities:**

* Manage and oversee day\-to\-day accounting operations and the full set of accounts.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.
* Maintain the general ledger and perform bank, balance sheet, and intercompany reconciliations.
* Manage accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll, and cash flow.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, and cost analysis.
* Monitor financial controls and ensure compliance with accounting standards and internal policies.
* Coordinate internal and external audits and provide required documentation.
* Analyze financial performance and provide meaningful reports and recommendations to management.
* Support healthcare\-related revenue, billing, procurement, and inventory accounting processes.
* Maintain accurate financial records and ensure timely month\-end and year\-end closing.
* Work closely with management and other departments on financial and operational matters.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **Professional accounting qualification such as CA, ACCA, CPA, CMA.**
* **Minimum 5\+ years of relevant accounting experience.**
* **Mandatory experience in a hospital, healthcare facility, medical centre, clinic, or healthcare group.**
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles, financial reporting, and internal controls.
* Experience with budgeting, forecasting, reconciliations, and audit coordination.
* Strong proficiency in **MS Excel** and accounting/ERP systems.
* Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Strong communication skills and ability to work independently.
* High level of integrity, confidentiality, accuracy, and professionalism.
* Willingness and ability to **relocate and work in Nauru**.
