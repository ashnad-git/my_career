# Business Analyst - Aggregator Growth & Analytics
**Company:** Americana Restaurants
**Location:** Sharjah Emirate, United Arab Emirates

## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Purpose:** 




 Lead with analytics and support the development and execution of commercial growth and cost\-efficiency strategies across 12 markets, with a strong focus on optimizing aggregator\-led revenue. The role aims to improve return on advertising and discount investments by shifting from blanket discounting to cohort\-based, data\-driven targeting models, enabling scalable and future\-ready growth for Americana Restaurants.
 



  





**Responsibilities:** 




**Strategic Accountabilities** 



* Use data and analytics to design and lead implementation of cohort\-based discounting and visibility models, replacing blanket discounting approaches.
* Partner with food delivery aggregators to align on a common discounting and growth framework, and influence the development of advanced targeting capabilities.
* Act as the regional point of contact for strategic growth initiatives across 30\+ aggregator platforms, aligned with NRL\-led frameworks.
* Contribute to commercial strategy by identifying opportunities to optimize spend, improve efficiency, and drive sustainable growth.



**Operational Accountabilities** 



* Work closely with aggregator data teams to define, build, and track key performance metrics.
* Collaborate with the BI team to automate reporting using aggregator data feeds.
* Develop a strong understanding of Americana’s data architecture with support from the BI team.
* Extract and analyze data directly from databases (e.g., store\-level sales, customer cohorts) to build a single, actionable customer view.
* Prepare insights, performance summaries, and recommendations to support decision\-making at market and regional levels.



**People \& Stakeholder Management** 



* Work cross\-functionally with Commercial, Marketing, BI, and Operations teams across markets to ensure alignment and execution.
* Communicate insights clearly and effectively to both technical and non\-technical stakeholders.



  





**Education** 



* Bachelors(s) degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Engineering, Data Analytics, or a related quantitative field/business field.



  





**Experience** 



* 6\+ years of experience in a growth, analytics, or central commercial function within a food delivery aggregator, e\-commerce platform, or large restaurant/QSR group.
* Strong experience in business analysis, growth analytics, or performance optimization roles.
* Exposure to multi\-market or regional responsibilities is highly preferred.



  





**Knowledge** 



* Strong proficiency in SQL and Excel.
* Experience with Power BI or similar visualization tools is a strong advantage.
* Knowledge of advanced analytics tools or programming languages such as R is considered favorable.



  





**Skills \& Competencies** 



* Ability to translate business questions into structured data requirements and analytical solutions.
* Strong logical reasoning and problem\-solving skills.
* Ability to independently analyze data, derive insights, and articulate findings clearly.
* Strong communication skills, with the ability to present insights and recommendations, not just data.
* Commercial mindset with a focus on growth, efficiency, and scalability.
