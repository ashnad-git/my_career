## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an experienced, hands\-on **Senior Accountant \& Executive Admin** to work directly with the Founder across multiple businesses, including a multi\-branch F\&B business, property investments and company formation company

This is not a traditional accounting\-only position. We need someone who is **strong with numbers, highly organised and willing to take ownership of day\-to\-day financial and administrative matters.**

Key Responsibilities

* Manage accounting for a multi\-branch F\&B business
* Prepare monthly P\&L, cash\-flow and management reports
* Manage payables, receivables, supplier payments and bank reconciliations
* Monitor food costs, expenses, margins and profitability
* Maintain accounts using **Zoho Books, Xero, QuickBooks or similar**
* Reconcile personal and business bank/credit card accounts
* Maintain financial records and P\&Ls for UAE \& UK property/investment portfolios
* Follow up on client payments and outstanding receivables
* Coordinate company formations, renewals, visas and PRO matters
* Manage general administration including vehicles, insurance, fines and renewals
* Liaise with banks, accountants, suppliers, PROs and other third parties
* Maintain clear schedules of payments, renewals and important deadlines

Requirements

* Strong accounting experience
* **F\&B / restaurant accounting experience preferred**
* Excellent understanding of P\&L, cash flow and reconciliations
* Experience with Zoho Books, Xero, QuickBooks or similar
* Advanced Excel skills
* UAE VAT and Corporate Tax knowledge
* Strong organisational and follow\-up skills
* UAE experience preferred
* UAE driving licence preferred

We are looking for a **proactive problem\-solver who takes ownership** rather than waiting for instructions. The right person will become the Founder's trusted point of contact for accounting, financial control and day\-to\-day administration.

Pay: AED5,000\.00 \- AED7,000\.00 per month

Experience:

* F\&B: 4 years (Required)
* Admin: 3 years (Preferred)
