## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Accountant – Vigil Technologies LLC**

**Location:** Abu Dhabi, UAE

**Employment Type:** Full\-Time

**Department:** Finance \& Accounting

**About Vigil Technologies LLC**

Vigil Technologies LLC is a premier Extra Low Voltage (ELV) system integration and digital infrastructure firm headquartered at the Al Jazeera Stadium in Abu Dhabi, UAE. We specialize in deploying cutting\-edge enterprise\-grade security, automation, and telematics solutions. As we scale our operations across the GCC, we are seeking a meticulous, qualified **Accountant** to take total ownership of our financial recording, compliance, reporting, and risk management functions.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1\. Financial Recording \& Bookkeeping**

* **Transaction Tracking:** Record daily financial data accurately, including sales, purchases, receipts, and payments.
* **Bank Reconciliation:** Match company ledger balances with bank statements regularly to ensure zero discrepancies.
* **Managing Ledgers:** Oversee accounts payable (what the company owes) and accounts receivable (what the company is owed) to maintain healthy cash flow.

**2\. Reporting \& Analysis**

* **Financial Statements:** Compile vital documents that reveal the company's financial health, including the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement.
* **Budgeting and Forecasting:** Assist management in creating robust budget proposals, predicting future financial trends, and analyzing operational costs.
* **Management Reporting:** Translate complex financial data into actionable, easy\-to\-understand insights for non\-financial stakeholders and executive leadership.

**3\. Tax Preparation \& Compliance**

* **Tax Returns:** Calculate, prepare, and submit accurate annual, quarterly, and monthly tax returns (including UAE corporate taxes and VAT).
* **Tax Planning:** Develop strategic plans based on current legislative guidelines to legally minimize tax liabilities.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensure all financial operations strictly adhere to local, state, and federal laws and regulations.

**4\. Auditing \& Risk Management**

* **Internal Audits:** Examine financial documents and internal procedures proactively to detect and rectify errors, fraud, or inefficiencies.
* **External Audit Support:** Collaborate seamlessly with external auditors to prepare necessary reports and verify company data.
* **Risk Evaluation:** Identify potential financial risks and advise management on practical solutions to protect reserves and prevent financial calamity.

**Key Requirements \& Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum 3–5 years of proven corporate accounting experience, preferably within technology, engineering, or systems integration sectors in the UAE.
* Thorough working knowledge of UAE tax regulations, VAT filings, and corporate tax compliance.
* Advanced proficiency in ZOHO accounting software and MS Excel.
* Exceptional attention to detail, strong analytical capabilities, and high ethical standards.

**What We Offer**

* Competitive salary commensurate with experience.
* Dynamic and growth\-oriented work environment within a leading tech integration enterprise.
* Health insurance and standard UAE employment benefits.
