## Full Job Description (verbatim)

About Druids

Druids is a rapidly growing international sportswear and ecommerce brand with an established customer base and ambitious expansion plans across golf, padel, fishing, snow and lifestyle apparel.

We are looking for an experienced Commercial Finance Manager to take ownership of commercial analysis, product pricing, margin management, stock profitability and decision support.

The Role

You will act as the financial partner to Druids’ Ecommerce, Product, Buying, Merchandise Planning and Marketing teams.

You will own the commercial rules and financial analysis behind website pricing, promotions, product margin, stock investment and clearance decisions.

This is not a traditional financial\-reporting or bookkeeping position. You will use financial and operational data to influence daily commercial decisions and improve revenue, contribution, cash and working\-capital performance.

You will approve pricing and promotional recommendations within delegated authority. The Ecommerce Trading team will normally upload, schedule and quality\-check approved website changes.

This division maintains financial control while allowing the trading team to execute changes efficiently.

Key ResponsibilitiesCommercial Finance

* Act as the primary financial partner to Ecommerce, Buying, Merchandise Planning and Marketing.
* Translate financial targets into practical commercial actions.
* Evaluate revenue, gross margin, contribution and cash performance.
* Identify the products, categories, countries and channels creating or destroying value.
* Challenge unsupported commercial assumptions.
* Build financial models to support important decisions.
* Separate revenue growth from genuinely profitable growth.
* Present clear recommendations to the CFO and leadership team.
* Track whether approved actions deliver the expected results.
* Maintain a forward\-looking view of commercial risks and opportunities.

Website Pricing

* Own the financial governance of website pricing.
* Develop pricing rules by product, category, country and currency.
* Establish target retail prices using landed cost, margin and market position.
* Review recommended prices for new products before launch.
* Ensure prices support Druids’ premium\-value positioning.
* Avoid pricing products solely by applying a standard markup.
* Compare proposed prices with customer value and relevant competitors.
* Review the impact of currency, duty, freight and supplier\-cost changes.
* Approve or escalate material price changes.
* Maintain a controlled record of approved prices.
* Work with Ecommerce Trading to ensure approved changes are uploaded correctly.
* Review live prices after implementation and investigate discrepancies.

International Pricing

* Develop market\-specific pricing strategies.
* Consider local taxes, duty, delivery cost, currency and customer expectations.
* Maintain consistent brand value without requiring identical prices in every country.
* Monitor exchange\-rate movements and recommend changes when required.
* Prevent unnecessary daily price fluctuations.
* Review international price gaps and potential customer confusion.
* Ensure promotional pricing remains commercially viable by market.
* Work with Ecommerce and Technology on market and currency implementation.
* Maintain appropriate approval and audit controls.

Product Cost and Margin

* Maintain accurate landed\-cost information.
* Include product cost, freight, duty, testing, packaging and other relevant expenses.
* Establish target margin before new products are approved.
* Review expected and achieved margin by product and category.
* Identify products that cannot support the proposed retail price or required margin.
* Work with Buyers and Product Development on cost\-reduction opportunities.
* Protect product quality when assessing cost changes.
* Monitor supplier\-cost and freight movements.
* Investigate differences between expected and achieved margin.
* Ensure pricing decisions use current and reliable cost data.
* Maintain margin bridges explaining important changes.
* Report products or categories operating below approved thresholds.

Promotions and Discounts

* Establish financial rules for discounts, promotions, bundles and packs.
* Model revenue, margin, contribution and stock impact before approval.
* Review campaign proposals from Ecommerce, CRM and Marketing.
* Ensure promotions have a clear commercial objective.
* Prevent discounting from becoming the default response to weak sales.
* Protect wholesale and international pricing relationships.
* Set appropriate promotional limits by product and campaign.
* Review proposed codes, thresholds and automatic discounts.
* Approve promotional economics before execution.
* Conduct post\-campaign profitability reviews.
* Identify campaigns that shifted purchase timing rather than creating incremental value.
* Ensure expired promotions and prices return to the approved position.

Stock Investment

* Work with Merchandise Planning on inventory budgets and open\-to\-buy.
* Assess the cash and profitability impact of proposed purchase quantities.
* Review major initial orders and replenishment recommendations.
* Challenge high\-risk stock commitments.
* Evaluate inventory using expected demand, margin and sell\-through.
* Balance product availability with working\-capital efficiency.
* Identify categories consuming disproportionate cash.
* Monitor committed purchase orders and future stock payments.
* Review inventory scenarios with the CFO.
* Ensure stock investment remains within approved financial limits.
* Support leadership approval of material or high\-risk purchases.

Stock Health

* Monitor stock value, stock turn, weeks of cover and sell\-through.
* Identify slow\-moving, aged, excess and potentially obsolete inventory.
* Analyse stock by product, colour and size rather than only category.
* Assess incoming inventory alongside current stock.
* Review broken size ranges and stranded variants.
* Quantify the cash tied up in unproductive stock.
* Work with Merchandise Planning to develop early\-warning thresholds.
* Challenge delayed action on stock that is deteriorating.
* Produce weekly stock\-risk and working\-capital reporting.
* Track whether approved stock actions are completed.
* Measure recovery following intervention.

Dead Stock and Clearance

* Own the financial assessment of dead\-stock and clearance proposals.
* Identify when products require commercial intervention.
* Model different markdown levels and expected sell\-through.
* Estimate revenue, contribution, cash recovery and closing stock.
* Compare discounting with alternative options such as bundles, transfers or targeted activity.
* Avoid discounting healthy products unnecessarily.
* Approve or escalate clearance recommendations.
* Ensure clearance is targeted and does not make the entire brand appear cheap.
* Coordinate approved activity with Ecommerce Trading and Marketing.
* Monitor live performance and recommend adjustments.
* Stop clearance activity once the intended objective is achieved.
* Conduct post\-clearance reviews and record lessons.

Product and Category Performance

* Produce product and category profitability reporting.
* Review sales, margin, returns, marketing cost and stock investment.
* Identify best sellers that are less profitable than they appear.
* Identify products with strong contribution but insufficient visibility.
* Compare product performance with the original business case.
* Support decisions on replenishment, colour extensions and product discontinuation.
* Review new\-product performance after launch.
* Identify categories requiring further investment or consolidation.
* Share commercial insight with Buying, Product, Ecommerce and Marketing.
* Ensure future decisions reflect previous product performance.

Marketing and Customer Economics

* Work with Performance Marketing on acquisition profitability.
* Review customer\-acquisition cost, contribution and lifetime value.
* Assess marketing results beyond platform\-reported ROAS.
* Review performance by country, channel and customer type.
* Establish commercially sustainable spending limits.
* Identify campaigns generating revenue without sufficient contribution.
* Work with CRM on retention, discount and incremental\-value analysis.
* Evaluate influencer, content, agency and production investment where required.
* Support marketing forecasts and budget allocation.
* Ensure customer growth is financially sustainable.

Commercial Forecasting

* Build rolling revenue, margin and contribution forecasts.
* Forecast by country, channel, category and major campaign.
* Work with departments to challenge assumptions.
* Create base, upside and downside scenarios.
* Incorporate stock availability and inbound\-product timing.
* Assess the impact of pricing, discounts and marketing investment.
* Update forecasts as conditions change.
* Explain variances between forecast and actual results.
* Identify required corrective actions.
* Maintain one agreed commercial forecast.

Trading and Performance Reporting

* Produce daily and weekly commercial performance summaries.
* Monitor revenue, gross margin, contribution and stock.
* Review performance against budget, forecast and prior periods.
* Highlight material risks and opportunities.
* Convert data into practical recommendations.
* Avoid producing reports without owners or actions.
* Ensure reporting definitions remain consistent.
* Work with Analytics and Technology on reliable data.
* Automate routine reporting where appropriate.
* Present commercial performance in clear language.

Controls and Governance

* Establish approval limits for pricing, promotions and stock decisions.
* Maintain evidence of material commercial approvals.
* Ensure sensitive website changes use appropriate separation of duties.
* Require Ecommerce Trading to implement and independently quality\-check approved changes.
* Investigate pricing and promotional errors immediately.
* Prevent unauthorised discounts or margin overrides.
* Maintain controlled master cost and pricing information.
* Ensure system access reflects employee responsibilities.
* Document recurring commercial processes.
* Support audits and financial reviews.
* Escalate suspected manipulation or control failures.

AI and Automation

* Develop automated pricing, margin and stock\-health reporting.
* Create alerts for margin erosion, pricing errors and aged stock.
* Use forecasting tools to identify commercial risks and opportunities.
* Apply AI to scenario modelling and product\-performance analysis.
* Automate routine price\-file preparation where reliable.
* Maintain human approval over final pricing, discount and stock\-clearance decisions.
* Validate automated recommendations against commercial reality.
* Protect confidential financial and customer data.
* Measure whether automation improves speed, accuracy or decision quality.

Cross\-functional Working

* Work with the CFO on financial strategy and governance.
* Work with the Head of Ecommerce \& Trading on pricing and campaign execution.
* Work with Merchandise Planning on stock forecasts and open\-to\-buy.
* Work with Senior Buyers on product costs and purchase decisions.
* Work with Product Development on target costs.
* Work with Performance Marketing on acquisition economics.
* Work with CRM on customer value and promotion performance.
* Work with Operations and Warehousing on inventory accuracy.
* Work with Technology and Analytics on systems and reporting.
* Ensure actions have clear owners and deadlines.
