## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Role :\-

* Preparing cost estimates for new and proposed products.
* Supporting management and sales managers in pricing discussions for projects, bids, and tenders.
* Providing precosting for job orders to aid profitability analysis before order confirmation.
* Coordinating with technical teams to offer pricing alternatives for customers.
* Maintaining standard costs for new and existing orders for comparative analysis.
* Reviewing standard and actual costs periodically to identify discrepancies.
* Keeping accurate records of work order costs, including materials, labour, and overheads.
* Analysing work order costs against estimates to detect variances.
* Recommending cost saving measures in new product designs.
* Conducting month end audits of work in progress and raw materials, reporting irregularities.
* Updating and maintaining the costing database for data integrity.
* Calculating and maintaining costs for ink mixtures and adhesives.
