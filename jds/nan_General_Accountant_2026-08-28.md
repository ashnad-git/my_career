## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a **responsible, experienced and detail\-oriented Accountant** to join our team in Sharjah.

Key Responsibilities:

* Handle day\-to\-day accounting transactions and bookkeeping
* Maintain Sales, Purchase, Payment and Receipt records
* Manage Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable
* Perform bank reconciliations
* Prepare customer and supplier statements and follow up on outstanding balances
* Maintain accurate records of expenses, invoices and payments
* Prepare and file **UAE VAT returns**
* Assist with UAE Corporate Tax requirements when required
* Prepare monthly financial reports, Profit \& Loss and Balance Sheet
* Maintain inventory and stock\-related accounting records
* Handle payroll and employee\-related accounting records
* Coordinate with auditors, banks and management when required
* Maintain accurate and properly organized accounting records and documentation

Requirements:

* **Previous accounting experience in the UAE is mandatory**
* **Hands\-on experience with UAE VAT filing is mandatory**
* Knowledge of UAE Corporate Tax is an advantage, but not mandatory
* **Strong knowledge of Microsoft Excel is required**
* **Experience with Zoho Books and/or QuickBooks is required**
* **Good command of English is required**
* Must be **responsible, reliable and well\-organized**
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy
* Ability to work independently and complete tasks on time
* Experience in manufacturing, trading or distribution companies is an advantage

Education:

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field is preferred.

Experience:

Minimum **2–3 years of accounting experience**, including relevant experience in the UAE.

Pay: From AED3,000\.00 per month

Application Question(s):

* How many years of accounting experience do you have in the UAE?
* Do you have hands\-on experience preparing and filing UAE VAT returns?

* English (Preferred)
