**Job Summary:**  
We are looking for a detail\-oriented and proactive **Accountant** to join our growing audit and accounting firm. The ideal candidate will assist in bookkeeping, financial reporting, VAT compliance, and audit\-related tasks while supporting clients across various industries.

Key Responsibilities:

* Maintain accurate accounting records and bookkeeping for clients.
* Prepare bank reconciliations, account schedules, and financial reports.
* Assist in the preparation of financial statements.
* Support audit engagements and client documentation requirements.
* Prepare and assist with VAT returns and other tax compliance matters.
* Coordinate with clients regarding accounting and regulatory requirements.
* Perform general accounting and administrative duties as assigned.

Requirements:

* Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* 1–2 years of accounting experience (audit firm experience is an advantage).
* Knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax regulations.
* Familiarity with the procedures and compliance requirements of the FTA (Federal Tax Authority), including VAT return filing and tax compliance.
* Proficient in Microsoft Excel and accounting software (QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Tally, or similar).
* Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* Detail\-oriented and able to meet deadlines.
* Comfortable using modern technology and AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or similar applications) to improve productivity, research, documentation, and workflow efficiency.
* Ability to learn and adapt to new digital tools and accounting technologies.

Preferred Candidates:

* Valid **UAE Spouse Visa** holders are highly preferred.
* Immediate joiners will be given preference.
* UAE audit or accounting firm experience is an advantage.
