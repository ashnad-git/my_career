# Offshore Cash & Bank Accountant

## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a highly detail\-oriented and trustworthy Cash \& Bank Accountant to join our Finance team. The role will have responsibility for maintaining accurate cash and bank records across the Group, performing regular bank reconciliations and, importantly, preparing and uploading payments through the banking platforms.

This is a role where accuracy, attention to detail, confidentiality and integrity are critical, as the individual will have access to sensitive financial information and will be responsible for handling the payment process.

The successful candidate will be comfortable working independently, following established controls and processes, and identifying discrepancies before they become issues.

Key Responsibilities

Cash \& Bank

* Maintain accurate cash and bank records for multiple Group entities and bank accounts.
* Perform daily/weekly bank reconciliations and ensure all transactions are accurately recorded in the ERP.
* Investigate and resolve unreconciled items, discrepancies and unusual transactions promptly.
* Monitor bank balances and provide regular cash position updates to the Finance team.
* Ensure bank transactions are correctly allocated to the appropriate entity, account and GL code.
* Maintain accurate records of transfers between bank accounts and entities.
* Assist with month\-end cash and bank close activities.

Payments

* Prepare and upload supplier, payroll, tax and other approved payments to the relevant banking platforms.
* Ensure payment details agree to the approved supporting documentation before uploading.
* Perform thorough checks on beneficiary names, bank details, amounts, currencies, entities and payment references.
* Ensure payments are uploaded to the correct bank account and entity.
* Follow the Company's payment approval and segregation\-of\-duties procedures at all times.
* Maintain a clear audit trail of payments uploaded and supporting documentation.
* Escalate any discrepancies, unusual payment requests or changes to bank details before processing.
* Ensure payment files are uploaded accurately and within agreed deadlines.
* Support the Finance team with payment runs and urgent payment requests where required.

Accounts \& Finance Support

* Assist with vendor and customer account reconciliations where required.
* Assist with intercompany reconciliations and bank\-to\-bank transfers.
* Support the wider Finance team during month\-end close.
* Provide supporting schedules and documentation for audit requirements.
* Assist with ad hoc finance tasks and reporting as required.

Key Requirements

* 2–4\+ years' experience in a cash, bank, treasury or accounting role.
* Strong experience with bank reconciliations and payment processing.
* Previous experience uploading payments through online banking platforms.
* Strong understanding of basic accounting principles and double\-entry bookkeeping.
* Experience working with an ERP system; NetSuite experience would be highly advantageous.
* Excellent attention to detail and a methodical approach to work.
* High level of integrity and trustworthiness — essential for the role.
* Strong ability to identify errors, inconsistencies and unusual transactions.
* Ability to work with large volumes of transactions while maintaining accuracy.
* Good Excel skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet strict deadlines.
* Strong written and verbal English communication skills.
* Experience working with multiple entities, currencies and bank accounts would be advantageous.

Personal Attributes

We are particularly looking for someone who is:

* Extremely detail\-oriented — small errors in payments or bank reconciliations cannot be overlooked.
* Trustworthy and highly responsible — the role involves access to banking platforms and sensitive financial information.
* Process\-driven — follows controls and procedures consistently rather than taking shortcuts.
* Organised — able to manage multiple payment runs and reconciliations simultaneously.
* Proactive — investigates discrepancies rather than simply flagging them.
* Reliable — consistently meets deadlines, particularly around payment runs and month\-end.
* Discreet — understands the confidentiality associated with financial and payment information.
* Comfortable working remotely and communicating effectively with a Dubai\-based Finance team.

What Success Looks Like

Within the role, the successful candidate will:

* Maintain clean and fully reconciled bank accounts.
* Ensure payments are uploaded accurately and on time, with minimal errors.
* Identify and resolve discrepancies quickly.
* Maintain a complete and reliable audit trail for all payment activity.
* Follow payment controls and approval procedures without exception.
* Provide the Finance team with confidence that cash and payment processing is being managed accurately and securely.
