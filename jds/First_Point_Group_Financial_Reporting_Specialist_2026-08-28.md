**Financial Reporting Specialist \| Dubai** 




  





 We're currently supporting a leading financial services organisation in Dubai in the search for a Financial Reporting Specialist to join its Finance team.
 



  





 This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Finance professional to take ownership of financial and regulatory reporting within a well\-established organisation, working closely with senior stakeholders and contributing to a strong financial control environment.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities:** 




  




* Lead financial and regulatory reporting in accordance with IFRS, CBUAE Prudential Regulations and Basel requirements.
* Prepare and review periodic financial statements and regulatory submissions, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
* Support and strengthen the organization's financial control framework and governance standards.
* Produce management reporting, financial analysis and performance reporting for senior leadership.
* Work closely with internal stakeholders and external auditors to support financial reporting and regulatory compliance.



  





**Requirements:** 




  




* Minimum of 3 years experience in financial reporting within banking or a similar financial services environment.
* Strong knowledge of IFRS, CBUAE regulatory reporting, Prudential Regulations and Basel requirements.
* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Commerce or a related discipline.
* Arabic speaker preferred
