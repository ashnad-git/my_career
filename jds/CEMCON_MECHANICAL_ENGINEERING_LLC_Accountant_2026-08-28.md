## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Key Responsibilities:1\. Accounting \& Bookkeeping**

* Record and reconcile all financial transactions (sales, purchases, expenses, bank).
* Maintain general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and trial balance.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements (P\&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).
* Conduct regular bank reconciliations and manage cash flow.

**2\. VAT (Value Added Tax) Compliance**

* Prepare and file quarterly VAT returns with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).
* Ensure accurate VAT treatment on sales, purchases, imports, and exports.
* Maintain VAT records and reconciliation as per FTA requirements.
* Handle VAT audits and correspondence with authorities.

**3\. Corporate Tax Compliance**

* Assist in preparing tax computations as per UAE Corporate Tax Law.
* Ensure proper accounting for taxable income, exemptions, and adjustments.
* Maintain records required for corporate tax filings.
* Coordinate with external auditors and tax advisors for compliance.

**4\. Payroll \& WPS (Wage Protection System)**

* Process monthly payroll in compliance with UAE labour laws.
* Upload salary files to the WPS system and ensure timely disbursement.
* Maintain employee records including leave, end\-of\-service benefits, and gratuity.

**5\. Budgeting \& Reporting**

* Assist management in preparing annual budgets and forecasts.
* Monitor variances between actual results and budgets.
* Provide financial analysis and reports for decision\-making.

**6\. Audit \& Compliance**

* Liaise with internal and external auditors during audits.
* Ensure compliance with UAE financial regulations and company policies.
* Safeguard company assets through proper internal controls.

**7\. Other Responsibilities**

* Handle petty cash management.
* Prepare customer/vendor account reconciliations.
* Support management with ad hoc financial reports and analysis.
* Implement improvements in accounting systems and processes.

**Qualifications \& Skills:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field.
* Minimum 3–5 years of accounting experience in UAE.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE VAT and Corporate Tax laws**.
* Experience with **WPS payroll system**.
* Proficiency in accounting software (Tally, QuickBooks, Peachtree, or ERP systems).
* Advanced skills in MS Excel.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Good communication skills .
