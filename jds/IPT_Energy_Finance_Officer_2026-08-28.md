## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Summary:** 

The Finance Officer is responsible for financial planning, analysis, reporting, treasury management, and procurement operations. This role involves compiling, consolidating, and analyzing financial data from multiple departments to support business reporting, budgeting, and strategic decision\-making. The Officer ensures cost\-effective acquisition of goods and services, monitors internal controls, and provides insights to optimize financial performance and resource allocation.

**Main Duties and Responsibilities:**

**Financial Planning \& Analysis (FP\&A)**

· Prepare monthly financial statements, including Profit \& Loss (P\&L), Balance Sheets (BS), Cash Flow (CF), ratios, dashboards, and variance analysis (Budget vs. Actual), highlighting key variances.

· Participate in the preparation and adjustment of the annual budget in collaboration with department heads.

· Review journal voucher (JV) transactions before monthly financial statement preparation and coordinate with the accounting team to ensure accuracy.

· Develop and maintain financial models to support budgeting, forecasting, long\-term planning, and financial studies.

· Support annual financial audits, including gathering documentation, reviewing drafts, and highlighting discrepancies.

· Ensure timely and accurate issuance of periodic financial reports, including gross margin analyses, receivables vs. credit limits, and discount analyses.

· Review daily receivables reports and highlight overdue payments, exceeded credit limits, or other payment issues.

· Provide daily financial comments and approvals on next\-day sales orders.

· Conduct ad\-hoc financial projects and studies as requested.

**Treasury Management**

· Track daily account balances and prepare daily banking reports by entity.

· Initiate daily payment transfers via online banking upon manager approval.

· Ensure banking documents and statements are received and shared with the accounting team.

· Monitor and validate bank interest calculations, fees, and commissions, highlighting discrepancies.

· Liaise with banks for account openings, discrepancies, and other banking requests.

**Procurement**

· Manage procurement for office and terminal purchases, ensuring competitive pricing and compliance with company policies.

· Review quotations, track purchase history, and analyze pricing to optimize procurement processes.

· Monitor procurement expenses and conduct studies to identify cost\-saving opportunities and process improvements.

**Requirements:**

Bachelor’s Degree in Finance or related field.

1–3 years of experience in FP\&A, financial analysis, or related roles.

Strong analytical, financial modeling, and problem\-solving skills.

Proficiency in financial software and ERP systems (e.g., advanced Excel).

Arabic Speaker is an advantage.
