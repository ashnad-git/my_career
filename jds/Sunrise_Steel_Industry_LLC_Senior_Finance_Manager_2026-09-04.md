## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**About Us** 




 Sunrise Steel Industry LLC is the dedicated Middle East processing and delivery center for Steel Searcher, a subsidiary of the publicly listed ZG Group (HKEX: 6676\). Originating in Shanghai in 2012, our parent group operates a major industrial platform with over 1,100 employees and an annual transaction volume of USD 25 billion. Backed by the financial stability and transparent governance of a listed parent company, Sunrise Steel brings a robust, globally vetted supply chain directly to our local operations in the GCC. We connect steel mills, traders, and project contractors across Asia and the Middle East, providing practical and integrated steel supply chain solutions. Experience precision in every bar—get in touch to discuss how we can support your next project.
 



  





**Responsibilities:** 



* Oversee daily financial operations for the steel processing factory. Actively design and continuously optimize internal financial systems, controls, and workflows to ensure maximum efficiency and compliance.
* Direct trade and supply chain finance activities, including LC issuance, inventory financing, and tray business.
* Act as a strategic financial partner to cross\-functional departments (Sales, Production, and Procurement). Provide actionable financial insights to drive overall business objectives, control costs, and mitigate risks.
* Lead, mentor, and build a high\-performing internal finance team, fostering a culture of accuracy, continuous learning, and operational excellence.



  





**Requirements:** 



* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
* Minimum of 5 years of financial experience, strictly within the steel, rebar, or heavy manufacturing industry.
* Strong expertise in LC issuance, trade finance, and supply chain financing.
* Excellent communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills for effective cross\-departmental alignment and stakeholder management.
* A solid network with local banks in the UAE is preferred.
