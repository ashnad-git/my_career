## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Finance Analyst Financial Planning & Analysis

Finance Analyst's main goal is to create business value through transforming data into insights that can help business unit to make better business decisions and can deliver better sales & margin opportunity, lower OPEX or optimizing Working Capital. Reporting to the FP&A Manager the Finance analyst is responsible to drive and coordinate reporting and Budgeting processes in line with Group deadlines. He/She can identify trends through use of data analytics, data visualization and data modeling techniques and technologies and will coordinate with Finance BP to make sure these insights can be translated into actionable deliverables.

Key Responsibilities

Lead Forecast and Budget Process for business units and responsible for review of Budgets.
Work closely with the accounting team and Finance BP, building relationships across departments (incl. Finance Business Partners).
Identify and drive reporting and Budgeting process improvements, oversee further development and implementation of automated reporting / business intelligence tools.
Develop fully automated financial reports to eliminate the need for manual reporting.
Work on assigned analytical projects in Group Finance, monitor, improve and sustain all implemented projects of Group Finance.
Implement new data analysis methodologies and tools.

Skills, Experience & Qualification

Min of 4-6 years of relevant finance experience in a well-developed corporate finance environment ideally a multinational company with advanced reporting and data analytics processes.
Graduate from top university or business school and ideally studying for an internationally recognized professional qualification (ICAEW, CIMA, CPA, CFA, CA...).
Proficiency with Microsoft Excel a clear must; familiarity with data query / data management tools is extremely highly valued.
Dynamic, driven and energetic, eager to drive business decision making.

Key Competencies

Data Analytics
Data Visualisation
Financial Analysis
Financial Accounting and Reporting
Data Analysis Tools
Project management
