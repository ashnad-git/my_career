## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Trustbend Packers \& Movers is looking for a skilled and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to join its finance team in **Dubai**. This role is suitable for a finance professional with strong accounting knowledge who can manage daily transactions, maintain accurate financial records, and support reporting and compliance activities. The successful candidate will help ensure efficient financial management across the company’s logistics and moving operations.


**Key Responsibilities:**


* Record and maintain daily financial transactions accurately in accounting systems.
* Manage **accounts payable and accounts receivable** activities.
* Prepare invoices, receipts, payment vouchers, and journal entries.
* Perform regular bank and account reconciliations.
* Maintain general ledger accounts and supporting financial records.
* Prepare monthly financial statements, reports, and account summaries.
* Monitor outstanding receivables and coordinate payment follow\-ups.
* Verify invoices, expenses, purchase documents, and payment requests.
* Assist with budgeting, cash\-flow monitoring, and financial planning.
* Prepare and maintain **VAT and tax\-related documentation** in accordance with UAE requirements.
* Support internal and external audits by providing accurate financial records.
* Coordinate with logistics, procurement, warehouse, and administration teams regarding financial matters.
* Maintain organized digital and physical accounting files.
* Ensure financial information is handled with strict confidentiality.

**Requirements:**


* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce**, or a related field.
* 2–5 years of relevant accounting or finance experience.
* Strong knowledge of bookkeeping, financial reporting, reconciliations, and accounting principles.
* Experience with accounting software, ERP systems, and **Microsoft Excel**.
* Knowledge of UAE VAT and applicable financial procedures is an advantage.
* Strong numerical, analytical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good communication and organizational abilities.
* Ability to meet deadlines and work effectively in a fast\-paced environment.

**Skills \& Competencies:**


* **Financial accounting and bookkeeping**
* Accounts payable and receivable management
* Bank reconciliation and invoice processing
* Financial reporting and record management
* VAT and tax documentation
* Microsoft Excel and accounting software
* Strong analytical and numerical skills
* Time management and organization
* Professional integrity and confidentiality

**Why Join Trustbend Packers \& Movers?**


At Trustbend Packers \& Movers, accurate financial management is essential for supporting efficient logistics and moving services. This **Accountant** position in **Dubai** offers an excellent opportunity to strengthen your accounting expertise, work alongside experienced professionals, and contribute to effective financial operations while building a rewarding long\-term career within a growing organization.
