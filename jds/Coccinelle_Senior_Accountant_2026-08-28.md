## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are seeking a highly experienced and detail\-oriented **Senior Accountant** to join our team. The successful candidate will oversee daily accounting operations, ensure accurate financial reporting, maintain compliance with accounting standards and tax regulations, and support management with financial analysis and business performance reporting. Experience in the FMCG retail or supermarket sector is essential.

**Responsibilities**

* Oversee day\-to\-day accounting operations, including general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.
* Monitor cash flow, working capital, and daily cash reconciliations.
* Ensure accurate recording of sales, purchases, inventory movements, and operating expenses.
* Reconcile inventory with accounting records and investigate stock variances.
* Supervise the month\-end and year\-end closing processes.
* Monitor supplier payments, customer receivables, and aging reports.
* Maintain fixed asset registers and depreciation schedules.
* Support ERP system improvements and ensure data accuracy.

**Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum **3 years** of accounting experience, with at least **1 years in the FMCG supermarket or retail industry**.
* Strong knowledge of IFRS, VAT regulations, and financial reporting.
* Experience with inventory accounting and cost control in a retail environment.
* Proficiency in Odoo, or similar.
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
* Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving abilities.
* Strong communication and leadership skills.
* High level of integrity, accuracy, and attention to detail.

Pay: AED5,000\.00 \- AED6,500\.00 per month
