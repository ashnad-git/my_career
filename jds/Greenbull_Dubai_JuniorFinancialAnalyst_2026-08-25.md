## Full Job Description (verbatim)

JOB TITLE: Junior Finance Analyst

COMPANY OVERVIEW:

Greenbull is an international group of companies in France, Switzerland, and the United Arab Emirates. Through our flagship brands, Greenbull undertakes and develops its activities in several industries: Finance, Real Estate, Education, Media, E-commerce, and Consumer.

Greenbull Ventures is a pioneering brand of Greenbull Group, specialising in mergers and acquisitions, diligently scouting for opportunities among ambitious companies, leveraging our experience in successful ventures within different industries.

JOB OVERVIEW:

We are seeking a highly motivated Junior Finance Analyst to join our team. This is an exciting opportunity for a recently graduated individual to develop their skills in mergers and acquisitions and contribute to our ongoing success. You will be reporting to the Head of M&A and Ventures and work closely with the VP of Ventures and Acquisitions.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Market Research:
· Conduct market research to identify potential acquisition targets and investment opportunities.
· Analyse industry trends, competitive landscapes, and market dynamics to support strategic decision-making.

Financial Analysis:
· Assist in financial modelling, valuation analysis, and due diligence processes.
· Review and analyse financial statements, business plans, and other relevant documents.

Deal Execution:
· Support the deal execution process, including coordination with internal teams and external advisors.
· Help prepare presentation materials, reports, and summaries for internal and external stakeholders.

Portfolio Management:
· Assist in monitoring the performance of portfolio companies, tracking key financial metrics and KPIs.
· Help identify risks and opportunities within the portfolio and provide actionable insights.

Administrative Support:
· Maintain accurate records and databases of deal flow, pipeline, and portfolio companies.
· Assist in organising and coordinating meetings, presentations, and other team activities.

REQUIREMENTS:

Education:
· Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field. Master's degree is a plus.

Experience:
· 0-2 years of experience in finance, investment banking, private equity, or related fields.
· Demonstrated interest in M&A and investment activities through internships, coursework, or personal projects.

Skills:
· Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with proficiency in financial modelling and data analysis.
· Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
· Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word).
· Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
· Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
