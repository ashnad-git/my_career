## Full Job Description (verbatim)

A **General Accountant \- AR/AP** for fit\-outs focuses and manages day\-to\-day financial operations, with a strong focus on project\-level cost tracking, supplier payments, and client invoicing. This role requires specialized knowledge of project costing, material procurement tracking, and compliance with local tax regulations

**DUTIES \& RESPONSIBILITIES**

· Maintain accurate accounting records and ensure all financial transactions are properly recorded

· Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly financial reports

· Handle accounts payable and receivable processes

· Monitor project costs, material expenses, subcontractor payments, and budget allocations for fit\-out projects

· Prepare and process supplier invoices, purchase orders, and payment vouchers

· Reconcile bank statements, petty cash, and company accounts

· Coordinate with procurement and operations departments regarding project expenses and cost tracking

· Maintain proper documentation for contracts, invoices, quotations, and delivery notes

· Assist in preparing financial statements, profit \& loss reports, and balance sheets

· Ensure compliance with UAE VAT regulations and prepare VAT filings

· Monitor employee payroll, leave salaries, and expense reimbursements

· Assist auditors during internal and external audits

· Track receivables and follow up on client payments

· Maintain confidentiality of financial information and company records

· Support management with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis

· Ensure compliance with company financial policies and procedures
