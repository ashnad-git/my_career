**This role is for our new e\-commerce entity.** 




 We are looking for a detail\-oriented Accountant / Finance Executive with experience in the e\-commerce industry and hands\-on knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics ERP. The candidate will be responsible for managing day\-to\-day accounting activities and supporting accurate financial reporting.
 



**Key Responsibilities** 



* Manage daily accounting transactions, including sales, purchases, receipts, payments, and journal entries.
* Handle Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and bank reconciliations.
* Reconcile e\-commerce sales, marketplace settlements, payment gateways, refunds, and returns.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and support month\-end closing.
* Maintain accurate accounting records and supporting documentation.
* Assist with
 
 VAT, audit, and statutory compliance requirements.
* Monitor outstanding receivables and payables.
* Ensure timely and accurate posting of transactions in Microsoft Dynamics ERP.
* Identify and resolve accounting discrepancies.
* Support management with financial analysis and other finance\-related activities.



**Requirements** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field.
* Hands on accounting/finance experience, preferably in the e\-commerce industry.
* Hands\-on experience with Microsoft Dynamics ERP.
* Good knowledge of accounting principles and MS Excel.
* Experience with e\-commerce platforms, payment gateways, and marketplace reconciliations is preferred.
* Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and ability to meet deadlines.
* Good communication and organizational skills.
