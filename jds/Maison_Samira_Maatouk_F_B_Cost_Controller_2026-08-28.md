Conduct regular inventory audits and stock reconciliations

Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly cost reports Review purchasing transactions and supplier pricing

Ensure compliance with internal controls and company policies

Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or related field
