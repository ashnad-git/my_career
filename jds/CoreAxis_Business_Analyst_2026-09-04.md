 We are looking for a detail\-oriented Business Analyst to bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. You will conduct discovery workshops, document as\-is and to\-be processes, and create functional specifications that guide development and configuration of enterprise solutions including ERP, CRM, and integration platforms.
   

  

**Responsibilities**
* Conduct stakeholder interviews, discovery workshops, and process\-mapping sessions.
* Document as\-is and to\-be business processes using standard notations (BPMN, flowcharts).
* Write detailed functional specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
* Perform gap analysis between client requirements and platform capabilities.
* Support solution design sessions alongside solution architects and technical leads.
* Facilitate user\-acceptance testing (UAT) and coordinate defect triage.
* Create training materials and deliver end\-user training during go\-live.
* Maintain requirements traceability matrices and change\-request logs.


**Requirements**
* 3\+ years of experience in business analysis within ERP, CRM, or enterprise IT projects.
* Strong skills in requirements gathering, process mapping (BPMN), and gap analysis.
* Experience writing functional specifications, user stories, and test scripts.
* Familiarity with Dynamics 365, Salesforce, or similar enterprise platforms.
* Proficiency with tools such as Visio, Miro, Jira, or Azure DevOps.
* Excellent analytical, documentation, and stakeholder management skills.
* Strong communication skills in English; Arabic is an advantage.


 What We Offer
   

  

* Competitive compensation with clear career progression to Senior BA or Functional Consultant.
* Exposure to diverse industries including retail, finance, government, and logistics.
* Investment in professional certifications (CBAP, PMI\-PBA, platform\-specific).
* Supportive team culture with mentorship from senior consultants.
* Hybrid work model and flexible hours.
* Modern workspace at Sharjah Research Technology \& Innovation Park.
