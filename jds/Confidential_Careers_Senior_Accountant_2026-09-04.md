## Full Job Description (verbatim)

A Senior Accountant will report to the Accounts Manager at our company's HO in Dubai Downtown.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities:** 




  




* Applies principles of accounting in analyzing financial information to prepare entries to accounts, such as general ledger accounts, documenting business transactions.



  




* Maintains General Accounting of Daily Transactions (Supplier Invoices, Supplier Payments, Customer Invoices.Intercompany Invoicing, Employee Declarations.







* Maintains general and subsidiary ledgers and journals; posts debits and credits; records receipts and balances to the accounting system; examines and records financial transactions.







* Determines funding opportunities and advises on investment opportunities.







* Maintains relations with banks regarding the following: compliance, LC, LG, hedging, and any other financial instruments.



  




* Ensure that record\-keeping and accounts meet the conditions of funders or statutory bodies.



  




* Ensure compliance with relevant legislation.







* Assists with the preparation of the Company’s budgets maintain proper usage of office equipment for cost\-effective purchasing.







* Substantiates financial transactions by auditing documents.
* Reconciles financial discrepancies by collecting and analyzing account information.







* Contributes to team effort by accomplishing related results as needed.







* Protects the company's value by keeping information confidential.



  





**Accountability** 
 :
 



  




* Receives and verifies suppliers’ invoices and ensures that transactions comply with financial policies and procedures.







* Compiles data for preparation of periodic and annual financial reports.



  




* Prepares trial balances and balance sheets; closes accounts at the end of the fiscal year and prepares opening balances and accounts at the beginning of the new fiscal year.



  




* Accomplishes the Finance Department and organization mission by completing related results and assignments as needed.



  




* In charge of all financial manager duties in the event of absence.

**Experience:** 




  





 10\+ years of financial accounting experience \- proven experience as a senior accountant/ Chief Accountant/ Treasurer \- Experience with general ledger functions and the month\-end/year\-end close process
 



  





**Skills:** 




  





 Thorough knowledge of basic accounting procedures
 



 Excellent knowledge in “bank relations”
 



 Understanding of International Financial Reporting Standard (IFRS)
 



 Excellent knowledge of MS Office and solid experience with accounting software packages – QuickBooks is preferable
 



 Reporting research results \- Deadline –Oriented
 



 Proactive personality \- Team player
 



 Communication \- General math
