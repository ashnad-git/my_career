## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Company:** Infolinks Accounting \& Bookkeeping

**Location:** Dubai, UAE

**Job Type:** Full\-time

**Reports To:** Account \& Finance Manager

**About the Role**

We are seeking a versatile Accountant who can manage day\-to\-day bookkeeping and compliance work for clients while also driving new business growth for the firm. This role suits someone with solid accounting fundamentals who is also confident, presentable, and comfortable engaging with prospective clients.

Key Responsibilities

***Accounting \& Bookkeeping***

● Perform daily bookkeeping entries: sales/purchase invoices, bank transactions, journal entries, and reconciliations

● Maintain accurate general ledgers across multiple client accounts

● Prepare monthly financial statements (P\&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)

● Prepare and file UAE VAT returns within FTA deadlines

● Assist with Corporate Tax computations and filings

● Perform bank reconciliations and resolve discrepancies

● Manage accounts payable/receivable and vendor payment follow\-ups

● Maintain organized, audit\-ready records for each client

● Support external/internal audits with required schedules and documentation

● Liaise with clients to collect documents and clarify transactions

Requirements

***Qualifications***

● Bachelor’s degree in accounting, Finance, or Business Administration

● Professional certification (ACCA, CMA, CPA) is a plus

● 2–5 years of experience in accounting/bookkeeping, ideally within a UAE accounting or corporate services firm

***Skills***

● Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax regulations

● Proficiency in accounting software: Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero, Tally, or similar

● Advanced Excel skills (VLOOKUP, pivot tables, reconciliations)

● Solid understanding of IFRS and general accounting principles

● Confident communicator with strong presentation and negotiation skills

● Proficiency with CRM tools (Zoho CRM, HubSpot, etc.) is a plus

***Other Requirements***

● Fluent English; Arabic/Hindi/Urdu a plus

● UAE driving license preferred (for client visits)

● UAE experience strongly preferred

● Must be based in Dubai or willing to relocate

**What We Offer**

● Competitive salary

● Visa sponsorship (if applicable)

● Growth path toward Senior Accountant, Operations Manager, or Business Development Manager roles

Pay: AED2,500\.00 \- AED3,500\.00 per month
