 At Ounass, we’re looking for a temporary
 **Finance Business Partner (FBP)** 
 acts as a strategic advisor to business units, providing financial insights and analysis to support decision\-making. This role ensures alignment between financial objectives and business strategy, driving efficiency, profitability, and sustainable growth
   

  

****What You Will Be Doing****
* Partner with leadership to develop financial strategies that align with business goals.
* Provide financial analysis and recommendations to support strategic initiatives and investment decisions.
* Identify risks and opportunities, proactively advising on financial performance and operational efficiency.
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams to optimize cost structures and enhance revenue growth.
* Lead the financial planning and forecasting processes, ensuring accuracy and alignment with corporate objectives.
* Analyze financial data and trends to provide actionable insights for business performance improvement.
* Support budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis, ensuring financial discipline across business units.
* Develop financial models to evaluate business cases, new projects, and investment opportunities.
* Drive process improvements to enhance financial reporting, planning, and cost control measures.
* Ensure compliance with financial regulations, policies, and internal controls.
* Prepare and present financial reports to stakeholders, translating complex data into clear recommendations.
* Provide financial guidance and training to non\-financial managers, fostering financial acumen within the organization.
* Collaborate with teams to enhance financial awareness and accountability in decision\-making.
* Support team development by sharing best practices and mentoring finance colleagues.
* Engage with stakeholders at all levels to facilitate a finance\-driven performance culture


****What We Are Looking For:****
* Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, or a related field.
* Professional certification (e.g., CA)
* Ongoing professional development in financial analysis, business strategy, and leadership.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills with a strategic mindset.
* Proficiency in financial modeling, forecasting, and data interpretation.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
* Advanced knowledge of SAP as ERP systems, financial software, and reporting tools.
* Ability to work in a fast\-paced, dynamic environment and influence decision\-making.
* Strong business partnering, commercial finance, FP\&A and stakeholder management experience
