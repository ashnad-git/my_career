## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Overview**
**JOB DESCRIPTION**
 The Cost Accountant / Analyst supports the Plant Accounting team at ADM Abu Dhabi in day\-to\-day costing and financial reporting activities within a JD Edwards (JDE) environment. Reporting to the Plant Controller, this role supports standard costing, variance analysis, inventory accounting, and period\-close processes while building the technical and analytical skills needed for progression within the function.
   

  

**Responsibilities**
* Support and help maintain standard costs across BOMs and routings within JDE, taking ownership of assigned areas under the direction of the Plant Controller.
* Support the annual cost roll process by preparing and validating cost component data.
* Help review and update labor and overhead rate schedules in line with inputs from operations and HR.
* Assist with month\-end cost accounting close activities, including work order closures and WIP reconciliation in JDE.
* Support accurate cost allocation and posting of manufacturing transactions in the ERP system.
* Assist in analyzing manufacturing variances including Job Cost Variance (JCV) and Purchase Price Variance (PPV).
* Prepare variance schedules comparing standard costs to actual production costs, document findings, and present summary reports to the Plant Controller.
* Support preparation of variance and performance reports for plant management review.
* Assist with financial modeling and ad hoc analysis as directed.
* Assist in coordinating cost accounting and reporting activities to meet monthly, quarterly, and annual deadlines.
* Support compliance with GAAP and internal financial policies in all cost accounting activities.
* Perform account reconciliations and assist with balance sheet reviews and general ledger activities during period close.
* Maintain accurate financial records and documentation to support audit readiness.
* Serve as a focal point for internal and external audit requests, coordinating documentation and analysis as required.
* Assist in coordinating physical inventory counts and fixed asset verification processes.
* Support cycle count programs, ensuring timely posting and reconciliation within JDE.
* Help investigate and resolve inventory discrepancies in coordination with operations and supply chain teams.
* Liaise with production teams to support alignment on costing assumptions and inventory valuation.
* Contribute ideas for improvement across costing, reporting, and inventory processes.
* Support ERP system improvements related to costing and financial reporting.
* Adhere to internal controls and support the strengthening of financial governance frameworks.
* Build collaborative working relationships across operations, supply chain, and finance teams.


**Skills**
* Good understanding of accounting principles and GAAP.
* Analytical mindset with strong attention to detail.
* Strong communication and teamwork skills.
* Ability to manage tasks and meet deadlines with guidance.
* Eagerness to learn and develop within a manufacturing finance environment.
* Solid Microsoft Excel skills, including pivot tables, VLOOKUP, and basic financial modelling.
* Exposure to JD Edwards (JDE EnterpriseOne) or another ERP system is an advantage.


**Qualifications**
* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field is required.
* CPA, CA, ICWA, or ACCA qualification is preferred.
* 2–4 years of accounting experience, ideally in a manufacturing or industrial environment.
* Some exposure to cost accounting, variance analysis, or inventory is preferred.
