## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Description: Finance \& Accounts Executive

Role Purpose

Own the day\-to\-day finance function — invoicing, VAT and corporate tax compliance, procurement processing, and job/project costing — ensuring the business is audit\-ready, cash position is visible at all times, and every commission, workshop, and retail transaction is accurately recorded.

Note on scope: This role processes and records procurement (raising POs, matching invoices, tracking supplier payments). Final approval authority for purchases above a set threshold sits with the Managing Director/Owner, not this role. Adjust or remove this line if a single\-approver structure is acceptable to you.Key ResponsibilitiesInvoicing \& Receivables

* Issue client invoices for commissions, corporate workshops, live events, and retail/franchise sales
* Track receivables and follow up on overdue payments
* Reconcile invoiced amounts against signed quotations/POs before issuing

VAT

* Prepare and file quarterly VAT returns with the FTA (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec)
* Maintain VAT\-compliant tax invoices and credit notes
* Reconcile input/output VAT monthly ahead of filing deadlines
* Flag any transactions with unclear VAT treatment to the Managing Director before filing

Corporate Tax

* Maintain accounting records aligned with UAE Corporate Tax requirements
* Prepare data for annual Corporate Tax return in coordination with the appointed external tax agent/auditor
* Track Small Business Relief eligibility and revenue thresholds year\-round, not just at filing time

Procurement (processing, not approval)

* Raise purchase orders for paint, materials, and supplies
* Match supplier invoices to POs and delivery notes before payment
* Maintain supplier records and payment terms
* Route any purchase above the approval threshold to the Managing Director/Owner for sign\-off before committing funds

Costing

* Build and maintain per\-job costing (materials, artist hours, logistics) for commissions and bids
* Compare quoted vs. actual cost on completed jobs and report variances
* Support pricing decisions on new quotes with real cost data

General Ledger \& Reporting

* Maintain the general ledger and month\-end close
* Produce monthly management accounts (P\&L, cash position, receivables/payables aging)
* Maintain fixed asset register and depreciation schedules
* Support external audit and any bank/lender documentation requests

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field
* 3–5 years UAE accounting experience, including hands\-on VAT filing (FTA portal)
* Working knowledge of UAE Corporate Tax compliance requirements
* Experience with job/project costing, ideally in a services, retail, or creative/events business
* Proficiency in accounting software (QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Tally, or similar) and Excel
* Arabic language skills a plus, not required
* High attention to detail; comfortable working with a small team and multiple concurrent projects

What Success Looks Like in 6 Months

* Zero late VAT filings; VAT reconciliation done monthly, not scrambled quarterly
* Every active job has a live cost sheet with quoted vs. actual tracked
* Receivables aging under control with a clear follow\-up cadence
* Clean handover file ready for year\-end audit / Corporate Tax filing with no last\-minute data chase

Pay: AED3,500\.00 \- AED5,500\.00 per month
