## Full Job Description (verbatim)

A leading semi\-government company based in Abu Dhabi is looking for an experienced Finance Business Partner to join its Business Partnership team on a renewable 6 month contract.
 



  





 This is a broad, commercially focused finance role combining FP\&A, business partnering, financial modelling, budgeting, forecasting and financial controllership. The successful candidate will work closely with business and senior stakeholders, providing financial insight and supporting strategic and operational decision\-making.
 



  





 We are searching for a qualified accountant with strong commercial finance experience and hands\-on expertise in Oracle Fusion and Hyperion/EPM. Candidates who have moved across different areas of finance, particularly from finance consultancy, FP\&A, financial planning or commercial finance, would be well suited to the role.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 



* Lead the annual budgeting, forecasting and long\-term financial planning processes across the business.
* Provide regular financial performance reporting, variance analysis and gap analysis, identifying key drivers, risks and opportunities.
* Act as a trusted Finance Business Partner to business leaders, developing a strong understanding of their operations, objectives and financial requirements.
* Provide financial insight and consultancy to support strategic decisions, investments, business cases and commercial initiatives.
* Develop and maintain detailed financial models, forecasts and scenario analyses to support decision\-making.
* Analyse key business value drivers, including revenue, tariffs, contracts, operating costs and capital projects.
* Support commercial negotiations and contract structuring, providing financial analysis and recommendations.
* Build and review bottom\-up budgets, ensuring assumptions are robust and aligned with business plans.
* Review and approve purchase requisitions (PRs) and purchase orders (POs) from a budget and financial control perspective.
* Maintain effective budgetary controls, including the appropriate allocation of profit centres, cost centres and natural accounts.
* Support month\-end and year\-end financial close and controllership activities, ensuring accurate and timely financial information.
* Assess the financial impact of regulatory, tax and other external developments and communicate implications to relevant stakeholders.
* Support audit activities, internal controls, financial governance and compliance requirements.
* Prepare clear and compelling management presentations and financial decks for business leaders, C\-suite stakeholders, regulators, vendors and customers.
* Support internalisation initiatives and portfolio expansion activities, providing financial analysis and commercial insight.
* Identify opportunities to improve profitability, operational efficiency and long\-term financial sustainability.
* Leverage financial systems and reporting tools to improve the quality, efficiency and automation of financial planning and reporting.



  





**Requirements** 



* Professional accounting qualification such as CA, ACCA, CPA, CMA or equivalent.
* Strong experience across FP\&A, commercial finance, business partnering and financial analysis.
* Hands\-on experience with Oracle Fusion and Hyperion / Oracle EPM is essential.
* Strong understanding of financial modelling, budgeting, forecasting and scenario analysis.
* Solid understanding of financial reporting, accounting principles and financial controls.
* Experience working with complex business environments, ideally within infrastructure, utilities, energy, waste management or other capital\-intensive industries.
* Demonstrated ability to translate financial data into commercial insights and actionable recommendations.
* Experience supporting contracts, negotiations, business cases and investment decisions.
* Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to work effectively with business leaders and senior management.
* Experience preparing financial presentations and reporting packs for a variety of audiences, including C\-suite, regulators, vendors and customers.
* Strong Power BI and data visualisation skills are preferred.
* Excellent analytical, communication and presentation skills.
* Comfortable working in a broad role that combines FP\&A, business partnering, commercial finance and controllership.
* Experience in a consulting/finance advisory environment combined with in\-house FP\&A or commercial finance experience would be advantageous.



  





 Availability: Preference will be given to candidates available immediately.
