## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Senior Accountant – PSA Academy

Location: Sharjah, UAE

Job Type: Full\-Time

About Us

PSA Academy is a leading multi\-sport training organisation operating across Dubai, Sharjah, and Ajman since 2014\. We deliver gymnastics, football, swimming, basketball, and tennis programmes to thousands of students across multiple locations. As a service\-based business with recurring enrolments, seasonal programmes, and multi\-site operations, our finance function plays a critical role in the academy’s continued growth.

The Role

We are looking for a highly experienced Senior Accountant to lead and take full ownership of the academy’s end\-to\-end accounting function. This is a leadership position — you will be responsible for driving the finance process, producing management\-level reporting, and overseeing a junior accountant in day\-to\-day operations.

The ideal candidate comes from a service\-based or subscription\-based business environment and has a deep understanding of revenue recognition, deferred income, multi\-location cost allocation, and seasonal cash flow management. Proficiency in Xero is essential.

Key Responsibilities

* Lead and manage the full accounting cycle end\-to\-end across multiple entities and branches
* Supervise, mentor, and manage a junior accountant, ensuring accuracy and timely completion of tasks
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports (P\&L, balance sheet, cash flow) for management and stakeholders
* Develop and maintain financial forecasts, budgets, and variance analysis to support strategic decision\-making
* Produce detailed centre\-wise and programme\-wise revenue, cost, and profitability analysis
* Manage revenue recognition, deferred income tracking, and term\-based billing cycles typical of a service\-based academy model
* Prepare and file VAT returns in compliance with UAE FTA requirements
* Manage WPS payroll processing and ensure timely, accurate salary payments
* Lead the annual audit process, preparing all schedules, reconciliations, and documentation for external auditors
* Maintain, optimise, and take ownership of the Xero accounting system across all entities
* Handle intercompany transactions, reconciliations, and group\-level consolidation
* Liaise with banks, government authorities (MOHRE, FTA, SEDD), auditors, and external stakeholders
* Identify and implement process improvements to strengthen financial controls and reporting accuracy

Requirements

* Minimum 5\+ years of experience in a senior accounting or finance role
* Previous experience in a service\-based, subscription\-based, or academy/education business is strongly preferred
* Proven experience leading the accounting function and managing junior finance staff
* Proficiency in Xero is essential — candidates without Xero experience will not be considered
* Strong expertise in financial reporting, forecasting, budgeting, and management accounts
* Solid understanding of revenue recognition and deferred income in a service environment
* In\-depth knowledge of UAE VAT legislation and FTA filing procedures
* Experience with WPS payroll processing
* Familiarity with multi\-entity, multi\-branch accounting environments
* Advanced Excel skills with the ability to build financial models and dashboards
* Excellent attention to detail, strong organisational skills, and the ability to work independently
* Strong communication skills in English (Arabic is a plus)
* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field. Professional qualifications (ACCA, CMA, CA, or equivalent) are strongly preferred.

What We Offer

* Competitive salary based on experience
* A senior role with real ownership and influence over the finance function
* A dynamic, growing organisation at an exciting stage of development
* Supportive leadership and a collaborative team environment
