## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Purpose**
 Support the timely and accurate financial close, consolidation and reporting of multiple entities and ledgers in accordance with IFRS and Group requirements
   

  

 Assist in maintaining financial data and general ledger integrity, including reconciliations, financial statements and audit requirements
   

  

 Support the use of AI\-enabled and digital finance tools to improve reporting efficiency, accuracy and financial analysis
   

  

**Roles And Responsibilities**
**Group Consolidation \& Financial Reporting**
 Support monthly, quarterly and annual Group consolidation and financial reporting
   

  

 Assist with reporting submissions, account mapping, intercompany eliminations and Group\-level adjustments
   

  

 Prepare consolidation schedules, reconciliations and supporting documentation
   

  

 Coordinate with subsidiaries and finance teams for accurate, timely information
   

  

**Financial Close \& General Ledger**
 Support monthly, quarterly and annual close across assigned entities and ledgers
   

  

 Prepare journals, accruals, provisions, reclassifications and period\-end adjustments
   

  

 Assist in reviewing trial balances and identifying accounting or reporting discrepancies
   

  

 Support accurate account mapping and financial reporting classifications
   

  

**Reconciliations \& Financial Controls**
 Prepare and review general ledger, balance sheet and intercompany reconciliations
   

  

 Follow up on outstanding reconciliation items and support timely resolution
   

  

 Assist in maintaining accurate financial records and supporting documentation
   

  

 Support compliance with Group policies, internal controls and reporting procedures
   

  

**Audit \& Compliance**
 Support internal and external audits with schedules, reconciliations, financial statements and documentation
   

  

 Assist in responding to audit queries and coordinating required information
   

  

 Support compliance with IFRS, statutory requirements and Group financial policies
   

  

**Financial Analysis \& Management Reporting**
 Assist in preparing variance analysis, financial analysis and management reports
   

  

 Support analysis of performance against budget, forecast and prior periods
   

  

 Prepare supporting analysis and commentary on key financial movements
   

  

 Assist with budget, forecast and cash\-flow reporting requirements
   

  

**AI, Digital \& Process Improvement**
 Use approved AI\-enabled, analytics and digital tools to support reconciliations, financial reporting and analysis
   

  

 Assist in identifying opportunities to automate repetitive finance activities
   

  

 Support improvements to reporting processes, controls and ERP workflows
   

  

 Apply appropriate validation and review to AI\-generated outputs and insights
   

  

**Related Year Of Experience**
 Min of 6\+ years of experience of financial accounting and management accounting experience
   

  

**Field Of Experience**
 Experience of accounting within real estate or related industry is preferred
   

  

 Experience in complex Group\-wide accounting and reporting, preferably in a public company
   

  

**Technical And Interpersonal Skills**
 Good knowledge of financial accounting, IFRS and financial reporting principles.
   

  

 Good understanding of general ledger, reconciliations and consolidation processes.
   

  

 Proficiency in ERP systems, Excel and financial reporting tools; Oracle experience preferred.
   

  

 Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills, with accuracy and attention to detail.
   

  

 Working knowledge of AI\-enabled and digital finance tools for analysis, reporting and process efficiency
   

  

 Good communication, stakeholder coordination and time\-management skills.
   

  

**QUALIFICATION**
 Bachelor’s degree in accounting
   

  

 Professional accounting qualification e.g., CPA, CIMA, CA, ACCA is preferred
