## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**ACCOUNTANT – PLASTIC INDUSTRY \& READY\-MIX CONCRETE**

**Location:** Dubai And Abu Dhabi  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Experience:** Minimum 4 years

We are looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to join our team. The ideal candidate must have a minimum of **4 years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably with hands\-on experience in the **plastic manufacturing industry and/or ready\-mix concrete industry**.

Key Responsibilities:

* Handle day\-to\-day accounting transactions and maintain accurate financial records.
* Manage Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), General Ledger, and bank reconciliations.
* Prepare invoices, receipts, payment vouchers, journal entries, and other accounting documents.
* Monitor and reconcile inventory, raw materials, production costs, and finished goods.
* Prepare and monitor **production costing and cost analysis**.
* Coordinate with production, purchasing, warehouse, and operations teams regarding accounting and inventory matters.
* Prepare monthly financial reports, including Profit \& Loss and Balance Sheet.
* Monitor customer collections and supplier payments.
* Assist with month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Ensure proper documentation and filing of accounting records.
* Assist with **UAE VAT and Corporate Tax** compliance and related documentation.
* Coordinate with auditors and provide required financial records and supporting documents.
* Maintain accurate records in the accounting/ERP system.
* Perform other accounting and financial duties as assigned by management.

Requirements:

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* Minimum **4 years of accounting experience**.
* **Experience in the plastic manufacturing industry and ready\-mix concrete industry is highly preferred.**
* Strong knowledge of general accounting, inventory accounting, and cost accounting.
* Experience with **production costing, stock reconciliation, and material consumption** is an advantage.
* Good knowledge of **UAE VAT and Corporate Tax**.
* Proficiency in **MS Excel** and accounting/ERP software.
* Strong analytical and numerical skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good communication and organizational skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
* UAE experience is preferred.

**Important:** Candidates without the required accounting experience and relevant industry background may not be considered.

Application Question(s):

* 1\. How many years of accounting experience do you have?
* 2\. Do you have accounting experience in the plastic manufacturing industry?
* 3\. Do you have accounting experience in the ready\-mix concrete industry?
* 4\. Do you have experience with production costing and inventory accounting?
* 5\. Do you have experience handling UAE VAT and Corporate Tax\-related accounting requirements?
* 6\. Which accounting/ERP software have you used?
* 7\. How many years of UAE accounting experience do you have?
* 9\. What is your current salary and expected salary?
* 10\. What is your notice period / availability to join?

Work Location: In person
