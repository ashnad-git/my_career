## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job responsibilities:**

* Analyze business operations and logistics processes to identify gaps, inefficiencies, and improvement areas across the supply chain
* Work closely with stakeholders (Operations, Finance, IT) to gather, document, and validate business requirements for new projects or system enhancements
* Develop reports, dashboards, and KPIs to support decision\-making and performance tracking
* Drive cost\-benefit analyses and financial modelling to assess the viability of logistics solutions and strategic initiatives
* Monitor logistics performance data to uncover trends, anomalies, and issues affecting efficiency or service levels
* Support implementation of logistics systems and automation tools across the organization
* Lead and facilitate business review sessions, workshops, and process\-mapping exercises
* Collaborate with IT and data teams to develop digital solutions that streamline logistics and supply chain workflows
* Conduct competitor and market research to benchmark best practices and support strategic planning

**Educational Qualification**

* Bachelor’s degree in business administration, Industrial Engineering or Operations Management with added qualification in business and data analytics

**Work Experience**

* Familiarity with UAE logistics landscape and regional regulatory requirements is advantageous
