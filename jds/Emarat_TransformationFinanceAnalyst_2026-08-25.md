## Full Job Description (verbatim)

The ideal candidate will support the transformation initiatives by providing financial analysis, insights, and recommendations that drive strategic decision-making and enhance operational efficiency within the organization.

**Responsibilities**

Assist in the creation of detailed financial reports that summarize the financial performance of transformation initiatives.

• Ensure that forecasts are accurate and reflect the latest data and assumptions, providing insights into future financial performance.

• Coordinate with relevant departments to gather necessary financial data and ensure timely reporting.

• Review financial data for completeness and accuracy, identifying any discrepancies or errors that need to be addressed.

• Implement processes to validate financial information and ensure compliance with internal standards and external regulations.

• Conduct regular audits of financial data to maintain high standards of data integrity.

• Analyze financial results to assess the effectiveness of transformation initiatives and identify trends or areas for improvement.

• Prepare presentations and reports that summarize financial performance for senior management and stakeholders.

• Collaborate with other departments to understand the financial implications of their activities and provide recommendations for improvement.

• Evaluate existing financial processes to identify inefficiencies or areas that require enhancement.

• Recommend changes to financial procedures to improve efficiency and effectiveness in reporting and analysis.

• Track the implementation of process improvements and assess their impact on financial performance.

• Conduct cost-benefit analyses to determine the financial implications of current operational processes and practices.

• Identify opportunities for cost savings and efficiency improvements within the organization.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement changes that optimize resource allocation and enhance financial performance.

**Qualifications & Experience**

Bachelor's degree in finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related field; advanced degree or professional certification (e.g., CFA, CPA) preferred.

Minimum of 6 years' experience in financial analysis, budgeting, and forecasting, preferably within a corporate or transformation environment.
