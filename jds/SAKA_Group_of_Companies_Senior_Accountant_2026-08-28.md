We are looking for an experienced Senior Accountant to join our team at our head office in Abu Dhabi, UAE. This is a full\-time, on\-site role for a finance professional ready to take ownership of our day\-to\-day accounting operations, financial reporting, and compliance.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Maintain books of accounts, AR/AP, and bank reconciliations
* Prepare monthly financial reports and cash flow statements
* Handle UAE VAT calculations and VAT return submissions
* Process payroll through WPS and manage EOSB
* Monitor expenses, budgets, and project costs
* Ensure compliance with UAE financial and labour regulations
* Track project costs and expenses.
* Assist in cost analysis and profitability reports.
* Coordinate with project managers on budgets and variations.
* Monitor cash flow, budgets, and financial forecasts.
* Preparing and analyzing financial statements
* Conducting internal and external audits to verify the accuracy of financial records and ensure compliance with regulations.

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance

* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and WPS
* Proficient in accounting software and MS Excel
