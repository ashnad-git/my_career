## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Contract : fix term contract - 1 year

Location : Dubai

Reporting : Commercial Director

Role Purpose

The Business Analyst is the analytical backbone of the Commercial team for the GCC region. The role owns the end-to-end sell-out reporting cycle across all brands and retailers, manages commercial finance operations (POs/CNs, finance requests, pricing files), and delivers the analysis behind retailer, brand, and budget decisions. The Business Analyst is the single point of contact between the Commercial and Finance teams and a key support to the Commercial Director in strategy, budgeting, and stakeholder meetings.

Key Responsibilities

Sell-Out Reporting & Data Management (~25%)

Own the monthly sell-out cycle: collect reports from all retail partners (complete by the 10th of each month), validate data quality, and keep Qlik updated on a timely basis.
Produce Sephora weekly and monthly sell-out reports for internal and external stakeholders, including ad-hoc additions.
Own price increase and ICF file completion for customers: populate RSP and cost prices, validate margins, and align RSPs with brand teams.
Resolve invoicing and price issues raised by the Customer Service team.
Act as the point of contact between Finance and Commercial: co-develop shared templates and reports and prepare the cost-to-serve analysis.

Analysis & Planning (~25%)

Prepare stock coverage and SOH / suggested order analysis at partner level for UAE across all brands and main retailers; identify areas of improvement and recommend actions.
Collect monthly budget packs from customers and distributors and analyse sell-in and inventory.
Support the annual budget process: complementary files, MOC analysis, and alignment decks per brand for budgeting meetings.
Deliver seasonal and event analysis, including Ramadan LFL reports (FSS, Sephora); maintain and improve the SISO file supporting UAE team meetings.
Consolidate Beauty Hub and pop-up extension sales for new launches and price changes, and upon request from CS and Marketing.
Maintain the consolidated distribution list for all brands and markets.

Key Working Relationships

Commercial Director and Commercial team (primary)
Finance and Accounting (RFs, POs/CNs, cost-to-serve, joint templates)
Brand and Marketing teams (RSP alignment, launches, order forms)
Customer Service (invoicing, price issues, order forms)
External: Sephora, Al Tayer, Faces, MAF, Chalhoub and other retail partners and distributors across the GCC

Candidate Profile

Education & Experience

Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
1–2 years of experience as a business / commercial / sales analyst, ideally in beauty, FMCG, or retail distribution; GCC market exposure a strong advantage.
Experience working with retailers and/or distributors (Sephora, Chalhoub, Al Tayer, MAF or similar) is a plus.

Technical Skills

Advanced Excel (Power Query, pivot models, complex consolidations) — essential.
BI tools: Qlik (or Power BI / Tableau with willingness to master Qlik).
Strong PowerPoint storytelling: ability to turn data into clear, decision-ready decks.
ERP exposure (e.g., SAP) for PO/CN flows is an advantage.

Competencies

Rigorous accuracy with numbers and deadlines: the role carries fixed monthly deadlines toward EMEA and external partners.
Ability to manage a high volume of parallel deliverables and prioritize between fixed-deadline reporting, transactional finance work, and urgent requests.
Strong stakeholder skills: daily interaction with Directors, Finance, brand teams, and external retail partners.
Process improvement mindset: drive to automate, templatize, and simplify recurring work.
Full professional proficiency in English; Arabic or French is a plus.
Absolute discretion: the role handles confidential retailer sell-out, pricing, and margin data.
