\# JOB DESCRIPTION

\#\# Finance Manager – UAE Experience

\*\*Job Title:\*\* Finance Manager

\*\*Department:\*\* Finance \& Accounts

\*\*Location:\*\* United Arab Emirates

\*\*Employment Type:\*\* Full\-Time

\*\*Reports To:\*\* General Manager / Managing Director

\*\*Required Experience:\*\* Minimum 5 years of relevant UAE experience

\#\#\# Job Summary

We are seeking an experienced and detail\-oriented \*\*Finance Manager\*\* with a minimum of \*\*5 years of UAE experience\*\* to oversee the company's financial operations, accounting activities, reporting, budgeting, cash flow, taxation, compliance and financial controls.

The successful candidate will have strong knowledge of \*\*UAE accounting practices, VAT, Corporate Tax, IFRS, financial reporting and UAE regulatory requirements\*\*, with the ability to manage the finance function independently and provide accurate financial information to senior management.

\#\#\# Key Responsibilities

\#\#\#\# 1\. Financial Management

\* Manage the company's day\-to\-day finance and accounting operations.

\* Prepare and monitor annual budgets, forecasts and financial plans.

\* Monitor cash flow, working capital, receivables and payables.

\* Develop and maintain effective financial controls and procedures.

\* Provide management with financial analysis and recommendations for business decisions.

\* Monitor company profitability, expenses and financial performance.

\* Identify financial risks and recommend appropriate corrective actions.

\#\#\#\# 2\. Accounting \& Financial Reporting

\* Supervise general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable and bank reconciliations.

\* Ensure accurate and timely recording of all financial transactions.

\* Prepare monthly, quarterly and annual financial statements.

\* Prepare management accounts and financial reports.

\* Review balance sheets, income statements, cash\-flow statements and supporting schedules.

\* Ensure proper accounting treatment in accordance with applicable accounting standards and company policies.

\* Maintain accurate and complete accounting records.

\#\#\#\# 3\. UAE VAT \& Corporate Tax

\* Manage the company's UAE VAT compliance and reporting requirements.

\* Review VAT calculations, records and supporting documentation.

\* Coordinate VAT return preparation and submission within applicable deadlines.

\* Maintain proper VAT documentation and records.

\* Assist with VAT audits and queries from the relevant authorities.

\* Manage or coordinate \*\*UAE Corporate Tax\*\* compliance, calculations, registration and filing requirements as applicable.

\* Monitor changes to UAE tax regulations and advise management accordingly.

\* Coordinate with external tax consultants and auditors when required.

\#\#\#\# 4\. Audit \& Compliance

\* Coordinate internal and external audits.

\* Prepare audit schedules, supporting documents and financial information.

\* Liaise with auditors, banks, consultants and other financial institutions.

\* Ensure compliance with applicable UAE financial, tax and regulatory requirements.

\* Establish and maintain strong internal financial controls.

\* Investigate discrepancies, irregularities and potential financial risks.

\* Ensure company financial policies and procedures are consistently followed.

\#\#\#\# 5\. Accounts Receivable \& Payable

\* Monitor customer outstanding balances and collections.

\* Establish appropriate credit\-control procedures.

\* Review customer statements and resolve account discrepancies.

\* Monitor supplier invoices, payments and outstanding balances.

\* Ensure payments are properly authorized and supported by documentation.

\* Maintain healthy working\-capital levels.

\#\#\#\# 6\. Cash Flow \& Banking

\* Prepare regular cash\-flow forecasts.

\* Monitor daily bank balances and company liquidity.

\* Manage banking relationships and banking documentation.

\* Review bank reconciliations and investigate discrepancies.

\* Coordinate bank transfers, payments and other banking transactions.

\* Assist management with financing and banking requirements where applicable.

\#\#\#\# 7\. Payroll \& Employee\-Related Finance

\* Supervise payroll\-related accounting and payment processes.

\* Coordinate with HR regarding employee salaries, benefits, deductions and final settlements.

\* Ensure payroll accounting records are accurate.

\* Support compliance with applicable UAE salary and employment\-related payment requirements.

\#\#\#\# 8\. Budgeting \& Cost Control

\* Prepare departmental and company budgets.

\* Monitor actual performance against budget.

\* Analyze significant variances and recommend corrective measures.

\* Control operating expenses and identify cost\-saving opportunities.

\* Prepare financial models and forecasts when required.

\#\#\#\# 9\. Inventory \& Costing

Where applicable to the business:

\* Monitor inventory accounting and valuation.

\* Review stock movements and inventory reconciliations.

\* Analyze product/service costs and margins.

\* Coordinate periodic stock counts and investigate variances.

\* Ensure appropriate accounting treatment for inventory and cost of sales.

\#\#\#\# 10\. Management Support

\* Provide accurate financial information to senior management.

\* Prepare financial presentations, reports and business analysis.

\* Support strategic planning and investment decisions.

\* Evaluate the financial impact of proposed business activities.

\* Advise management on financial risks, profitability and cost management.

\#\#\# UAE Regulatory \& Professional Knowledge

The candidate should have strong practical knowledge of:

\* UAE accounting and financial reporting requirements.

\* \*\*UAE VAT regulations and procedures.\*\*

\* \*\*UAE Corporate Tax regulations and compliance.\*\*

\* IFRS / International Financial Reporting Standards.

\* UAE business and commercial practices.

\* Financial controls and audit requirements.

\* UAE banking and payment practices.

\* Payroll and employee\-related financial procedures.

\* Relevant UAE regulatory authority requirements.

\* Accounting software and ERP systems.

\#\#\# Mandatory Requirements

\* \*\*Minimum 5 years of Finance Manager / Senior Accountant experience in the UAE.\*\*

\* Strong UAE accounting experience.

\* Strong knowledge of \*\*UAE VAT and Corporate Tax\*\*.

\* Good knowledge of IFRS.

\* Experience in preparing financial statements and management reports.

\* Experience with budgeting, forecasting and cash\-flow management.

\* Experience coordinating external audits.

\* Strong knowledge of accounts payable and receivable.

\* Excellent Excel skills and experience with accounting/ERP software.

\* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.

\* Excellent English communication skills.

\* Ability to work independently and manage a finance team.

\* High level of integrity, confidentiality and attention to detail.

\#\#\# Preferred Qualifications

\* Bachelor's degree in \*\*Accounting, Finance or Commerce\*\*.

\* ACCA, CPA, CMA, CA or equivalent professional qualification is highly desirable.

\* UAE VAT and Corporate Tax training/certification is an advantage.

\* Experience with ERP/accounting systems such as SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Tally, Zoho Books or similar.

\* Experience in the same industry as the company is an advantage.

\#\#\# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

\* Timely and accurate monthly financial reporting.

\* Timely completion of VAT and Corporate Tax obligations.

\* Accurate financial statements and accounting records.

\* Effective cash\-flow and working\-capital management.

\* Reduction and control of outstanding receivables.

\* Accurate budgeting and forecasting.

\* Successful completion of external audits.

\* Effective implementation of internal financial controls.

\* Improved cost control and profitability.

\* Compliance with applicable UAE financial and tax requirements.

\#\#\# Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is a \*\*UAE\-experienced Finance Manager with at least 5 years of local experience\*\*, strong accounting and financial\-management skills, and practical knowledge of \*\*UAE VAT, Corporate Tax, IFRS, audit, budgeting, cash flow and financial reporting\*\*.

The candidate must be capable of independently managing the finance function, leading accounting staff, maintaining strong financial controls and providing reliable financial information and strategic recommendations to senior management.
