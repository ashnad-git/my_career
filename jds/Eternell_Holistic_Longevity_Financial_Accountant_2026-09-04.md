**Job Description – Accountant** 




**Industry:** 
 Healthcare / Clinic
 



**Location:** 
 Dubai, UAE
 



**Employment Type:** 
 Full\-Time, On\-Site
 



  





**Job Summary** 




**We are seeking an experienced and detail\-oriented Accountant** 
 to manage the day\-to\-day accounting, taxation, financial reporting and compliance requirements of our clinic in Dubai. The ideal candidate should have strong knowledge of
 **UAE VAT, Corporate Tax, balance\-sheet preparation, profit and loss reporting, cash\-flow management and healthcare accounting operations** 
 .
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 




**Accounting and Financial Management** 



* Maintain accurate books of accounts and complete accounting records.
* Record and reconcile daily clinic revenue, patient payments, insurance collections and other income.
* Manage accounts payable, accounts receivable and the general ledger.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly and annual financial statements.
* Prepare and review the
 **Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Statement and Cash\-Flow Statement** 
 .
* Perform bank, cash, credit\-card, payment\-gateway and POS reconciliations.
* Monitor outstanding receivables, supplier balances and payment schedules.
* Manage petty cash and verify supporting documents for all expenses.
* Assist management with budgeting, forecasting and financial planning.
* Prepare department\-wise, service\-wise and doctor\-wise profitability reports.
* Analyse revenue, costs, margins and financial performance, highlighting any discrepancies or leakages.



  





**UAE VAT and Corporate Tax** 



* Maintain VAT\-compliant accounting records and supporting documentation.
* Prepare and file UAE VAT returns accurately and within the required deadlines.
* Review input and output VAT calculations and reconcile VAT accounts.
* Maintain proper classification of taxable, zero\-rated and exempt transactions.
* Support UAE Corporate Tax registration, computation, documentation and return filing.
* Maintain records required by the Federal Tax Authority.
* Coordinate with external tax consultants and auditors when required.
* Monitor changes in UAE VAT, Corporate Tax and accounting regulations.



  





**Clinic\-Specific Responsibilities** 



* Reconcile daily patient billing with cash, card, bank transfer, insurance and online collections.
* Verify discounts, complimentary services, refunds, credit notes and package utilisation.
* Track prepaid treatment packages, memberships and unearned revenue.
* Monitor inventory consumption and the cost of medicines, supplements, consumables and treatment materials.
* Reconcile pharmacy, laboratory, dental, aesthetic and clinical service revenues, where applicable.
* Track doctor commissions and revenue\-sharing arrangements.
* Coordinate with reception, operations, insurance, procurement and HR teams to ensure complete financial documentation.
* Support insurance receivable reconciliation and monitor outstanding claims, where applicable.
* Maintain fixed\-asset registers for medical equipment, furniture, computers and other clinic assets.
* Ensure proper depreciation, asset tagging and accounting treatment.



  





**Payroll and Compliance** 



* Assist with monthly payroll preparation, staff deductions, incentives and commissions.
* Maintain records relating to employee advances, reimbursements and final settlements.
* Support WPS\-related payroll documentation in coordination with HR.
* Coordinate with auditors during statutory and internal audits.
* Ensure proper document control, filing and retention of invoices, receipts, contracts and financial records.
* Implement and maintain appropriate internal financial controls.



  





**Qualifications and Experience** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Commerce or a related field.
* Minimum
 **3–5 years of accounting experience** 
 , preferably within a clinic, medical centre, hospital or healthcare business.
* Practical experience in
 **UAE VAT and Corporate Tax preparation and filing** 
 is essential.
* Strong knowledge of balance\-sheet preparation, P\&L reporting, cash flow and financial analysis.
* Experience with accounting software such as
 **Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Odoo or a similar ERP system** 
 .
* Advanced working knowledge of Microsoft Excel.
* Familiarity with clinic\-management, billing, POS and insurance systems is preferred.
* Previous UAE experience is strongly preferred.\\



  





**Required Skills** 



* Strong analytical and numerical ability.
* High attention to detail and accuracy.
* Sound understanding of accounting principles and financial controls.
* Ability to identify revenue leakage, incorrect billing and cost overruns.
* Strong organisational and time\-management skills.
* Ability to meet strict tax, reporting and audit deadlines.
* Excellent communication and coordination skills.
* High level of integrity, confidentiality and professional ethics.
* Ability to work independently and provide clear financial reports to management.
