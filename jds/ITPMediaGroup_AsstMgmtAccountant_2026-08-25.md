## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Join a dynamic finance team within a leading media group and advance your career in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

ITP Media Group reaches millions across the region through websites, portals, social media, events, conferences, magazines and more. Portfolio includes: Time Out, Arabian Business, Harper's Bazaar Arabia, Construction Week, Grazia Middle East.

Key responsibilities:
- Prepare and assist in the production of monthly management accounts, ensuring accuracy and timely submission
- Perform variance analysis and provide commentary on financial performance vs budget/forecast
- Support the budgeting and forecasting process, working closely with budget holders
- Assist in preparation of management reports and board packs
- Support month-end and year-end close processes
- Reconcile balance sheet accounts and ensure accuracy of financial data
- Assist in preparation of cash flow forecasts
- Liaise with internal stakeholders to gather financial information

Requirements:
- Part-qualified ACCA/CIMA or equivalent
- 2–4 years finance/accounting experience
- Strong Excel skills
- Experience with accounting software (ERP)
