## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Company Description** 
 Maison Tini is a Dubai\-based kids concept store offering a curated, design\-led shopping experience for children and families. We bring together premium children’s brands across toys, books, clothing, furniture, décor and lifestyle products through our physical showroom and e\-commerce platforms. As a growing SME with retail, e\-commerce, B2B, events and consignment operations, we are looking for a hands\-on Accountant to take ownership of our day\-to\-day accounting function and financial reporting.
 



  





**Role Description** 
 The Accountant will be responsible for the day\-to\-day accounting and financial operations of Maison Tini, ensuring our accounts are accurate, up to date and closed on time.
 



 This is a hands\-on role for someone who is highly organized, proactive and comfortable taking ownership of the accounting function within a fast\-paced SME environment. The Accountant will work closely with management and provide clear, reliable visibility over the financial position of the business.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities:** 



* Manage day\-to\-day bookkeeping and accounting, ensuring transactions are accurately and promptly recorded
* Take ownership of the monthly closing process and prepare accurate P\&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable reports
* Manage accounts receivable and payable, including invoicing, supplier payments and proactive follow\-up on outstanding receivables
* Reconcile bank accounts, sales, cash and payment gateways across our retail, e\-commerce and B2B channels
* Manage inventory accounting, including stock purchases, landed costs, inventory reconciliation and investigation of discrepancies
* Manage consignment accounting, including monthly sales reporting, invoice reconciliation and payments to consignment partners
* Perform monthly balance\-sheet reconciliations and ensure discrepancies and outstanding accounting issues are resolved promptly
* Prepare payments, monitor upcoming liabilities and receivables, and support cash\-flow planning and forecasting
* Prepare VAT reconciliations and returns and support UAE Corporate Tax and other statutory filing requirements
* Maintain accurate and organized accounting records and supporting documentation
* Provide management with timely financial reporting and clear visibility over cash flow, profitability, receivables and payables
* Take ownership of accounting processes, proactively identify and resolve issues, and continuously improve internal financial controls and processes



  





**Qualifications and Experience** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field
* Minimum 3–5 years of relevant accounting experience, preferably in the UAE
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT, Corporate Tax and local accounting requirements
* Previous experience in retail, e\-commerce, consumer goods or an inventory\-based business is strongly preferred
* Strong experience with inventory accounting, reconciliations and landed\-cost allocation
* Experience managing accounts receivable, accounts payable and month\-end closing
* Proficiency in accounting software and strong Microsoft Excel skills
* Experience with Shopify and e\-commerce/payment gateway reconciliations is an advantage
* ACCA, CPA or other relevant professional qualification is an advantage



  





**We are looking for someone who is:** 



* Highly detail\-oriented and organized
* Proactive and comfortable taking full ownership of their responsibilities
* Able to identify discrepancies and follow through until they are resolved
* Comfortable working independently without requiring continuous follow\-up
* Able to prioritize and meet reporting and filing deadlines
* A strong communicator who can confidently follow up with customers, suppliers and other external partners
* Comfortable working in a hands\-on, fast\-moving SME environment
