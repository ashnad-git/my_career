## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a detail\-oriented and responsible **Account Assistant** to support our accounts and finance department with daily accounting, documentation, data entry, and administrative tasks.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Assist with daily accounting entries and bookkeeping.
* Prepare and maintain invoices, receipts, payment vouchers, and other financial documents.
* Handle accounts payable and receivable records.
* Follow up on customer payments and outstanding balances.
* Verify supplier invoices and supporting documents.
* Assist with bank reconciliation and other account reconciliations.
* Maintain accurate and organized accounting records.
* Assist with VAT\-related documentation and reports.
* Coordinate with sales, purchases, and warehouse teams regarding invoices and payments.
* Prepare basic reports and summarize as required by management.
* Perform general administrative and accounting duties assigned by the Accounts Manager.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree/Diploma in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* 1\-2 years of UAE accounting experience preferred.
* Good knowledge of MS Excel and accounting software.
* Familiarity with UAE VAT is an advantage.
* Good numerical and analytical skills.
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good communication and organizational skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.

**Preferred Candidate:**

Someone reliable, organized, detail\-oriented, and willing to learn, with a strong understanding of basic accounting procedures.
