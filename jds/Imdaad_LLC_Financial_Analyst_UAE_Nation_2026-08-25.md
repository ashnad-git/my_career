## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**1\. Job Purpose:**

Responsible for managing daily cash activities, bank transactions, cash forecasting, and liquidity monitoring to ensure sufficient funds are available for business operation.

**2\. Role Relationships:**

Internal: Business Units: Finance, Procurement, Contracts, Operations, Collections, Credit Control, General Ledger, Financial Planning, Legal \& Audit.

External: Suppliers \& Statutory Audit.

**3\. Principal Accountabilities / Key Results Area:**

· Monitor daily cash balances and bank transactions.

· Prepare cash flow forecasts and liquidity reports.

· Manage bank reconciliations and investigate discrepancies.

· Open and close bank accounts.

· Raise bank guarantees in line with the company’s policy.

· Review and close expired bank guarantee monthly

· Manage and update the bank mandates.

· Process and monitor payments and fund transfers.

· Coordinate with banks regarding transactions and account matters.

· Monitor company bank accounts and optimize cash utilization.

· Obtain best offered rates from banks and recommend Fixed Deposits to line manager.

· Prepare treasury reports for management.

· Support foreign currency transactions and exchange rate monitoring.

· Maintain accurate treasury records and documentation.

· Assist with month\-end and year\-end closing activities.

· Ensure compliance with internal controls and treasury policies.

· Submit all required documents requested for internal/external auditors.

· Providing ad\-hoc reports as required.

· Undertakes any other duties requested by Line Manager to support department objectives.

**4\. Knowledge, Skills, Experience and Competencies:**

Qualification: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance is a must. Or equivalent professional accounting certificate.

Experience: Minimum 3 \- 5 years of relevant accounting experience, preferably in Treasury.

**5\. Job Specific Skills:**

· Accounting, Excel, Oracle Fusion and Power BI.

· Understanding of IFRS.

· Good understanding of VAT and basic tax requirements and provisioning.

· Good knowledge of cash management and bank reconciliation.

· Process Orientation, Problem\-Solving skills, Interpersonal skills \& Attention to details.

Work Location: In person
