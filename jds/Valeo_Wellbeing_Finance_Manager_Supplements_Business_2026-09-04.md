## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Role Overview**  
The Finance Manager will serve as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for the Supplements division, managing the end\-to\-end financial operations of the business. This role is critical for maintaining liquidity through bank liaison and optimizing the working capital cycle of the supplements' "in and out" supply chain.

**Key Responsibilities**  
**1\. Working Capital \& Treasury Management**  
● Liaison with Banks: Act as the primary contact for banks and short\-term lenders to secure and manage working capital facilities.  
● Cash Flow Optimization: Monitor "in and out" business flows to ensure sufficient liquidity for procurement while maintaining healthy cash reserves.  
● Facility Management: Manage short\-term lending instruments and ensure compliance with lender requirements.

**2\. Inventory \& Supply Chain Finance**  
● Valuation \& Compliance: Oversee the valuation of raw materials and finished supplement products in compliance with IFRS/IAS standards.  
● Inventory Control: Identify and forecast "at\-risk" inventory (short\-dated or expired supplements) to enable proactive liquidation and protect gross margins.  
● Loss Prevention: Conduct deep\-dive analysis into returns and logistical losses (e.g., UPS/transportation) to recommend SOP improvements.

**3\. FP\&A and Business Partnering**  
● Budgeting \& Forecasting: Develop and manage annual Opex and Capex budgets for the supplements division.  
● Variance Analysis: Deliver monthly "Budget vs. Actual" reports, shifting focus from general ledger entries to vendor\-level insights to improve accuracy.  
● Performance Dashboards: Partner with IT and Dashboard teams to automate reporting and provide real\-time visibility into business metrics.

**4\. Financial Governance \& Reporting**  
● Audit Management: Lead statutory and internal audits, resolving queries related to inventory and financial controls.  
● Regulatory Compliance: Ensure adherence to UAE/Regional VAT regulations and IFRS reporting standards.  
● Process Improvement: Streamline month\-end closing processes to accelerate decision\-making timelines.

**Candidate Profile**  
● Education: Chartered Accountant (ICAI) or equivalent professional qualification.  
● Experience: Minimum 5\+ years of experience in Business Finance or FP\&A  
● Technical Skills: Proficient in Zoho ERP, Advanced Excel.  
● Soft Skills: Strong negotiation skills for banking relationships and the ability to translate "numbers to strategies".

Work Location: In person
