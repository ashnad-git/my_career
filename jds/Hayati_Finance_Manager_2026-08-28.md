## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Finance Manager – UAE Accounting, Tax \& Treasury** 




**Location:** 
 Dubai, United Arab Emirates
 



  





**Job Purpose** 




 The Country Finance Manager will take end\-to\-end responsibility for the finance and accounting activities of the company’s UAE operations.
 



 This is a hands\-on role covering financial accounting and reporting, UAE tax and statutory compliance, treasury and cash management, banking, financial controls, budgeting and audit coordination. The role will act as the principal finance contact for the UAE entity and work closely with Group Finance, local management and external professional advisers.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 



* Manage day\-to\-day accounting activities and monthly, quarterly and year\-end closing, including general ledger, accounts payable and receivable, expenses, bank reconciliations, accruals, provisions and fixed assets.
* Prepare accurate financial statements, management reports, budgets, forecasts, cash\-flow projections and variance analyses in accordance with IFRS, applicable UAE requirements and Group accounting policies.
* Manage UAE Corporate Tax and VAT compliance and, where applicable, Excise Tax, including registration, calculations, filings, payments, reconciliations, record keeping and correspondence with the Federal Tax Authority.
* Coordinate customs duties, import VAT, transfer\-pricing and related\-party compliance where relevant to the company’s operations.
* Oversee inventory accounting, product costing, landed\-cost calculations, stock reconciliations and inventory provisions.
* Manage treasury and banking activities, including liquidity planning, payment scheduling, working\-capital monitoring, foreign\-exchange exposure, bank\-account administration and KYC documentation.
* Coordinate intercompany settlements, cross\-border fund transfers and funding arrangements with Group Finance, ensuring appropriate approvals and supporting documentation.
* Establish and maintain appropriate financial controls, approval procedures, payment controls and segregation\-of\-duties arrangements to safeguard company funds and assets.
* Coordinate payroll accounting and salary\-payment processes, including applicable WPS requirements, in cooperation with HR and external payroll providers.
* Liaise with external auditors, tax advisers, banks and other professional service providers to complete statutory audits, tax filings and other finance\-related regulatory requirements.
* Monitor relevant UAE regulatory developments and support the implementation of accounting systems, finance processes and applicable e\-invoicing requirements.
* Undertake other finance, tax, treasury and business\-support assignments as required.



  





**Qualifications and Experience** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related discipline.
* A recognised professional accounting qualification, such as ACCA, ACA, CPA, CMA or CA, is strongly preferred.
* At least seven years of progressive finance and accounting experience, including substantial hands\-on experience in the UAE and experience at Finance Manager, Financial Controller or an equivalent level.
* Proven ability to independently manage the end\-to\-end finance function of a UAE\-based company, subsidiary or business unit.
* Strong practical knowledge of UAE Corporate Tax, VAT, Federal Tax Authority procedures and the EmaraTax platform.
* Knowledge of Excise Tax, customs duties, import VAT and designated\-zone requirements is required or strongly preferred where relevant to the company’s products and operating model.
* Strong knowledge of IFRS, financial reporting, closing processes, audit coordination, budgeting and internal financial controls.
* Hands\-on experience in cash\-flow forecasting, banking operations, intercompany settlements and cross\-border funding arrangements.
* Experience in inventory accounting, product costing and working\-capital management within a product\-based, distribution or trading business.
* Previous audit, tax or advisory experience with a recognised accounting or professional services firm would be an advantage.
* Proficiency in ERP or accounting systems and advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
* Able to work independently in a hands\-on environment, manage multiple priorities and communicate effectively with local stakeholders and overseas headquarters.
* Full professional proficiency in English is required. Chinese or Arabic language capability would be an advantage but not mandatory.
