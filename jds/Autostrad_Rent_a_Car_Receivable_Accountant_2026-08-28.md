## Full Job Description (verbatim)

1\-Check the customer’s account on a regular basis and ensure that it is maintained upto date matched with invoices and tallied with customer’s books.

2\-Check the detailed ageing report of customers to ensure that all the transactions are properly matched and made up to date.

3\-Intimate the concerned accountant in charge regarding rectification of errors or omission noticed in customer account.

4\-Verify monthly invoices and supporting documents prepared by billing team to ensure correctness and completeness in all respects before sending to the customer.

5\-Attached copy of the statement of account made up to date with the invoice and forward back to the billing team for sending to customers.

6\-Call the Accounts Department of the customer to confirm the receipt of the statement of account and if not send fresh copy and obtain acknowledgment.

7\-Maintain a copy of the statement send to the customer with the proof of delivery.

8\-Follow up with the customer for releasing the payment before the due date and arrange to collect the cheque once it is ready.

9\-Intimate the sales executive regarding those customers who have not release the payment within the due date by e\-mail with copy to Senior Accountant Receivables

10\-Remind the Sales Executive regarding the defaulting customers and provide summary ageing report of all his customers on a weekly basis.

11\-Report cases of customers with overdue bills over 30 days to Senior Accountant on a weekly basis.

12\-Ensure to comply with all the steps as per the collection procedure for defaulting customers.

13\-Support the sales executives with the statement of account of customers and supporting documents whenever needed.

14\-Co\-ordinate with customer for the payment details wherever not received.

15\-Maintain a separate correspondence file for each customer containing prescribed documents and correspondence as per check list given.

Job Type: Full\-time

Pay: From AED5,000\.00 per month
