**Meaningful Work** **From** **Day One**


The Supply Planning Analyst will oversee forecasting, inventory planning, reporting, and operations for Brown\-Forman’s third\-party warehouses. This role leads Integrated Business Planning (IBP) activities and partners with Global Supply Chain, Production Operations, Marketing/Sales, Finance, and Customer Service teams to support effective regional supply chain management.


**What You Can Expect**


**’** **1\. Demand Planning \& Strategic Forecasting**


* Forecast Management:Facilitatethe monthly consensus forecast by collaborating with Commercial teams and customers;maintaincloud\-based forecasting tools and ensureaccuratedata integration intoKinaxis.
* Challenge \&Validate: Critically evaluate forecast updates against financial forecast, category trends, and historical performance to ensure realistic projections.
* New Product Introduction (NPI): Lead the planning for new product launches, ensuring timeline adherence and cross\-functional stakeholder alignment.


**2\. Integrated Business Planning (IBP) \& Performance**


* Meeting Facilitation: Lead monthly IBP alignment meetings and prepare comprehensive analyses of demand shifts, risks, opportunities, and gap\-to\-goal performance.
* KPI Management: Track and report on key metrics including Forecast Accuracy (FA), Forecast Bias (FB), and Slow Moving \& Obsolete Goods (SMOG).
* Root Cause Analysis: Perform deep\-dive analytics on variances between actuals and forecasts to drive continuous improvement in planning cycles.


**3\. Supply, Inventory \& Operations Planning**


* Inventory Optimization: Partner with Global Supply Planning tomaintaintarget stock levels at regional warehouses, proactivelyidentifyingOut\-of\-Stock (OOS) risks or excess inventory.
* Allocation Management: Oversee the allocation of high\-demand, limited\-edition, and Value\-Added Packs (VAPs); coordinate quantities with Brand and Supply teams.
* Production \& Rework: Manage local rework scheduling and packaging material planning; execute technical transactions in SAP (MIGO) for inbound shipments and local production.
* Monitor and receive all inbound shipments in SAP for the regional warehouse.
