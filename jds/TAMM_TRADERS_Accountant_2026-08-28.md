## Full Job Description (verbatim)

ACCOUNTANT – RV VEHICLE / WORKSHOP INDUSTRY

**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Experience Required:** **1–2 Years UAE Experience**

Job Summary

We are looking for an **Accountant with 1–2 years of UAE experience**, preferably with a background in the **RV, caravan, automobile, or workshop industry**. The candidate will be responsible for daily accounting activities, invoicing, expenses, payments, inventory records, and maintaining accurate financial records.

Key Responsibilities

* Handle daily accounting transactions and bookkeeping.
* Prepare customer invoices, receipts, quotations, and credit notes.
* Record workshop service charges, spare parts sales, labor costs, and expenses.
* Maintain **Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable** records.
* Follow up on customer outstanding payments.
* Process supplier invoices and payment transactions.
* Reconcile bank, cash, customer, and supplier accounts.
* Maintain petty cash records and expense documentation.
* Verify purchase invoices, delivery notes, job cards, and supporting documents.
* Coordinate with the workshop team regarding job costs, spare parts, and service revenue.
* Maintain proper records of spare parts and workshop inventory.
* Prepare regular accounting and financial reports for management.
* Assist with VAT\-related records and documentation.
* Ensure all financial transactions are properly recorded and supported by documents.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* **1–2 years of UAE accounting experience is required.**
* Previous experience in an **RV, caravan, automobile workshop, vehicle service center, or spare parts business** is highly preferred.

Pay: AED1,500\.00 \- AED2,500\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
