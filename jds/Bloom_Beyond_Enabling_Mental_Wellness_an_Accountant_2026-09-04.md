We are seeking a detail\-oriented Accountant to manage our day\-to\-day accounting operations and take ownership of the accounts through to finalization.

* Handle day\-to\-day accounting operations and maintain accurate accounting records

* Manage and reconcile petty cash

* Good written and spoken English
