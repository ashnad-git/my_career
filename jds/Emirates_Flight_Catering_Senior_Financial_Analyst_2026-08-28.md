## Full Job Description (verbatim)

The job holder will play a key role in research, analyzing financial information, and monitoring financial movements to support the Sr Manager Financial Analysis with analytics and recommendations to enable decision making.
 



  





**Key Result Areas:** 




  




* Analyse financial results, monitor P\&L variances and identify trends to provide actionable insights that drive informed decision\-making
* Support the Sr. Manager \- Financial Analysis with preparation of reports that evaluate financial risks and ROI to aid the management in business decisions
* Prepare business and feasibility studies for new projects, acquisition and joint ventures to minimize risks and maximize returns
* Track implementation of process improvement and policy development initiatives that impact the business to ensure effectiveness, sustainability and alignment with the organizational goals
* Develop financial models, conduct benchmarking and process analysis to ensure financial strategies align with organizational goals
* Consolidate and analyze financial data (budgets, income statement forecasts etc) proactively toto review during monthly discussions with budget holders.
* Prepare weekly reports highlighting trends in catering industry and associated industries such as hospitality, aviation to support management decisions in staying relevant and competitive in a dynamic market
* Analyse and define impact of decisions and make recommendations relating to operational decisions such as buy/hire, outsource/inhouse to assess financial impact.
* Prepare presentation and progress reports, on business performance and status of capex projects to facilitate timely completion.



  





**Education Qualification:** 



* Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting or Business Admin
* Professional qualification in Business MBA or in Finance, ACA, CPA, CFA or working towards such qualifications is preferred.



  





**Work Experience:** 



* Minimum 5 years of experience in relevant field
* Big four Audit and forensic accounting experience is preferred



  





**Skills:** 



* Proficient in using ERP, Excel for analysis
* Well informed in current financial subjects, tax laws, money market and business environment
* Proven knowledge of financial forecasting and diagnosis, corporate finance and information analysis
