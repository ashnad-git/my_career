## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Position:** Senior Accountant  
**Department:** Finance \& Accounts  
**Location:** UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced and responsible **Senior Accountant** to manage the company’s accounting operations, financial records, VAT compliance, and reporting. The candidate should have strong knowledge of accounting principles and practical experience with **Tally Prime and UAE VAT**.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage daily accounting transactions, including sales, purchases, receipts, payments, and journal entries.
* Maintain accurate accounting records in **Tally Prime**.
* Prepare monthly and annual financial reports.
* Handle accounts payable and accounts receivable.
* Reconcile bank, cash, supplier, and customer accounts.
* Manage petty cash and branch\-wise accounting transactions.
* Prepare and review VAT calculations and VAT reports.
* Assist with **UAE VAT return preparation and FTA compliance**.
* Verify purchase invoices, sales invoices, credit notes, and debit notes.
* Monitor expenses and maintain proper supporting documents.
* Handle inter\-branch transactions and reconciliations.
* Assist with payroll and salary\-related accounting entries.
* Prepare profit \& loss statements and other management reports.
* Coordinate with suppliers, customers, banks, auditors, and management when required.
* Ensure accounting records are accurate and maintained on time.
* Perform other accounting duties assigned by management.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum **3–5 years of accounting experience**, preferably in the UAE.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE VAT and accounting practices**.
* Good working knowledge of **Tally Prime and Microsoft Excel**.
* Experience in bank reconciliation, supplier reconciliation, and financial reporting.
* Good communication and organizational skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
* UAE experience is preferred.

Salary \& Benefits

* **Competitive salary**, based on experience and qualifications.
* **Employment visa provided by the company.**
* **Accommodation provided by the company.**
* **Medical insurance/medical benefits provided by the company.**
* Annual leave and other benefits as per UAE labour law and company policy.

Working Conditions

The Senior Accountant will work closely with management and other departments to ensure smooth financial and accounting operations across the company and its branches.
