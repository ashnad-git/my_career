## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced and results\-oriented **Senior Accountant** to oversee day\-to\-day accounting operations, financial reporting, cost analysis, and month\-end/year\-end financial closing. The ideal candidate should have strong leadership skills, excellent accounting knowledge, and preferably experience in the **printing press industry**.

Key Responsibilities Financial Accounting

* Manage daily accounting operations and ensure accurate financial records.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.
* Ensure timely month\-end and year\-end closing of financial books.
* Perform bank reconciliations and monitor cash flow.
* Maintain the general ledger and ensure all accounting entries are accurate.

Cost Analysis \& Financial Control

* Conduct detailed cost analysis for products and operations.
* Monitor production costs and identify opportunities for cost savings.
* Prepare profitability reports and financial analysis for management.
* Assist management in budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning.

Accounts Management

* Supervise Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), and General Ledger functions.
* Ensure timely supplier payments and customer collections.
* Verify accounting transactions and ensure compliance with company policies.

Team Leadership

* Lead, supervise, and mentor a team of **two accounting staff**.
* Allocate daily tasks, monitor performance, and provide guidance.
* Ensure smooth coordination within the accounts department.

Compliance \& Audit

* Ensure compliance with IFRS, UAE VAT regulations, and company policies.
* Coordinate with external auditors during annual audits.
* Prepare schedules and supporting documents for audit and tax requirements.

Requirements

* Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, or Commerce.
* Professional qualification (ACCA, CMA, CPA, CA – completed or pursuing) is an advantage.
* Minimum **2\-5 years of UAE accounting experience**, with at least **2 years in a Senior Accountant role is an advantage**.
* **Printing press industry experience is highly preferred.**
* Strong experience in **cost analysis, inventory accounting, and manufacturing/production costing**.
* Proven experience in closing financial books within deadlines.
* Experience leading a small accounting team.
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and financial reporting.
* Proficient in Microsoft Excel and accounting/ERP software.
* Excellent analytical, leadership, and communication skills.
