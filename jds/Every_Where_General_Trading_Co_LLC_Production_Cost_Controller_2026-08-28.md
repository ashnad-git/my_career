## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Position:** Production Cost Analyst – Manufacturing / Production (SAP S/4HANA – PP Mandatory)  
**Location:** Business Bay, Dubai

**Job Summary**

We are seeking a **highly analytical and detail\-driven Costing Analyst** to monitor and control costs across **multiple manufacturing and production units**. This role requires strong hands\-on experience in **SAP S/4HANA**, deep understanding of **production costing**, and the ability to **analyze variances, identify inefficiencies, and drive cost optimization**.

The ideal candidate must be capable of **tracking actual vs standard consumption, validating production data, and highlighting operational gaps** with clear, actionable reporting.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Monitor and analyze **end\-to\-end production costing** across multiple manufacturing units
* Track and validate **Bill of Materials (BOM) vs actual raw material consumption**, identifying variances and inefficiencies
* Analyze **raw material cost, activity cost, and operational/production costs**
* Monitor **expected production vs actual output**, identifying shortfalls and performance gaps
* Review **daily production data**, verify accuracy, and highlight issues such as:
* Process inefficiencies and wastage
* Work closely with production teams to **collect, verify, and validate production data**
* Identify **loopholes and cost leakages** in production processes and recommend corrective actions
* Drive **cost reduction initiatives** by controlling material usage and improving operational efficiency
* Maintain and update costing data and structures in **SAP S/4HANA**
* Prepare detailed **MIS reports, variance analysis, and cost performance reports** for management
* Support budgeting, forecasting, and standard costing processes
* Ensure accuracy and integrity of cost data through **strict verification and validation checks**
* Coordinate with finance, production, and supply chain teams for data alignment and reporting

**Mandatory Requirements**

* 4–8\+ years of experience in **costing / production cost analysis**
* **Hands\-on SAP S/4HANA experience (compulsory)** — especially costing, production, and inventory modules
* Strong understanding of **manufacturing processes and production costing**
* Proven experience in **BOM analysis vs actual consumption tracking**
* Strong expertise in **analysis and cost control**
* **Advanced Excel skills (mandatory)** — data analysis, reporting, and reconciliation
* Experience handling **large volumes of production data with high accuracy**

**Preferred Background**

* Experience in **food manufacturing / food processing industry** is highly preferred
* Experience managing costing across **multiple production units or plants**
* Familiarity with **standard costing, activity\-based costing, and production KPIs**

**Skills \& Competencies**

* Strong analytical and problem\-solving mindset
* High attention to detail with a **verification\-driven approach**
* Ability to identify inefficiencies and **drive cost optimization**
* Strong coordination skills with production and operations teams
* Excellent reporting and presentation skills
* Ability to work in a **fast\-paced, data\-intensive environment**

**Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Costing, or related field
* Professional certifications (CMA / ACCA – partly or fully qualified) are an advantage
