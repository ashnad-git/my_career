## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are seeking a skilled and detail\-oriented Accountant to join our team. As an Accountant, you will play a critical role in ensuring the accuracy and efficiency of financial processes within our organization. You will be responsible for preparing, analyzing, and maintaining financial records, reports, and transactions. This position requires a strong understanding of accounting principles, attention to detail, and excellent analytical and problem\-solving skills.

Responsibilities:

Financial Reporting: Prepare and analyze financial statements, including balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements. Present financial data to management and provide insights on financial performance.

General Ledger Maintenance: Ensure accurate and timely recording of financial transactions in the general ledger. Reconcile accounts and resolve discrepancies.

Accounts Payable and Receivable: Oversee accounts payable and receivable processes. Process invoices, issue payments, and follow up on outstanding payments.

Budgeting and Forecasting: Collaborate with management to create budgets and financial forecasts. Monitor budget variances and provide recommendations for improvement.

Tax Compliance: Prepare and file tax returns in compliance with local, state, and federal regulations. Stay updated on tax laws and changes to ensure accurate tax reporting.

Auditing: Assist with internal and external audits, providing necessary documentation and explanations.

Financial Analysis: Conduct financial analyses to identify trends, opportunities, and potential risks. Provide recommendations for cost\-saving measures and financial efficiency.

Compliance: Ensure adherence to accounting principles, company policies, and relevant financial regulations.

Financial Software: Utilize accounting software to maintain financial records and generate reports. Recommend and implement improvements to streamline processes.

Month\-end and Year\-end Closing: Participate in month\-end and year\-end closing processes, including preparing adjusting entries and financial statements.

Qualifications:

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Proven work experience as an Accountant or in a relevant financial role.
* Solid understanding of accounting principles, standards, and regulations.
* Proficiency in financial software and MSOffice Suite, particularly Excel.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
