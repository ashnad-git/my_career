## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Overview**

Preparing routine journal entries, reconciliation of daily store sales and cash reconciliation, bank reconciliation, monthly closing activities and balance sheet schedules. Assistance to the supervisor in carrying out responsibilities of the Accounts department.

**Key Accountabilities**

**Roles and Responsibilities**

\- To ensure that cash collection for daily sales at stores is deposited in the bank.

\- To ensure that daily cash and card transactions are recorded in the system.

\- Reconciliation of daily store sales and cash collection and timely resolution of discrepancy.

\- Reconciliation of credit card payments and timely resolution of discrepancy.

\- Reconciliation of customer ledgers and timely resolution of discrepancy

\- Management of petty cash of all the locations which includes its timely verification, resolution of any discrepancy, recording relevant expenses and reimbursement.

\- Reconciliation of trade in partners ledger accounts and resolution of any discrepancy.

\- Preparing nontrade purchase orders in ERP.

\- Preparing vendor payment.

\- Ensure timely preparation and payment of monthly utility invoices.

\- Assist supervisor in monthly closing activities e.g. posting of accruals etc.

\- Assist supervisor during financial audit activity e.g. preparation of relevant balance sheet schedule etc.

\- VAT Compliance and filing.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

\- Daily cash and card transactions are recorded in the system without fail.

\- Timely notification of any discrepancy found during reconciliations with the relevant person to resolve the matter.

\- Petty cash reimbursement should be made within 5 working days of receipt.

\- Timely clearing of open transaction in SAP.

**Key Performance Areas (KPAs)**

\- Financial reporting

\- VAT management

\- Vendor relation

\- Audit support

\- Analysis

**Qualifications/Requirements**

Minimum 4 years’ experience as an Accountant preferably in retail industry.

Proven experience as an Accountant, preferably in consumer retail electronics.

**Formal Qualifications and Experience**

Bachelor’s degree in accounting or equivalent.

**Knowledge/Skills/Abilities/Attributes**

\- Sound accounting knowledge.

\- Well versed with MS Excel.

\- Good working knowledge of UAE VAT laws

\- Good interpersonal skills to build relationships with people to get the job done.

Application Question(s):

* Do you have experience in a Retail Industry?
* How many years of experience do you have in accounting, including VAT preparation and filing?
* Are you willing to work on Saturdays as required by the position?
