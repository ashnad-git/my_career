## Full Job Description (verbatim)

***Key Responsibilities:***

**Accounting and Compliance**

* Maintain accurate accounting records and supporting documents.
* Post daily journal entries accurately and on time.
* Maintain the general ledger and prepare account reconciliations.
* Prepare financial statements and management reports.
* Prepare and submit UAE VAT returns.
* Support compliance with UAE Corporate Tax requirements.
* Assist with UAE e\-invoicing requirements and implementation.
* Process payroll in compliance with the Wages Protection System (WPS).

**Accounts Payable (AP)**

* Record and process supplier invoices, credit notes and payment entries.
* Verify invoices against purchase orders, contracts, delivery notes and approvals.
* Prepare payment requests with complete supporting documentation.
* Prepare and issue cheques after obtaining the required approvals.
* Maintain a proper cheque register and monitor issued, cleared and outstanding cheques.
* Maintain payment schedules based on due dates and available cash flow.
* Reconcile supplier statements and resolve discrepancies promptly.
* Monitor outstanding payables and ensure suppliers are paid on time.
* Maintain accurate AP ageing reports.
* Communicate with suppliers regarding invoices, payments and account balances.

**Accounts Receivable (AR)**

* Prepare and issue customer invoices, statements and receipts.
* Record customer payments and allocate them to the correct invoices.
* Monitor outstanding receivables and maintain accurate AR ageing reports.
* Follow up with customers regarding overdue balances and collections.
* Reconcile customer accounts and resolve payment discrepancies.
* Track post\-dated cheques and other receivable instruments.
* Provide regular collection and overdue\-payment reports to management.

**Banking and Finance Operations**

* Perform daily bank reconciliations for all company bank accounts.
* Prepare and submit a daily banking report to management.
* Monitor daily bank balances, transactions and upcoming obligations.
* Prepare, update and monitor cash\-flow forecasts.
* Prepare annual budgets and periodic budget revisions.
* Monitor actual results against approved budgets and report variances.
* Maintain accurate records of petty cash and operational expenses.
* Coordinate with management, banks, suppliers, customers and external auditors.
* Maintain an organised financial filing and document\-control system.
* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties as required.

**Candidate Requirements**

* **Candidate should be from the Philippines or Myanmar.**
* Relevant degree or professional qualification in accounting or finance.
* Experience using **Tally accounting software**, or willingness and ability to learn it.
* Practical experience managing **accounts payable and accounts receivable**.
* Experience preparing cheques, bank reconciliations and daily banking reports.
* Good knowledge of **UAE VAT regulations and filing**.
* Knowledge of **UAE Corporate Tax**.
* Familiarity with **UAE e\-invoicing requirements**.
* Experience with the **Wages Protection System (WPS)**.
* Experience in cash\-flow management, budgeting and payment processing.
* Strong general accounting and financial\-reporting skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
* Ability to join Immediately.

**Candidates from the Philippines and Myanmar are encouraged to apply.**
