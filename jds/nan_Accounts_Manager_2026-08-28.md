## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Responsible for managing daily accounting operations, maintaining accurate financial records, preparing reports, and supporting the company’s financial activities.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and finance operations.
* Maintain books of accounts, ledgers, and financial records.
* Handle accounts payable, accounts receivable, invoicing, and collections.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and statements.
* Perform bank and account reconciliations.
* Monitor expenses, payments, and cash flow.
* Assist with budgeting and financial planning.
* Handle VAT\-related accounting and documentation.
* Coordinate with auditors, banks, suppliers, and clients.
* Ensure compliance with company policies and applicable UAE accounting regulations.
* Supervise and support accounting staff when required.
* Maintain confidentiality and accuracy of financial information.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **Minimum 2–3 years of relevant accounting experience.**
* UAE experience is preferred.
* Good knowledge of accounting principles and financial procedures.
* Knowledge of **UAE VAT** is an advantage.
* Proficiency in MS Excel and accounting software.
* Good analytical and communication skills.
* Strong attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines.
