## Full Job Description (verbatim)

MAVI partners with high\-growth US businesses, embedding experienced accounting professionals directly into their finance operations. In the UAE, we build long\-term partnerships with professionals who want genuine general ledger ownership and direct exposure to US finance environments.
 



  





 We are hiring a Lead Accountant with strong US GAAP experience to take senior ownership of accounting operations for US\-based clients operating in lean, fast\-moving SME environments.
 



  





 You will be the most senior accountant on the client's books — the person US leadership comes to when an accounting question needs a definitive answer. This role is ideal for professionals who want stable US\-facing work, real authority over the general ledger, and direct collaboration with US Controllers and business leaders.
 



  





**What You Will Own** 




  




* Own full\-cycle accounting operations under US GAAP, from first accrual to final statement
* Own the month\-end, quarter\-end, and year\-end close, with accountability for the calendar and cycle time
* Own the general ledger and the balance sheet, driving reconciling items to resolution rather than reporting them
* Prepare and finalize financial statements, reporting packages, and supporting schedules, and stand behind them
* Own technical accounting treatment — revenue recognition, deferred revenue, accruals, and capitalisation — and document the judgements behind each
* Hold final review over journal entries, reconciliations, and schedules prepared by other accountants, and send nothing to the client you have not satisfied yourself on
* Review, mentor, and develop the accountants working alongside you, and set the standard their work is held to
* Maintain and enforce accounting policies, internal controls, and close procedures, and flag where the current design no longer fits
* Own audit readiness, prepare PBC deliverables, and respond directly to auditor queries through to resolution
* Support US tax advisers with the accounting inputs they depend on
* Partner directly with US Controllers, finance teams, and business stakeholders as the senior accounting voice in the relationship
* Identify accounting issues early and drive them through to resolution
* Build the close checklists and SOPs that keep the close reproducible, and improve the processes that slow it down



  





**What We're Looking For** 




  




* 6\+ years of accounting experience with deep US GAAP expertise
* Demonstrated ownership of a full close cycle and the financial statements on an independent basis, rather than contribution to someone else's
* Experience reviewing, supervising, or mentoring other accountants
* Strong technical judgment, with the ability to reach a defensible conclusion and document it
* Experience supporting or managing an audit, including direct interaction with external auditors
* Experience working with US clients or international stakeholders
* ERP/accounting systems experience (QuickBooks, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Xero, or similar)
* Advanced Excel or Google Sheets, including comfort building schedules others can follow
* Strong written and verbal English communication skills, with the confidence to explain an accounting conclusion to a non\-accountant
* High attention to detail with a problem\-solving mindset
* Ability to work effectively in remote, fast\-paced environments



  





**Preferred** 




  




* CPA, CMA, ACCA, or an equivalent professional qualification
* Big 4, mid\-tier audit, or outsourced accounting practice experience followed by industry experience
* Experience with multi\-entity accounting, consolidation, and intercompany elimination
* Practical experience applying ASC 606, including deferred revenue and recurring\-revenue mechanics
* Experience at a venture\-backed, PE\-backed, or high\-growth US company
* Experience through an ERP migration or close automation project



  





**Why Join MAVI?** 




  




* Own the books outright for US\-based clients, with real authority over the general ledger
* Be the definitive accounting voice in the relationship, not one contributor among several
* Long\-term partnerships with US\-based clients
* Stable remote work environment with international exposure
* Direct collaboration with US finance leaders and businesses
* Opportunity to work in high\-growth SME environments
* Career growth into Accounting Manager, Controller, and Head of Accounting roles
