## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Manco Engineering and Technical Industries FZC** is a trusted and leading contracting company based in Hamriyah Free Zone, UAE. We specialize in electro\-mechanical, civil engineering, steel fabrication, tank construction, piping, and industrial construction projects, delivering high\-quality turnkey solutions for oil storage terminals, pipelines, refineries, warehouses, and infrastructure developments across the UAE.

Job Title: AccountantJob Summary

We are seeking a qualified and experienced **Accountant** to join our finance and administration team. The ideal candidate should have strong experience in accounting within the **UAE contracting, construction, engineering, or oil \& gas industry**. The candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, managing accounts payable and receivable, preparing financial reports, monitoring project\-related expenses, handling VAT compliance, and supporting the company's overall financial operations.

Key Responsibilities

* Maintain accurate and up\-to\-date accounting records and financial transactions.
* Manage **accounts payable (AP) and accounts receivable (AR)**.
* Verify and process supplier invoices, LPOs, POs, delivery notes, and supporting documents.
* Prepare payment vouchers and coordinate supplier payments.
* Monitor customer invoices, outstanding receivables, and payment collections.
* Prepare and maintain project\-wise and company\-wise expense records.
* Monitor project costs, budgets, and expenditures in coordination with the Project and Procurement Departments.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.
* Maintain proper records of cash, bank transactions, petty cash, and company expenses.
* Perform bank, supplier, customer, and general ledger reconciliations.
* Prepare and maintain **VAT records, VAT returns, and supporting documentation** in compliance with UAE Federal Tax Authority requirements.
* Verify VAT calculations on purchase and sales invoices.
* Maintain proper documentation for input and output VAT.
* Assist with corporate tax\-related accounting records and supporting documentation.
* Prepare payroll\-related accounting entries and coordinate with HR for salary processing.
* Monitor employee advances, reimbursements, and expense claims.
* Maintain fixed asset and depreciation records.
* Assist in preparing budgets, cash\-flow forecasts, and financial analysis.
* Coordinate with auditors and provide required financial documents and schedules.
* Ensure proper filing and maintenance of financial records and supporting documents.
* Review and verify invoices against approved quotations, LPOs, POs, and delivery documents.
* Monitor subcontractor and supplier accounts and prepare reconciliation statements.
* Support management with financial analysis, cost control, and reporting.
* Ensure compliance with company financial procedures and internal controls.
* Perform any other accounting and finance\-related duties assigned by management.

Qualifications \& Experience

* Bachelor's Degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related discipline**.
* Minimum **3–5 years of UAE/GCC experience** as an Accountant, preferably within contracting, construction, engineering, or oil \& gas companies.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE VAT and accounting requirements**.
* Experience with project accounting and cost monitoring is highly preferred.
* Good knowledge of **accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting**.
* Experience in handling supplier, subcontractor, and customer accounts.
* Good understanding of purchase orders, LPOs, invoices, delivery notes, and payment documentation.
* Proficiency in **MS Excel** and accounting software/ERP systems.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and numerical skills.
* Good communication and coordination skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet reporting deadlines.
* UAE experience in a contracting or industrial company is highly preferred.

Accounting Standards, Tax \& Regulatory Knowledge

* **UAE VAT Law and Executive Regulations**
* **Federal Tax Authority (FTA) requirements**
* **UAE Corporate Tax regulations and compliance requirements**
* **International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)**
* **Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)**
* **Double\-entry bookkeeping principles**
* **General Ledger (GL) accounting**
* **Accounts Payable (AP) and Accounts Receivable (AR)**
* **Bank and supplier/customer reconciliation**
* **Accruals, provisions, prepayments, and depreciation**
* **Fixed Asset Accounting**
* **Project Cost Accounting and Cost Control**
* **Cash Flow Management and Financial Reporting**
* Knowledge of **audit procedures and internal financial controls**

Preferred Skills \& Certifications

* ACCA / CMA / CPA qualification or part qualification is an advantage.
* UAE VAT/Tax certification or relevant training is an advantage.
* Experience with accounting software such as **Tally, QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle, Zoho Books, or similar ERP systems** is preferred.
* Advanced MS Excel skills, including **Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, and financial reporting**, are an advantage.
* UAE Driving License is an advantage.
