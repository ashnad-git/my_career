## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**We’re Hiring: Accountant \| UAE**
We are looking for a detail\-oriented and experienced **Accountant** to join our team. The ideal candidate will have strong knowledge of accounting operations, UAE VAT, Corporate Tax, WPS, reconciliations, and financial reporting.
**Key Responsibilities**
* Manage end\-to\-end accounting operations, including **AP, AR, GL, journal entries, accruals, and prepayments**.
* Handle **month\-end closing** and support preparation of financial statements and management reports, including P\&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow.
* Apply appropriate **revenue recognition** principles for prepaid memberships, packages, and subscriptions.
* Prepare **bank reconciliations** and reconcile membership management system reports, including collections, refunds, discounts, and outstanding balances.
* Maintain the **Fixed Assets Register** and subsidiary ledgers for stocks and consumables.
* Process **UAE payroll and WPS**, including salary reconciliations and preparation/review of WPS files.
* Ensure accurate **VAT accounting and reconciliations** and support VAT returns and UAE Corporate Tax compliance.
* Investigate and resolve accounting discrepancies accurately and in a timely manner.
* Maintain accounting records using **Zoho or similar ERP/accounting software**.
* Prepare schedules and supporting documentation for **internal and external auditors and regulatory authorities**.
* Use **Excel** for financial analysis and reconciliations, including Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP/VLOOKUP, and SUMIFS.
* Communicate effectively with management, staff, auditors, banks, customers, vendors, and other external parties.

**Education**
* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.

**Experience**
* **3–5 years of relevant accounting experience**.
* UAE experience is preferred.
* Experience in a **gym, fitness centre, hospitality, or other high\-volume membership/subscription\-based business** will be an advantage.

**Ideal Candidate**
We are looking for someone who is **accurate, organized, analytical, deadline\-oriented, and able to work independently** while maintaining strong communication with internal and external stakeholders.
