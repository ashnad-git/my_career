 Wafi Hospitality is seeking an experienced and detail\-oriented
 **Cost Controller** 
 to oversee and lead cost control operations across our central stores and restaurant outlets. This role plays a key part in protecting profitability through effective inventory management, accurate cost monitoring, recipe control, and comprehensive financial analysis.
 



  





 Key Responsibilities
 


* Oversee all cost control activities across central stores and outlets.
* Manage inventory control processes, stock movement monitoring, and recipe costing.
* Monitor food and beverage wastage and identify opportunities for cost optimization.
* Conduct periodic production yield tests and spot checks to ensure compliance with approved recipes and operating standards.
* Perform detailed variance analysis on food cost, beverage cost, and inventory movements.
* Investigate cost discrepancies and provide recommendations for corrective action.
* Prepare and present timely cost control reports to management.
* Maintain and update POS pricing structures and coordinate with delivery aggregators and system providers.
* Prepare monthly Cost of Sales reports, menu engineering analysis, inventory reports, and other month\-end closing requirements.
* Support operational teams in implementing best practices to improve efficiency and cost performance.
* Ensure compliance with company policies, internal controls, and audit requirements.



  





 Qualifications \& Experience
 


* Minimum 3 years of proven experience in a Cost Controller role.
* Finance, Accounting, or related educational background preferred.
* Strong knowledge of inventory management, recipe costing, yield testing, menu engineering, and cost control procedures.
* Hands\-on experience with POS systems, inventory management software, and delivery aggregator platforms.
* Strong analytical and reporting skills with attention to detail.
* Ability to identify operational inefficiencies and implement practical solutions.
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
* Demonstrated ability to lead, coach, and motivate team members while driving accountability and performance.



  





 Why Join Us?
 



  





 Join a dynamic hospitality group with a diverse portfolio of restaurants and dining concepts, where you will play a critical role in improving operational performance, driving profitability, and supporting business growth.
