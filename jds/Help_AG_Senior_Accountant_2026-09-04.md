## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Help AG is looking for an experienced Senior Accountant who will be responsible for overseeing day\-to\-day accounting activities, ensuring accuracy in financial reporting, and maintaining compliance with internal policies and external regulations. This role plays a critical part in supporting month\-end and year\-end close processes, preparing financial statements, and providing insights for decision\-making.
   

  

**Responsibilities**
* Responsible for the month\-end closing activities, including posting of journal entries, calculation of revenue for each project, cost allocation, review of contracts, etc.
* Prepare Financial Pack as per the current practices – this includes monthly/quarterly P\&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statements, Project P\&L, Forecast, Segment P\&L.
* Ensure supporting schedules/reconciliations are available for Balance Sheet items.
* Resolve queries from clients by investigating the issue and coordinating with other departments.
* Design and ensure that proper controls are in place for the preparation of financial statements.
* Prepare financial schedules that may be required by department managers.
* Respond to queries from internal and external stakeholders.
* Monitor the status of projects under the entity to be managed.
* Come up with initiatives to improve efficiency of the existing processes.
* Assist in preparation of annual budget/monthly rolling forecast.
* Coordinate with IT support in resolving issues that may arise in ERP.
* Other tasks that may be assigned from time to time.


**Qualifications \& Skills**
* Bachelor's degree in accounting or related field.
* Professional Qualification CPA/CMA/CiMA.
* Audit experience with Big 4 preferred – Senior Associate Level.
* Thorough knowledge of rules and application of VAT in UAE/KSA.
* Knowledge of practical application of controls in preparation of FS.
* Knowledge of Microsoft NAV, highly preferred.
* Knowledge of Project/Segment and Group Accounting.
* Knowledge of preparing Statutory Financial Statements up to Consolidation.
* TMT Sector experience will be an advantage.
* Excellent presentation skills.
* Advance Microsoft Excel skills.
* Good organizational skills.
* Being able to understand complex business processes and activities.
* Flexible and structured work approach based on the job requirements.
* Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate well with others and reconcile differences among resources/departments.
* Ability to speak and write in English is required; Ability to speak and write in both English and Arabic is preferred.
* Able to manage high volume workload and tight deadlines.
* Excellent oral and written communication skills.
* Takes initiative and ownership of work.
* Self\-motivated and enthusiastic.
