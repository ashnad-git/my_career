 As the Regional Financial Controller Northern Africa, you bring an unparalleled level of financial acumen, a strategic mindset geared towards results, and an unwavering passion for driving excellence in our dynamic Finance Team. Your commitment to precision and leadership will play a pivotal role in shaping the financial success of our organization.
   

  




* Supports the Area Leadership Team in the strategic and profitable development of the Region.
* Provide the necessary operational and financial expertise to the Region/ Area representatives, and Hotels where required in order to assist the decision making process.
* Responsible for supporting the coordination of the reporting activities in the Region; analysis and interpretation of statistical and financial information to appraise profitability, quality of reporting, capital expenditure and performance against comparable periods and budgets as required by management.
* Provides an independent, objective assurance and through consulting activity, identifies opportunities aligned to the Area strategy for improving management control, designed to add value, improve the organization’s operations and achieve Rezidor’ s Corporate objectives.
* Supports the Hotels and acts as Mentor to the respective Hotel Financial Controllers within the Region.
* Supports the strategic development in the area by providing input to ADFC when assisting both Business development and Asset management.
