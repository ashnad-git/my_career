**Position Overview**

Bin Saleh Travel LLC is seeking a highly skilled and detail\-oriented Travel Agency Accountant / Senior Accountant with proven experience in the UAE travel industry.

**Key Responsibilities**

· Monitor and follow up on outstanding receivables to ensure timely collection

· Manage end\-to\-end accounts payable and accounts receivable processes

· Upload invoices to government and private portals and track LPO status

· Maintain accurate, complete, and up\-to\-date accounting records

· Prepare periodic financial reports and management reports

· Perform tax computations, including VAT, Municipality Fees (MF), and Tourism Taxes, ensuring full compliance with UAE regulations

· Support month\-end and year\-end closing activities

· Reconcile receivables with GDS and travel booking systems

· Manage commission accounting, including calculation, tracking, and reconciliation

**Candidate Requirements**

· Minimum 5 years of relevant accounting experience within a travel agency in the UAE (mandatory)

· Strong knowledge of travel industry accounting practices and systems

· Proficiency in financial reporting and reconciliation processes

· Fluency in Arabic and English (both written and verbal)

· High level of accuracy, attention to detail, and organizational capability

· Ability to work independently and meet deadlines in a fast\-paced environment
