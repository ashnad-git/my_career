**Job Summary**

We are looking for a reliable and detail\-oriented Accountant to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining financial records, processing accounting transactions, preparing reports, reconciling accounts, and ensuring compliance with company policies and accounting standards.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Maintain accurate financial records and general ledger entries.
* Process accounts payable and accounts receivable transactions.
* Reconcile bank statements and account balances.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and assist with month\-end closing.
* Support budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
* Assist with internal and external audits.
* Ensure compliance with VAT regulations, accounting standards, and company policies.
* Perform other finance and accounting duties as assigned.

**Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* 2–4 years of relevant accounting experience.
* Professional certification (ACCA, CPA, CMA, or equivalent) is an advantage.
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial procedures.
* Experience with accounting software or ERP systems.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office.
* Good understanding of VAT and tax compliance requirements.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail with the ability to meet deadlines.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills.
