## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented **Finance Associate** to join our team in the UAE. The ideal candidate should have a minimum of **5 years of relevant finance and accounting experience in the UAE**, preferably within the **construction or contracting industry**.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and finance activities.
* Handle **accounts payable and accounts receivable**, including invoice processing, reconciliations, and follow\-ups.
* Prepare and maintain accurate **financial records, reports, and schedules**.
* Assist with **monthly, quarterly, and annual financial closing**.
* Perform bank, supplier, customer, and general ledger reconciliations.
* Monitor project\-related costs, expenses, and budgets.
* Assist with **project costing, cost control, and variance analysis**.
* Support cash\-flow management and forecasting.
* Coordinate with project teams, suppliers, clients, and internal departments regarding financial matters.
* Assist with VAT\-related accounting and ensure compliance with applicable UAE requirements.
* Support external and internal audits by providing required documentation and schedules.
* Maintain proper documentation and filing of financial records.
* Assist management with financial analysis and other finance\-related reports as required.

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum **5 years of relevant UAE experience** in finance/accounting.
* **Construction or contracting industry experience is highly preferred.**
* Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial processes.
* Experience with project costing and cost control will be an advantage.
* Good knowledge of **UAE VAT and accounting practices**.
* Proficiency in **MS Excel** and accounting/ERP software.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and reconciliation skills.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.

Work Location: In person
