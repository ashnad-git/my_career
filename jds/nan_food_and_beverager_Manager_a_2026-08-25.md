KRestaurant Cost Controller \& Operations Accountant

We are looking for a **hands\-on Restaurant Cost Controller \& Operations Accountant** with proven experience working in restaurants, cafés, or F\&B operations.

This is **not a purely office\-based accounting position**. The successful candidate must understand how a restaurant operates and be able to control **money, stock, food costs, wastage, purchasing, inventory, and daily sales** from the back office to the kitchen and front of house.

The ideal candidate should be able to answer, at any time:

**Where did the money go? Where did the stock go? What was purchased? What was consumed? What was wasted? What was sold? What should the theoretical cost have been, and where is the difference?**

Key Responsibilities1\. Restaurant Cost Control — MAIN RESPONSIBILITY

* Take full responsibility for monitoring and controlling the restaurant's **Food \& Beverage Cost**.
* Calculate and analyze daily and weekly food cost, beverage cost, and overall COGS.
* Compare **theoretical food cost vs. actual food cost** and investigate any discrepancies.
* Identify excessive usage, over\-portioning, wastage, spoilage, theft, incorrect recipes, and operational losses.
* Monitor food cost percentages and recommend corrective action when costs are above target.
* Conduct regular cost analysis for individual dishes, beverages, and menu items.
* Review recipes and portion sizes to ensure the actual cost of producing each item is known and controlled.
* Analyze selling price vs. ingredient cost and identify items with poor margins.
* Monitor changes in supplier prices and their effect on menu profitability.
* Assist management in setting appropriate selling prices based on actual food and beverage costs.

2\. Complete Stock \& Inventory Control

The candidate must be able to **track every item that enters and leaves the restaurant**.

This includes not only food ingredients, but **every consumable and operating material**, such as:

* Meat, poultry, seafood, vegetables, fruits, dairy and dry goods
* Cooking oils, sauces, spices and condiments
* Soft drinks, juices, water and other beverages
* Coffee, tea and beverage ingredients
* Takeaway cups, lids, containers, bags, tissues and packaging
* Cleaning chemicals and detergents
* Gloves, cleaning materials and kitchen consumables
* Gas and other operational supplies
* Any other item purchased or consumed by the restaurant

The objective is to maintain clear accountability for **every item and every unit**.

The candidate should be able to establish:

**Opening Stock \+ Purchases − Transfers/Adjustments − Wastage − Closing Stock \= Actual Consumption**

and compare this against the **theoretical consumption based on sales**.

3\. Physical Stock Counts \& Reconciliation

* Conduct daily/weekly/monthly physical inventory counts depending on the item.
* Reconcile physical stock against the inventory system and accounting records.
* Investigate shortages, excesses, unexplained variances and abnormal consumption.
* Maintain proper stock cards and inventory records.
* Monitor stock movement between kitchen, bar, storage and other operational areas.
* Ensure all stock received is properly recorded.
* Verify that goods received match purchase orders and supplier invoices.
* Monitor expiry dates, slow\-moving stock and potential spoilage.
* Ensure stock is properly stored and controlled.

4\. Kitchen Wastage \& Portion Control

The candidate must actively monitor **what is happening inside the kitchen**, not simply enter numbers into Excel.

Responsibilities include:

* Track daily kitchen wastage.
* Record spoiled, expired, damaged, over\-produced and incorrectly prepared items.
* Monitor staff meals and complimentary food.
* Monitor over\-portioning and excessive ingredient usage.
* Compare recipe standards against actual usage.
* Investigate unusually high consumption of expensive ingredients.
* Identify recurring wastage and recommend solutions.
* Work with the Chef/Kitchen Manager to reduce unnecessary food cost.
* Ensure wastage is recorded accurately rather than disappearing from stock without explanation.

5\. Beverage \& Consumables Control

Particular attention must be given to **beverages and small consumables**, where losses can easily go unnoticed.

The candidate must monitor items such as:

* Bottled water
* Soft drinks
* Juices
* Coffee and tea
* Syrups and beverage ingredients
* Cups, lids and straws
* Takeaway packaging
* Detergents and cleaning chemicals
* Tissues and other consumables

The candidate should be able to determine **how much was purchased, how much was used, how much should have been used based on sales, and what the actual variance is.**

6\. Daily Sales Reconciliation \& Audit

* Audit daily POS sales.
* Reconcile POS sales against cash collections, credit/debit card settlements and delivery platforms.
* Verify cash drops and cash deposits.
* Reconcile delivery app statements and commissions.
* Track voids, discounts, refunds and complimentary meals.
* Investigate unusual discounts, cancellations and voids.
* Ensure all sales are properly recorded and accounted for.
* Identify discrepancies between POS reports and actual collections.
