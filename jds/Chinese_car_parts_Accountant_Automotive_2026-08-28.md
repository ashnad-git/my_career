## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an Accountant to take ownership of the company's end\-to\-end accounting and Record\-to\-Report (R2R) cycle across multiple branches.

The business operates in the automotive spare\-parts sector, making strong inventory controls, branch reconciliations, cash management, and ERP experience essential (Zoho Books \& Zoho Inventory).

The successful candidate will work closely with branch teams and management to ensure that transactions are accurately recorded, reconciled, reported, and finalized.

Key Responsibilities

1\. End\-to\-End Accounting \& Record\-to\-Report

* Take full ownership of the Record\-to\-Report cycle, from transaction recording and review through reconciliations, month\-end closing, reporting, and finalization. **Maintain and review the General Ledger, ensuring accurate classification and accounting treatment.**  Manage month\-end and year\-end closing processes independently. **Prepare and analyze Profit \& Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and other financial reports.**  Perform trial balance review, ledger scrutiny, account reconciliations, and year\-end adjustments. **Manage accounts independently through to the finalization stage.**  Ensure all accounting transactions are supported by proper documentation and maintained for audit and compliance purposes. **Identify unusual transactions, discrepancies, or accounting issues and ensure timely resolution.**

2\. Multi\-Branch Accounting \& Reconciliation

* Manage and monitor accounting activities across multiple branches and locations. **Perform regular branch\-to\-branch reconciliations and ensure inter\-branch transactions are properly recorded and matched.**  Reconcile branch sales, collections, expenses, inventory movements, and cash balances with the accounting system. **Conduct periodic visits to branches to verify cash, documents, inventory records, and operational controls.**  Coordinate with branch staff to resolve discrepancies and ensure compliance with accounting procedures. **Ensure consistency of accounting processes and controls across all branches.**

3\. Inventory \& Stock Accounting

* Take ownership of accounting controls for a high\-volume, inventory\-dependent automotive spare\-parts business. **Monitor purchases, goods receipts, sales, returns, transfers, adjustments, and other inventory movements.**  Reconcile physical stock with Zoho Inventory and accounting records. **Investigate and resolve stock variances.**  Coordinate and participate in periodic stock counts across branches. **Monitor inventory valuation, purchase costs, and inventory\-related accounting entries.**  Ensure inventory movements are properly reflected in the financial accounts.

4\. Cash, Bank \& Payment Reconciliations

* Monitor and reconcile daily branch cash collections. **Perform regular physical cash counts and investigate cash shortages or excesses.**  Manage and reconcile petty cash across branches. **Reconcile bank accounts, credit cards, payment gateways, and other collection channels.**  Ensure daily collections are accurately recorded and deposited. **Investigate differences between actual collections, system records, bank statements, and accounting records.**

5\. Accounts Receivable \& Payable

* Maintain and reconcile customer and supplier ledgers. **Monitor outstanding receivables and payables.**  Review customer invoices, supplier bills, credit notes, debit notes, returns, and payments. **Perform customer and supplier statement reconciliations.**  Follow up on discrepancies and ensure ledgers remain accurate and up to date.

6\. Zoho Books \& Zoho Inventory

* Use Zoho Books and Zoho Inventory as the primary accounting and inventory systems. **Review transactions entered by sales, warehouse, and operational staff.**  Ensure accurate integration and flow of information between sales, inventory, purchasing, and accounting. **Maintain accurate customer, supplier, item, branch, and financial records.**  Generate and analyze accounting, inventory, sales, purchasing, and reconciliation reports. **Identify system/process issues and work with management to improve ERP controls and workflows.**

7\. UAE VAT \& Corporate Tax

* Support UAE VAT compliance, including transaction review, VAT reconciliations, and VAT return preparation. **Assist with UAE Corporate Tax compliance, including preparation of relevant schedules and supporting information.**  Ensure proper maintenance of tax\-related records and documentation. **Coordinate with external auditors, tax consultants, and advisors when required.**

8\. Internal Controls \& Process Improvement

* Ensure proper financial controls are maintained across branches. **Identify weaknesses in accounting, cash, inventory, purchasing, and sales processes.**  Recommend and implement improvements to accounting procedures and ERP workflows. **Ensure accounting policies and SOPs are consistently followed.**  Support management with accurate and timely financial information for decision\-making.

Candidate Requirements

* Minimum 2\+ years of relevant accounting experience. **Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field or/ and Part\-qualified ACCA, CMA, or equivalent professional qualification is preferred.** Strong knowledge of General Ledger, double\-entry accounting, reconciliations, and financial reporting. **Practical experience managing month\-end/year\-end closing and financial statements.**  Experience with inventory accounting and stock reconciliation. **Experience in a multi\-branch environment is strongly preferred.**  Good understanding of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax. **Hands\-on experience with Zoho Books and Zoho Inventory is highly preferred.**  Strong Excel skills. **Experience in automotive spare parts, trading, wholesale, retail, or distribution is an advantage.**  Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, problem\-solving ability, and ownership mindset.
