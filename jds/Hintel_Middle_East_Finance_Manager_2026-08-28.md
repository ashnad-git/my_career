## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Finance Manager** 




  





 We're partnering with a globally recognised organisation within the entertainment industry to appoint a Finance Manager for its regional headquarters in Dubai. This position offers the opportunity to take ownership of the finance function across the MENA region, working closely with senior leadership to support commercial decision\-making, maintain robust financial controls, and help drive the continued growth of the business.
 



  





**Responsibilities:** 



* Take ownership of the day\-to\-day finance activities across the regional business.
* Produce accurate monthly management accounts, financial reports, budgets and rolling forecasts.
* Deliver meaningful financial analysis and reporting to support strategic and operational decisions.
* Manage month\-end and year\-end processes, ensuring reporting deadlines are met and financial information is accurate.
* Oversee cash flow, treasury activities and tax compliance across multiple jurisdictions.
* Coordinate external audits and maintain strong financial governance and internal controls.
* Evaluate the financial viability of commercial opportunities and provide recommendations to senior stakeholders.
* Build strong relationships with both regional and international teams, acting as a trusted finance business partner.
* Identify opportunities to streamline processes, improve reporting and enhance operational efficiency through finance systems and technology.



  





**Requirements:** 



* Qualified accountant (ACCA, ACA, CIMA, CMA or equivalent), or finalist with relevant industry experience.
* At least three years' experience within financial or management accounting.
* Strong background in financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting and commercial finance.
* Experience working with ERP systems and advanced Microsoft Excel skills; exposure to Power BI is advantageous.
* Good understanding of IFRS and financial compliance within a corporate environment.
* Comfortable managing multiple priorities while maintaining exceptional accuracy and attention to detail.
* Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to build relationships across all levels of an organisation.
* Experience supporting multi\-country operations or international businesses would be beneficial.
* Previous experience within media, entertainment, publishing or another fast\-paced commercial sector is advantageous.
