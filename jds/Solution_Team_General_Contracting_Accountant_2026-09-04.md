## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Glamour Group International** is a UAE\-based company headquartered in Abu Dhabi and operating as part of the Glamour Group. The company specializes in **turnkey construction and interior solutions**, including flooring systems, artificial turf, and fit\-out services. With a comprehensive approach, Glamour Group International manages projects from **design and material supply through to installation and completion**, serving a wide range of sectors such as commercial, residential, and institutional developments.

We are currently seeking a **qualified and experienced Accountant** to join our **Abu Dhabi branch on an immediate basis**. The ideal candidate should have a strong background in **building contracting, construction, and interior fit\-out projects**, with solid knowledge of project accounting, cost control, and financial reporting.

The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, monitoring project\-related costs, handling accounts payable and receivable, and supporting the management team with financial reporting and analysis.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting activities and maintain accurate financial records.
* Handle **accounts payable, accounts receivable, supplier payments, and customer collections**.
* Monitor and record **project\-related costs, material purchases, labour costs, subcontractor expenses, and overheads**.
* Prepare project\-wise cost reports and monitor actual costs against budgets.
* Perform bank, supplier, customer, and ledger reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and support month\-end closing activities.
* Verify invoices, purchase orders, payment documents, and supporting records.
* Assist with **UAE VAT compliance, documentation, and reporting**.
* Coordinate with project managers, procurement, stores, suppliers, and subcontractors.
* Maintain proper documentation for construction and interior fit\-out projects.
* Support management with budgeting, forecasting, cost analysis, and financial reporting.
* Ensure compliance with company accounting procedures and internal controls.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum 3**–5 years of accounting experience**, preferably in the **UAE construction, building contracting, or interior fit\-out industry**.
* Strong knowledge of **construction/project accounting and cost control**.
* Good knowledge of **UAE VAT and accounting practices**.
* Proficiency in **MS Excel and accounting/ERP software**.
* Strong analytical and numerical skills with excellent attention to detail.
* Good communication and coordination skills.
* Ability to travel to project sites when required.
* Candidates available to **join immediately** will be highly preferred.
* UAE experience in building contracting or interior fit\-out is an advantage.
