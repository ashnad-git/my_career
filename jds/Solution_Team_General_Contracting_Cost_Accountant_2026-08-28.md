## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Glamour Group International** is a UAE\-based company headquartered in Abu Dhabi and operating as part of the Glamour Group. The company specializes in **turnkey construction and interior solutions, including flooring systems, artificial turf, and fit\-out services**. With a comprehensive approach, Glamour Group International manages projects from **design and material supply through to installation and completion**, serving a wide range of sectors such as commercial, residential, and institutional developments.

We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced **Cost Accountant** with a strong background in the **UAE construction, contracting, and building materials industry**. The ideal candidate will have solid expertise in project costing, cost control, budgeting, financial analysis, and management reporting, with the ability to monitor project profitability and identify cost variances.

Key Responsibilities

* Prepare and maintain **project\-wise cost reports**, budgets, and cost forecasts.
* Monitor and analyse **material, labour, subcontractor, equipment, and overhead costs**.
* Compare actual project costs against approved budgets and identify **cost variances and overruns**.
* Prepare monthly **project profitability, cost\-to\-complete, WIP, and variance reports**.
* Review purchase orders, supplier invoices, subcontractor invoices, and project\-related expenses.
* Ensure proper allocation of costs to the relevant projects, cost centres, and activities.
* Coordinate with project and procurement teams to monitor project commitments and expenditures.
* Support management with **cost analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and financial decision\-making**.
* Reconcile project costs with the general ledger and assist with month\-end closing activities.
* Monitor project progress, billing, variations, retention, and related financial information.
* Identify opportunities for **cost savings and improved operational efficiency**.
* Maintain accurate records and documentation for internal and external audits.
* Ensure compliance with company financial policies, procedures, and applicable UAE requirements.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum 2 **years of relevant experience**, preferably within the UAE construction, contracting, fit\-out, or building materials industry.
* Strong knowledge of **project accounting, job costing, budgeting, cost control, and variance analysis**.
* Experience in handling **construction project costs, materials, labour, subcontractors, and overheads**.
* Strong proficiency in **Microsoft Excel** and accounting/ERP software.
* Good understanding of UAE VAT and accounting practices.
* Strong analytical, organisational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent communication and coordination skills.
* Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects and deadlines.
* Candidates who can join immediately or are available on short notice will be preferred.
