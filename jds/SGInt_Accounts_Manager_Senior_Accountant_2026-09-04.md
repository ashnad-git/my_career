## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Description:**

\*Manage day\-to\-day accounting, bookkeeping, and accounting entries in Tally Prime.  
· Maintain accurate records of sales, purchases, receipts, payments, expenses, and ledgers.  
· Manage Accounts Receivable and Payable, including ageing, reconciliations, collections, and supplier payments.  
· Verify sales orders, purchase invoices, PI quantities/rates, and shipping/logistics invoices.  
· Prepare daily/monthly bank reconciliations and monitor cash flow, loans, overdrafts, bank charges, and interest.  
· Handle UAE VAT accounting, records, calculations, and timely filing coordination.  
· Manage stock\-related accounting, including transfers, repacking, inventory records, and stock reports, in coordination with Warehouse.  
· Prepare export invoices and coordinate required documentation.  
· Review salary sheets and maintain sales commission workings, PDCs, and payment records.  
· Coordinate with Sales, Purchase, Warehouse, and Management on collections, payments, stock, and financial matters.  
· Prepare monthly P\&L, Balance Sheet, Outstanding, Cash Flow, and other management reports, including month\-end closing.  
· Maintain the Fixed Assets Register and depreciation records.  
· Coordinate with auditors, banks, suppliers, tax consultants, and other external parties.  
· Supervise the Accounts Team, allocate work, review performance, and ensure timely and accurate completion.  
· Maintain confidentiality, accuracy, proper documentation, and compliance with company procedures.

**Skills and** **Requirements:**

* Qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) with a minimum of 5\+ years of relevant accounting experience, preferably in a UAE\-based trading company.
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and accounting practices.
* Excellent working knowledge of Tally Prime and MS Excel.
* Strong analytical, problem\-solving, communication, and reporting skills.
* High attention to detail with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
* Immediate Joining.
