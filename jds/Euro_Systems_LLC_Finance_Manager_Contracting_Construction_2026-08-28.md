Finance Manager – Contracting \& Construction

**Location:** Al Quoz, Dubai, UAE  
**Company:** Euro Systems  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Industry:** Contracting, Construction \& Interior Fit\-Out

About Euro Systems

Euro Systems is a leading engineering and technology solutions company in the UAE, delivering high\-quality projects across construction, architectural systems, interior fit\-out, ELV, smart systems, and infrastructure sectors. With a strong commitment to innovation, operational excellence, and client satisfaction, we continue to expand our footprint across the region.

We are seeking a commercially focused and highly experienced **Finance Manager** to lead our finance operations and support the company's continued growth within the contracting and construction industry.

Position Overview

The Finance Manager will be responsible for overseeing the complete financial and accounting operations of the organization, ensuring strong financial controls, compliance, profitability, and financial stability across multiple projects and business units.

The ideal candidate must have substantial **UAE contracting or construction industry experience** and possess strong expertise in project accounting, job costing, cash flow management, budgeting, financial reporting, banking facilities, Letters of Credit (LCs), bank guarantees, and contract\-related financial management.

**Candidates holding a CA qualification will be highly preferred.**

Key Responsibilities

* Lead and manage the overall finance and accounting operations of the company.
* Prepare accurate monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.
* Oversee project accounting, job costing, budgeting, forecasting, and profitability analysis.
* Monitor Work\-in\-Progress (WIP), revenue recognition, retention accounting, progress billing, and project variations.
* Manage Accounts Receivable (AR) and Accounts Payable (AP) functions to optimize cash flow and working capital.
* Handle banking relationships, credit facilities, Letters of Credit (LCs), bank guarantees, and trade finance activities.
* Review project contracts and identify financial risks, obligations, and opportunities.
* Develop and monitor project budgets and financial performance indicators.
* Ensure compliance with IFRS, UAE VAT regulations, corporate policies, and statutory requirements.
* Lead annual budgeting, financial forecasting, and strategic financial planning processes.
* Coordinate with external auditors, banks, consultants, and government authorities.
* Implement and strengthen financial controls, policies, procedures, and ERP systems.
* Provide financial insights and recommendations to senior management for strategic decision\-making.
* Lead, mentor, and develop the finance and accounts team.

Requirements

* CA preferred; ACCA, CPA, CMA, or Master's Degree in Finance/Accounting will also be considered.
* Minimum **15–20 years of progressive finance and accounting experience**.
* Minimum **5 years of experience as a Finance Manager, Finance Controller, or similar senior finance leadership role**.
* Mandatory UAE experience within **Contracting, Construction, Engineering, or Interior Fit\-Out companies**.
* Strong expertise in:
* Project Accounting
* Job Costing
* WIP Reporting
* Revenue Recognition
* Variations \& Retention Management
* Accounts Receivable \& Accounts Payable
* Cash Flow \& Working Capital Management
* Letters of Credit (LCs)
* Bank Guarantees
* Contract Finance Management
* Financial Planning \& Analysis
* Strong understanding of **UAE VAT regulations and IFRS standards**.
* Experience with ERP systems such as **SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Tally, or equivalent platforms**.
* Excellent analytical, leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
* Proven experience leading and developing finance teams.

Preferred Candidate Profile

* Strategic and commercially driven finance professional.
* Strong leadership and team management capabilities.
* Excellent communication and negotiation skills.
* Experience supporting large\-scale contracting or construction projects.
* Strong understanding of project\-based financial management.
* Ability to work effectively in a fast\-paced and project\-driven environment.
* Strong commercial awareness with the ability to provide actionable financial insights to senior management.
