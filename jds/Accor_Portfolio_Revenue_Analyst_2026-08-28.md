 **Job Description**  

* Develop and implement revenue strategies for a portfolio of hotels (2 or 3 hotels).
* Conduct regular performance reviews and optimize pricing and inventory.
* Helps the growth of Revenue and Net Profit of the hotel through the Revenue Management tools.
* Participates to the actions of the business plan to achieve revenue goals.
* Coordinates promotions and merchandising opportunities.
* Ensures the inventory is available to the specific markets at the right prices at the right time.
* Effectively provides the service to all negotiated deals on the relevant channels.
* Ensures rate competitiveness in room only and packages and ensure the Best Rate Guarantee concept is happening by controlling the distribution channels.
* Analyzes weekly reports and communicate the trends to the Sales, Reservations and Front Office team.
* Participates in weekly Strategy Meeting to provide forecast supporting major market trends.

  

**Qualifications** **Education**

* Experience with Opera (PMS and S\&C)
* Hospitality industry experience is highly preferred
