**About Speed Auto Systems**

Speed Auto Systems is a global automation company providing intelligent SaaS solutions to the automotive industry. Our technology supports businesses across vehicle leasing, car rental, limousine services, workshops, dealerships, and automotive manufacturing.

With a growing presence across the Middle East, we help automotive businesses streamline operations, improve financial visibility, and accelerate digital transformation through scalable ERP and business management solutions.

**About the Role**

We are seeking a **re**liable and detail\-focused Accountant to support our finance operations in Dubai. This role will involve overseeing core accounting activities, maintaining the integrity of financial data, supporting financial controls, preparing reports, and ensuring that transactions and records are accurately maintained.

The successful candidate will have a strong foundation in accounting, practical experience with financial systems, and the ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining accuracy and confidentiality.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Handle day\-to\-day accounting operations and ensure transactions are recorded correctly and on time.
* Maintain general ledger accounts and review accounting entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Process sales, purchase, expense, receipt, payment, and journal transactions.
* Manage customer and vendor accounts, including account maintenance and balance monitoring.
* Review and process supplier invoices and ensure payments are properly supported and authorized.
* Generate customer invoices and monitor collections against agreed payment terms.
* Reconcile bank accounts, customer ledgers, supplier accounts, and other balance sheet accounts on a regular basis.
* Investigate and resolve differences identified during reconciliations.
* Maintain supporting schedules for key accounts and ensure financial records are properly documented.
* Assist in preparing periodic financial statements, management reports, and financial summaries.
* Support the Finance Manager during monthly and annual closing procedures.
* Review expense claims and ensure expenses are recorded under the appropriate accounts and cost centers.
* Assist with cash\-flow monitoring and provide relevant financial information when required.
* Support the preparation of VAT workings, tax\-related schedules, and compliance documentation.
* Coordinate with external auditors and accountants by providing accounting records and supporting documentation.
* Assist in maintaining internal financial controls and improving accounting procedures where required.
* Ensure accounting documents are systematically maintained and readily available for review.
* Assist with budget monitoring and compare actual expenses against approved budgets.
* Provide financial information and analysis to management to support business decisions.
* Handle confidential financial information with a high level of professionalism and discretion.
* Undertake additional accounting and finance responsibilities as assigned by the Finance Manager.

**Requirements**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related discipline.
* 2–4 years of practical accounting experience.
* Prior UAE accounting experience will be an advantage.
* Strong knowledge of bookkeeping, general accounting, and financial reporting.
* Practical understanding of AP, AR, general ledger, reconciliations, and month\-end procedures.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and familiarity with accounting or ERP platforms.
* Working knowledge of UAE VAT and relevant financial compliance requirements.
* Ability to analyze financial information and identify accounting discrepancies.
* Strong numerical ability with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.
* Good organizational skills with the ability to manage deadlines effectively.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.
* Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with cross\-functional teams.
* High level of integrity and confidentiality when handling financial information.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Previous accounting experience within the UAE.
* Experience with cloud\-based ERP or accounting systems.
* Familiarity with UAE VAT compliance and tax documentation.
* Experience supporting audits and financial statement preparation.
* Knowledge of accounting controls and financial reporting processes.
* ACCA, CMA, CA, or another recognized professional accounting qualification is an advantage.
* Experience in automotive, SaaS, technology, leasing, rental, or other service\-based businesses is preferred.

**What We Offer**

* Supportive and professional working environment.
* Opportunities for professional development and career progression.
* Exposure to international business and financial operations.
* Opportunity to work with modern ERP and SaaS technologies.
* Practical experience within a growing automotive technology company.
