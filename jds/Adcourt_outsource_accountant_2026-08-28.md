A boutique firm based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, offering legal and corporate services to a diverse range of clients in various industries.

We are looking for an experienced Outsourcing Accountant to join our team in Dubai.

IMPORTANT: We are specifically looking for an outsource accountant with experience managing the full accounting cycle for multiple clients. In-house accounting experience will be rejected.

Requirements:

- Minimum 3 years of experience in an accounting firm, audit firm, tax advisory firm or corporate services provider.
- Experience managing the bookkeeping and tax compliance of multiple clients.
- Good knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax.
- Experience preparing financial statements and liaising with auditors.
- Experience with Xero is an advantage.

The ideal candidate should be able to work independently, demonstrate strong analytical skills, identify accounting and tax issues, and perform technical research where required.
