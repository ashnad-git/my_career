## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a detail\-oriented and organized General Accountant to join our team. The ideal candidate should have good accounting knowledge and practical experience in the UAE, preferably within the hospitality or F\&B industry.

Key Responsibilities

* Record daily accounting transactions and maintain accurate financial records.
* Prepare invoices, receipts, payments, and journal entries.
* Handle accounts payable and accounts receivable.
* Perform bank and account reconciliations.
* Assist with monthly closing and preparation of financial reports.
* Maintain accurate records of expenses and company transactions.
* Assist with inventory and cost control.
* Maintain VAT\-related records and assist with UAE VAT compliance.
* Monitor outstanding payments and follow up on receivables.
* Assist with budgeting, financial analysis, and reporting when required.
* Support internal and external audit requirements.
* Ensure accounting records are maintained accurately and in accordance with company policies.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* 2–4 years of accounting experience, preferably in hospitality, restaurants, cafés, hotels, or F\&B.
* Good knowledge of accounting principles and UAE VAT.
* Experience with reconciliations, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and financial reporting.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and accounting/ERP systems.
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good analytical and organizational skills.
* UAE accounting experience is preferred

Work Location: In person
