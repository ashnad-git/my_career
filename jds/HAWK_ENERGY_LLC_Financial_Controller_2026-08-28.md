## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Introduction**

We are a knowledge\-based business solutions provider making us the ideal partner to professionally service companies within the oil, gas, and power sectors. Using the latest technological advancement in the oil \& gas industry, coupled with our highly professional and dedicated staff, we offer solutions from gas treatment off/shore, on/shore drilling, compression packages, EPF…etc to the latest in renewable energy solutions.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced, detail\-oriented Financial Controller to join our EPC organization on a full\-time basis. This is a key leadership role for a finance professional who thrives in project\-driven environments and wants to take full ownership of the company's financial operations, controls, and strategic reporting.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Oversee day\-to\-day financial operations, bookkeeping, and internal reporting processes
* Manage budgeting, forecasting, and rolling cash flow monitoring across active projects
* Ensure full compliance with financial regulations, tax requirements, and applicable accounting standards (IFRS/local GAAP)
* Handle Letter of Credit (LC), bank guarantees, and trade finance documentation for project procurement and contracts
* Liaise directly with banks, auditors, insurers, and regulatory bodies on financial and compliance matters
* Prepare accurate monthly and quarterly financial statements, management accounts, and board\-ready reports
* Support project costing, WIP (work\-in\-progress) analysis, and profitability tracking across EPC contracts
* Monitor project\-level budgets vs. actuals and flag cost overruns or cash flow risks early
* Maintain and improve internal controls, approval workflows, and financial policies
* Lead month\-end and year\-end close processes, including reconciliations
* Support management with financial modeling, scenario planning, and strategic decision\-making
* Coordinate with project managers and procurement teams on contract financials, retentions, and milestone billing
* Supervise and mentor junior finance/accounting staff (if applicable)
* Drive process improvements and automation within the finance function

**Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field; professional certification (CMA, CA, CPA, or ACCA) strongly preferred
* Minimum 3\+ years of experience in financial control, ideally within EPC, construction, contracting, or trading sectors
* Strong working knowledge of trade finance instruments, LCs, bank guarantees, and related banking processes
* Solid understanding of project\-based accounting, WIP reporting, and cost\-to\-complete analysis
* Proficiency in accounting/ERP software (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Tally, QuickBooks, or similar) and advanced MS Excel skills
* Strong analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving abilities
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to liaise confidently with banks, auditorsand senior management
* High attention to detail and a strong sense of ownership over financial accuracy and compliance
* Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively under deadlines in a fast\-paced, full\-time role

**Nice to Have**

* Prior experience in the GCC/Middle East market (if applicable to your region)
* Familiarity with project finance and multi\-currency transactions
* Experience supporting external audits and due diligence processes
* Experience managing or building out a finance team

**What We Offer**

* Opportunity to work with a growing EPC company and play a central role in its financial strategy
* Competitive compensation based on experience
* Full\-time, stable role with growth potential
* Exposure to diverse projects and direct interaction with senior leadership
