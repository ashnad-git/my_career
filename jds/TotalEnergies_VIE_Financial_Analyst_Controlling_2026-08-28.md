Roles & Responsibilities

Context & Environment: The affiliate will provide an accommodation. Activities: Join our affiliate TotalEnergies EP UAE in Abu Dhabi! You will hold a Controlling position within the Finance team of TotalEnergies in the United Arab Emirates. You will be involved in the monitoring and steering of operating expenses for a key Group entity (TotalEnergies UAE Services), in a demanding international environment with strong exposure to both local management and headquarters. At the core of the financial planning and analysis process, you will be responsible for:

1. Budget steering and closing activities
Actively contribute to monthly closings, budgets, and forecasts by department
Produce performance analyses and explain variances versus budget/forecasts
Contribute to the preparation of management reporting

2. Accounts reliability and control
Monitor and challenge cost recognition (invoices, accruals)
Ensure proper allocation of expenses across departments and entities
Contribute to the continuous improvement of control processes

3. Interfaces and coordination
Work closely with accounting, operational teams, and headquarters
Manage intercompany recharges and service requests (RFS)

4. Management support
Respond to ad hoc requests (analyses, presentations, studies)
Contribute to high value-added cross-functional topics
