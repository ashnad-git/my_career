About the Role

We are looking for a **highly experienced and detail\-oriented Senior Accountant** with strong experience in the **foodstuff/FMCG industry** to manage financial accounting, reporting, reconciliations, and day\-to\-day accounting operations.

The ideal candidate should have excellent knowledge of accounting principles, strong analytical skills, and practical experience working in a fast\-paced foodstuff business.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and financial transactions.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.
* Maintain accurate general ledger, accounts payable, and accounts receivable records.
* Perform bank, supplier, customer, and account reconciliations.
* Monitor receivables, payables, cash flow, and outstanding balances.
* Handle inventory accounting and stock\-related financial reconciliations.
* Analyze foodstuff purchasing, sales, costs, margins, and inventory movements.
* Prepare financial statements and management reports.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
* Ensure accurate recording of expenses, purchases, sales, and other transactions.
* Coordinate with auditors, suppliers, customers, and internal departments.
* Ensure compliance with company accounting policies and applicable regulations.
* Identify accounting discrepancies and implement corrective actions.
* Support management with accurate financial information for business decisions.

Requirements

* **Minimum 5 years of strong accounting experience.**
* **Excellent experience in the foodstuff/FMCG industry is highly preferred.**
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Experience with inventory and cost accounting.
* Excellent Excel and accounting software/ERP skills.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving abilities.
* High attention to detail and accuracy.
* Excellent organizational and time\-management skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

Preferred Candidate

Candidates with **proven experience in foodstuff trading, distribution, wholesale, or FMCG companies** will be strongly preferred.
