## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Summary**

We are seeking an experienced and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to manage financial operations related to property management and real estate activities. The role involves handling property accounts, landlord and tenant reconciliations, service charge accounting, and ensuring full compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Manage accounting operations for managed properties and real estate portfolios
* Prepare and maintain accurate financial records for landlords, tenants, and properties
* Handle rent collection postings, service charges, and utility allocations
* Prepare landlord statements and tenant account reconciliations
* Manage accounts payable and receivable related to properties
* Reconcile bank accounts and escrow/client accounts
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports
* Assist in budget preparation for properties and service charges
* Ensure compliance with UAE VAT regulations related to real estate transactions
* Support internal and external audits by providing required documentation
* Coordinate with property management, leasing, and operations teams

**Requirements**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field
* Minimum 3 years of experience in **property management or real estate accounting**
* Strong understanding of real estate financial processes and reporting
* Knowledge of UAE VAT laws (mandatory)
* Proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy
* Ability to manage multiple properties and deadlines

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience in property management systems
* Experience handling service charge and trust/client accounts
* Prior experience in UAE real estate market
