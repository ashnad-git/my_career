## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Senior Technical Product Manager – CRM / SaaS**

**Location:** Dubai, UAE**Working model:** Full\-time, in\-house**Reporting to:** CEO and CTO

**About the Role**

We are hiring a **Senior Technical Product Manager** to take full ownership of **Real Estate CRM**, our proprietary CRM and SaaS platform built for real estate operations.

Our CRM brings together lead management, property inventory, portal integrations, client\-to\-property matching, agent workflows, listings, deal tracking, commissions, reporting, automation, and AI\-assisted tools.

This is a senior, hands\-on product leadership role combining:

* Product strategy and roadmap ownership
* Prioritisation and stakeholder alignment
* User research and operational implementation
* Production stability and continuous improvement

You will work directly with the CEO, CTO, Business Analysts, developers, and operational teams.

Your responsibility is to ensure the teams are building the **right products, in the right order, with clear business outcomes**.

**What You Will OwnProduct Strategy and Roadmap**

* Own the product strategy, roadmap, priorities, and release sequencing
* Translate company objectives into clear and executable product initiatives
* Decide what should be built, improved, postponed, or removed
* Make clear trade\-offs between scope, timing, technical quality, and business impact
* Maintain a strong understanding of users, workflows, competitors, and market opportunities

**Delivery Governance**

* Govern product delivery across two Scrum teams, each supported by a Business Analyst
* Set clear priorities, expected outcomes, milestones, and acceptance criteria
* Ensure Business Analysts translate product direction into complete functional requirements
* Protect development teams from unnecessary scope changes and conflicting requests
* Identify delivery gaps early and drive them toward resolution
* Ensure releases are properly tested, communicated, launched, and measured

**Executive and Stakeholder Alignment**

* Work directly with the CEO and CTO on product direction and technology priorities
* Act as the single point of accountability for product decisions and progress
* Interview agents, managers, administrators, finance teams, and other internal users
* Convert operational problems into scalable product solutions
* Communicate decisions clearly and manage stakeholder expectations
* Say “no” when requests do not support the agreed strategy or priorities

**Timelines, Reporting and Risk Management**

* Own product timelines, milestones, dependencies, and release expectations
* Provide clear executive reporting on progress, risks, blockers, and decisions required
* Identify technical, operational, adoption, and delivery risks before they become critical
* Escalate problems early and recommend practical solutions
* Ensure stakeholders have an accurate view of product status without unnecessary reporting complexity

**Technical Product Ownership**

* Understand CRM and SaaS architecture at a product\-management level
* Work confidently with APIs, third\-party integrations, data flows, permissions, databases, automation, and system dependencies
* Collaborate effectively with engineering leadership on architectural and technical decisions
* Challenge technical estimates and proposed solutions when necessary
* No coding is required, but strong technical literacy is essential

**User Feedback and Production Stability**

* Build structured feedback loops with internal users
* Separate isolated requests from recurring and high\-impact problems
* Prioritise production issues based on business impact and urgency
* Coordinate post\-release support, bug resolution, and continuous improvement
* Balance new product development with reliability, usability, and system stability

**Operational Implementation**

* Ensure new features are embedded into real company workflows
* Coordinate training, documentation, communication, and implementation plans
* Track adoption and identify why teams are or are not using delivered features
* Close the gap between “feature released” and “business value realised”
* Measure success through operational results, adoption, efficiency, revenue impact, and user satisfaction

**What We Are Looking ForRequired Experience**

* Senior experience as a Technical Product Manager, Senior Product Manager, Product Lead, or equivalent
* Proven ownership of complex product roadmaps and prioritisation decisions
* Experience working on CRM, ERP, marketplace, proptech, fintech, or complex SaaS platforms
* Experience governing delivery across multiple engineering teams or product squads
* Strong understanding of APIs, integrations, workflows, permissions, and system architecture
* Experience working directly with founders, CEOs, CTOs, or other senior executives
* Strong Jira and Confluence proficiency
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills
* Currently based in Dubai or willing to relocate to Dubai

**Strong Advantages**

* Fluent in Russian or Ukranian
* Experience with real estate, brokerage, proptech, or property marketplaces
* Experience building products used by sales or operational teams
* Experience with property portals, lead routing, inventory systems, commissions, or transaction workflows
* Experience introducing AI and automation into existing business processes
* Experience managing the replacement, migration, or major redevelopment of a CRM platform

**The Right Mindset**

You are:

* Strategic but comfortable working closely with execution
* Decisive, structured, and commercially minded
* Comfortable challenging senior stakeholders and defending priorities
* Able to create clarity when information is incomplete
* Highly accountable for timelines and outcomes
* Focused on solving operational problems rather than simply delivering features
* Comfortable working in a fast\-moving founder\-led environment
