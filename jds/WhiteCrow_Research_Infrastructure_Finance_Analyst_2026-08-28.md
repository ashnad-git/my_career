**As an Infrastructure Finance Analyst, you will be responsible for...** 




  





***Financial Analysis \& Modelling Support:***



* Assisting in preparing, maintaining and updating financial models for infrastructure projects.
* Supporting financial analysis including business cases, feasibility studies, valuation exercises and investment assessments.
* Collecting and organising financial and operational data required for project evaluations.
* Performing sensitivity analyses and scenario modelling under supervision of senior team members.
* Assisting in reviewing project budgets, forecasts, and cost estimates.



  





***Research \& Market Intelligence;***



* Conducting research on infrastructure sectors, market trends, financing structures and industry developments.
* Monitoring relevant regulatory developments, government initiatives and infrastructure investment activity.
* Gathering benchmarking information relating to infrastructure assets, concessions, PPP projects and financing structures.
* Maintaining internal databases of comparable transactions and market intelligence.



  





***Transaction \& Project Support:***



* Assisting with the preparation of presentations, information memoranda, briefing notes and investment committee materials.
* Supporting due diligence activities by coordinating information requests and organising documents.
* Assisting in tracking transaction workstreams, key milestones and deliverables.
* Supporting the review and organisation of technical, commercial, legal and financial reports.



  





***Stakeholder Coordination:***



* Coordinating with internal departments including Development, Legal, Commercial, Strategy and Operations.
* Supporting engagement with consultants, advisors and external stakeholders.
* Attending meetings, prepare meeting summaries and track follow\-up actions.
* Assisting in preparing materials for senior management discussions.



  





***Reporting \& Administration:***



* Preparing periodic reports, dashboards and management presentations.
* Maintaining organised electronic filing systems and document repositories.
* Supporting preparation of monthly, quarterly and annual reporting requirements.
* Assisting in ensuring data quality, consistency and completeness across reports and analyses.



  





***Learning \& Development:***



* Developing understanding of project finance, infrastructure investments, PPP structures and financial analysis techniques.
* Participating in internal training programmes and professional development initiatives.
* Building familiarity with infrastructure sectors including utilities, transport, district cooling, energy and public infrastructure.
