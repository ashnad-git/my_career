## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a **Fresher Accountant** to join our Accounts team. This position is suitable for a recent graduate who has good knowledge of basic accounting principles and is willing to learn and grow within the company.

Key Responsibilities

* Record daily accounting transactions accurately in the ERP/accounting system.
* Assist with **sales, purchase, receipt and payment entries**.
* Maintain proper filing of invoices, receipts, payment vouchers and supporting documents.
* Assist in **bank and cash reconciliations**.
* Follow up and maintain records of **customer receivables and supplier payables**.
* Assist with petty cash entries and verification.
* Support preparation of VAT\-related records and reports.
* Verify invoices and accounting documents for accuracy.
* Assist senior accountants with monthly closing and financial reports.
* Coordinate with Sales, Purchase, Warehouse and other departments for accounting\-related information.
* Perform other accounts and administrative duties as assigned.

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce or a related field**.
* **Fresh graduates are welcome to apply.**
* Good understanding of basic accounting concepts such as Debit/Credit, Journal Entries, P\&L and Balance Sheet.
* Basic knowledge of **VAT** is preferred.
* Good knowledge of **Microsoft Excel**.
* Experience or knowledge of any **ERP/accounting software** will be an advantage.
* Good numerical and analytical skills.
* Attention to detail and accuracy.
* Willingness to learn and take responsibility.
* Good communication skills in English.

Preferred Candidate

We are looking for someone who is **responsible, organized, quick to learn and comfortable working with numbers and computer systems**. Training will be provided for our accounting procedures and ERP system.
