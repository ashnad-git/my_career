## Responsibilities

**Planning & Forecasting:**
- Support annual budgets, business plans, rolling forecasts, and long-term financial models
- Build and maintain models for planning, scenario analysis, and decision-making
- Coordinate with departments to gather budget inputs, forecasts, assumptions, and updates

**Reporting & Analysis:**
- Produce monthly management reports, dashboards, and performance summaries
- Run budget-to-actual, forecast-to-actual, and year-on-year variance analysis
- Investigate material variances and provide explanations and recommendations
- Analyse revenue, costs, margins, cash flow, capital expenditure, and key KPIs
- Prepare ad hoc financial analysis and reports for Finance Manager or senior leadership

**Operations:**
- Collect, validate, and consolidate financial and operational data across departments
- Support month-end and year-end close by reviewing data and assisting schedules
- Monitor departmental spend and support cost control initiatives
- Maintain organised planning files, reports, models, and supporting documentation
- Improve reporting processes, data quality, financial controls, and efficiency
- Ensure compliance with company policies, accounting standards, and internal controls
