5\. Monitor supplier aging, advances, and outstanding payments, and coordinate timely settlements.

6\. Prepare and process branch sales reports, cashier short/excess reports, and weekly/monthly financial reports.

10\. Support senior management and accounting teams with daily financial activities, reporting, transaction approvals, and general accounting tasks.
