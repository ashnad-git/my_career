## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Main Function:**

\- Manage and oversee the financial operations and reporting for AJHC, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and financial health.

**Scope of Job Activities:**

\- Managing Accounts Receivables and Payables.

\- Overseeing and maintaining general ledger, including the trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, and specific required reports.

\- Ensuring precise and suitable recording and analysis of both revenues and expenses.

\- Compiling and scrutinizing financial data for the creation of entries in various accounts, such as general ledger accounts, while documenting business transactions.

\- Handling payroll administration, encompassing calculations for applicable deductions, overtime, end\-of\-service benefits, and other staff compensation and benefits\-related matters.

\- Managing inventory records.

\- Reviewing bank statements for accuracy.

\- Resolving any accounting discrepancies and irregularities.

\- Maintaining all electronic and manual accounting records that support financial statements.

\- Preparing reconciliations at the end of each reporting period and the associated journal entries for all balance sheet accounts.

\- Establishing and monitoring the implementation of accounting control procedures.

\- Generating profit and loss statements as well as monthly closing and cost accounting reports.

\- Analyzing financial data to propose or develop efficient resource utilization and procedures, offering strategic recommendations, and devising solutions for business and financial challenges.

\- Providing explanations of billing invoices and accounting policies to staff, vendors, and clients.

**Qualifications, Skills \& Competency Requirements:**

\- ECPA License as a Certified Public Accountant

\- Excellent communications skills in both English and Arabic Language (highly preferred).

\- Physically fit to adequately perform described duties

\- Minimum 2 years’ experience in a related field

\- Self\-directed with the ability to work with minimal supervision

\- Strong professional ethics

\- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field; CPA or equivalent certification preferred.

\- Proven experience as an accountant, preferably in healthcare or a similar industry.

\- Proficiency in accounting software and financial management systems.

\- Strong knowledge of financial principles, regulations, and reporting standards.

\- Attention to detail and accuracy in financial data management.

\- Analytical and problem\-solving skills.

\- Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.

\- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

\- Organizational and time management skills.

\- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and financial modeling.

\- Familiarity with healthcare finance and regulations is a plus.
