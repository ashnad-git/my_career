## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Purpose

The Business Analyst translates **du Pay’s commercial and product initiatives into clear, user\-centric, technically feasible and compliant requirements**. The role bridges Commercial/P\&L Owners, Product, Technology, Solution Architecture, UI/UX, Operations, Compliance, Risk, Customer Support, QA and external partners to deliver scalable fintech solutions and seamless customer journeys.

The role requires strong **Business Analysis, Stakeholder Management and end\-to\-end delivery capability across Agile, Waterfall and Hybrid methodologies**.

Key Responsibilities

**Business Analysis \& Delivery**

* Elicit, analyze, prioritize, document and validate **functional and non\-functional requirements** through workshops, interviews, discovery sessions and stakeholder discussions.
* Produce appropriate BA artefacts including **BRD, FRD/FSD, epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, use cases, business rules, RTM, process flows, data/reporting requirements and interface/integration requirements**.
* Perform **As\-Is/To\-Be, gap analysis, impact analysis and process analysis**.
* Work across **Agile/Scrum/Kanban, Waterfall and Hybrid** environments, adapting documentation and governance to the delivery model.
* Support backlog refinement, sprint planning, requirements baselining/sign\-off, traceability, change control, testing, UAT, release and implementation.
* Maintain end\-to\-end requirements traceability from business need through solution design, development, testing and implementation.

**Stakeholder Management**

* Manage stakeholders across **Commercial, Product, Technology, Architecture, Engineering, UI/UX, Operations, Compliance, Risk, Fraud, Customer Support, QA and external partners/vendors**.
* Facilitate workshops, requirement walkthroughs, solution discussions, validation and approvals.
* Translate business needs into actionable technical requirements and technical constraints into clear business impacts.
* Manage conflicting requirements, priorities, expectations, dependencies, risks, scope changes and stakeholder sign\-offs.
* Build consensus and influence cross\-functional stakeholders without direct authority.

**Customer Journey, Process \& Technology**

* Define requirements for **mobile/web channels, customer journeys, operational workflows, reporting and third\-party integrations**.
* Collaborate with UI/UX on personas, usability, Figma/designs, validations, error handling, accessibility, business rules, happy paths and edge/exception scenarios.
* Design and maintain **BPMN 2\.0 end\-to\-end processes**, including happy paths, exceptions, reversals and failure scenarios, within the Central Business Process Repository.
* Work with Solution Architects on **APIs, microservices, platforms, integrations, security, performance and scalability requirements**.
* Analyze processor, payment scheme, network, partner and API documentation and convert it into business\-ready requirements and process flows.

**FinTech, Payments \& Compliance**

* Define requirements covering **wallets, ledgers, balances, transaction lifecycles, physical/virtual card issuance, card processing, four\-party card model, payments, reversals, refunds, reconciliation and disputes**.
* Support customer lifecycle journeys including **onboarding, KYC/KYC refresh, limits, account servicing and customer support**.
* Incorporate **CBUAE/SVF, AML/CFT, Consumer Protection, Data Localization, Fraud, Dispute Management, PCI\-DSS, security and data protection** requirements in collaboration with Compliance.
* Define data/reporting requirements for **commercial dashboards, operational monitoring, reconciliation, management reporting and audit**.
* Support UAT, defect clarification, requirement validation and business sign\-off.

**Qualifications \& Experience**

* **5\+ years' Business Analyst experience**, preferably within **FinTech, Payments, Digital Banking, Wallets, Neobanking or regulated financial services**.
* Experience across **Agile, Waterfall and/or Hybrid delivery methodologies**.
* Strong stakeholder management, facilitation, negotiation and influencing capability.
* Experience with **wallets, card issuing, payment processing and regulated fintech platforms**.
* UAE financial services / **CBUAE regulatory exposure preferred**.
* Bachelor's degree in **Computer Science, Information Systems, Finance, Engineering, Business or related discipline**.
* **CBAP, CCBA, CSPO/PSPO, Agile/Scrum, PMP, BPMN, ISO 27001 or PCI\-DSS exposure/certification** is advantageous.

**Key Skills \& Tools**

Strong capability in **requirements elicitation and lifecycle management, stakeholder management, BRD/FRD/FSD, user stories, acceptance criteria, RTM, BPMN 2\.0, As\-Is/To\-Be, gap/impact analysis, customer journey mapping, process modelling, UAT, change control, APIs, microservices, integrations and solution architecture concepts**.

Hands\-on exposure to **Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, Visio, Lucidchart, Miro, PlantUML, Mermaid and Figma or equivalent tools**.

**Core Competencies**

**Business Analysis \| Stakeholder Management \| Agile/Waterfall/Hybrid Delivery \| Payments \& FinTech \| Requirements Management \| Customer Journey Analysis \| BPMN/Process Management \| Communication \& Facilitation \| Negotiation \& Influencing \| Problem Solving \| Regulatory Awareness \| Cross\-Functional Collaboration \| Customer Focus \| Results Orientation**

**EITC Competencies:** Think Strategically – L3 \| Achieve Tangible Results – L4 \| Lead Breakthrough Change – L4 \| Exceed Customer Expectations – L2 \| Nurture, Inspire \& Motivate – L2 \| Target Win\-Win Outcomes – L3
