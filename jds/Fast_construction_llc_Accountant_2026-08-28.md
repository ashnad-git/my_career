**Position: Accountant**

Fast Construction L.L.C. is seeking a detail‑oriented and experienced **Accountant** to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing daily accounting operations, financial reporting, and supporting the finance department in maintaining accurate and compliant records.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Manage daily accounting entries, bookkeeping, and financial records.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.
* Handle accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations.
* Maintain petty cash, expense tracking, and vendor payments.
* Assist in budgeting, forecasting, and cost control activities.
* Coordinate with auditors and support annual audit requirements.
* Ensure compliance with UAE accounting standards and VAT regulations.
* Maintain organized documentation and support management with financial reports.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field.
* Minimum 2–4 years of accounting experience (UAE experience preferred).
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and VAT filing.
* Proficiency in MS Excel and accounting software (Tally/QuickBooks/ERP).
* Excellent communication and organizational skills.
* **Preference for candidates on Spouse Visa** (company will provide insurance).
