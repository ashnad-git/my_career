## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**What you will do**

We are looking for an experienced iScala Functional Analyst to join our global team. You will **provide functional expertise, application support, and deployment guidance** for the iScala ERP platform, ensuring stable operations and continuous improvement across **global business units**.


The role focuses on supporting users, resolving complex functional issues, driving enhancements, and improving service performance in a managed services environment.

**How you will do it**

**Application Design \& Deployment**

* Configure and support iScala ERP solutions aligned with business requirements and global standards.
* Lead functional deployment activities and support ERP enhancement initiatives.
* Participate in testing, upgrades, and implementation projects.

**ERP Support \& Managed Services**

* Provide advanced functional support for iScala ERP and related Edge Apps solutions.
* Troubleshoot complex business and system issues.
* Ensure SLA targets are met and support tickets are resolved efficiently.
* Partner with business users and regional IT Business Partners to address critical operational needs.

**Business Analysis \& Stakeholder Management**

* Gather and analyze business requirements.
* Translate business needs into system solutions and process improvements.
* Act as a key link between business stakeholders and technical teams.
* Collaborate with global teams to support ERP optimization and standardization.

**Compliance \& Continuous Improvement**

* Support localization, electronic invoicing, taxation, and statutory compliance requirements.
* Assist with financial reporting processes and SOX\-related governance.
* Identify opportunities to improve system performance, user experience, and support effectiveness.

**Data \& Integration Support**

* Support data migration and ERP mapping activities.
* Work with technical teams on integrations and data\-related requirements.
* Basic understanding of SQL and ETL concepts is beneficial.

**What we look for**

**Required**

* Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
* Strong managed services and functional application support background in **iScala**.
* Experience working in complex international environments supporting ERP implementations, upgrades, and process improvements.
* Strong stakeholder management, communication, and problem\-solving skills.

**Preferred**

* Experience with iScala Financials, Supply Chain, Logistics, Project Management, Contract Management, or Service Management modules.
* Edge Apps ecosystem experience.
* Team leadership, coaching, or mentoring experience.
