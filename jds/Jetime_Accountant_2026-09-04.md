**About The Role**
 We are looking for experienced Accountant to join our Team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing financial records, preparing accurate reports, handling VAT Tax returns \& Corporate Tax, and ensuring compliance with UAE financial regulations. The role also includes HR\-related financial tasks, such as payroll processing, WPS transactions, coordinating employee renewals, and additional administrative duties.
   

  

**Key Responsibilities**
* Manage and oversee Odoo software for accounting operations
* Prepare and maintain financial records, including ledgers, accounts payable/receivable, and reconciliations
* Perform bank reconciliations and manage cash flow
* Ensure accurate invoicing, billing, and reconciliation of transactions
* Ensure compliance with UAE VAT laws and financial regulations, including corporate tax and VAT filings
* Support internal and external audits by preparing necessary documentation
* Identify and resolve discrepancies in financial reports
* Generate and analyse financial statements, ensuring compliance with IFRS standards
* Conduct budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning activities
* Oversee inventory stock control and vehicle documentation management
* Coordinate with PRO for trade licenses, tenancy contracts, and employee renewals
* Process payroll through Odoo, execute WPS transactions, and oversee HR\-related financial transactions, including recruitment procedures
