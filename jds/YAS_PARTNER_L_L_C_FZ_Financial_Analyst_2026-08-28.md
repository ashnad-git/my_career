Job Description

Roles & Responsibilities

Prepare detailed financial analysis, reports, and management dashboards.
Develop and maintain financial forecasts, budgets, and financial models.
Analyze revenue, costs, profitability, cash flow, and business performance.
Create and manage Power BI dashboards and reports to support business decision-making.
Monitor actual performance against budgets and forecasts and investigate variances.
Provide insights and recommendations to management based on financial and operational data.
Analyze sales and profitability across different products, categories, channels, and business segments.
Support budgeting and annual planning processes.
Prepare monthly and periodic management reports.
Identify financial trends, risks, and opportunities for improving profitability.
Work closely with Finance, Sales, Procurement, and Operations teams to gather and analyze business data.
Assist in developing financial KPIs and performance metrics.
Ensure accuracy and integrity of financial data used for reporting and analysis.
