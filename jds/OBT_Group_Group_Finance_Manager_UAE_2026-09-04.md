## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Description**
 With a strong footprint across Africa and the Middle East, OBT continues to expand its regional operations through disciplined financial governance, operational excellence, and a hands\-on leadership approach.
   

  

**Looking for your next opportunity?**
 As 
 **Group Finance Manager,** 
 you will be based in our Dubai head office and closely collaborate with the Group Finance Director. You will play a key role in overseeing group\-level accounting operations, consolidations, audits, tax coordination, compliance, and financial governance across multiple African jurisdictions.
   

 This is a high\-visibility role requiring strong technical accounting expertise, attention to detail, and the ability to coordinate across multiple entities, auditors, and stakeholders within a fast\-paced international environment.
   

  

**Job Requirements**
* Strong experience in group accounting, consolidations, audit management, and financial reporting within a multi\-country environment.
* Solid understanding of IFRS, statutory compliance, tax coordination, and internal controls.
* Experience managing audits and working directly with external auditors across multiple jurisdictions.
* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field.
* Professional accounting qualification such as CPA, ACCA, or CA preferred.
* Experience working across Africa and multicultural environments is highly preferred.
* Strong technical accounting knowledge with a hands\-on and detail\-oriented approach.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
* Ability to work in a fast\-paced environment with high ownership and accountability.
* Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.


**Job Responsibilities**
* Oversee end\-to\-end group accounting activities across multiple entities and jurisdictions.
* Set up new entities in line with group standards and local compliance.
* Manage monthly, quarterly, and annual group consolidation and financial reporting processes.
* Ensure accuracy and integrity of financial statements in line with IFRS, OHADA, and local GAAP requirements.
* Coordinate and manage external audits across African entities and act as the primary point of contact for auditors.
* Work closely with local finance teams to ensure timely and accurate reporting submissions.
* Oversee intercompany reconciliations and ensure proper balance sheet governance across the group.
* Coordinate tax compliance matters across multiple African jurisdictions alongside local advisors and finance teams.
* Support implementation and continuous improvement of accounting policies, controls, and reporting procedures.
* Monitor statutory compliance requirements and ensure adherence to local regulations.
* Partner closely with the Group Finance Director on strategic finance initiatives, governance, and process improvements.
* Support finance transformation initiatives, ERP/system enhancements, and reporting automation projects.
* Assist in managing banking, treasury, and cash flow reporting requirements at group level.


**Job Benefits**
* High\-impact regional role working directly with senior leadership.
* Exposure to complex international operations across Africa and the Middle East.
* Opportunity to work closely with experienced finance and operational leaders.
* Dynamic, hands\-on environment with strong career growth potential.
* Competitive package and long\-term development opportunities within a growing group.
* Based in Dubai with regional exposure across multiple African markets.
