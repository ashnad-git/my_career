## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a detail\-oriented and analytical Cost Controller to join our team. The ideal candidate will have strong experience in F\&B cost control, inventory management, and financial analysis, preferably within restaurants, cafés, hotels, or the hospitality industry.

Key Responsibilities

* Monitor and control food and beverage costs to ensure they remain within budget.
* Conduct regular inventory counts and maintain accurate stock records.
* Monitor stock movements, purchases, transfers, and consumption.
* Prepare and maintain accurate recipe and menu costing.
* Analyze food and beverage cost variances and identify discrepancies.
* Monitor wastage, spoilage, and portion control.
* Reconcile physical stock with system records and investigate variances.
* Review purchasing prices and supplier invoices to ensure accuracy.
* Coordinate with the kitchen, purchasing, warehouse, and operations teams regarding stock and cost control.
* Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly cost control reports for management.
* Monitor theoretical versus actual food and beverage costs.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and cost\-saving initiatives.
* Ensure all inventory and cost control procedures are properly followed.
* Use POS and ERP systems to monitor sales, inventory, and cost data.
* Support internal and external audits by providing required inventory and cost\-related documents.
* Identify opportunities to reduce costs while maintaining product quality and operational standards.

Requirements:

* Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Hospitality Management, or a related field.
* 2–4 years of experience in F\&B Cost Control, preferably in restaurants, cafés, hotels, or hospitality.
* Strong knowledge of food and beverage costing, inventory management, recipe costing, and stock reconciliation.
* Good understanding of wastage control and variance analysis.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and POS/ERP systems.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good communication and coordination skills.
* Knowledge of hospitality operations and F\&B processes is essential.

Work Location: In person
