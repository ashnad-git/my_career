## Full Job Description (verbatim)

ASAS Uniforms is a dynamic and growing apparel manufacturing company known for its commitment to quality and sustainability. As we expand across local and international markets, we are looking for a highly skilled and strategic **Accountant – FP\&A and Office Manager** to join our team. This hybrid role combines financial planning and analysis responsibilities with office and sales supervision to ensure smooth operations and drive business performance.

**Position Overview:**  
We are seeking a **UAE\-experienced Finance professional** with strong expertise in manufacturing, ideally within the manufacturing industry. The ideal candidate will not only lead the company’s **financial planning and analysis efforts** but also manage **office administration** and play a key role in **weekly sales reviews**. This dual role requires a proactive, organized and detail\-oriented individual with excellent communication skills to support strategic initiatives across departments.

**Financial Planning \& Analysis (FP\&A):**

* Develop, manage and analyze the company’s **annual budget**, long\-term financial plans and monthly forecasts.
* Conduct **variance analysis** comparing actual results against forecasts and budgets, providing actionable insights for corrective actions.
* Prepare detailed **financial reports, presentations, and analyses** for management, highlighting key trends, risks, and opportunities.
* Collaborate with production and inventory teams to support **costing strategies**, optimize margins and improve process efficiencies.
* Analyze **manufacturing costs, pricing strategies and production performance** to drive profitability.
* Manage **cash flow** and ensure timely **supplier payments**.
* Support **statutory audits, tax (VAT) filings** and compliance with financial regulations and internal controls.

**Sales \& Office Administration Management:**

* Oversee day\-to\-day **office operations**, including administrative tasks, facilities management and vendor coordination.
* Ensure smooth office workflows by managing general supplies, equipment and ensuring compliance with company policies.
* Supervise **HR\-related administrative processes** such as employee records, attendance and payroll coordination.
* Lead **weekly sales review meetings** to monitor performance against targets, identify trends and develop corrective strategies where necessary.
* Provide financial insights during sales meetings to drive revenue growth and improve decision\-making.
* Track and report **key sales metrics** to management, ensuring timely follow\-up on action items.

**Key Attributes:**

* Highly detail\-oriented with strong analytical and quantitative skills.
* Assertive in presenting financial findings and recommendations.
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate across departments.
* Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast\-paced environment.
* Proactive and adaptable, with a focus on continuous improvement in financial, operational, and sales processes.

**Qualifications and Experience:**

* Master’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
* Minimum of 5 years of experience in UAE in financial planning and analysis or a similar role within the manufacturing industry; experience in apparel manufacturing is preferred.
* Proficiency in financial reports, analysis and ERP software (**SAP Business One**) and Microsoft Excel.
* Solid understanding of financial regulations, standards and best practices.
* Experience in budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis.
* Prior experience in office management and coordinating sales activities is a plus.

**What We Offer:**

* Competitive salary and benefits package.
* Opportunities for professional growth and development.
* A dynamic and collaborative work environment in a growing industry.
* The chance to make a significant impact within a global organization.
