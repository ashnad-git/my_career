## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**About Fruitful Day**
Fruitful Day (FFD) is a UAE\-based premium healthy food and wellness group headquartered in Dubai Investment Park, operating four sub\-brands: Fruitful Day (fresh fruit subscriptions, gifting, events \& catering), Juice Joy (cold\-pressed juices), Pop Culture (artisan ice pops) and Lemon Lane (boutique café). Fruit and ingredient cost is the single largest driver of profitability in the business, making disciplined cost control a core function rather than a back\-office task.
**Role Purpose**
This is a hybrid role combining hands\-on cost control (tracking fruit/ingredient cost, wastage, and purchasing discipline) with junior accounting support. The role exists to give management a daily, granular view of the cost drivers that determine whether FFD is profitable, while also strengthening the accuracy and timeliness of day\-to\-day bookkeeping.
**Key Responsibilities**
**Cost Control (primary focus)**
* Track daily/weekly fruit and ingredient cost % by SKU and channel; flag deviations from target immediately.


* Log all direct market purchases (bananas, watermelon, mango, etc.) same\-day in Odoo, per the current direct\-purchasing process.


* Maintain a weekly wastage log alongside cost %, since wastage is the direct offset to purchasing gains — track both together, never in isolation.


* Reconcile supplier invoices against purchase orders and delivery notes; investigate price or quantity variances.


* Support the Operations team with procurement: supplier selection, purchase order accuracy, and pricing input for kitchen and market purchases.


* Support standard and AVCO (average cost) costing/recipe costing for new products and menu items across all four sub\-brands.


* Prepare weekly cost dashboards for the leadership meeting.


**Junior Accounting**
* Handle day\-to\-day transactional accounting: accounts payable/receivable processing, invoicing, and payment reconciliation.


* Support payroll data checks (e.g., verifying payroll files against confirmed HR arrangements before figures are used in reporting).


* Assist with month\-end close tasks: accruals, inventory valuation, journal entries under supervision.


* Track and reconcile OPEX lines — utilities, subscriptions, and IT contracts — against budget; support periodic subscriptions/utilities audits.


* Maintain accurate, well\-filed supporting documentation for VAT filing, corporate tax, and the annual audit.


* Escalate data discrepancies to the Lead Accountant rather than resolving unilaterally.


**Requirements**
* 1–3 years' experience in cost control, F\&B/hospitality accounting, or a junior accounting role; F\&B/food cost experience strongly preferred.


* Working knowledge of inventory/cost accounting principles (COGS, wastage, standard and AVCO costing).


* Comfortable in Excel/Google Sheets; Odoo experience a strong plus.


* High attention to detail and comfortable with daily/weekly reporting cadence — this role lives in the numbers, not just the month\-end close.


* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field (or actively pursuing ACCA/CMA).


* UAE experience preferred; Arabic a plus but not required.


**Success Looks Like**
* Fruit/ingredient cost % and wastage are tracked and reported weekly without prompting.


* Direct purchases are logged same\-day, every time, with no backlog.


* Supplier invoice variances are caught before payment, not after.


* The Lead Accountant has reliable junior support, freeing capacity for higher\-value accounting work.


**A note on this role**
*Cost Controller / Junior Accountant is a recognized hybrid — pairing food\-cost/inventory control with general accounting duties is standard in UAE hospitality and F\&B businesses of this size, where a dedicated cost controller alone wouldn't be full\-time work. As FFD scales, the two halves can split into separate cost control and accounting hires.*
