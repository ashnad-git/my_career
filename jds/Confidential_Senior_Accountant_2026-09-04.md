## Full Job Description (verbatim)

A leading diversified group in Dubai is looking for a
 **Senior Accountant** 
 to join our growing team. We are looking for a detail\-oriented and proactive professional with strong UAE accounting experience and the ability to independently manage end\-to\-end accounting functions.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 




  




* Manage day\-to\-day accounting operations and ensure accurate and timely recording of all financial transactions.
* Oversee bookkeeping, journal entries, sales, purchases, expenses, receipts and payments.
* Perform and review bank, cash, customer and supplier reconciliations.
* Manage accounts receivable and accounts payable and ensure timely follow\-up on outstanding balances.
* Review accounting records and supporting documents to ensure accuracy, proper classification and compliance.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly and annual financial reports and management accounts.
* Assist with the preparation and review of financial statements in accordance with applicable accounting standards.
* Monitor and ensure compliance with UAE VAT and other applicable financial regulations.
* Coordinate with internal teams and follow up on missing documents and information required for accounting and reporting.
* Assist with budgeting, cash flow monitoring and financial analysis where required.
* Support external auditors and provide the required schedules, reconciliations and supporting documentation.
* Identify accounting discrepancies and implement appropriate corrective actions.
* Ensure accounting procedures and internal controls are properly followed.
* Provide financial information and analysis to management when required.



  





**Requirements** 




  




* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field.
* 4\+ years of relevant accounting experience, with
 **strong UAE experience preferred.**
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT, accounting principles and applicable financial regulations.
* Professional qualification such as
 **ACCA, CPA or equivalent** 
 is preferred.
* Strong knowledge of accounting software such as
 **Zoho, SAP, QuickBooks or similar.**
* Experience in preparing financial statements and management reports.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Ability to independently manage multiple accounting functions and meet deadlines.
* Good communication and organisational skills.



  





**What We Offer** 




  




* Professional and collaborative work environment.
* Opportunity for career growth within a diversified group.
* Exposure to multiple business activities and accounting functions.
