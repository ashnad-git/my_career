**About the Role:**  
Ganjina Real Estate is looking for an experienced Accountant cum Admin to manage day\-to\-day accounting and administrative operations. The ideal candidate has a solid background in real estate accounting — rental transactions, tenancy contracts, and property\-related bookkeeping — and can handle general office administration alongside financial duties.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Manage day\-to\-day bookkeeping, invoicing, and financial record\-keeping
* Handle rental/lease transaction accounting and reconciliations
* Prepare monthly financial reports and support the HR/Admin team
* Manage petty cash, vendor payments, and expense tracking
* Handle general administrative duties: filing, correspondence, and office coordination
* Liaise with banks, auditors, and government portals (RAKEZ, EJARI\-equivalent, etc.) as needed
* Support payroll processing and employee documentation

**Requirements:**

* Minimum 3 years of accounting experience; real estate industry background preferred
* Proficiency in accounting software (CRM QuickBooks or similar) and MS Excel
* Strong understanding of UAE VAT and basic compliance requirements
* Good communication skills in English (Arabic is a plus)
* Detail\-oriented, organized, and able to multitask across accounting and admin functions
* Must be based in or willing to relocate to Ras Al Khaimah, AL Jazeera.
