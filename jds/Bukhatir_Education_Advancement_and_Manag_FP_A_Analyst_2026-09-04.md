## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Bukhatir Education Advancement \& Management (BEAM)** 
 is a leading education group with over two decades of experience in developing and managing high\-quality schools across the UAE and internationally. Our Creative Science Schools combine international curricula, innovation and academic excellence with strong values and a clear commitment to developing future leaders.
   

  

 We are looking for a
 **FP\&A Analyst** 
 to join our Finance team and play a key role in supporting financial planning, business performance analysis and strategic decision\-making across the education business.
   

  

**What you’ll do**
* Support the preparation of the Annual Budget and Business Plan across education entities.
* Analyze business performance, forecasts and key financial drivers, providing meaningful insights to business leaders.
* Challenge planning assumptions and support effective and accurate forecasting.
* Develop financial models, scenario and sensitivity analyses to support business cases and investment decisions.
* Prepare management reports, dashboards and ad\-hoc analysis using Excel and BI tools.
* Identify opportunities to improve processes, reduce costs and enhance financial performance.
* Partner with Finance and business teams to turn data into clear, actionable recommendations.


  

  

**What we’re looking for**
* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in
 **Finance** 
 .
* **CFA Level 1 is mandatory** 
 ; progress towards CFA qualification is an advantage.
* 2–4 years of relevant experience in FP\&A, corporate finance, investment banking, private equity, consulting or a similar analytical environment.
* **Advanced Microsoft Excel skills** 
 , including financial modelling, scenario analysis and data analysis.
* Strong commercial and business acumen with excellent analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Experience with BI/reporting tools is an advantage.
* Strong communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills.
* A proactive, curious mindset with the confidence to challenge assumptions and identify better ways of working.


  

  

**Why join BEAM?**
 Join a forward\-thinking education group where finance plays an important role in shaping growth and strategic decisions. You’ll work closely with senior stakeholders, gain exposure to a diverse education business and contribute to a culture built around excellence, innovation and values.
   

  

**If you are a commercially minded finance professional who enjoys turning numbers into insights and decisions, we would like to hear from you.**
