## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Group Finance Manager – Contracting \& Construction

**Location:** Al Quoz, Dubai, UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time

About Euro Systems

Euro Systems is a leading Architectural Solutions Provider in the UAE, delivering high\-quality solutions across commercial, residential, hospitality, healthcare, education, and government projects. Our core expertise includes architectural fit\-out, aluminium and glass systems, metal works, interior solutions, and customized engineering products.

With a strong focus on quality, innovation, and operational excellence, Euro Systems works closely with developers, consultants, architects, and contractors to deliver complex architectural and construction solutions.

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced and commercially driven **Group Finance Manager** to lead the finance function across the organization and its various business units.

The ideal candidate will have **15–20 years of progressive finance experience in the UAE, specifically within Contracting, Construction, Architectural Fit\-Out, or related industries**. The successful candidate will be responsible for financial strategy, project finance, cash flow management, banking, reporting, compliance, risk management, and overall financial governance.

This position requires a strategic finance leader who can work closely with the Managing Director, senior management, project teams, banks, auditors, and other key stakeholders to drive financial performance and support business growth.

Key Responsibilities

* Lead and oversee the Group Finance function across multiple companies and business units.
* Develop and execute financial strategies in line with the organization's business and growth objectives.
* Manage annual budgets, financial forecasts, cash flow projections, and long\-term financial planning.
* Prepare and present monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.
* Monitor project financial performance, profitability, job costing, margins, and cost controls.
* Oversee project accounting, including billing, revenue recognition, retention, variations, accruals, and contract\-related financial matters.
* Manage **Letters of Credit (LCs), bank guarantees, trade finance facilities, working capital facilities, and banking relationships**.
* Monitor receivables, payables, collections, cash utilization, and working capital requirements.
* Ensure effective financial controls are implemented across all business units and projects.
* Ensure compliance with **IFRS, UAE VAT, UAE Corporate Tax, statutory requirements, and applicable financial regulations**.
* Lead month\-end, quarter\-end, and year\-end closing activities.
* Coordinate with internal and external auditors and ensure timely completion of audits.
* Review financial risks and recommend appropriate mitigation and control measures.
* Provide senior management with financial analysis, commercial insights, business performance reviews, and strategic recommendations.
* Support management in evaluating new projects, contracts, investments, and major business decisions.
* Establish and improve finance policies, procedures, reporting systems, and internal controls.
* Lead, mentor, and develop the finance team to ensure high performance and professional development.
* Maintain strong relationships with banks, auditors, tax consultants, financial institutions, and government authorities.
* Drive process improvements, automation, and financial reporting efficiency across the Group.

Requirements

* Bachelor's Degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related discipline**.
* **15–20 years of progressive finance experience in the UAE is mandatory.**
* Strong industry experience in **Contracting, Construction, Architectural Fit\-Out, Aluminium \& Façade, or related project\-based industries is mandatory.**
* Minimum **5 years of experience in a Finance Manager, Group Finance Manager, Financial Controller, or similar senior leadership position**.
* Proven experience managing finance operations across **multiple companies, projects, or business units**.
* Strong expertise in **Letters of Credit, Bank Guarantees, Trade Finance, Banking Facilities, and Working Capital Management**.
* Strong knowledge of **project accounting, job costing, contract accounting, revenue recognition, and project profitability analysis**.
* Hands\-on experience in budgeting, forecasting, cash flow management, financial planning, and management reporting.
* Strong understanding of **IFRS, UAE VAT, UAE Corporate Tax, and statutory compliance**.
* Previous experience working with an **audit firm or in an external/internal audit environment is highly preferred**.
* Strong ERP experience with advanced knowledge of **Microsoft Excel and financial reporting systems**.
* Excellent analytical, commercial, leadership, negotiation, and decision\-making skills.
* Strong communication and stakeholder\-management capabilities.
* Ability to work effectively with senior management, project teams, banks, auditors, and external stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications

* Professional qualification such as **CA, ACCA, CPA, CMA, CFA, or equivalent**.
* Experience in a **Group Finance / Multi\-Company environment**.
* Experience within a large\-scale contracting, construction, architectural fit\-out, aluminium, façade, or engineering organization.
* Strong knowledge of UAE banking practices and construction\-sector financial requirements.
* Experience with ERP implementation, financial process improvement, or finance transformation initiatives.
* **Immediate joiners or candidates with a short notice period will be preferred.**

Key Competencies

* Budgeting \& Forecasting
