**Job Title: Finance Manager** 




**Location: Dubai, UAE** 




**Salary: AED 27,000 – 30,000 per month** 




**Reporting To: Finance Director** 




  





**Overview** 




 A leading international design studio with a strong presence in the Middle East is seeking an experienced Finance \& Operations Manager to join its Dubai office. This is a senior, hands\-on role responsible for overseeing financial management, operational performance, and business reporting across the regional business.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 




  





**Finance \& Reporting** 



* Prepare and manage monthly management accounts and financial reports.
* Produce accurate statistics, forecasts, and performance reporting for senior management.
* Support budgeting, cash flow management, and financial planning.
* Ensure financial compliance with internal and external standards.
* Liaise closely with the London Finance Director on reporting and controls.



  





**Payroll \& Team Management** 



* Oversee payroll processes, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
* Manage and develop a team of four finance/operations professionals.
* Implement best practice systems and procedures across the team.



  





**Operations Management** 



* Oversee day\-to\-day operational functions of the Dubai office.
* Support business efficiency across departments including design, project delivery, and administration.
* Manage supplier relationships, contracts, and operational costs.
* Contribute to process improvement and business optimisation initiatives.



  





**Business Support \& Strategy** 



* Provide commercial insight and data\-driven recommendations to leadership.
* Support senior management with financial and operational decision\-making.
* Assist with regional and group\-wide initiatives as required.



  





**Candidate Profile** 



* Proven experience in a Finance Manager, Financial Controller, or Finance \& Operations Manager role.
* Strong background in management accounting and reporting.
* Experience managing payroll and leading small teams.
* Previous experience in a design, architecture, construction, or professional services environment is preferred.
* Strong organisational, analytical, and communication skills.
* Comfortable working with international stakeholders.
* Experience in the Middle East is advantageous but not essential.
