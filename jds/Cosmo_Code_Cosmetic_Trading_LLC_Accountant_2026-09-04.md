WE ARE HIRING – ACCOUNTANT

**Company:** Cosmo Code Cosmetics Trading LLC  
**Location:** Office 504, Al Attar Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, UAE  
**Job Type:** Full\-Time  
**Preferred Candidate:** Female

Cosmo Code Cosmetics Trading LLC is looking for a reliable, organized, and experienced **Accountant** to join our team in Dubai and take responsibility for the company's day\-to\-day accounting and financial activities.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage the company's accounting activities using **Zoho Books**
* Maintain accurate and up\-to\-date bookkeeping and accounting records
* Record and categorize daily financial transactions in Zoho Books
* Manage accounts payable and accounts receivable
* Prepare and issue invoices, credit notes, receipts, and payment records
* Perform regular bank reconciliations
* Manage petty cash and company expense records
* Monitor customer outstanding balances and follow up on receivables
* Monitor supplier invoices and payment schedules
* Prepare monthly financial reports for management
* Maintain proper filing of invoices, receipts, bank records, and supporting documents
* Handle UAE VAT calculations, documentation, and filing
* Assist with UAE Corporate Tax accounting and documentation
* Monitor company expenses and cash flow
* Prepare accounting reports and supporting documents for management, auditors, banks, and government authorities when required
* Ensure all transactions and supporting documents are properly recorded and maintained in **Zoho Books**
* Handle other accounting and finance\-related responsibilities as required by management

Candidate Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field
* Previous accounting experience is required
* Good working experience with Zoho Books
* UAE accounting experience is highly preferred
* Good knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax requirements
* Strong Microsoft Excel skills
* Good understanding of the complete accounting cycle
* Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy
* Good organizational and documentation skills
* Ability to work independently and take responsibility for the company's accounting records
* Good written and verbal communication skills
* Experience in a trading, cosmetics, medical/aesthetic products, or distribution company would be an advantage
