## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an experienced ( minimum 5 years’ experience ) and detail\-oriented Accountant to join our medical center in Abu Dhabi.

Key Responsibilities:

\* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and financial transactions.

\* Prepare and maintain accurate financial records, reports, reconciliations, and statements.

\* Handle VAT, tax compliance, and related documentation in accordance with UAE requirements.

\* Coordinate and support internal and external audits, ensuring all required documents and records are available.

\* Prepare financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and variance reports.

\* Perform clinical costing and healthcare service cost analysis.

\* Monitor revenue, expenses, receivables, payables, and cash flow.

\* Review and reconcile insurance\-related financial transactions and healthcare revenue where applicable.

\* Coordinate with management, auditors, banks, suppliers, and other stakeholders.

\* Ensure compliance with company financial policies and applicable UAE regulations.

\* Identify discrepancies and implement appropriate corrective actions.

\* Support management with financial insights and recommendations for cost control and operational efficiency.

Requirements:

\* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.

\* Minimum 5 years of accounting experience, preferably within a medical center, clinic, hospital, or healthcare organization in Abu Dhabi/UAE.

\* Strong experience in UAE VAT and tax requirements.

\* Proven experience dealing with audits and financial compliance.

\* Knowledge of clinical costing / healthcare costing is highly preferred.

\* Experience with healthcare revenue cycles, insurance claims, and medical billing is an advantage.

\* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.

\* Proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel.

\* Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.

\* Ability to work independently, meet deadlines, and maintain strict confidentiality.
