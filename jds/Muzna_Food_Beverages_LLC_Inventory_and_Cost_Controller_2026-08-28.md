## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Core Responsibilities** :

**1\. Inventory and Stock Control**

* Monitor inventory levels of food, beverages and operational supplies across outlets and warehouse.
* Conduct periodic stock counts (monthly or quarterly) and reconcile physical stock with system records.
* Identify and report stock variances, wastage, shrinkage or potential losses.

2\. **Product Costing and Margin Monitoring**

* Calculate and monitor food cost and beverage cost percentages.
* Track changes in ingredient costs and assess impact on product margins.
* Support menu pricing reviews based on cost analysis.
* Maintain recipe costing and ensure portion control standards are followed.

3**. Variance Analysis and Cost Monitoring**

* Monitor supplier price changes and maintain standard cost records for raw materials.
* Review purchasing data to ensure cost efficiency and compliance with approved suppliers.
* Compare standard costs with actual consumption and identify variances.
* Analyze reasons for cost fluctuations and operational inefficiencies such as wastage or over\-portioning.

**5\. Reporting and Financial Analysis**

* Prepare weekly and monthly cost control reports including food cost, beverage cost and operational cost analysis.
* Provide insights and recommendations to management to support decision making.

**6\. Internal Controls and Process Improvement**

* Ensure proper documentation and control over purchases and inventory movements.
* Identify opportunities to strengthen cost control processes and reduce operational cost leakage.

**7\. Cross\-Functional Coordination**

* Work closely with outlets, operations, procurement, IT and finance teams to ensure compliance with costing standards and purchasing policies.
* Support internal and external audits related to inventory and cost controls.

**Requirements:**

* Minimum 3 years of experience in Inventory or Stock Management; F\&B / Retail or warehouse experience preferred.
* High school diploma or Bachelor’s in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or related field.
