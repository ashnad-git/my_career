## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Position:** Accountant  
**Industry:** Property Management / Real Estate  
**Location:** UAE  
**Experience Required:** 3–4 Years  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Reporting To:** Finance Manager / Projects Manager

Job Summary

We are seeking a qualified and detail\-oriented **Accountant with 3–4 years of UAE experience**, preferably within a **property management or real estate company managing owner\-owned properties**.

The Accountant will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records for the property portfolio, including **rental income, tenant accounts, security deposits, property\-related expenses, supplier payments, bank reconciliations, VAT compliance, and management reporting**. The role requires strong knowledge of UAE accounting practices and the ability to maintain **property\-wise financial records and profitability reports** for management and the property owner.

Key Responsibilities

* Maintain complete books of accounts and day\-to\-day accounting transactions for the company's property portfolio.
* Record and monitor **rental income, tenant payments, outstanding receivables, security deposits, and other property\-related income**.
* Maintain **property\-wise and unit\-wise accounting records** to ensure accurate allocation of income and expenses.
* Prepare and issue tenant invoices, receipts, credit notes, and account statements as required.
* Monitor rental receivables and coordinate with the property management/leasing team regarding overdue payments and collections.
* Record and reconcile **tenant security deposits** and ensure accurate tracking of refundable amounts.
* Process supplier invoices and payments relating to maintenance, utilities, service contracts, repairs, and other property expenses.
* Verify invoices and supporting documents before processing payments.
* Perform **bank reconciliations** and maintain accurate cash and bank records.
* Maintain accounts payable and accounts receivable ledgers and perform regular reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly **property\-wise income and expense reports**, including rental collection and outstanding receivables reports.
* Prepare **Profit \& Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and other management reports** as required.
* Assist management/property owner with **property profitability, operating cost, rental income, and cash flow analysis**.
* Handle **UAE VAT accounting**, including input/output VAT reconciliation and preparation of supporting schedules for VAT return filing.
* Assist with **UAE Corporate Tax accounting, documentation, and compliance** in coordination with management, auditors, or tax consultants.
* Maintain fixed asset registers and depreciation schedules where applicable.
* Maintain proper accounting records for **maintenance charges, utilities, service charges, property\-related government fees, and other operating expenses**.
* Assist in annual budgeting and comparison of actual property expenses against budget.
* Coordinate with external auditors and provide schedules, reconciliations, invoices, contracts, and other supporting documentation for audits.
* Ensure proper filing and maintenance of accounting documents in accordance with UAE regulatory and company requirements.
* Coordinate with leasing, property management, maintenance, and administration teams to resolve tenant and property accounting matters.
* Perform other accounting and financial duties assigned by management.

Qualifications \& Experience

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum **3–4 years of relevant accounting experience in the UAE**.
* Previous experience in a **property management, real estate, or property\-owning company** is strongly preferred.
* Good understanding of **rental accounting, tenant ledgers, security deposits, property expenses, and supplier accounts**.
* Working knowledge of **UAE VAT and Corporate Tax requirements**.
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Experience using accounting software such as **Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, ERP systems, or similar platforms**.
* Strong **Microsoft Excel** skills, including formulas, reconciliations, and financial reporting.
* Good written and verbal communication skills in English.

Key Skills \& Competencies

* Property\-wise accounting and reporting
* Rental income and receivables management
* Tenant ledger reconciliation
* Security deposit accounting
* Accounts payable and supplier reconciliation
* Bank reconciliation
* UAE VAT and Corporate Tax knowledge
* Month\-end closing and financial reporting
* Budgeting and expense monitoring
* Strong Excel and accounting software skills
* High attention to detail and accuracy
* Good organizational and time\-management skills
* Ability to maintain confidentiality of financial and property information
* Ability to work independently and coordinate directly with management/property owner

Preferred Candidate

The ideal candidate will have hands\-on experience handling the **complete accounting cycle for an owner\-managed property portfolio in the UAE** and be capable of independently preparing accurate property\-level financial reports for management decision\-making.
