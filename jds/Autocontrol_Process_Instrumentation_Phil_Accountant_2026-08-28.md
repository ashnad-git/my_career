## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are seeking a detail\-oriented and reliable **Accountant** to join our team in the UAE. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, processing accounting transactions, preparing financial reports, and supporting the company's overall financial operations.

Key Responsibilities

* Record and maintain accurate accounting transactions and financial records.
* Prepare and process invoices, receipts, payments, and other accounting documents.
* Monitor accounts payable and accounts receivable.
* Perform bank and account reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and accounting schedules.
* Assist in the preparation of budgets, cash flow reports, and financial analysis.
* Monitor and maintain proper documentation for all financial transactions.
* Assist with month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Coordinate with internal departments regarding invoices, expenses, and payments.
* Ensure accounting records are complete, accurate, and properly maintained.
* Assist with tax and other financial compliance requirements applicable in the UAE.
* Support external auditors and provide required financial documents when necessary.
* Maintain confidentiality of financial and company information.
* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties as assigned.

Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in Accountancy, Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **2–5\+ years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably in the UAE or GCC.
* Strong knowledge of basic accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Experience with accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliation, and general ledger.
* Familiarity with UAE VAT and other applicable UAE tax/compliance requirements is an advantage.
* Proficient in Microsoft Excel and other Microsoft Office applications.
* Experience with accounting software or ERP systems is preferred.
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Good written and verbal communication skills in English.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
