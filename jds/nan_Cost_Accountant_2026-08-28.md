**About the Role**

We are seeking a detail\-oriented and analytical **Cost Accountant** responsible for monitoring costs, analyzing financial data, supporting budgeting activities, and providing actionable insights to improve operational efficiency and profitability across the Group.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Prepare and maintain product, project, and operational cost records.
* Analyze cost variances and identify opportunities for cost optimization.
* Develop and maintain standard costing models and cost allocation methodologies.
* Monitor inventory valuation and reconciliation in coordination with operations.
* Prepare monthly cost reports, margin analysis, and management dashboards.
* Assist in budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning activities.
* Support month\-end and year\-end closing processes related to cost accounting.

**Requirements**

* Minimum **2 years of experience** in cost accounting.
* Strong understanding of cost accounting principles, inventory accounting, and financial reporting.
* Proficiency in Advanced Excel and ERP Systems.
* Excellent analytical, numerical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Strong communication and interpersonal abilities.
* Ability to work in a fast\-paced, multicultural environment and manage multiple priorities.
* Knowledge of IFRS and internal control frameworks.
