## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**F\&B Accountant (Food \& Beverage)**

We are seeking a detail\-oriented and analytical **F\&B Accountant** to manage our financial records, monitor operational costs, and streamline our restaurant accounting procedures. In this role, you will oversee day\-to\-day transaction matching, handle supplier distributions, manage local tax filings (VAT), and audit point\-of\-sale (POS) systems across our multi\-branch operations. The ideal candidate has strong expertise in cost control, inventory reconciliation, and food industry accounting.

**Key Responsibilities**

* **Daily Bookkeeping**: Process daily point\-of\-sale (POS) transactions, cash collections, and third\-party delivery platform settlements.
* **Cost Control**: Conduct food and beverage cost analysis to calculate variance percentages and waste metrics.
* **Inventory Management**: Reconcile monthly physical stock takes against system balances to identify variances.
* **Accounts Payable**: Verify supplier invoices, reconcile vendor statements, and manage corporate petty cash allocations.
* **Tax Compliance**: Prepare and submit accurate monthly or quarterly local value\-added tax (VAT) filings.
* **Financial Reporting**: Assist in generating Profit \& Loss (P\&L) statements, cash flow charts, and balance sheets.
* **Payroll Processing**: Execute staff monthly salary disbursements and expense report reimbursements.

**Required Qualifications**

* **Education**: Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a business\-related field.
* **Experience**: Minimum of 2 to 4 years of proven accounting experience explicitly within the F\&B, hospitality, or restaurant industry.
* **Software Skills**: Hands\-on proficiency with specialized accounting ERP programs (e.g., Odoo, Zoho Books, SAP) and restaurant POS management systems.
* **Core Skills**: Exceptional spreadsheet management skills (Advanced Microsoft Excel) and strong analytical math capabilities.
* **Compliance Knowledge**: Complete familiarity with local financial regulatory codes and business tax structures.

**Benefits \& Compensation**

* Competitive monthly salary package (dependent on background and certifications).
* Comprehensive medical insurance coverage.
* Annual paid leave with travel allowance options.
* Employee dining discounts across our venues.
