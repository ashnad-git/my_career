About the Role

We are looking for a **detail\-oriented and experienced Accountant** to join our Finance \& Accounts team in Dubai. The candidate will be responsible for managing day\-to\-day accounting operations, maintaining accurate financial records, handling receivables and payables, preparing financial reports, and ensuring compliance with UAE VAT and other applicable regulations.

The ideal candidate should have strong practical accounting experience, excellent knowledge of **UAE VAT, bank reconciliation, AR/AP, payroll accounting, and financial reporting**, along with good proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel.

Key Responsibilities

* Maintain complete and accurate accounting records and ledgers.
* Record daily sales, purchases, receipts, payments, expenses, and journal entries.
* Manage **Accounts Receivable (AR)**, including customer invoicing, receipts, outstanding balances, and collection follow\-ups.
* Manage **Accounts Payable (AP)**, including supplier invoices, payment schedules, and supplier reconciliations.
* Prepare and monitor **Statement of Accounts (SOA)** for customers and suppliers.
* Perform regular **bank, customer, supplier, and ledger reconciliations**.
* Prepare monthly **Profit \& Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and management reports**.
* Monitor company cash flow, petty cash, and daily cash transactions.
* Prepare supporting schedules for month\-end and year\-end closing.
* Assist with **UAE VAT return preparation and FTA compliance**.
* Maintain proper accounting documentation and filing systems.
* Assist with **Corporate Tax** requirements and supporting documentation.
* Coordinate with external auditors and provide required financial records and schedules.
* Handle payroll\-related accounting entries and coordinate with HR/WPS when required.
* Maintain fixed asset records, depreciation schedules, accruals, and prepayments.
* Identify accounting discrepancies and take corrective action.
* Provide financial information and reports to management whenever required.
* Ensure all accounting transactions are recorded accurately and on time.

Requirements

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* Minimum 2\-**3 years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably in the UAE.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE VAT regulations and accounting practices**.
* Good understanding of **Corporate Tax** is an advantage.
* Strong experience in **AR, AP, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting**.
* Proficiency in **Microsoft Excel**, including formulas, Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, and reporting.
* Experience with **Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally, or similar accounting software**.
* Good knowledge of general accounting principles and financial controls.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good written and verbal communication skills in English.
* Ability to work independently, manage multiple tasks, and meet deadlines.
* UAE experience is preferred.

Preferred Qualifications

* ACCA / CMA / CA or partially qualified candidates will be an advantage.
* Experience in the **UAE service, facilities management, cleaning, hospitality, or similar industry** is preferred.
* Experience with accounting system migration or ERP implementation is an advantage.
* Immediate joiners are preferred.

What We Offer

* Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications.
* Professional and supportive working environment.
* Opportunity for career growth and development.
* Exposure to complete accounting operations and UAE compliance.
