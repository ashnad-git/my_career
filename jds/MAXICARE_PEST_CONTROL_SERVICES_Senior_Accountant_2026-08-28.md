## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Summary**

We are seeking an experienced and detail\-oriented Senior Accountant to lead general ledger operations, manage month\-end close procedures, and ensure compliance with GAAP/IFRS standards. In this role, you will perform complex reconciliations, conduct financial variance analysis, support annual audit preparation, and help streamline internal accounting workflows.

**Key Responsibilities**

* **General Ledger \& Month\-End Close:** Oversee the month\-end and year\-end close processes; prepare and review complex journal entries, accruals, and balance sheet reconciliations.
* **Financial Reporting \& Analysis:** Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements; perform budget\-to\-actual variance analysis and provide actionable insights to management.
* **Compliance \& Technical Accounting:** Ensure all financial practices comply with GAAP/IFRS, relevant tax laws (VAT, corporate tax), and regulatory guidelines.
* **Audit \& Tax Coordination:** Prepare audit workpapers and act as a primary liaison for internal and external auditors and tax advisors.
* **Cash Flow \& Asset Management:** Monitor cash flow, oversee fixed asset schedules, and maintain revenue recognition and depreciation schedules.
* **Process Improvement \& Mentorship:** Identify inefficiencies to automate workflows and provide guidance and review for junior and staff accountants.
