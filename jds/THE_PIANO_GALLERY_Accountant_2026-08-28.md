## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a **mid\-level Accountant** with a minimum of **4 years of relevant UAE experience**, preferably within the **retail industry**, to join our growing finance team.

The ideal candidate will have strong hands\-on experience in **day\-to\-day accounting, VAT and tax compliance, reconciliations, reporting, and financial documentation**, with a good understanding of the operational requirements of a retail business.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting activities, including **accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, and journal entries**.
* Maintain accurate financial records and ensure accounting transactions are properly recorded.
* Perform **bank, supplier, customer, and general ledger reconciliations**.
* Handle **VAT accounting, VAT returns, and UAE tax\-related requirements** in coordination with internal teams and external consultants.
* Ensure compliance with applicable **UAE tax and accounting regulations**.
* Assist with monthly, quarterly, and annual **financial closing and reporting**.
* Prepare financial statements, management reports, schedules, and supporting documentation.
* Monitor invoices, payments, receipts, expenses, and outstanding balances.
* Manage supplier and customer accounts and follow up on discrepancies and outstanding payments.
* Support **budget preparation, expense monitoring, and variance analysis**.
* Assist with cash flow monitoring and financial planning.
* Coordinate with auditors and provide required financial records and supporting documents.
* Maintain proper documentation for audits and ensure financial records are organized and up to date.
* Assist in identifying accounting discrepancies and resolving financial issues promptly.
* Support management with financial analysis and other accounting\-related requirements as needed.

Retail Accounting Responsibilities

* Handle accounting requirements related to **multiple retail outlets/stores**, where applicable.
* Reconcile **daily sales, cash, card, online, and other payment collections**.
* Monitor store\-level revenue and expenses.
* Reconcile POS reports with accounting records and investigate discrepancies.
* Track inventory\-related accounting entries and coordinate with operations/procurement teams.
* Assist with **stock valuation, inventory reconciliation, and cost\-of\-goods analysis**.
* Monitor retail expenses and support management with store\-level financial reporting.

Requirements

* **Minimum 4 years of accounting experience in the UAE**.
* Strong preference for candidates with **retail accounting experience**.
* Good practical knowledge of **UAE VAT and tax requirements**.
* Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Hands\-on experience with **reconciliations, month\-end closing, AP/AR, GL, and financial reporting**.
* Experience working with **POS systems, inventory, and retail sales reconciliation** is highly preferred.
* Good knowledge of accounting software/ERP systems and **MS Excel**.
* Strong analytical and numerical skills with excellent attention to detail.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines in a fast\-paced environment.
* Strong communication and coordination skills.

Pay: AED5,000\.00 \- AED6,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
