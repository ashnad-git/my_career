## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Summary**

**We are looking for an experienced Senior Accountant with 10–20 years of accounting experience, preferably within MEP contracting, construction, or engineering companies in the UAE. The candidate will be responsible for managing the company’s accounting operations, financial reporting, project accounting, cash flow, payables/receivables, and compliance with UAE accounting and tax requirements.**

**Key Responsibilities**

* **Manage day\-to\-day accounting operations and maintain accurate financial records.**
* **Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.**
* **Handle Accounts Payable (AP) and Accounts Receivable (AR), including supplier and customer reconciliations.**
* **Monitor project\-related costs, expenses, revenue, and profitability.**
* **Prepare and monitor project budgets, cost reports, and variance analysis.**
* **Coordinate with the procurement, commercial, project, and operations teams regarding invoices, LPOs, subcontractor payments, and project costs.**
* **Review supplier invoices, subcontractor bills, payment certificates, and supporting documents.**
* **Monitor customer collections, outstanding receivables, retention amounts, and project\-related payments.**
* **Prepare cash flow forecasts and monitor company cash positions.**
* **Manage bank reconciliations, petty cash, journal entries, accruals, and prepayments.**
* **Ensure proper accounting treatment for MEP projects, materials, manpower, subcontractors, equipment, and project expenses.**
* **Assist with project costing and profitability analysis.**
* **Ensure compliance with UAE VAT, Corporate Tax, and other applicable UAE financial requirements.**
* **Coordinate with external auditors and provide required financial schedules and supporting documents.**
* **Assist in preparing budgets, forecasts, and financial analysis for management.**
* **Maintain proper documentation and accounting records in accordance with company policies and applicable accounting standards.**
* **Review and improve internal accounting controls and procedures.**
* **Supervise and guide junior accountants and accounting staff.**
* **Perform other accounting and finance duties assigned by management.**

**Required Qualifications \& Experience**

* **Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.**
* **10–20 years of accounting experience, with substantial experience in the UAE.**
* **Previous experience in MEP contracting, construction, engineering, or project\-based companies is highly preferred.**
* **Strong knowledge of project accounting and construction/contracting accounting.**
* **Good knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax requirements.**
* **Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.**
* **Experience with accounting/ERP software such as Tally, SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, or similar systems.**
* **Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Excel.**
* **Experience dealing with suppliers, customers, subcontractors, auditors, and banks.**
* **Strong analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.**
* **Ability to work independently and meet strict reporting deadlines.**
* **Strong communication and interpersonal skills.**

**Preferred Candidate Profile**

* **10–20 years total accounting experience**
* **Minimum 15\+ years UAE experience**
* **MEP/construction contracting background**
* **Strong understanding of project costing and profitability**
* **UAE VAT \& Corporate Tax knowledge**
* **Experience handling subcontractor and supplier accounts**
* **Ability to supervise accounting staff**

***Candidates currently in UAE and ready to join immediately are preferred. Kindly email your CV with a Position in the subject line including your salary expectation to: wartung.secretary@gmail.com***
