## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Role Overview**
We are looking for an experienced Finance Manager to lead financial planning, accounting, auditing, budgeting, costing, and financial control activities for a manufacturing organization. The ideal candidate will have strong manufacturing finance experience, excellent analytical skills, and hands\-on expertise in ERP systems.  

**Key Responsibilities:****Financial Management \& Reporting:*** Manage day\-to\-day finance and accounting operations.
* Prepare and review monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and management reports.
* Ensure accurate and timely financial reporting in accordance with applicable accounting standards and company policies.
* Monitor cash flow, working capital, receivables, payables, and overall financial performance.
* Provide financial analysis and insights to support management decision\-making.

**Budgeting \& Financial Planning:*** Lead the annual budgeting and forecasting process.
* Develop budgets for operating expenses, capital expenditure, production, and working capital.
* Monitor actual performance against budgets and analyze variances.
* Prepare financial forecasts and recommend corrective actions where required.

**Costing \& Manufacturing Finance:*** Manage product costing, standard costing, and cost analysis.
* Analyze manufacturing costs, material consumption, labor costs, overheads, and production variances.
* Partner with production and operations teams to identify cost\-saving and efficiency opportunities.
* Monitor inventory valuation, stock movements, wastage, and production\-related / financial metrics.
* Support pricing and profitability analysis for products.

**Audit \& Compliance:*** Coordinate internal and external financial audits.
* Ensure compliance with statutory, tax, accounting, and financial control requirements.
* Strengthen internal controls and identify areas of financial risk.
* Address audit observations and ensure timely closure of corrective actions.

**ERP \& Process Improvement:*** Manage and utilize ERP systems for financial reporting, accounting, costing, inventory, and procurement processes.
* Ensure accuracy and integrity of financial data within the ERP environment.
* Work with IT and business teams on ERP enhancements, process automation, and system improvements.
* Identify opportunities to streamline finance processes and improve reporting efficiency.

**Team Leadership:*** Lead, mentor, and develop the finance and accounting team.
* Establish clear processes, controls, and performance expectations.
* Collaborate closely with Operations, Procurement, Sales, HR, and senior management.

  

**Key Skills \& Competencies:*** Strong knowledge of financial accounting, auditing, budgeting, costing, and financial controls.
* Proven experience in manufacturing finance.
* Strong understanding of inventory and product costing.
* Excellent financial analysis and problem\-solving skills.
* Hands\-on experience with ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, or equivalent.
* Strong Excel and financial reporting skills.
* Excellent communication, leadership, and stakeholder\-management abilities.
* High attention to detail, integrity, and commercial acumen.

  

**Preferred Candidate Profile:*** 12\+ years of overall finance/accounting experience, preferably within a manufacturing environment.
* CA Finalist is mandatory.
* Strong exposure to manufacturing costing, budgeting, audits, and financial reporting.
* Experience managing finance teams and working directly with senior leadership.
* Proven ability to improve financial controls, processes, and cost efficiency.
