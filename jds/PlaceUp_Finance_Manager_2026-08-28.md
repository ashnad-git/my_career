## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Tasks and Responsibilities – Finance Manager / Senior Accountant:**

* Managing and overseeing the Finance/Accounting team consisting of 2other resources
* Preparation of monthly and annual closing of profit and loss account \& balance sheet
* Monitoring receivables and payables
* Preparing regular MIS analysis and reports for the management
* Overseeing the passing of various GL entries
* Liaison with customers \& suppliers for the reconciliations of accounts
* Passing of monthly depreciation, amortization \& deduction entries
* Running and checking of trial balance
* Vouching and proper allocation of receipts in order to generate debtors ageing
* Preparation of audit schedules
* Verification and vouching of payments to Suppliers
* Monthly Bank reconciliations \& liaison with the bank when required
* Preparation and submission of quarterly VAT filings and corporate Tax
* Other ad\-hoc reports

 **Desired Profile:**

* Candidate must be already in the UAE with at least 2 years UAE related experience
* Ideally, 5 years of relevant accounting experience up to finalization and closing of the accounts
* Internationally recognized accounting qualification e.g. ACCA, CA, CPA, CMA or similar
* Have previous experience cooperating with auditors for submission of annual financials for audit
* Knowledgeable of VAT and corporate tax regulations and practices
* Self\-starter and works with minimum supervision
* Fluent English
* Previous experience with accounting software (e.g. Peachtree, Tally, QuickBooks, Xero or similar)
* Proficient in MS\-Excel
