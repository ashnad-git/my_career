## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are actively searching for a
 **Financial Reporting Senior Manager** 
 to join our Finance Team in Abu Dhabi, reporting directly to the Chief Financial Officer.
 



  





  





**MAJOR DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIE** 



* Establish and maintain internal controls and guidelines for accounting transactions
* Responsible to prepare business activity reports, financial forecasts, and assisting in preparation of annual budgets. This includes monthly management reporting deliverables on consolidated \& standalone basis and yearly financial statements and developing insightful analysis and reporting to support decision making and monitor performance
* Ensure the accuracy of financial data through supervising and maintaining the Company’s General Ledger accounts and ensure that all Sub\-ledgers are reconciled and are well maintained
* Responsible for Monthly and Annual Closing Processes, ensure all required accounting transactions are booked properly at the end of each period, and perform period\-end account closing activities and reconciliations
* Responsible to produce periodic financial reports; ensures that the reported results comply with IFRS and financial reporting standards
* Responsible for tax planning throughout the fiscal year; files periodical tax return
* Ensure compliance with local regulations; coordinates with external and internal auditors and provides needed information for the audit
* Present recommendations to management on short\- and long\-term financial objectives and policies
* Provide financial analysis with an emphasis on capital investments, pricing decisions, and contract negotiations
* Perform other related duties as necessary or assigned
* Prepare cost allocation models and recording appropriate entries
* Prepare and maintain revenue models in line with applicable IFRS and recording appropriate entries
* Perform ad hoc financial analysis and perform root cause investigations, as requested by management
* Analyse and interpreting financial information and data and providing concise explanations on trends and variances
* Serve as a subject matter expert on IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards)
* Adhere to standard accounting standards and Company procedures



  





**.** 




**MINIMUM QUALIFICATIO** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Finance or Accounting from a reputed University
* CPA or CA or its equivalent
* 10\+ years of experience in the same capacity
* Preferably experience in Real Estate organizations or reputed Audit Firms
* Proficiency in using ERP systems (Oracle)



  





**REQUIRED SKILLS/ABILITIES** 



* Excellent management and supervisory skills
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills
* Excellent organizational, time management skills and attention to detail
* Proficient in accounting and tax preparation software
* Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite or similar software
* Proficient in excel and building financial models
* Strong understanding of IFRS
* Excellent practical knowledge of accounting/bookkeeping
* Highly organized and manage multiple priorities and assignments at the same time
* Ability to solve complex accounting problems and errors
* Great Team player
