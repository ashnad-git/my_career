**About the Role**


The American University of Ras Al Khaimah (AURAK) is seeking a detail\-oriented and analytical Financial Analyst – Budgeting \& Reporting to join its Office of Finance.


This role will support financial reporting, accounting, budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis, while helping strengthen the University’s financial systems, reporting tools, and data\-driven decision\-making.


**Key Responsibilities**


* Support accounting, financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting and financial analysis activities.
* Ensure accuracy, completeness and timely availability of financial information for management and decision\-making.
* Support the effective implementation, adoption and ongoing enhancement of ERP\-enabled finance processes, including Oracle and Banner.
* Contribute to process improvements, system controls, workflow enhancements and standardization of Finance processes within the ERP environment.
* Develop, maintain and utilize financial dashboards, reports and analytical tools to support management reporting and performance monitoring.
* Monitor financial data and transactions across relevant systems, identify discrepancies or trends, and coordinate timely resolution.
* Support budgetary controls, expenditure monitoring, forecasting and analysis against approved plans.
* Provide Finance\-related support for system changes, testing, reporting requirements, data validation and user requirements.
