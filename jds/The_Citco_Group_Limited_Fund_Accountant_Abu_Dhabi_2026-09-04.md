**About The Team \& Business Line**
 Fund Administration is Citco’s core business, and our alternative asset and accounting service is one of the industry’s most respected. Our continuous investment in learning and technology solutions means our people are equipped to deliver a seamless client experience.
   

  

**Your Role**
* Prepare periodical financial reporting for our Institutional clients, including the determination of "Net Asset Value" and prepare the Statement of Asset and Liabilities and Profit and Loss Statement
* Execute the financial control elements covering assets, liabilities, income and expenses, and equity
* Independently complete cash, portfolio and capital reconciliations and investigate and resolve any discrepancies
* Review and process notices received for capital calls and distributions on underlying portfolio Fund holdings
* Maintain contact with client operations staff, Banks, Custodians and Brokers
* Monitor compliance with various client service agreements
* Assist with new client onboarding and new launches to successful completion
* Participate in the fiscal year end audit process and provide information to auditors as required
* Liaise with internal and external parties to ensure that client needs are met
* Assist with ad hoc projects


**About You**
* University degree in accounting, finance, economics or professional accounting designation (CFA, CGA, CMA, CA, CPA)
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills
* Proficient with Microsoft Office (especially Excel) and Outlook
* Proven ability to work in a challenging, professional, and fast\-paced work environment
* Experience working in the financial industry or public accounting is an advantage
