Job Overview

We are seeking a qualified and responsible **Accountant** to join our school team. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records and supporting the school’s day\-to\-day financial operations.

Key Responsibilities

* Maintain accurate financial records, accounts, and transactions.
* Manage accounts payable and receivable, invoicing, and receipts.
* Assist with payroll preparation and financial documentation.
* Prepare financial statements, reports, and reconciliations.
* Monitor school fees, outstanding payments, and related records.
* Assist in budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning.
* Ensure compliance with applicable financial policies and regulations.
* Perform other accounting and finance\-related duties as assigned.

Qualifications \& Skills

* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* Previous experience as an Accountant, preferably in a school or educational institution. (2 Years minimum)
* Good knowledge of **MS Office, especially Microsoft Excel**.
* Experience with accounting software is preferred.
* Strong numerical, organizational, and analytical skills.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills.
* Ability to maintain confidentiality and work effectively under pressure.

What We Offer

* Monthly salary of **AED 2,500–3,000**.
* Professional and supportive school environment.
* Opportunity to work within an established educational institution.
