**About the job**


Based at the Mandarin Oriental Downtown, Dubai within the Finance Department in Dubai, the Cost Controller is responsible for maintaining effective cost control across the Hotel and for ensuring the accuracy and integrity of inventories, purchasing prices, receiving, stores, product costing and related system interfaces. Cost Controller reports to the Chief Accountant.


**As a** **Cost Controller, you will be responsible for the following duties:**


* Monitor and analyze food, beverage, and inventory\-related costs against budgets and forecasts, investigate variances, prepare cost reports, and recommend actions to improve profitability and operational efficiency.
* Manage inventory accuracy by overseeing stock records, physical counts, reconciliations, par levels, inventory turnover, and initiatives to reduce wastage, excess stock, and obsolescence.
* Maintain the integrity of Check SCM, POS, and master data by ensuring accurate item setup, pricing, recipes, system interfaces, reconciliations, and reliable consumption and cost reporting.
* Support purchasing and supplier management through price validation, invoice verification, cost impact analysis, sourcing recommendations, and data\-driven support for negotiations and procurement decisions.
* Oversee receiving and stores operations, ensuring compliance with policies, accurate documentation and system entries, proper stock storage and rotation, inventory security, food safety standards, and resolution of discrepancies.
* Ensure compliance with accounting, procurement, inventory, and HACCP standards while managing month\-end activities, supporting audits, training and supervising team members, escalating control issues, and delivering ad hoc analysis and projects.

**As a** **Cost Controller, we expect from you:**


* Diploma or Degree in Hospitality Management, Accounting, Finance, or a related field, with a minimum of 3 years' experience in hotel cost control, preferably within a luxury hospitality environment.
* Strong expertise in Food \& Beverage cost control, inventory management, receiving processes, and procurement controls within hotel operations.
* Experience supervising receiving, stores, or finance\-related teams, with the ability to manage workflows, maintain controls, and support operational efficiency.
* Proficient in inventory and procurement systems, preferably Check SCM, with experience maintaining POS integrations and familiarity with SUN System or similar hotel accounting platforms.
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including the preparation of analytical reports, reconciliations, cost models, and data\-driven insights to support decision\-making.
* Excellent analytical, communication, organizational, and team management skills, with the ability to identify trends, control gaps, cost\-saving opportunities, and ensure compliance with food safety and HACCP
