Finance manager overview
The finance manager oversees the daily operations of the accounting function, ensuring the accuracy, integrity, and efficiency of financial processes. Key responsibilities include financial analysis and reporting, budgeting and forecasting, audit compliance, internal controls, asset and liability reconciliations, working capital management, and cash flow oversight.
The role supports the effective execution of general ledger activities and financial processes, providing guidance to internal stakeholders and ensuring a clear understanding of accounting and financial procedures. Core duties include account reconciliations, ledger management, financial reporting, and the investigation and resolution of discrepancies.
Additionally, the finance manager leads and supervises finance and accounting personnel, with responsibility for cost control, credit management, accounts payable, and the implementation of best practices to enhance financial performance and operational effectiveness.
