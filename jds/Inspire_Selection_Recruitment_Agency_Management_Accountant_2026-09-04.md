## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Management Accountant** 




**AED 10,500 plus medical \& 2 x annual air tickets for self** 




 Our client, a Global Operations and Logistics Consultancy in Dubai is looking to expand its accounting team and hire a Management Accountant to oversee its UAE and Bahrain Subsidiaries.
 



 This is a hands\-on, multi\-entity role responsible for managing the full accounting cycle across several Group subsidiary companies, with exposure to management accounts, month\-end and year\-end close, VAT, budgeting, reporting and financial controls.
 



  





**Responsibilities:** 



* Manage the full accounting cycle across multiple Group entities, including AP, AR and general ledger
* Create, record and allocate customer invoices and credit notes, and post customer receipts
* Perform bank reconciliations, process bank transfers and manage suspense accounts
* Lead month\-end activities including accruals, prepayments, deferred/accrued income, depreciation, fixed assets, petty cash and AP/AR reconciliations
* Prepare and review management accounts and financial reporting
* Support quarterly VAT/GST returns and liaise with local accountants and external advisors
* Coordinate year\-end audit requirements, including auditor liaison, bank confirmations and review of year\-end accounts
* Maintain financial analytics, cost centre/area reporting and company\-specific reporting requirements
* Support annual budgeting, forecasting and variance analysis
* Review and process employee expense claims and salary payments
* Maintain accurate and timely general ledger records and ensure appropriate financial controls are followed



  





**Requirements:** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related discipline, with 3\-5 years relevant management accounting experience
* Solid understanding of month\-end and year\-end closing processes
* Strong experience in management accounts and financial reporting
* Good knowledge of IFRS
* Understanding of tax and regulatory requirements
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills
