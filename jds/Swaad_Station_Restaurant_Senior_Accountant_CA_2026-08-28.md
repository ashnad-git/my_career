## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Senior Accountant (CA Qualified Or Inter) at Thomsun Group will be responsible for managing the company's financial accounting, reporting, and compliance. This role requires a highly skilled professional to oversee accounting processes, ensure adherence to tax and regulatory requirements, and provide strategic financial insights for decision\-making.

Location: Dubai, UAE

**Key Responsibilities**:

* Prepare and review financial statements, ensuring they comply with local, national, and international accounting standards.
* Ensure accurate and timely reporting of financial data to senior management, stakeholders, and regulatory bodies.
* Oversee tax planning and compliance with local tax regulations, including VAT, income tax, and other applicable taxes.
* Prepare and file tax returns and ensure timely payment of taxes.
* Keep up to date with changes in tax laws and regulations.
* Provide detailed financial analysis and reports to management to support decision\-making.
* Analyze variances in financial performance and recommend corrective actions.
* Monitor key financial metrics and implement controls to optimize financial performance.
* Maintain and monitor internal controls to safeguard company assets and ensure compliance with accounting policies.
* Lead the preparation of annual budgets and periodic forecasts.
* Work closely with departments to track expenditures and compare them to the budget, advising on necessary adjustments.
* Oversee the accounts payable and receivable functions, ensuring timely processing and resolution of outstanding payments.
* Manage cash flow to ensure optimal liquidity and financial health of the organization.
* Lead and mentor accountants, providing training and support to develop their skills.
* Supervise day\-to\-day accounting operations and ensure effective teamwork.
* Identify areas for process improvement and implement accounting system upgrades or new tools to streamline workflows.
* Ensure proper documentation and support for all financial transactions.

**Qualifications**:

* **Education**: Chartered Accountant (CA) certification Inter or Final
* **Experience**: Minimum of 5\+ years of accounting experience post qualification.
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles, financial reporting, and taxation.
* Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail.
* Proficiency in accounting software.
* Leadership and team management skills.
* Strong communication skills, both verbal and written.

Job Type: Full\-time
