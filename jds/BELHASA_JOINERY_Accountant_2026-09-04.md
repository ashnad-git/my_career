## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an **Accountant** with strong experience in the **construction and joinery industry**, particularly in **job costing and project costing**. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing financial operations, ensuring accurate cost tracking, and supporting project profitability.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage and oversee daily accounting operations.
* Handle **job costing and project costing** for ongoing projects.
* Monitor project budgets, costs, and profitability.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.
* Ensure proper allocation of costs to respective projects.
* Reconcile accounts, general ledger, and bank statements.
* Coordinate with Project Managers for cost control and financial planning.
* Ensure compliance with UAE financial regulations and company policies.
* Manage accounts payable, accounts receivable, and payroll processes.
* Assist with audits and prepare required documentation.
* Analyze financial data and provide financial insights to management.
* Monitor and support **collection of outstanding customer payments** and follow up on overdue receivables.

**Requirements**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Proven experience in the **construction / joinery industry is mandatory**.
* Strong knowledge of **job costing and project costing**.
* **Driving license is mandatory.**
* Strong experience in **accounts receivable and collection follow\-up**.
* Proficiency in accounting software and MS Excel.
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
* Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.

**Driving License:** Mandatory.

Work Location: In person
