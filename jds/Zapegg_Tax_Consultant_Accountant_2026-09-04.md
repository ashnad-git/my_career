## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Zapegg Tax Consultant is seeking a skilled and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to join its finance team in **Dubai**. This role is ideal for a finance professional with strong accounting knowledge who can manage financial transactions, maintain accurate records, prepare reports, and support tax\-related activities. The successful candidate will contribute to sound financial management and help ensure the company’s accounting operations remain accurate, organized, and compliant.


**Key Responsibilities:**


* Record and maintain daily financial transactions in accounting systems.
* Manage **accounts payable and accounts receivable** activities.
* Prepare invoices, receipts, payment vouchers, journal entries, and other financial documents.
* Maintain general ledger accounts and supporting financial records.
* Perform regular bank, customer, and supplier reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.
* Monitor outstanding receivables and coordinate payment follow\-ups.
* Review invoices, expenses, purchase documents, and payment requests for accuracy.
* Assist with budgeting, cash\-flow monitoring, and financial planning.
* Prepare and maintain **VAT and tax\-related documentation** in accordance with UAE requirements.
* Support internal and external audits by providing required accounting records.
* Coordinate with clients and internal departments regarding invoices, payments, and financial documentation.
* Ensure financial records are properly organized and securely maintained.
* Identify accounting discrepancies and assist in resolving financial issues promptly.
* Maintain strict confidentiality of company and client financial information.


**Requirements:**


* Bachelor’s degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce**, or a related field.
* 2–5 years of relevant accounting experience.
* Strong understanding of bookkeeping, financial reporting, reconciliations, and accounting principles.
* Experience with accounting software, ERP systems, and **Microsoft Excel**.
* Knowledge of UAE VAT and tax procedures is an advantage.
* Strong numerical, analytical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good communication and organizational abilities.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.


**Skills \& Competencies:**


* **Financial accounting and bookkeeping**
* Accounts payable and receivable management
* Bank reconciliation and financial reporting
* Invoice and payment processing
* VAT and tax documentation
* Microsoft Excel and accounting software
* Strong analytical and numerical skills
* Time management and organization
* Professional integrity and confidentiality


**Why Join Zapegg Tax Consultant?**



At Zapegg Tax Consultant, accurate financial management is an important part of delivering reliable tax and business consultancy services. This **Accountant** position in **Dubai** offers an excellent opportunity to strengthen your accounting and tax expertise, work alongside experienced professionals, and build a rewarding long\-term career within a professional and growing consultancy organization.
