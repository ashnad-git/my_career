## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Title:** BU Accountant

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting transactions for the assigned Business Unit.
* Prepare and post journal entries, accruals, provisions, and adjustments.
* Maintain accurate general ledger accounts and supporting schedules.
* Perform monthly bank, balance sheet, intercompany, customer, and supplier reconciliations.
* Support month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Prepare monthly financial reports, management reports, and variance analysis.
* Monitor accounts receivable and accounts payable and follow up on outstanding balances.
* Review invoices, expenses, purchase orders, and supporting documentation for accuracy and compliance.
* Ensure proper allocation of revenues, costs, and expenses to the relevant BU, projects, or cost centres.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning.
* Investigate financial variances and provide explanations to management.
* Maintain proper documentation and accounting records for audit purposes.
* Support internal and external audits and provide requested schedules and documentation.
* Perform other finance and accounting duties as assigned.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **2–5 years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably in a business\-unit or multi\-entity environment.
* Strong knowledge of general accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Experience with month\-end closing, reconciliations, accruals, and journal entries.
* Good Excel skills; experience with ERP systems
* Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills.
* High attention to detail and accuracy.
* Ability to manage deadlines and work independently.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills.

Work Location: In person
