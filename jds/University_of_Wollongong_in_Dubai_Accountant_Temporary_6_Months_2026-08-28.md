About the Role  

The University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD) is seeking a technically strong and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to join the Finance team on a **6\-month temporary contract**. This role has been created to provide additional support during a period of significant regulatory change and strategic finance projects, including VAT reforms, Corporate Tax developments, internal VAT audits, and the implementation of E\-Invoicing initiatives.


This is not a transactional accounting position. The successful candidate will act as a genuine working partner within the Finance team, taking ownership of the preparation and analysis of technical accounting outputs, enabling senior finance leaders to focus on oversight, compliance, and strategic delivery.

### **Key Responsibilities**


You will support a wide range of financial accounting, reporting, audit, tax, payroll, and finance automation activities, including:

* Preparing month\-end close entries, reconciliations, revenue schedules, and adjustment journals for review.
* Supporting year\-end financial reporting and audit preparation activities.
* Managing audit requests, supporting documentation, and draft responses to auditors.
* Assisting with VAT and Corporate Tax reconciliations and supporting compliance activities.
* Supporting payroll accounting and reconciliation processes.
* Contributing to finance process improvement and automation initiatives, including Robotic Process Automation (RPA) workflows.
* Maintaining finance process documentation and standard operating procedures.
* Assisting with student account and accounts receivable matters requiring technical accounting support.


Qualifications, Experience \& Skills  

We are looking for an accountant who combines strong technical capability with a proactive, solutions\-focused mindset.

**Essential Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline.
* 3\-5 years' experience in financial accounting, including month\-end close, reconciliations, and audit support activities.
* Working knowledge of VAT and tax compliance processes.
* Strong Excel skills and experience using accounting systems. Experience with Oracle is advantageous.
* Strong attention to detail and sound accounting fundamentals.

**Desirable**

* Part\-qualified or currently pursuing a professional accounting qualification (CPA, ACCA, CMA, or equivalent).
* Exposure to process automation, analytics, or Robotic Process Automation (RPA) tools

**Important Eligibility Requirement**

**This is a temporary 6\-month appointment and does not include visa sponsorship.** Candidates must already hold a **valid UAE residency visa** and be legally eligible to work in the UAE at the time of appointment.
