## Full Job Description (verbatim)

OFIXO Technical Services LLC is looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented **Accountant** to manage the company’s complete accounting and financial operations. The candidate should have strong practical knowledge of **UAE VAT, project accounting, bookkeeping, and financial reporting**.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and bookkeeping activities.
* Maintain complete and accurate company accounts and financial records.
* Handle **UAE VAT**, including VAT records, input/output VAT, VAT reconciliation, and VAT return preparation.
* Maintain **project\-wise accounts**, including project income, expenses, material costs, subcontractor costs, payments, and profitability.
* Prepare and record sales invoices, purchase invoices, receipts, expenses, and payments.
* Manage **Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable** and follow up on outstanding payments.
* Maintain supplier, customer, subcontractor, and project accounts.
* Perform regular bank and account reconciliations.
* Monitor project costs and ensure expenses are correctly allocated to the relevant projects.
* Prepare monthly **Profit \& Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and other financial reports**.
* Monitor company cash flow, expenses, and financial commitments.
* Handle month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Maintain proper documentation for **VAT, audits, and financial records**.
* Coordinate with management, suppliers, clients, auditors, and other parties when required.
* Ensure accounting records comply with applicable **UAE accounting and tax regulations**.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Proven experience as an **Accountant in the UAE**.
* Strong knowledge of **UAE VAT and accounting procedures**.
* Experience in **technical services, contracting, construction, maintenance, or project\-based companies** is preferred.
* Good understanding of **project costing and project\-wise accounting**.
* Proficiency in accounting software and MS Excel.
* Strong attention to detail and ability to manage accounts independently.
* Good communication, organizational, and analytical skills.

**Preferred Candidate:** An accountant with UAE experience who can independently handle **complete company accounts, VAT, project\-wise accounting, costing, receivables/payables, and financial reporting**.
