## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Title: Purchase Accountant – Aksara Gold \& Diamonds Jewellery

Industry: Gold \& Diamonds Jewellery

Job Summary

We are seeking a detail\-oriented Purchase Accountant to manage purchasing accounts, Generate and print barcode labels for gold,supplier transactions, inventory\-related accounting, and financial records for our gold and diamond jewellery business. The ideal candidate should have experience in jewellery accounting, stock reconciliation, and supplier payment processing.

Key Responsibilities

* Record and verify purchase invoices for gold, diamonds, gemstones, and jewellery materials.
* Maintain accurate purchase accounts and supplier ledgers.
* Coordinate with the purchasing and inventory teams regarding stock movements.
* Generate and manage barcode creation, printing, and tagging for jewellery items, ensuring accuracy in inventory tracking and stock control.
* Reconcile supplier statements and resolve discrepancies.
* Process supplier payments and monitor outstanding balances.
* Coordinate with the purchasing and inventory teams regarding stock movements.
* Prepare purchase reports and cost analyses.
* Assist in inventory valuation and stock reconciliation.
* Maintain proper documentation of purchase orders, invoices, and delivery notes.
* Ensure compliance with company policies and accounting standards.
* Support month\-end and year\-end closing activities.

Requirements

* Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum 2\-3 years of accounting experience, preferably in the gold and diamond jewellery industry.
* Knowledge of inventory accounting and stock reconciliation.
* Proficiency in MS Excel and accounting software/ERP systems.
* Strong analytical and organizational skills.
* Good communication and teamwork abilities.

Preferred Qualifications

* Experience working in a jewellery retail or wholesale business.
* Familiarity with gold, diamond, and precious stone inventory management.
* Knowledge of VAT regulations and financial reporting requirements.

Pay: AED3,000\.00 \- AED4,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
