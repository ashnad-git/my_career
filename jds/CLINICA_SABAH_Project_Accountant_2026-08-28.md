## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Project Accountant**

**United Contracting Company – Dubai, UAE**

**Job Summary:**

United Contracting Company is seeking a detail\-oriented and experienced **Project Accountant** to join our Finance team. The successful candidate will be responsible for monitoring project costs, preparing financial reports, supporting budgeting activities, and ensuring accurate accounting records for construction projects.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Monitor and control project costs and budgets.
* Prepare project cost reports and profitability analysis.
* Track project revenues, expenses, and work\-in\-progress (WIP).
* Process project\-related invoices, payments, and subcontractor accounts.
* Reconcile project accounts and ensure accurate financial records.
* Assist in preparing monthly financial statements and management reports.
* Coordinate with project managers to review project performance and cost variances.
* Support budgeting, forecasting, and cash flow planning.
* Ensure compliance with company policies and accounting standards.
* Assist with internal and external audits.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* Minimum **3–5 years of experience** as a Project Accountant, preferably in a contracting or construction company in the UAE.
* Strong knowledge of project costing, WIP accounting, budgeting, and financial reporting.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and ERP accounting systems.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.

Work Location: In person
