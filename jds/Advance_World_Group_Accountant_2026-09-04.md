## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Company Description** 
 Advance World Group is dedicated to providing global solutions that enhance human comfort while protecting the environment. With a focus on refrigeration and air conditioner parts, the company aims to be a leading manufacturer and supplier in its field. Advance World Group emphasizes quality talent and innovation to deliver reliable products and services to customers worldwide. Team members join a growing organization committed to sustainability, technical excellence, and continuous improvement.
 



**Role Description** 
 This is a full\-time, on\-site Accountant role based in Dubai. The Accountant will manage day\-to\-day financial operations, including recording transactions, maintaining the general ledger, and preparing monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports. Responsibilities include processing accounts payable and receivable, reconciling bank statements, supporting budgeting and forecasting activities, and assisting with audits and compliance requirements. The role also involves monitoring cost controls, analyzing financial data to support management decisions, and ensuring adherence to relevant accounting standards and company policies. The Accountant will collaborate with cross\-functional teams and contribute to continuous improvement of financial processes and internal controls.
 



**Qualifications** 



* Strong skills in general accounting, including journal entries, general ledger maintenance, and financial reporting.
* Experience with accounts payable, accounts receivable, invoicing, and bank reconciliations.
* Knowledge of budgeting, forecasting, cost control, and basic financial analysis.
* Proficiency in accounting software and MS Excel, with attention to detail and accuracy.
* Understanding of relevant accounting standards, internal controls, and compliance practices.
* Masters degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field; professional certification (e.g., CPA, ACCA) is an advantage.
* Effective communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work in a structured, on\-site environment.
* Experience in manufacturing or industrial sectors, especially refrigeration or HVAC, is beneficial.
