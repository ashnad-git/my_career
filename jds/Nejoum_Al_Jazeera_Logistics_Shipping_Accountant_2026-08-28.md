**About the Role**

Nejoum Al Jazeera is looking for an experienced Accountant with strong accounting and finance experience in the Shipping / Freight Forwarding industry. The role involves managing accounting activities, supplier and carrier accounts, invoices, payments, reconciliations, and financial reporting.

Shipping industry finance experience is a key requirement, with practical knowledge of ocean freight transactions, shipping line invoices, freight charges, detention, demurrage, storage, terminal charges, and related financial transactions.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting and finance activities.
* Manage Accounts Payable (AP) and Accounts Receivable (AR).
* Process, verify, and record supplier and shipping line invoices.
* Reconcile shipping line, supplier, and vendor statements.
* Monitor outstanding balances and payment due dates.
* Coordinate directly with shipping lines regarding invoices, payments, credit notes, refunds, and account statements.
* Follow up on outstanding invoices and ensure payments are made within agreed payment terms and credit periods.
* Review shipping\-related invoices and identify discrepancies, duplicate charges, incorrect rates, or unsupported charges.
* Coordinate with shipping lines and internal teams to resolve billing discrepancies and payment\-related issues.
* Maintain accurate records of carrier invoices, payments, credit notes, refunds, and outstanding balances.
* Handle accounting for ocean freight, terminal charges, detention, demurrage, storage, documentation charges, and other shipping\-related expenses.
* Perform bank, supplier, and carrier reconciliations.
* Prepare journal entries and support monthly, quarterly, and year\-end closing activities.
* Prepare financial reports and provide accurate financial information to management.
* Monitor cash flow and upcoming payment obligations.
* Maintain proper accounting documentation and financial records.
* Ensure compliance with UAE VAT regulations and company financial policies.
* Coordinate with internal and external auditors when required.
* Work with the Operations and Shipping teams on finance\-related matters.
* Perform other accounting and finance duties as assigned.

**Shipping / Freight Forwarding Finance Experience – Key Requirement**

* Shipping / Freight Forwarding finance experience is a key requirement for this position.
* Must have practical experience working in the Finance or Accounts Department of a shipping line, freight forwarder, NVOCC, logistics company, or similar organization.
* Direct experience dealing with major ocean shipping lines from a finance/accounting perspective is strongly preferred.
* Experience dealing with major carriers such as Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag\-Lloyd, Evergreen, Arkas, Turkon, SeaLead, ZIM, HMM, OOCL, or similar ocean carriers will be an advantage.
* Must be comfortable communicating directly with shipping line Finance, Billing, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, Collections, or Customer Service teams regarding financial matters.

**Requirements**

* Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.

**Preferred Experience**

Preference will be given to candidates who have:

* Worked in the Finance / Accounts Department of a shipping line, freight forwarder, NVOCC, or logistics company.
* Managed financial accounts for major ocean carriers.
* Experience handling high\-volume shipping line invoices.
* Experience resolving carrier billing and invoice disputes.
* Experience with shipping\-related payment terms and credit facilities.
* Experience with Odoo, SAP, Cargo Wise, or similar ERP/accounting systems.
* Experience in the automotive shipping industry is an advantage.

**Minimum Qualifications \& Skills**

* Minimum 1\-2 years of accounting experience.
* Mandatory experience in Shipping, Freight Forwarding, Logistics, NVOCC, or a Shipping Line environment.
* Strong accounting experience within a shipping/freight\-forwarding company.
* Experience dealing directly with shipping lines is highly preferred.
* Strong understanding of shipping\-related invoices and financial transactions.
* Good knowledge of UAE VAT regulations.
* Experience with ERP and accounting systems.
* Strong Microsoft Excel skills.
* Excellent English communication skills, both written and verbal.
* Strong reconciliation and analytical skills.
* Excellent attention to detail.
* Ability to manage multiple accounts, invoices, and payment deadlines.
* Ability to work independently and meet strict financial deadlines.

**Important – Please Read Before Applying**

This is a **Finance \& Accounting position, not a Shipping Operations position**.

Candidates must have strong accounting/finance skills **and practical finance experience in the shipping/freight\-forwarding industry**.

Candidates with general accounting experience but no shipping/freight\-forwarding background may not be suitable for this position.
