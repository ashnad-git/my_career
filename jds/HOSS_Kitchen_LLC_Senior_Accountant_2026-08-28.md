## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Accounting \& Bookkeeping**

* Manage full\-cycle bookkeeping using Zoho Books and Tally ERP / TallyPrime
* Process accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliations, and petty cash
* Handle daily retail POS reconciliation with accounting software
* Prepare month\-end and year\-end closing entries and financial statements

**UAE VAT \& FTA Compliance**

* Prepare and file quarterly VAT returns on the FTA EmaraTax portal
* Ensure correct VAT treatment for retail transactions (standard, zero\-rated, exempt)
* Manage VAT\-compliant invoices, credit notes, and maintain proper tax records
* Handle FTA audit queries and voluntary disclosures if required

**Corporate Tax (CT)**

* Support UAE Corporate Tax registration and annual filing (effective June 2023\)
* Maintain documentation for qualifying income, allowable deductions, and transfer pricing
* Liaise with external tax consultants as required

**Zoho Books**

* Manage invoicing, expenses, banking, and inventory modules in Zoho Books
* Generate P\&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow reports from Zoho Books
* Set up automated workflows, payment reminders, and approval flows
* Integrate Zoho Books with Zoho Inventory / Zoho CRM for multi\-branch retail operations

**Tally ERP / TallyPrime**

* Maintain ledgers, vouchers, cost centres, and stock groups in Tally
* Generate MIS reports, trial balances, and audit\-ready exports
* Manage inventory valuation (FIFO / Weighted Average) and stock movement in Tally

**Payroll \& HR Finance**

* Process monthly payroll in compliance with UAE Labour Law and WPS (Wages Protection System)
* Calculate end\-of\-service gratuity, leave salary, and final settlements
* Maintain salary registers and payroll journals

**Reporting \& Audit**

* Prepare monthly MIS reports, variance analysis, and management dashboards
* Coordinate with internal and external auditors
* Track KPIs: gross margin, COGS, inventory turnover, and working capital

**Requirements**

**Education**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or Commerce (B.Com / BBA Finance)
* Professional certification preferred: CA (Inter/Final), ACCA, CMA, or CPA

**Experience**

* 3–4 years of accounting experience with at least 2 years in UAE retail sector
* Hands\-on Zoho Books experience — MANDATORY
* Hands\-on Tally ERP 9 / TallyPrime experience — MANDATORY
* Practical UAE VAT filing and FTA EmaraTax portal experience — MANDATORY
* UAE Corporate Tax exposure is a strong advantage

**Technical Skills**

* Zoho Books — invoicing, expenses, inventory, banking, reports
* Tally ERP 9 / TallyPrime — ledgers, MIS, inventory, payroll
* UAE VAT Law (Decree\-Law No. 8 of 2017\) and Corporate Tax Law (Decree\-Law No. 47 of 2022\)
* MS Excel — Advanced (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, financial modelling)
* POS system integration and multi\-branch retail accounting
