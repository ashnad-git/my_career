## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Accountant**

**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time

**About ICAN**

ICAN is a UAE\-based management consultancy supporting organizations across strategy, transformation, human capital, talent, technology, and professional services. We work with government entities, large corporates, and leading regional and international organizations across the UAE and wider GCC.

We are looking for an **Accountant** to manage ICAN’s day\-to\-day accounting and financial administration while ensuring accurate reporting, strong financial controls, and compliance with UAE requirements.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Manage day\-to\-day bookkeeping and maintain accurate accounting records.
* Prepare and issue client invoices and track outstanding receivables.
* Manage supplier invoices, consultant payments, employee reimbursements, and accounts payable.
* Perform bank and credit card reconciliations.
* Maintain project\-level revenue, cost, and profitability records.
* Track consultant, contractor, and third\-party costs against individual projects.
* Maintain accurate records of purchase orders, contracts, invoices, and payment milestones.
* Support monthly management accounts, including P\&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and supporting schedules.
* Monitor cash flow, upcoming collections, and payment obligations.
* Prepare VAT calculations, supporting documentation, and VAT return submissions.
* Support UAE Corporate Tax compliance and coordinate with external tax advisors/auditors where required.
* Maintain payroll\-related financial records and support salary processing.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
* Maintain proper financial documentation and audit trails.
* Support year\-end closing, external audits, and regulatory requirements.
* Ensure appropriate financial controls and identify discrepancies or financial risks.

**Requirements**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **3\+ years of relevant accounting experience**, preferably within the UAE.
* Strong understanding of UAE VAT, Corporate Tax, and general accounting requirements.
* Experience managing accounts payable, accounts receivable, reconciliations, and month\-end closing.
* Strong Microsoft Excel skills.
* Experience with accounting platforms such as **Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero, or similar systems**.
* High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
* Ability to independently manage multiple entities, clients, projects, and transactions.
* Strong English communication skills; Arabic is an advantage.
* Experience within consulting, professional services, recruitment, or project\-based businesses is an advantage.

**What We Are Looking For**

We are looking for someone who goes beyond bookkeeping. The right candidate should be able to maintain clean financial records while also giving management clear visibility into **cash flow, collections, project profitability, costs, and overall financial performance**.

Candidates must currently be based in the **UAE**. **Must have UAE Experience.**

Pay: AED4,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
