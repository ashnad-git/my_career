## Full Job Description (verbatim)

About AccouConsult

AccouConsult Accounting is a Dubai\-based accounting and financial services company providing accounting, taxation, VAT, financial advisory, and business support services to clients in the UAE.

We are looking for a detail\-oriented and responsible **Accountant – Company Accounts** to manage the company's internal accounting activities, with particular emphasis on **billing, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and day\-to\-day financial record keeping**.

Key Responsibilities

1\. Company Accounting

* Manage and maintain the company's day\-to\-day accounting records.
* Record and reconcile all company income and expenses accurately.
* Maintain proper accounting documentation and supporting records.
* Perform regular bank, cash, receivable, and payable reconciliations.
* Monitor company expenses and ensure proper accounting classification.
* Maintain accurate ledgers and accounting schedules.
* Assist management with monthly and periodic financial reports.Bills \& Invoicing
* Prepare and issue company invoices and bills to customers/clients.

2\. Accounts Payable \& Receivable

* Maintain accurate records of company payables and receivables.
* Process supplier payments after obtaining the required approvals.
* Track outstanding supplier invoices and customer receivables.
* Reconcile supplier and customer accounts periodically.
* Prepare payment schedules and outstanding reports for management.
* Ensure timely settlement of approved company expenses and supplier bills.

3\. VAT \& Compliance Support

* Maintain proper VAT\-related records and supporting documents for company transactions.
* Ensure invoices and expenses are recorded with the correct VAT treatment.
* Assist in preparing information required for VAT return filing.
* Maintain organized financial records for audit and compliance purposes.
* Coordinate with external accountants, auditors, tax consultants, or management when required.

4\. Financial Reporting \& Management Support

* Prepare monthly accounting reports and schedules.
* Assist in preparing profit and loss statements, balance sheet schedules, and cash\-flow information.
* Prepare expense and payroll reports for management review.
* Monitor company cash flow and upcoming payment obligations.
* Highlight unusual transactions, discrepancies, or financial issues to management.
* Provide financial information and reports whenever required by management.

5\. General Finance \& Administrative Responsibilities

* Maintain proper filing of invoices, bills, receipts, payment vouchers, payroll records, and other financial documents.
* Ensure confidentiality of company and employee financial information.
* Coordinate with banks and financial institutions when required.
* Assist with audit requirements and provide necessary financial documents.
* Support management with other accounting and finance\-related tasks as required.

Required Qualifications \& Experience

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum **2–4 years of relevant accounting experience**.
* UAE accounting experience will be an advantage.
* Strong practical experience in **payroll and payslip preparation**.
* Experience in **billing, invoicing, accounts payable, and accounts receivable**.
* Good understanding of UAE accounting and VAT practices.
* Knowledge of UAE payroll and WPS processes is preferred.
* Experience using accounting software such as **Odoo, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally, or similar systems**.
* Strong knowledge of Microsoft Excel.
* Good numerical and analytical skills.
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

Preferred Skills

* UAE VAT knowledge
* Invoice and bill management
* Bank and ledger reconciliation
* Accounts payable and receivable
* Financial reporting
* Excel and accounting software
* WPS/payroll processing
* Time management and organization
* Confidentiality and integrity
* Ability to work independently

Key Competencies

* **Accuracy \& Attention to Detail:** Ensures payroll, bills, invoices, and accounting entries are error\-free.
* **Confidentiality:** Maintains strict confidentiality of employee salaries and company financial information.
* **Accountability:** Takes ownership of the company's accounting and payroll activities.
* **Organization:** Maintains systematic and up\-to\-date financial records.
* **Analytical Thinking:** Identifies discrepancies and financial issues promptly.
* **Time Management:** Ensures payroll, invoices, bills, and reports are completed within deadlines.
* **Communication:** Coordinates effectively with HR, management, suppliers, employees, and external parties.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* 100% accurate and timely monthly payroll processing.
* Payslips issued accurately and within the required payroll timeline.
* Timely preparation and recording of company invoices and bills.
* Timely reconciliation of bank, supplier, customer, and ledger accounts.
* Minimum accounting/payroll errors and discrepancies.
* Timely submission of required financial information to management.
* Proper maintenance of accounting and payroll documentation.
* Timely processing of approved supplier payments and employee\-related payments.
* Compliance with company accounting procedures and applicable UAE requirements.
* Verify supporting documents before raising invoices.
* Record supplier invoices and company expenses accurately.
* Review bills for accuracy, approvals, VAT treatment, and supporting documentation.
* Maintain proper records of outstanding receivables and payables.
* Follow up on pending customer payments where required.
* Coordinate with management regarding billing requirements and payment status.
* Ensure invoices are properly recorded in the accounting system.
* Ensure all financial transactions are recorded accurately and on time.
