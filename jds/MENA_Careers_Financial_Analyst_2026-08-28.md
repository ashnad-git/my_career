## Full Job Description (verbatim)

The Role
   

  

 The Senior Financial Analyst supports the Senior Finance Manager within the global corporate finance team, covering corporate finance, treasury operations, and funding requirements across a Financial Services Division of ten entities. The role operates under a structured governance framework and spans financial modeling, cash management, asset and liability management, and policy compliance.
   

  

 Key Responsibilities
   

  

 Corporate Financing
   

  

* Build financial models and consolidated projections to support strategic planning, funding execution, and cost optimization.
* Prepare Direct Cash Flow (DCF) forecasts aligned with the annual budget and quarterly forecasts.
* Analyze Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and funding costs and provide recommendations to improve financial efficiency.
* Evaluate and support optimization of capital and funding structures to enhance returns and reduce funding costs.
* Assess the funding impact of capital expenditure and provide management insights on portfolio changes.
* Support execution of funding transactions through coordination with the Global Treasury Centre (GTC).
* Monitor capital adequacy and liquidity to support decision\-making.


 Treasury \& Cash Management
   

  

* Prepare and review bi\-weekly cash flow forecasts and ensure stakeholder alignment.
* Monitor compliance with debt limits and facility covenants, identifying potential breaches in advance.
* Maintain real\-time visibility of cash positions to support operational funding and debt servicing.
* Optimize liquidity through intercompany funding structures and cash reallocation strategies.
* Coordinate with GTC on bank management and facility limits.
* Develop dashboards for real\-time monitoring of facility utilization and funding cost drivers.


 Asset and Liability Management
   

  

* Implement asset and liability strategies in line with ALCO guidelines.
* Prepare assessments of asset liability mismatches for ALCO reporting.
* Monitor capital, liquidity, and interest rate risks through risk analysis and stress testing.
* Assist in determining funding strategies and balance sheet optimization objectives.


 Policies, Procedures \& Governance
   

  

* Manage accounting procedures related to debt and equity funding.
* Support preparation of fiscal records, financial plans, and policies at the divisional level.
* Ensure compliance with relevant policies and regulatory guidelines.
* Prepare internal approvals related to funding and treasury activities.
* Develop SLAs to govern treasury activities with Business Units.
* Assist in ALCO policy development and compliance.
* Review documentation for compliance with Delegation of Authority, including banking mandates.
* Perform KYC duties and update documents with banks in consultation with relevant teams.


**Skills \& Experience**
* 2 to 5 years of experience in corporate finance, treasury, banking, or financial services.
* Qualified in Finance and/or Accounting; professional certifications such as MBA, ACCA, CA, CIMA, or CFA are preferred.
* Strong financial analysis and modeling skills.
* Advanced proficiency in Excel and Power BI.
* Proficiency in SAP BPC and Anaplan.
* Understanding of financial markets, forex, and treasury accounting, including FX, liquidity, and financial instruments.
* Understanding of balance sheet risks and mitigation strategies.
* Effective stakeholder management and communication skills.
