**Job Title: Finance Manager**  
**Location: Dubai, UAE**  
**Company: AKA Management Consultancy**

**About the Role:**  
We are seeking a highly skilled and detail\-oriented **Finance Manager** to lead the Finance Department of AKA Management Consultancy. The ideal candidate must be an **ACCA Member, CA or CPA**, with at least **2\-3 years of experience in the UAE**. This role requires a proactive professional with strong financial management, reporting, and leadership capabilities.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Oversee all financial operations and ensure full compliance with UAE regulations.
* Prepare and analyze financial reports, budgets, and forecasts.
* Manage day\-to\-day operations of the Finance Department.
* Monitor cash flow, fund management, and financial performance.
* Ensure timely and accurate month\-end and year\-end closings.
* Coordinate with auditors, tax consultants, and external stakeholders.
* Support management with strategic financial planning and decision\-making.

**Requirements:**

* ACCA, CA or CPA qualification is mandatory.
* Minimum of 3 years UAE experience in a finance/accounting leadership role.
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax regulations.
* Proven experience in budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
* Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
* Proficient in accounting software and MS Excel.

**What We Offer:**

* Competitive salary and benefits package
* Collaborative and supportive work environment
* Opportunity for professional growth and leadership
