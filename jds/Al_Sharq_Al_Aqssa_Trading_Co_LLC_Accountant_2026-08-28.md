## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Position Overview**

We are seeking a competent and detail\-oriented Accountant \& Taxation Executive to manage day\-to\-day accounting activities and support UAE VAT and Corporate Tax compliance. The ideal candidate should have practical UAE accounting experience and a good understanding of applicable tax regulations.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1\. Accounting \& Bookkeeping**

* Maintain complete and accurate accounting records in accordance with applicable accounting standards.
* Record sales, purchases, receipts, payments, expenses, and other financial transactions.
* Prepare and maintain general ledgers, journals, and supporting schedules.
* Perform bank, customer, supplier, and inter\-company reconciliations.
* Monitor accounts receivable and payable and follow up on outstanding balances.
* Assist in preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.

**2\. UAE VAT Compliance**

* Handle VAT accounting and reconciliation.
* Prepare and review VAT returns and ensure timely submission to the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).
* Verify tax invoices, credit notes, and supporting documentation.
* Review VAT treatment of local, import, export, and other transactions.
* Maintain VAT records and supporting documents for audit and FTA requirements.
* Assist with VAT registration, deregistration, amendments, and voluntary disclosures where required.

**3\. Corporate Tax**

* Assist with UAE Corporate Tax registration and compliance.
* Review accounting records for Corporate Tax purposes.
* Assist in determining taxable income and applicable adjustments.
* Prepare information and supporting schedules for Corporate Tax return filing.
* Monitor Corporate Tax deadlines and compliance requirements.
* Assist with Corporate Tax assessments, queries, and documentation requested by the FTA.

**4\. Financial Reporting \& Audit**

* Prepare management reports and financial schedules.
* Assist in year\-end closing and preparation of audit working papers.
* Coordinate with external auditors and provide required financial information.
* Identify accounting discrepancies and recommend appropriate corrections.

**5\. Compliance \& Documentation**

* Ensure proper maintenance of accounting and tax records.
* Maintain organized documentation for audit and regulatory purposes.
* Keep updated with changes in UAE accounting, VAT, and Corporate Tax regulations.
* Support management in implementing appropriate financial controls and procedures.

**Required Skills \& Experience**

· Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting / Finance.

· Minimum 2 years of UAE accounting experience.

· Good practical knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax.

· Proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel.

· Strong understanding of bookkeeping and reconciliations.

· Good analytical and problem\-solving skills.

· Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

· Ability to manage multiple assignments and meet statutory deadlines.

· Good written and verbal communication skills.

**Preferred Candidate Profile**

* Practical experience in UAE accounting and taxation.
* Familiarity with Federal Tax Authority (FTA) requirements.
* Ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
* Professional approach with high standards of confidentiality and integrity.

Willingness to stay updated with changes in UAE tax and accounting regulations.

Pay: AED2,000\.00 \- AED3,500\.00 per month
