## Full Job Description (verbatim)

General Accountant

**Company:** Al Maimana Accounting \& Bookkeeping  
**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Employment Type:** Full\-Time  
**Department:** Accounting \& Finance

Job Summary

Al Maimana Accounting \& Bookkeeping is looking for a **skilled and detail\-oriented General Accountant** with strong hands\-on experience in **bookkeeping, VAT compliance, and UAE Corporate Tax**.

The ideal candidate should be capable of independently managing day\-to\-day accounting activities for multiple clients, maintaining accurate books of accounts, preparing financial reports, and handling **VAT returns and Corporate Tax filing/compliance** in accordance with UAE regulations.

This position is particularly suitable for an accountant who has strong practical experience in an **accounting/bookkeeping or consultancy firm** and is comfortable managing multiple client accounts.

Key ResponsibilitiesBookkeeping \& Accounting

* Maintain complete and accurate books of accounts for multiple clients.
* Record daily accounting transactions, including sales, purchases, expenses, receipts, and payments.
* Maintain accounts payable and accounts receivable records.
* Prepare and reconcile bank, customer, supplier, and general ledger accounts.
* Perform monthly and year\-end account closing procedures.
* Prepare trial balances and financial statements.
* Review accounting records to identify discrepancies and ensure accuracy.
* Maintain proper accounting documentation and supporting records.
* Assist clients with accounting queries and provide practical accounting support.

VAT Compliance \& Filing

* Prepare and review VAT workings and supporting schedules.
* Prepare and submit **UAE VAT returns** through the FTA portal.
* Ensure VAT transactions are properly recorded and classified.
* Reconcile VAT accounts with accounting records.
* Review input and output VAT calculations.
* Monitor VAT filing deadlines and ensure timely submissions.
* Assist with VAT registration, deregistration, and related FTA requirements when required.
* Handle VAT\-related queries and support clients during FTA reviews or audits.

Corporate Tax Compliance

* Assist in preparing **UAE Corporate Tax calculations and returns**.
* Review accounting records and financial statements for Corporate Tax compliance.
* Prepare supporting schedules and working papers for Corporate Tax filing.
* Ensure taxable income and applicable adjustments are properly calculated.
* Monitor Corporate Tax registration and filing deadlines.
* Assist clients with Corporate Tax\-related requirements and FTA correspondence.
* Maintain proper documentation and records to support Corporate Tax filings.

Client Management

* Handle accounting and tax requirements for multiple clients.
* Communicate professionally with clients to obtain required accounting documents and information.
* Follow up with clients for missing invoices, bank statements, expense records, and other supporting documents.
* Explain accounting, VAT, and Corporate Tax matters to clients in a clear and professional manner.
* Ensure client work is completed accurately and within agreed deadlines.

Required Qualifications \& Experience

* Bachelor's degree in **Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field**.
* Minimum **2–3 years of relevant UAE accounting experience**.
* Strong hands\-on experience in **bookkeeping and general accounting**.
* Practical experience in **UAE VAT preparation and filing** is mandatory.
* Practical knowledge of **UAE Corporate Tax and Corporate Tax return filing** is highly preferred.
* Experience working in an **accounting, audit, bookkeeping, or tax consultancy firm** is strongly preferred.
* Candidates should have experience handling accounts for multiple clients.

Technical Skills

The candidate should have strong practical knowledge of:

* General Ledger
* Accounts Payable \& Receivable
* Bank Reconciliation
* Trial Balance
* Financial Statements
* VAT Reconciliation
* VAT Return Preparation \& Filing
* UAE Corporate Tax
* Corporate Tax Return Preparation \& Filing
* Accounting software and ERP systems
* Microsoft Excel

Experience with **Odoo, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Xero, or similar accounting software** is an advantage.

Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone who is:

* Strong in **bookkeeping and accounting fundamentals**.
* Technically competent in UAE **VAT and Corporate Tax**.
* Detail\-oriented and highly accurate.
* Able to manage multiple client accounts and deadlines.
* Comfortable working independently with minimal supervision.
* Good at identifying and correcting accounting discrepancies.
* Professional and confident when communicating with clients.
* Able to work under pressure and meet tax filing deadlines.
* Willing to continuously learn and stay updated with UAE tax regulations.

Salary \& Benefits

* **Starting Salary: AED 4,000 per month.**
* Final salary is **negotiable during the interview** based on the candidate's experience, qualifications, and technical capabilities.
* Employment visa.
* Medical insurance.
* Annual leave and public holidays as per UAE Labour Law.
* Professional development and career growth opportunities.
* Opportunity to work with a diverse portfolio of UAE\-based clients.

Important Requirement

**Strong practical bookkeeping experience is mandatory.**

Candidates must have hands\-on experience with **UAE VAT filing**, and candidates with practical **Corporate Tax filing/compliance experience** will be given preference.

Candidates with only theoretical accounting knowledge or without practical UAE accounting and tax experience will not be considered.
