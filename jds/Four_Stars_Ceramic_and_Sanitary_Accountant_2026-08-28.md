## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Title: Accountant (Female Preferred)

**Job Location:** Sharjah, UAE

**Job Type:** Full\-time

We are seeking a detail\-oriented, trustworthy, and organized **Female Accountant** to manage our day\-to\-day financial operations, bookkeeping, and tax compliance. The ideal candidate will have strong analytical skills, experience handling corporate accounts, and the ability to work independently.

*Note: Possession of a valid UAE driving license is highly preferred.*

**Key Responsibilities**

* **Bookkeeping \& Ledgers:** Manage daily accounting entries, accounts payable/receivable, and general ledger reconciliation.
* **Financial Reporting:** Prepare accurate monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements (Balance Sheet, Profit \& Loss, Cash Flow).
* **Invoicing \& Payments:** Issue commercial invoices, track outstanding payments, and follow up closely on collections.
* **VAT Compliance:** Prepare, review, and file quarterly UAE VAT returns in accordance with FTA regulations.
* **Banking Operations:** Handle bank reconciliations, monitor cash flow, and manage petty cash accounts.
* **Inventory \& Costing:** (If applicable) Monitor inventory valuations and coordinate with operations to reconcile physical stock with system data.

**Requirements \& Qualifications**

* **Gender:** Female Preferred
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **Experience:** Minimum of 2–4 years of accounting experience (preferably within the UAE).
* **Software Skills:** Proficiency in MS Excel and accounting software (such as Zoho Books, QuickBooks, or Tally).
* **Driving License:** A valid UAE driving license is a strong advantage.
* **Languages:** Fluent in English (both written and verbal). Knowledge of Urdu/Hindi or Arabic is a plus.
