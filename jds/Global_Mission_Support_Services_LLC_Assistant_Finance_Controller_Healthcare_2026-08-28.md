## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Title:** Assistant Finance Controller  
**Location:** Naoero  
**Industry:** Healthcare/Hospital

**Job Description:**  
We are seeking a detail\-oriented and experienced **Assistant Finance Controller** to support our finance team in the healthcare sectors. The ideal candidate will assist with financial reporting, budgeting, and internal controls while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Assist the Finance Controller in preparing monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.
* Support budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis for departments (medical services, pharmacy, etc.).
* Maintain accurate financial records and general ledger entries.
* Assist with internal and external audits, ensuring compliance with tax and statutory regulations.
* Monitor cash flow, accounts payable, and accounts receivable.
* Help implement financial policies, procedures, and internal controls.
* Provide financial insights and reports to management for strategic decision\-making.
* Support process improvements and automation initiatives in finance operations.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field.
* Professional certification required: **CPA, ACCA, CMA, or equivalent**.
* This job is overseas job in Naoero country, must be willing to relocate alone for the job.
* Minimum 3–5 years’ experience in finance, preferably in healthcare sectors.
* Strong understanding of financial reporting, budgeting, and accounting principles.
* Proficient in ERP systems (e.g., Oracle, Odoo, Opera, Micros) and Microsoft Excel.
* Excellent analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* Ability to work under pressure and meet tight deadlines.
