## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Accountant – Job Description

We are looking for an **Accountant** with relevant accounting experience and strong knowledge of **Tally**. The ideal candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, recording daily transactions, preparing reports, reconciling accounts, and supporting day\-to\-day accounting operations.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Maintain accurate and up\-to\-date accounting records using **Tally**.
* Record daily sales, purchases, receipts, payments, and journal entries.
* Prepare and issue invoices, receipts, and payment vouchers.
* Perform **bank, supplier, and customer account reconciliations**.
* Monitor accounts receivable and accounts payable.
* Follow up on outstanding payments and maintain proper records.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and accounting summaries.
* Assist with VAT calculations, records, and filing requirements.
* Maintain proper documentation of all financial transactions.
* Check and verify invoices, expenses, and supporting documents.
* Assist with payroll and other accounting\-related administrative tasks.
* Coordinate with management, suppliers, customers, and other departments regarding financial matters.
* Ensure accounting records are accurate and properly maintained.
* Assist with audits and provide required financial documents when needed.

**Requirements:**

* Degree or diploma in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* Minimum **2–3 years of relevant accounting experience**.
* Previous experience in the **retail industry** is highly preferred and will be considered an advantage.
* Strong hands\-on experience with **Tally / Tally ERP** is required.
* Good knowledge of bookkeeping and accounting principles.
* Experience with **UAE VAT** is an advantage.
* Proficiency in **MS Excel** and other accounting software.
* Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and organizational skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
* Good communication and problem\-solving skills.
* **UAE experience is preferred.**

Pay: AED3,000\.00 \- AED3,500\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
