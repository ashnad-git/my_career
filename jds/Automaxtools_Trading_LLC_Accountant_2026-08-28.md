## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Title:** Senior Accountant  
**Department:** Finance \& Accounts  
**Reports To:** Finance Manager

**Job Summary:**

The Senior Accountant is responsible for overseeing daily accounting operations, ensuring accurate financial reporting, and maintaining compliance with relevant tax laws and financial regulations. The role involves managing accounts payable/receivable, general ledger, inventory accounting, and financial analysis tailored to the trading industry.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* **General Accounting:**
* Maintain the general ledger and ensure accurate month\-end and year\-end closings.
* Prepare journal entries, reconcile accounts, and ensure proper documentation.
* **Financial Reporting:**
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in compliance with IFRS/local GAAP.
* Support the preparation of budgets, forecasts, and financial reports for management.
* **Accounts Payable \& Receivable:**
* Oversee the processing of vendor invoices, payments, and customer billing.
* Monitor aging reports and ensure timely collections and disbursements.
* **Inventory \& Cost Accounting:**
* Reconcile inventory accounts and ensure accurate valuation.
* Monitor COGS, import costs, and landed costs related to trade operations.
* **Compliance \& Audit:**
* Ensure compliance with tax regulations, VAT, customs duties, and other statutory requirements.
* Liaise with external auditors and support internal audits.
* **Process Improvement:**
* Identify opportunities for automation and process optimization in financial operations.
* Contribute to the development of internal controls and accounting policies.
* **Team Support:**
* Guide and mentor junior accountants and assist in team development.
* Collaborate with procurement, logistics, and sales teams to ensure financial accuracy.

**Qualifications \& Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field (CPA, CMA, or equivalent is a plus).
* Minimum 2\-5 years of experience in accounting, preferably in a trading or distribution environment.
* Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Proficient in ERP/accounting systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, or similar).
* Advanced MS Excel skills.
* Excellent analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* Ability to meet tight deadlines and handle multiple tasks.

Job Types: Full\-time, Permanent
