 **Finance Manager / Financial Controller**

 The successful candidate will oversee financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, treasury, cash\-flow management, compliance and finance\-related procurement activities while supporting the company’s operational and strategic objectives.
   

  

**Job Description**
 The Finance Manager / Financial Controller will be responsible for managing the company’s finance function and providing reliable financial information to support management decision\-making.
   

  

 The position requires a hands\-on finance professional with strong knowledge of financial reporting, IFRS, UAE VAT, budgeting, forecasting, cash\-flow management and internal controls.
   

  

 Key Responsibilities
   

  

* Prepare and present monthly financial\-performance reports to management.
* Coordinate the annual budgeting process and quarterly financial forecasts.
* Prepare and issue accurate internal and external financial reports and statements.
* Manage and regularly update cash\-flow forecasts.
* Monitor accounts receivable and implement effective collection strategies in coordination with other departments.
* Monitor accounts payable and maintain efficient payment processes.
* Manage payroll\-related financial activities, remuneration controls and associated reporting.
* Coordinate with appointed financial, statutory and local\-content auditors.
* Ensure audits are initiated and completed within the required timelines.
* Ensure compliance with applicable UAE financial, tax and corporate regulations.
* Ensure the proper application of IFRS and relevant accounting standards.
* Maintain effective financial controls, policies and procedures.
* Develop and implement finance\-related Standard Operating Procedures.
* Support management with financial, corporate, contractual and compliance\-related matters.
* Manage banking activities, including transfers, bank guarantees, treasury operations, hedging and fixed investments.
* Act as an authorized financial signatory in accordance with the applicable authority matrix.
* Oversee and guide procurement activities in line with internal policies, ethical standards, controls and cost\-efficiency objectives.
* Support the negotiation and implementation of purchasing and service agreements.
* Work closely with operational and support departments to improve financial performance and resource efficiency.
* Coordinate financial\-system updates, upgrades and implementations.
* Prepare financial dashboards, reports and decision\-support tools.
* Promote digitalization and ensure the proper organization of financial records.
* Lead, coach and develop the finance team.
* Conduct performance reviews and identify training and development requirements.
* Promote continuous improvement across finance and procurement activities.
* Ensure confidentiality, professional integrity and ethical conduct at all times.
* Comply with applicable quality, health, safety and environmental procedures.
