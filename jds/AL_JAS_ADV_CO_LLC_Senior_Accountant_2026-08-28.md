## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**About Us:**  
JAS Advertising is a leading signage and branding company in the GCC, providing innovative solutions across signage, advertising, interiors, exhibitions, and BTL activations. As we expand our operations, we are seeking a skilled **Accountant** to manage our financial operations and ensure smooth execution of all accounting activities.

**Role Overview:**  
The Senior accountant will be responsible for handling end\-to\-end accounting processes, ensuring compliance with financial regulations, preparing accurate reports, and supporting management with financial insights.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Manage full\-cycle accounting activities including accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, petty cash and bank reconciliations.
* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.
* Monitor cash flow, expense tracking, and budget controls.
* Handle VAT computation, filing, and compliance with UAE regulations.
* Assist with audits, financial analysis, and internal reporting.
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams for smooth financial operations.
* Maintain accurate documentation and records.

**Requirements:**

* **Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field (B.Com or similar mandatory).**
* **4\+ years of hands\-on accounting experience** in a corporate environment.
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles, UAE VAT, and financial regulations.
* Proficiency in MS Excel and accounting software; **Odoo ERP experience is an advantage.**
* Excellent analytical, problem\-solving, and communication skills.
* High level of accuracy, attention to detail, and ability to meet deadlines.
* Immediate Joining is required.
