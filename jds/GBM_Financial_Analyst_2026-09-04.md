## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Job Summary** 




 The Financial Analyst is qualified finance business partner which partners with the business and the business LOBs to drive actionable analytics across all key areas. He/she is a link between finance and management, acting as trusted advisors, providing valuable analysis and insights to influence the decision\-making process.
 



  





***Job Responsibilities***



* Financial activities including producing periodical reports, cash flow analysis, financial strategy, budgeting and forecasting
* Developing financial models and providing financial forecasts
* Supports on front\-end and back\-end activities as required
* Analyzing financial performance, providing financial KPIs
* Reporting back to management on the company’s finances, setting and checking targets
* Provide analysis and commentary against weekly and monthly performance
* Monitoring departmental activities, auditing and overseeing all regulatory compliances and tax affairs
* Providing analysis and insight, linking financial reports to business strategies
* Introducing effective commercial strategy
* Implementing changes to ensure that key targets are met
* Building and maintaining relationships with senior managers and their departments
* Acting as a financial advisor – offering financial support to decision makers. Proactively contribute to the delivery of a high performing, customer focused finance service to the delivery unit or commissioning area



  





***Qualifications \& Education Requirements***



* 5\+ years of financial consulting or accounting experience
* Substantial experience in a business partnering related role, capitalizing on opportunities to drive performance through collaboration
* Bachelors’ degree in Business Administration or Accounting/Finance
* MBA (optional/added advantage)
* Certifications (added advantage): CFC, CPA, ACCA, ACA
* English mandatory, Arabic is plus



  





***Skills***



* Utilizing the best financial analysis and modeling practices to inform business and financial decisions
* Excellent analytical skills with the ability to interpret raw data and translate it into clear, concise information
* The ability to explain complex financial data to non\-finance individuals
* Keeping up to date with current financial and accounting practices in the market
* SAP, Oracle, SQL, PPT, Advanced Excel
