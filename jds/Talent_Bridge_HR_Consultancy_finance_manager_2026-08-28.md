We have a vacancy for a Finance Manager at a holding company, located in Mirdif, Dubai. The group have assets in the healthcare and education.

This role will manage a small finance team, with some team members centrally allocated and others part of the various companies. Excellent management and leadership skills would therefore be essential.

A solid background in all the facets of accounting, from budgeting to auditing, would be essential, and therefore, a chartered financial professional would be essential.

Finally, candidates must have at least 5 years of experience locally, must be locally available, while candidates that are immediately available and are Arabic speakers would have a distinct advantage.

Requirements:

The ideal candidate will be a chartered financial professional with at least 5 years of experience as a senior accountant or finance manager.

You will have experience in leading a team of accountants, and you will be experienced in managing the full cycle of accounting, from budgets to statements, as well as auditing and analysis.

English would be an absolute essential skill, and Arabic would be a strong advantage.
