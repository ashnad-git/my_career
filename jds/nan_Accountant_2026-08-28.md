## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Junior Accountant

**Industry:** Manufacturing and supply of ceramic tiles, porcelain products, and sanitary ware for residential and commercial construction projects.

Duties \& Responsibilities

* Maintain accurate financial records and accounting transactions.
* Assist in managing **Accounts Payable (AP)** and **Accounts Receivable (AR)**.
* Process invoices, purchase orders, receipts, and payment transactions.
* Assist with monthly financial reports and account reconciliations.
* Assist in preparing and filing **VAT returns** and maintaining VAT records.
* Support **Corporate Tax** compliance and preparation of required documents.
* Perform bank reconciliations and monitor financial transactions.
* Maintain accounting records using **Zoho Books** or related accounting software.
* Assist with payroll\-related reports and salary calculations when required.
* Maintain proper filing and documentation of invoices, receipts, payments, and other financial records.
* Assist with month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Support external auditors by preparing financial documents and records as required.
* Ensure accounting transactions comply with UAE regulations and company policies.
* Perform other accounting and administrative duties assigned by management.

Key Skills / Requirements

* Bachelor's Degree in **Accounting, Finance, or a related field**.
* **1–2 years of accounting experience**; experience in the UAE is preferred.
* **Hands\-on experience with Zoho Books is required.**
* Basic to good knowledge of **UAE VAT filing and compliance**.
* Knowledge of **UAE Corporate Tax** is an advantage.
* Good proficiency in **Microsoft Excel and MS Office**.
* Strong numerical, analytical, and problem\-solving skills.
* Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
* Good organizational and time\-management skills.
* Ability to work independently and meet deadlines.
* Good communication and interpersonal skills.
* Experience in **manufacturing, trading, construction, building materials, or a related industry** is an advantage.
