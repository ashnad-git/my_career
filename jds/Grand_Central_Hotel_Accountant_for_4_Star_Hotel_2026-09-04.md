## Full Job Description (verbatim)

**Join our Finance team! We’re looking for an experienced Hotel Accountant** to manage daily financial operations, ensure compliance with accounting standards, and support management with timely and accurate financial reporting.

**Key Responsibilities:**

**\*Daily Operations**

* Review and verify F\&B and outlet sales reports, ensuring accurate revenue recording
* Monitor rate variances across reservations, OTA bookings, and travel agent contracts
* Reconcile daily sales reports, night audit postings from PMS/POS systems
* Prepare proforma invoices for corporate clients, travel agents, and groups
* Handle accounts payable, receivable, and general ledger transactions
* Reconcile bank statements, cash, and credit card deposits

**\*Monthly / Periodic Tasks**

* Prepare monthly financial statements, management reports, and internal audit schedules
* Submit accurate DTCM and DM reports on time
* Assist in monthly audits, VAT filing, and compliance reporting

**\*Banking \& Government Tasks**

* Visit the bank for deposits, cheque clearances, and related transactions
* Handle payments for municipality fees, DTCM fees, VAT, and other statutory bills

**\*Other Duties**

* Maintain proper documentation and filing of all financial records

**Qualifications:**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field (CPA or equivalent preferred)
* 3–5 years of accounting experience in hospitality or F\&B (UAE experience preferred)
* Strong knowledge of hotel accounting systems (Opera PMS, Sun System, QuickBooks, Sage, etc.)
* Deep understanding of UAE hotel finance operations, including DTCM, DM, and VAT reporting
* Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and data analysis
* Exceptional attention to detail and time management

Pay: From AED4,000\.00 per month

Work Location: In person
