## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a strong, experienced accountant in Dubai to manage our company accounting, UAE VAT, Corporate Tax records, payroll, and monthly reporting.

You must be able to independently set up and manage a full company in QuickBooks from zero: chart of accounts, bank feeds, customers, vendors, invoicing, bills, payroll entries, reconciliations, month\-end closing, reports, and cleanup of old incorrect transactions.

**Responsibilities**:

\* Manage complete accounting in QuickBooks Online

\* Bank, credit\-card, payroll, customer, and vendor reconciliations

\* Accounts payable, receivable, invoicing, and collections

\* Monthly P\&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and management reports

\* UAE VAT returns, VAT records, and Corporate Tax compliance

\* UAE payroll, WPS documents, leave records, and end\-of\-service calculations

\* Work with management, auditors, banks, and tax advisors as needed

**Requirements**:

\* 5\+ years of UAE accounting experience

\* 3\+ years of advanced, hands\-on QuickBooks Online experience

\* Real experience filing UAE VAT returns

\* Knowledge of UAE Corporate Tax and payroll/WPS

\* Strong Excel and English skills

\* ACCA, CPA, CMA, CA, or similar qualification is a plus

Please do not apply if your experience is limited to invoicing, data entry, or basic bookkeeping.
