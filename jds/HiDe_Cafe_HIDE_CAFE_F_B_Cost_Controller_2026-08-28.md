**About the Role**

HIDE CAFE is looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented **F\&B Cost Controller** to manage food \& beverage costing, inventory control, purchasing, wastage, and profitability across our operations.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Monitor daily food, beverage, packaging, and operational costs.
* Prepare and maintain **recipe costing and menu costing**.
* Monitor actual vs. theoretical food cost and investigate variances.
* Conduct weekly and monthly stock counts and reconcile physical stock with the POS/inventory system.
* Monitor wastage, spoilage, staff meals, complimentary items, voids, discounts, and stock losses.
* Check purchase orders, invoices, delivery notes, and supplier pricing.
* Monitor supplier price changes and identify cost\-saving opportunities.
* Maintain accurate inventory movements, transfers, production, and wastage entries.
* Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly **food cost and inventory variance reports**.
* Work closely with the Kitchen, Operations, Purchasing, Stores, and Finance teams.
* Support menu pricing and profitability analysis.
* Identify areas of leakage, over\-ordering, excessive wastage, and cost inefficiency.
* Ensure accurate POS and inventory system data.
* Support month\-end inventory closing and COGS reporting.

**Requirements**

* **2–4 years of F\&B Cost Control experience** preferred.
* UAE F\&B / restaurant / café experience is preferred.
* Strong knowledge of food costing, inventory management, and purchasing.
* Excellent Excel and analytical skills.
* Experience with POS and inventory management systems.
* Strong attention to detail and ability to identify discrepancies.
* Good communication and coordination skills.
* Ability to work independently and follow up with operational teams.
* Accounting, Finance, Hospitality, or related qualification is an advantage.

**What We’re Looking For**

Someone who doesn’t only prepare reports — **you must actively control costs, find discrepancies, reduce wastage, improve margins, and challenge unnecessary spending.**
