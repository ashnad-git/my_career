**Job Summary**
 Responsible for the controlling, receiving of F\&B items and store inventories through timely feedback and reporting on food cost to maximize profitability.
   

  

 Key Responsibilities
   

  

* Calculates F\&B costs.
* Compares invoices, requisitions, sales \& prepares reports showing cost vs sales.
* Verify Receiving Report and code correction
* Daily transfer of Sales from Point\-of\-Sale System to Inventory System
* Spot checks on purchasing, receiving, storing, issuing functions and bar inventories.
* Sales key creation and Inventory System code creation including recipe creation.
* Conducts monthly food \& butcher tests to determine yields.
* Alcohol test to determine their strength or compliance with standards.
* Compares menus vs food cost vs sales price.
* Controls portion and minimize wastage and pilferage.
* Conducts market surveys.
* Checks quality of goods received
* Ensures security in storage of goods.
* Checks invoices against goods ordered and received.
* Checks unit costs.
* Prepares recipe costs with Executive Chef and Director of F\&B.
* Monitors FOC and rebates from suppliers
* Entertainment and Duty Meal checks verification
* Posting of Daily Inter Outlet Beverage Transfers
* Produces monthly F\&B costs by outlet.
* Checks store requisitions and receipts against stock records.
* Conducts monthly spot checks of F\&B Outlets
* Participates in half yearly stock take – linen and Operating equipment
* Menu engineering.
* Inventory Turnover Report
* Beverage Potential Report
* Spot check and verify housekeeping inventories on half yearly basis
* Month End Closing Inventories (Stores and F\&B Outlets) and prepare the Bar Variance Report
* Posting of Month End JV's
* Conducts periodic purchase price comparisons of food \& beverage items and operating supplies with other hotels.
* Checks Daily Receiving Records as prepared.
* Verifies goods and services received are in line with quantities, quality and prices ordered.
* Assists F\&B decision making by providing the Management with analysis
* Preparation of P\&L for major events, Brunch and Race.
