About Paw Revolution
Paw Revolution is a growing pet food company focused on producing high-quality, nutritious products for pets. We are looking for an experienced, detail-oriented Accountant who understands the financial and operational needs of a food manufacturing business, preferably within the pet food industry.

This role is ideal for someone who is strong in day-to-day accounting, monthly reporting, inventory control, cost of sales calculations, and manufacturing cost analysis. The Accountant will play a key role in maintaining accurate financial records, monitoring inventory movement, and helping management understand the company’s key cost drivers.

Role Overview
The Manufacturing Accountant will be responsible for managing the company’s accounting records, preparing monthly financial reports, maintaining QuickBooks, calculating cost of sales, and overseeing inventory-related record keeping.
The ideal candidate must have prior experience in food manufacturing and should be comfortable working with recipes, ingredients, production inputs, finished goods, treats, packaging materials, and inventory movements. Experience in pet food manufacturing is highly preferred.

Key Responsibilities
Accounting & Financial Reporting
• Manage the company’s day-to-day accounting activities.
• Maintain accurate and up-to-date financial records in QuickBooks.?
• Prepare monthly financial statements, including profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow reports.
• Reconcile bank accounts, supplier accounts, customer accounts, and internal ledgers.
• Manage accounts payable and accounts receivable.
• Record invoices, payments, expenses, purchase orders, and other accounting entries accurately.
• Ensure all financial transactions are properly categorized and supported with documentation.
• Assist management with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
• Support year-end closing and provide required documentation to external accountants, auditors, or tax advisors.

Cost of Sales & Manufacturing Accounting
• Calculate and monitor cost of sales for the company’s products.
• Track the cost of ingredients, treats, recipes, packaging materials, and other production inputs.
• Maintain accurate product costing based on recipes, ingredient usage, yield, wastage, and production volumes.
• Review and update product costs regularly to reflect changes in ingredient prices, supplier costs, packaging costs, and other manufacturing expenses.
• Analyze gross margins by product, recipe, treat category, or production batch.
• Identify cost variances and report key findings to management.
• Support pricing decisions by providing accurate cost and margin analysis.

Inventory Record-Keeping & Stock Control
• Manage the record-keeping of the company’s inventory, including ingredients, packaging materials, finished goods, treats, and other production materials.
• Maintain accurate ingredient “in and out” records.?
• Monitor stock movements from purchase to production to finished goods.
• Ensure inventory is recorded and monitored using FIFO principles.
• Track raw materials, semi-finished goods, finished products, packaging, labels, and other manufacturing supplies.
• Coordinate with the production and operations teams to verify stock usage and production output.
• Monitor stock levels and flag shortages, overstocking, expiry risks, wastage, or unusual usage patterns.
• Assist with regular physical stock counts and inventory reconciliations.
• Investigate and resolve inventory discrepancies between physical stock and accounting records.
• Maintain proper documentation for stock adjustments, damaged goods, expired materials, and production wastage.
