About the Role

Najoom Al Jazeera is seeking an experienced **Accountant** to join our Finance Department. The ideal candidate must have solid accounting experience within **well\-established shipping, freight forwarding, logistics, or supply chain companies in the UAE**.

The successful candidate will be responsible for managing day\-to\-day accounting operations, preparing financial reports, reconciling accounts, and ensuring compliance with UAE financial regulations while supporting the company's continuous growth.

Key Responsibilities

* Maintain accurate accounting records and prepare journal entries.
* Manage Accounts Receivable (AR) and Accounts Payable (AP).
* Perform bank reconciliations and monitor cash flow.
* Prepare monthly financial reports and assist with financial statements.
* Process supplier invoices and customer payments.
* Support month\-end and year\-end closing activities.
* Ensure compliance with UAE VAT regulations and company policies.
* Coordinate with internal and external auditors.
* Maintain proper financial documentation and records.
* Assist management with budgeting, financial analysis, and reporting.
* Perform other finance\-related duties as assigned.

Requirements

* Bachelor's Degree in Accounting or Finance.
* Minimum **3 years of accounting experience in the UAE.**
* **Mandatory experience in well\-known Shipping, Freight Forwarding, Logistics, or Supply Chain companies within the UAE.**
* Experience with companies such as **Maersk, CMA CGM, MSC, Hapag\-Lloyd, Evergreen,Arkas,Turkon,**or similar organizations is highly preferred.
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT regulations.
* Experience using ERP systems and accounting software.
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
* Excellent analytical and problem\-solving abilities.
* Strong English communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

* Experience in the automotive shipping industry.
* Experience handling high\-volume financial transactions.
* Ability to work in a fast\-paced environment with multiple priorities.
