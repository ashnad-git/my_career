## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for an experienced and detail\-oriented Senior Accountant to join our finance team in Dubai. The role covers the full accounting cycle, day\-to\-day accounting operations, and the preparation of accurate reconciliations, schedules and reports for management review.

**Reporting to:** Finance Manager

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Handle the full accounting cycle, from transaction recording through month\-end closing
* Record accounts payable, accounts receivable and general ledger transactions
* Perform bank reconciliations
* Perform balance sheet and general ledger account reconciliations
* Maintain accurate inventory records and process stock movements
* Prepare month\-end closing schedules
* Prepare monthly financial and management reporting schedules
* Record and maintain accruals, prepayments and fixed asset schedules
* Prepare VAT schedules and supporting documents
* Assist with audit requirements and provide requested documentation
* Prepare supplier payment schedules for review and approval
* Prepare customer statements and receivables ageing reports
* Review accounting transactions and investigate discrepancies
* Maintain organised and complete accounting records
* Prepare accounting schedules, analyses and supporting documents as required
* Follow established accounting controls and approval procedures

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field
* 3–5 years of accounting experience, including at least 3 years in the UAE
* Hands\-on experience with the full accounting cycle and inventory accounting
* Strong knowledge of UAE VAT and accounting principles
* Experience with QuickBooks is preferred; familiarity with Odoo, Zoho, Tally or other accounting/ERP systems is an advantage
* Experience with inFlow or a similar inventory management system is an advantage
* Experience in trading, retail or e\-commerce is preferred
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills
* Strong ownership, attention to detail and ability to work independently
* Good communication skills and ability to work effectively with internal stakeholders
* Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
* Immediate availability is preferred

Please apply only if you meet the above experience requirements.

Pay: AED5,500\.00 \- AED7,500\.00 per month

Application Question(s):

* Do you have hands\-on experience with inventory accounting, including stock movements, stock reconciliations, adjustments, COGS and landed cost?
* Are you comfortable with the offered salary range of AED 5,500–7,500 per month?

Experience:

* Accounting: 3 years (Required)
* Inventory accounting : 1 year (Required)
