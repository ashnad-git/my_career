## Full Job Description (verbatim)

ACCOUNTANT / FINANCE SPECIALIST

Job Description

About the opportunity

We are looking for a detail\-oriented Accountant / Finance Specialist to manage the day\-to\-day finance function of a fast\-paced marketing agency. This role combines accurate accounting and UAE compliance with commercial support across client projects, campaign budgets, production costs, collections, and vendor payments.

What you will own

* Maintain accurate bookkeeping, general ledger entries, accounts payable, and accounts receivable.
* Prepare timely monthly management reports, including profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, and supporting schedules.
* Manage client invoicing, monitor receivables, and follow up professionally on outstanding payments.
* Process and schedule payments to suppliers, production partners, and other vendors.
* Track campaign and project budgets, committed costs, billable expenses, margins, and profitability.
* Reconcile bank accounts, customer and vendor balances, and key control accounts.
* Prepare UAE VAT and Corporate Tax workings and support accurate, on\-time filings in line with FTA requirements.
* Coordinate with account management, production, and leadership teams to improve financial visibility and cost control.

What we are looking for

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline.
* Two to three years of relevant accounting experience; UAE agency, creative, media, production, or professional\-services experience is preferred.
* Working knowledge of UAE VAT and Corporate Tax requirements.
* Hands\-on experience with Zoho Books; strong Excel skills are an advantage.
* Strong attention to detail, ownership, organization, and the ability to manage competing deadlines.
* Clear written and verbal communication skills in English, with confidence following up with clients and vendors.
