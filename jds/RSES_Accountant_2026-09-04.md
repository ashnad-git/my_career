**Job Responsibilities:**

* Follow up and collect with the suppliers for their invoices for purchases.

* Update the invoice reference in the system against the purchase bookings.
* Maintain document control, sorting and Filing the supplier invoices with respective original supplier delivery notes and with an original purchase order

* Ensures control procedures are followed.

* Maintains prepaid reconciliations and post month amortization entries.
