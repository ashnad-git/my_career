Position: CA Intern / Senior Accountant  
Location: Kerala  
Company: Mark \& Save Head Office

Role Purpose

Responsible for end\-to\-end accounting and General Ledger management across all regions, ensuring accurate books of accounts, timely month\-end closure, reconciliations, and preparation of the Trial Balance for Balance Sheet and financial statement preparation.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage and review the General Ledger across all regions/entities and ensure completeness and accuracy of accounting records.
* Coordinate and complete monthly, quarterly and annual book closing within agreed timelines.
* Review and post necessary accruals, provisions, prepayments, depreciation and closing adjustments.
* Prepare/review Balance Sheet reconciliations, investigate abnormal or long\-outstanding balances and ensure timely resolution.
* Manage and reconcile vendor and customer accounts, including ageing, advances, debit/credit balances and outstanding items.
* Maintain and reconcile the Fixed Asset Register, including capitalization, depreciation, transfers, disposals and CWIP.
* Review and reconcile intercompany/inter\-region balances and coordinate resolution of differences with regional teams.
* Review clearing, suspense and other GL accounts and ensure timely clearance.
* Prepare and review the Trial Balance, ensuring books are fully reconciled and ready for financial statement preparation.
* Maintain proper accounting schedules, supporting documentation and audit trails.
* Support internal/statutory audits, financial reporting and management reporting requirements.
* Ensure compliance with company accounting policies, SOPs and internal controls and support process improvements across regions.

Work Location: In person
