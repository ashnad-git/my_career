## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a talented Business Analyst to join our team to work on a project to develop a trading platform for the financial sector.
  

We have a strong team, challenging tasks and comfortable working conditions: flexible schedule, remote work.
#### **Responsibilities:**


* Performing business systems analysis, collecting and identifying requirements;
* Design of data models, integration interactions, description of requirements, development of status models of objects, analysis of business and system processes;
* Working in an agile team (scrum);
* Preparation of project documentation.
#### **Requirements:**


* Knowledge and outlook in the business field (financial markets, crypto instruments)
* From 4 years of experience as an analyst on web development projects (backend);
* Experience in collecting and developing business requirements from the customer, conducting business and system analysis, developing functional requirements / technical specifications with subsequent setting of development tasks;
* Knowledge of methods for preparing business and system requirements in technical documentation (UseCases, UserStories, BPMN 2\.0\);
* Knowledge of software design, development and testing methodologies;
* Understanding of microservice architecture, design skills;
* Experience in conducting cross\-platform analysis and building integration between different systems;
* Experience in supporting the development team at all stages of the product life cycle (from the beginning of requirements development to release);
* Scrum master/product owner experience is an additional plus.
* Experience in managing a digital product with 100 thousand\+ users is an additional plus.
#### **It will be an advantage:**


* Load testing experience;
* Basic knowledge of encryption algorithms;
* Understanding of the principles of operation of exchange trading;
* Introduction to blockchain technologies.
#### **We offer:**


* Comfortable working conditions: flexible schedule, the possibility to work remotely or in one of our offices.
* Team environment: working side by side with IT professionals who are ready to share their experience.
* Wide opportunities for growth and development: our working process creates numerous opportunities for learning and experimentation.
* Exciting projects: we strive for perfection in delivering results, so that every team member can proudly say, "I was involved in this project."
