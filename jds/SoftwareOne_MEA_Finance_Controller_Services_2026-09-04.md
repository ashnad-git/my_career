 Revenue Recognition \& Financial Control
 
* Review monthly revenue recognition, WIP, and unbilled revenue balances, identifying delivery issues and revenue risks.
* Ensure compliance with IFRS and internal revenue recognition policies, including percentage\-of\-completion accounting.
* Monitor backlog yield, TCV metrics, and overall financial control effectiveness.



 Project Profitability \& Risk Management
 
* Monitor project profitability, costs, and margins, proactively identifying risks and recommending corrective actions.
* Analyse resource utilisation and identify profit leakage or cost overrun risks.
* Provide timely visibility of financial risks and support intervention on underperforming projects.



 Budgeting, Forecasting \& Business Partnering
 
* Lead the annual services budgeting process and support monthly forecasting activities.
* Partner with Delivery and Services leadership to improve financial performance and decision\-making.
* Deliver structured financial reporting, analysis, and insights to support operational and strategic objectives.


**Job Requirements**

* Strong analytical and financial modeling skills
* Ability to work closely with delivery and operational teams
* Strong understanding of project profitability drivers and forecasting methodologies
* Advanced Excel and ERP/reporting system capabilities
