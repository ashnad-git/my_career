## Full Job Description (verbatim)

A leading wellness group in the UAE is looking for an experienced **Accountant** to join our finance team. The ideal candidate will have strong UAE accounting experience, hands\-on knowledge of **VAT and Corporate Tax**, and the ability to manage accounting functions across multiple locations.

Key Responsibilities

* Manage day\-to\-day accounting operations, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, reconciliations, and month\-end closing.
* Prepare and review financial reports, management accounts, and accounting schedules.
* Handle **UAE VAT compliance**, including VAT calculations, reconciliations, and timely VAT return filing.
* Maintain working knowledge of **UAE Corporate Tax** and support Corporate Tax compliance and reporting requirements.
* Manage accounting activities for **multiple branches/locations**, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting.
* Monitor intercompany transactions, branch reconciliations, and expense allocations.
* Ensure compliance with UAE accounting standards, tax regulations, and internal financial policies.
* Assist with budgeting, forecasting, cash\-flow monitoring, and financial analysis.
* Coordinate with auditors, banks, tax consultants, and other external stakeholders when required.
* Identify discrepancies and implement effective controls to improve financial accuracy and efficiency.
* Maintain proper documentation and ensure all financial records are complete and up to date.

Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
* **3–5 years of UAE accounting experience**, preferably with experience managing multiple locations.
* Strong hands\-on experience with **UAE VAT** and VAT filing.
* Good knowledge of **UAE Corporate Tax** and related compliance requirements.
* Strong knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting.
* Proficiency in accounting software and advanced Excel/spreadsheet skills.
* Strong analytical, organizational, and problem\-solving skills.
* Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities.
* Excellent attention to detail and communication skills.
* Experience in the **wellness, hospitality, retail, or multi\-outlet environment** will be an advantage.
