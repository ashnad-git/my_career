## Full Job Description (verbatim)

Job Title: Food and Beverage (F\&B) Cost Controller

**Job Type:** Full\-time

**Job Summary**  
We are seeking a detail\-oriented and analytical F\&B Cost Controller to join our finance team. In this role, you will be responsible for managing, monitoring, and optimizing all operating costs within our food and beverage operations. You will oversee inventory management, conduct variance analyses, and implement control procedures to ensure maximum profitability while maintaining high quality standards. The ideal candidate has strong financial acumen and an extensive background in the hospitality, restaurant, or catering industry.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Monitor and analyze daily food, beverage, and consumable costs across all outlets.
* Calculate actual versus theoretical food and beverage costs and thoroughly investigate any discrepancies or variances.
* Establish and maintain recipe costing models to evaluate item profitability and support menu engineering decisions.
* Oversee regular physical stock counts, inventory audits, and spot\-checks in kitchens, bars, and storage areas.
* Reconcile inventory management software data with Point of Sale (POS) system sales reports.
* Review supplier pricing, evaluate cost efficiencies, and collaborate with the procurement team to optimize purchasing agreements.
* Track waste, spoilage, and employee consumption to implement targeted waste\-reduction strategies.
* Prepare comprehensive daily, weekly, and monthly cost control reports and key performance indicator (KPI) dashboards for management review.
* Promote cost awareness and accountability across the operational kitchen and front\-of\-house teams

**Qualifications and Skills**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Hospitality Management, or a related field.
* Proven experience working specifically as an F\&B Cost Controller, Cost Accountant, or Inventory Controller in a fast\-paced restaurant group, hotel, or catering operation.
* Strong proficiency in F\&B inventory management software and POS systems (such as Materials Control, Oracle Micros, Symphony, or similar platforms).
* Advanced Microsoft Excel skills for data analysis, pivot tables, and financial reporting.
* Deep knowledge of inventory control procedures, yield testing, and butchery/recipe costing.
* Strong analytical mindset with exceptional attention to detail and data accuracy.
* Excellent communication skills to effectively collaborate with chefs, operations managers, and vendors

Qualified candidates may forward their CV and cover letter to hr@rdhsgroup.com.

Work Location: In person
