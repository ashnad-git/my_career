## Full Job Description (verbatim)

*Golden Goose is a journey of love and authenticity, leading you to embrace your true self, where tradition and innovation merge into timeless, one\-of\-a\-kind creations.* 




  





 We are looking for a passionate talent to build our regional financial foundation. The position will report directly to the Regional Head of Finance (MEINA) and will be based in our Dubai HQ.
 



  





**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES AND DUTIES** 



* Receive, accurately verify, and code vendor invoices, retail\-related expenses, and supplier bills into the corporate ERP system;
* Prepare and execute timely weekly and monthly payment runs via bank platforms, ensuring proper internal approvals and authorization compliance;
* Monitor customer/wholesale balances, allocate incoming payments, and follow up on aging receivables.
* Nurture positive relationships with local and international suppliers, performing regular statement reconciliations and resolving discrepancies with empathy and efficiency;
* Support the senior finance team and the Regional Head of Finance during month\-end closing procedures, actively contributing to accruals preparation and reporting;
* Maintain organized physical and digital financial archives, ensuring readiness for internal controls and audit requirements.



  





**REQUIREMENTS AND QUALIFICATIONS** 



* **Experience:** 
 3\-5 years of operational experience in Accounts Payable or general accounting, ideally within a dynamic retail or multinational environment in the UAE;
* **Education:** 
 Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related business field;
* **Technical Skills:** 
 Solid understanding of basic bookkeeping, double\-entry principles, and accounts payable processing workflows. Good command of MS Excel and familiarity with modern accounting software or ERPs;
* **Soft Skills:** 
 Strong organizational and time\-management skills, a proactive attitude toward problem\-solving, and a natural team\-player spirit;
* **Languages:** 
 Fluency in English is mandatory.
