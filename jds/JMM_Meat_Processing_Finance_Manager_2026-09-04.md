## Full Job Description (verbatim)

We are looking for a hands\-on **Finance Manager** to take ownership of finance and accounting for our **meat and seafood processing business**.

The role combines strong financial control with commercial finance. You will be responsible for accurate and timely reporting, budgeting and FP\&A, cash\-flow management, tax compliance and improving the systems and processes behind the finance function.

**What You Will Own**

* Month\-end close, financial reporting and balance\-sheet control.
* Budgeting, forecasting, FP\&A and variance analysis.
* Cash flow, working capital and financial discipline.
* UAE Corporate Tax, VAT, transfer pricing and e\-invoicing.
* Product costing, inventory valuation, yield, waste and margin analysis.
* Audit coordination, accounting policies and internal controls.
* Finance process standardization, automation and system improvement.

**The Best Fit**

You should bring:

* Prior experience in the **food industry** across distribution or manufacturing/processing.
* At least **5 years of finance leadership experience** within a business with annual revenue above **AED 100 million**.
* Strong technical accounting, reporting, budgeting and FP\&A capability.
* Hands\-on experience with UAE Corporate Tax and VAT.
* Practical exposure to transfer pricing and e\-invoicing.
* Strong understanding of cash flow, working capital, inventory and product costing.
* Experience improving finance processes, controls and automation.
* **CA or CMA preferred.**
