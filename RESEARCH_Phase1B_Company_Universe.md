## New Skills Evidence from Phase 1B

No major new skills categories found beyond Phase 1 matrix. However Phase 1B confirms:

1. **Jedox, Anaplan, IFS** appearing in niche FP&A tools roles — FP&A planning software is an emerging skill. Not yet high frequency but growing.
2. **"Business Partner / BP&A"** phrasing increasingly common (Al-Futtaim uses it heavily) — emphasizes communication + stakeholder management.
3. **"Commercial Analyst"** as adjacent title (Shiseido, Cobblestone) — slightly different from FP&A but overlapping skills; could be an entry path.
4. **Fixed-term / temp contracts** (Huda Beauty, Ounass, Shiseido) exist in the market — these are realistic entry points.
