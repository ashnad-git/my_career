## Detailed Role Profiles

### Rank 1: FP&A Associate

**What it is:** Junior/entry-level FP&A role; appears in larger companies, luxury brands, MNCs (e.g., Zimmermann); doesn't require prior FP&A title

**Typical requirements:**
- Finance degree (M.Sc. qualifies ✅)
- Advanced Excel (gap → close with Sprint 1)
- Financial modeling basics (gap → close with Sprint 1)
- Analytical mindset, "can learn" attitude

**Your fit:**
- M.Sc. Financial Economics — exceeds education bar
- EY brand — recognized in UAE
- Odoo ERP migration — real technical experience
- No FP&A experience — honest gap, but role is designed for entry
- Gap addressed by: 1 portfolio project demonstrating FP&A mechanics

**Application timing:** Apply now, even without projects. EY + M.Sc. alone can get you interviews.

**Salary:** AED 6,000–10,000 (significant upgrade from current AED 4,000)

**Target examples:** Zimmermann, luxury/retail brands, financial services companies, any company posting "Associate" or "Analyst I" or "Graduate"

---

### Rank 2: Financial Analyst — Junior/Mid (Smaller Companies)

**What it is:** "Financial Analyst" title at SMEs, family businesses, trading companies, mid-size private firms; less rigorous experience matching than MNCs

**Typical requirements:**
- Finance degree ✅
- Advanced Excel (gap → Sprint 1)
- Budgeting/forecasting exposure (gap → Sprint 1)
- ERP experience (Odoo qualifies ✅)
- 2–4 years finance experience (your accounting + EY counts toward this)

**Your fit:**
- Overall finance experience (EY + Promotech 2+ years) matches the years threshold
- Gap is FP&A-specific work, but smaller companies are more flexible
- Odoo migration story differentiates you from generic accountants

**Application timing:** Apply with 1 portfolio project (FP&A model). Without a project, CV looks like accounting-only.

**Salary:** AED 6,000–12,000

**Target examples:** GBM, Mohamed Hilal Group, small private equity offices, family offices, professional services firms

---

### Rank 3: Management Reporting Analyst

**What it is:** Focused on producing management packs, MIS reports, dashboards; less forecasting-heavy than pure FP&A; bridges accounting and analytics well

**Typical requirements:**
- Finance degree ✅
- Advanced Excel / Power BI
- Reporting and data accuracy focus
- Some FP&A exposure

**Your fit:**
- Your current Promotech work (management summaries, SOA, reconciliations) overlaps directly
- Gap: No formal Power BI / dashboard experience
- Addressed by: Power BI project (Sprint 2) + Power Query in Sprint 1

**Application timing:** Apply with 1–2 projects; this role title makes your current experience more directly transferable

**Salary:** AED 6,000–10,000

**Target examples:** Any company with "Reporting" or "MIS" or "Business Analyst — Finance" in the title

---

### Rank 4: FP&A Analyst (2–4 Year Requirement)

**What it is:** Dedicated FP&A function at larger companies; NAFFCO-style roles; expect real budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis work

**Typical requirements:**
- Finance degree ✅
- 2–4 years FP&A-specific experience (gap — your experience is accounting, not FP&A)
- Advanced Excel, Power BI preferred
- ERP experience (Odoo ✅, but SAP/Oracle preferred)

**Your fit:**
- Gap is significant: no FP&A job title, no formal budgeting/forecasting
- EY brand + M.Sc. help but don't fully close gap
- Portfolio projects (FP&A model + Power BI dashboard) demonstrate technical capability
- Honest: 30–40% chance of interview even with strong portfolio; competition includes people with 2+ years actual FP&A title

**Application timing:** Apply once you have 2 portfolio projects (FP&A model + Power BI). Frame cover letter around "demonstrated FP&A skills through independent projects + unique technical depth"

**Salary:** AED 8,000–15,000

**Target examples:** NAFFCO, mid-size manufacturing, logistics, FMCG companies

---

### Rank 5+: Finance Business Partner / Senior Financial Analyst / FP&A Analyst (5+ yrs)

**Skip for now.** These require 4–7 years of FP&A-specific experience, often in FP&A departments. Not realistic in 5 months. Your next role after landing Tier 1/2 is where you build toward these.

## What to Do If No Response After 30 Applications

If volume applying (Tier 1 + Tier 2) produces minimal responses after 30+ applications in month 1:

1. **Check resume / CV gap messaging:** Have someone in finance review your CV (LinkedIn connection, mentor)
2. **Add cover letter to every application** — "Easy Apply" without cover letter loses to candidates with it
3. **Expand geography:** Abu Dhabi (avg AED 16,432/month — higher pay), Sharjah (lower competition)
4. **Expand role titles:** "Finance Executive", "Finance Analyst", "Analyst — Finance", "Business Analyst (Finance)", "Planning Analyst"
5. **Try temp/contract:** TASC Outsourcing, Hays, Robert Half, Michael Page — they place Finance Analysts frequently; contract role → permanent
