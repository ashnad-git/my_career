Job Description
Roles & Responsibilities
**What you'll be doing**
As a Financial Planning and Analysis Associate for Zimmermann, you will be responsible for providing financial support for the management of the P&L and Balance Sheet for the Middle East region. Your responsibilities will include the below:
**Month End:**
  * Assist in the completion of month end close in a timely and accurate manner.
  * Support in reviewing assigned monthly P&L s providing commentary on key variances to budget / forecast.
  * Check assigned monthly balance sheet reconciliations in a timely and accurate manner.
  * Liaise with internal stakeholders on key variances and forecasts.
  * Liaise with and provide support to Chalhoub Group's Accountants, as required.


**Budget**
  * Work with internal teams to gather and consolidate budget data and supporting information.
  * Compile and format financial budgets in line with internal templates and reporting requirements.
  * Communicate budget updates, assumptions and timelines to internal stakeholders, ensuring timely follow-up.
  * Update budget trackers (ie, marketing, travel, retail miscellaneous) and circulate to relevant stakeholders.


**Board meetings / 3YP:**
  * Support Finance Manager and General Manager in the preparation of board meetings by updating & compiling various templates and support in preparing board presentations.


**Year End Audit:**
  * Support Finance Manager during the year end audit process, as required.


**Indirect Taxes:**
  * Assist with the preparation of indirect tax calculations and returns across allocated geographies, including but not limited to GST, VAT, WHT & Sales Tax
  * Support the Finance Manager as required.


Desired Candidate Profile
You have a minimum of 2 years of experience in a similar commercial finance role. You have a solid FP&A experience, including budgeting, reporting, tax calculations, invoice booking, and payment processing. You have advanced knowledge of MS office, particularly in Excel. You preferably have experience in SAP. You possess a high level of enthusiasm and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment. You are an effective communicator. You possess a hands-on proactive approach and the ability to work in a team environment.
